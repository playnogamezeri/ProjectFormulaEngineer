Research and editorial standards

  • Use the original manga, light novels, official guidebooks, publisher material and official anime sources where applicable.
  • Separate source-canon facts, anime-original material, adaptation changes, promotional content and editorial interpretation.
  • Attach chapter, episode, volume or official source references to specific claims whenever possible.
  • Use reliable image sources, verify attribution and avoid hotlinking assets that may disappear.
  • Mark uncertain, disputed or unverified information as a research gap instead of inventing an answer.
