05Relationships & Ensemble BondsEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Key ensemble counterparts and relational dynamics within Arrested Development:

  • Michael Bluth — Portrayed by Jason Bateman: Widowed single father and pragmatic 'straight man' trying to save the Bluth Company despite his family's constant ingratitude.
  • George Bluth Sr. — Portrayed by Jeffrey Tambor: Corrupt real-estate patriarch (and twin to laid-back Oscar) who schemes from prison, the attic, and house arrest.
  • Lucille Bluth — Portrayed by Jessica Walter: Sharp-tongued, hard-drinking matriarch who pits her children against each other while fiercely controlling Buster.
  • Gob Bluth — Portrayed by Will Arnett: Pompous older brother and inept magician whose grandiose illusions and brief company presidency backfire.
  • Lindsay & Tobias Fünke — Portrayed by Portia de Rossi & David Cross: Michael's vain, activist-in-name-only twin sister and her oblivious, discredited-psychiatrist-turned-actor husband.
06Story Chronology & Character DevelopmentEXPAND / COLLAPSE
SPOILER WARNING — SERIES-WIDE CHARACTER & PLOT ARCS:
  • George Sr.'s 'Light Treason': George Sr. fakes a heart attack to flee custody, hiding in Mexico and the model home's attic as the Bluths' Iraq mini-palace construction is revealed to have been secretly commissioned by the CIA.
  • The Queen Mary Finale: Season 3 reveals Lindsay was adopted (meaning George Michael and Maeby are not blood cousins) and Annyong turns over evidence against Lucille, prompting Michael and George Michael to flee on Gob's yacht.
  • Netflix Revival: Season 4 maps overlapping timelines toward Cinco de Cuatro, while Season 5 centers on the disappearance and murder trial of Lucille Austero ('Lucille 2').

Series Trajectory: After patriarch George Bluth Sr. is arrested by the SEC for corporate fraud, his level-headed son Michael Bluth tries to hold together the Bluth Company and his selfish, dysfunctional Newport Beach family.
