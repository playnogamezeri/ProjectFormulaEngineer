Series Premise Integration: Armed with a list of corrupt elites from his late father's notebook, Oliver Queen operates first as 'The Hood,' then 'The Arrow,' and finally 'Green Arrow' alongside allies John Diggle and Felicity Smoak.

06Story Chronology & Character DevelopmentEXPAND / COLLAPSE
SPOILER WARNING — SERIES-WIDE CHARACTER & PLOT ARCS:
  • No-Kill Vow & Slade Wilson: After Tommy Merlyn's death in Season 1's 'The Undertaking,' Oliver vows to stop killing, only to face his former island mentor Slade Wilson (Deathstroke) in Season 2.
  • Public Outing & Prison: In Season 6's finale, Oliver strikes an FBI deal to defeat Ricardo Diaz, publicly revealing he is Green Arrow and serving time in federal prison before being deputized in Season 7.
  • Crisis on Infinite Earths: In Season 8, Oliver sacrifices himself and becomes the Spectre to defeat the Anti-Monitor and birth a new multiverse, reuniting with Felicity in a paradise dimension.

Series Trajectory: After spending five years shipwrecked on the island of Lian Yu, billionaire playboy Oliver Queen returns to Starling City to fight crime and corruption as a bow-wielding vigilante—launching The CW's shared Arrowverse.
