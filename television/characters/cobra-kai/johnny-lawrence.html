06Story Chronology & Character DevelopmentEXPAND / COLLAPSE
SPOILER WARNING — SERIES-WIDE CHARACTER & PLOT ARCS:
  • Kreese & Silver's Takeover: John Kreese usurps Cobra Kai in Season 2, leading to a high school brawl that temporarily paralyzes Miguel. Daniel and Johnny ally in Seasons 3–4 as Terry Silver expands Cobra Kai across the Valley until being toppled in Season 5.
  • Sekai Taikai Finale: In Season 6, the united Valley dojo competes at the Sekai Taikai world tournament in Barcelona. Following tragic losses and the deaths of Kreese and Silver, Johnny reclaims Cobra Kai with Daniel's blessing and wins the world title.

Series Trajectory: Set 34 years after the 1984 All-Valley Karate Tournament, down-on-his-luck Johnny Lawrence reopens the Cobra Kai dojo to teach bullied teens—reigniting his lifelong rivalry with successful car dealer Daniel LaRusso.
