05Relationships & Ensemble BondsEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Key ensemble counterparts and relational dynamics within Breaking Bad:

  • Walter White / Heisenberg — Portrayed by Bryan Cranston: 50-year-old chemistry teacher whose cancer diagnosis catalyzes his transformation into a prideful, ruthless drug kingpin.
  • Jesse Pinkman — Portrayed by Aaron Paul: Walt's former student and partner; impulsive yet deeply empathetic, enduring severe trauma before escaping captivity.
  • Skyler White — Portrayed by Anna Gunn: Walt's wife, who moves from suspecting his lies to laundering his drug money through a car wash to protect her children.
  • Hank Schrader — Portrayed by Dean Norris: Walt's boisterous brother-in-law and dedicated DEA agent whose pursuit of Heisenberg leads to a devastating discovery.
  • Gustavo 'Gus' Fring — Portrayed by Giancarlo Esposito: Meticulous owner of Los Pollos Hermanos who secretly runs a vast meth distribution empire.
06Story Chronology & Character DevelopmentEXPAND / COLLAPSE
SPOILER WARNING — SERIES-WIDE CHARACTER & PLOT ARCS:
  • Gus Fring's Empire: In Seasons 3–4, Walt and Jesse cook in a subterranean superlab for Los Pollos Hermanos owner Gus Fring. When Gus tries to replace Walt, Walt manipulates Jesse and uses Hector Salamanca's wheelchair bomb to assassinate Gus ('Face Off').
  • Ozymandias & Finale: In Season 5, Hank discovers Walt's identity via a copy of Leaves of Grass. Hank is executed by Jack Welker's gang, Walt flees to New Hampshire, and he returns to Albuquerque in 'Felina' to rescue an enslaved Jesse and die in the lab.

Series Trajectory: Diagnosed with stage-three lung cancer, overqualified Albuquerque high school chemistry teacher Walter White teams up with former student Jesse Pinkman to cook ultra-pure methamphetamine—transforming into ruthless drug kingpin 'Heisenberg.'
