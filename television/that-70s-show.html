Narrative Context

Created by Bonnie Turner, Terry Turner, and Mark Brazill, That '70s Show aired on Fox from August 23, 1998, to May 18, 2006.

Famous for its 360-degree 'Circle' basement camera shots and nostalgic period soundtrack, it launched the careers of Topher Grace, Mila Kunis, Ashton Kutcher, and Laura Prepon.

Spoiler-Light Premise & Central Conflict

Nerdy Eric Forman, girl-next-door Donna, dim-witted Kelso, spoiled Jackie, rebellious foster brother Hyde, and foreign exchange student Fez hang out in Eric's basement under strict father Red's roof.

SPOILER WARNING — MAJOR STORY ARCS & TURNING POINTS:
  • Departures & 1980 Countdown: Eric and Donna navigate an engagement and breakup before Eric leaves to teach in Africa at the end of Season 7. Kelso moves to Chicago in Season 8, Jackie dates Hyde and later Fez, and Eric and Kelso return in the finale as the group counts down to January 1, 1980.

14Purposes & Moral of the StoryEXPAND / COLLAPSE

The Purpose of That '70s Show

That '70s Show captures the awkward, music-filled transition from adolescence to adulthood in late-1970s small-town Wisconsin.

Anchored in Eric Forman's basement and Red and Kitty's kitchen, the sitcom pairs period nostalgia with working-class recession anxieties and teenage loyalty.

The Moral: Growing Up Means Outgrowing the Basement Without Losing Your Friends

As the 1970s end, Eric, Donna, Jackie, Hyde, Kelso, and Fez learn that adulthood brings messy detours—but the friendships forged in youth stay with you.

Why This Story & Format Matter

Its 200-episode run launched a generation of comedic stars and defined period teen sitcoms.

Editorial Interpretation

This essay reflects the eight-season Fox series.
