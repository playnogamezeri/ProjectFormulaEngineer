01 / SERIES PROFILE

Overview

Thorfinn grows up in the world of Viking raids and warfare, driven first by revenge and later by the search for a life beyond violence. His journey explores the consequences of conquest, slavery, leadership and the possibility of building a peaceful home.

Creator / source

Makoto Yukimura

02 / SETTING

World & setting

The story begins in the early eleventh century around the North Sea, involving Iceland, England, Denmark and other regions. Historical rulers and conflicts are woven into a fictional narrative about Viking society and expansion.
