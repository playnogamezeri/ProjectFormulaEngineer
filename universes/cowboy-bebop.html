01 / SERIES PROFILE

Overview

In 2071, bounty hunters travel across the solar system aboard the spaceship Bebop. Each pursues fugitives while carrying unresolved histories, losses and attachments.

Creator / source

Hajime Yatate; directed by Shinichirō Watanabe

02 / SETTING

World & setting

A hyperspace-gate accident reshaped human settlement across the solar system. Mars and other inhabited worlds contain diverse communities, crime syndicates and uneven economies.
