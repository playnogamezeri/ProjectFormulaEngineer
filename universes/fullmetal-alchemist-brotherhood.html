Synopsis & Production Context

Seeking to resurrect their late mother, brothers Edward and Alphonse Elric break alchemy's ultimate taboo. Joining the State Military as the 'Fullmetal Alchemist' to restore their bodies, Edward and Alphonse cross paths with Scar, Xingese envoys, and Fort Briggs—exposing Amestris itself as a giant transmutation circle built by Father.

02World & SettingEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Set in the militarized steampunk nation of Amestris, bordered by Xing across the Great Desert, Drachma to the north, and the devastated Ishval region, where automail engineering coexists with Alchemy and Xingese Alkahestry.

Central City & Resembool

Amestris's military capital and the Elrics' pastoral hometown.

Rush Valley & Fort Briggs

Automail engineering hub and the elite northern mountain fortress.

Ishval & Xerxes Ruins

Sites of the genocidal Ishval Civil War and ancient Philosopher's Stone sacrifice.

Xing

Eastern empire of 50 clans practicing medical Alkahestry.

Chronological Progression

Moves from early quests (Liore, Tucker, Fifth Lab, Dublith) to Xingese allies, Briggs Fortress, the Ishval flashback, and the Promised Day solar eclipse finale.

ANIME VS. SOURCE CONTINUITY DIFFERENCES: Unlike the 2003 anime (which diverged into an original plot led by Dante), Brotherhood adapts Hiromu Arakawa's entire 108-chapter manga faithfully, adding only an anime-original Episode 1 (Isaac McDougal) and compressing a few early chapters across Episodes 2–14.

06Battle & Combat SystemEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Alchemy governed by Equivalent Exchange and tectonic energy competes with Xingese Alkahestry (Dragon's Pulse), Truth's circle-less transmutation, and soul-condensed Philosopher's Stones.

Equivalent Exchange & Truth

Surviving Human Transmutation costs a body part but grants circle-less clap alchemy.

Alkahestry & Philosopher's Stones

Alkahestry bypasses Father's tectonic seal; Philosopher's Stones bypass Equivalent Exchange using finite human souls.

11Ending ExplainedEXPAND / COLLAPSE
SPOILER DISCLOSURE: Detailed resolution of Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood across anime and source material.

Hohenheim's counter-circle returns 50 million Amestrian souls and Scar's array restores alchemy, letting Edward defeat Father. To bring Alphonse back, Edward trades his own Gate of Truth—permanently giving up alchemy—proving he needs no magic when he has his friends and family.

12PurposesEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Moral of the Story: A Heart Made Fullmetal Needs No Alchemy to Stand Tall

Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood asks what remains of a person when they strip away every shortcut and face reality with their own hands.

By choosing his brother and humanity over godlike transmutation, Edward proves that pain endured together creates a stronger world than any Philosopher's Stone.
