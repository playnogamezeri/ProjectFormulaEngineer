Synopsis & Production Context

After saving a child and enduring three years of 100 push-ups, sit-ups, squats, and a 10 km run daily, Saitama becomes unbeatable—yet utterly bored. Joining the Hero Association with cyborg disciple Genos, Saitama rises quietly from C-Class to A-Class while confronting Lord Boros, Monster King Orochi, Hero Hunter Garou, and the Neo Heroes.

03Story & ChronologyEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Chronological Progression

Spans Saitama's debut, Hero Association entry, Boros's invasion, Garou's Hero Hunt and Super Fight, the Monster Association raid (including Cosmic Fear Garou), Psychic Sisters & Ninjas, and the ongoing Neo Heroes / Robot Invasion arcs.

ANIME VS. SOURCE CONTINUITY DIFFERENCES: Murata's manga massively expands ONE's webcomic—adding Super Fight, Orochi, early Blast scenes, and the Cosmic Fear Garou time-travel battle—alongside frequent chapter redraws. Anime S2 and S3 Pt. 1 drew criticism for animation drops compared to S1.

12PurposesEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Moral of the Story: Heroism Is Measured by Who Shows Up, Not by Class Rank or Spectacle

One-Punch Man satirizes power creep to ask what makes life meaningful when you've already won every fight.

Between Mumen Rider's stubborn bravery and Saitama's quiet friendships, the series affirms that caring for others matters more than strength.
