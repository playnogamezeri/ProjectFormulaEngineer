Synopsis & Production Context

14-year-old brawler Yusuke Urameshi dies in a selfless act Spirit World never expected, earning resurrection and the job of Spirit Detective under Koenma and Botan. Alongside rival Kazuma Kuwabara and demons Kurama and Hiei, Yusuke masters Spirit Energy under Genkai, conquers Younger Toguro in the Dark Tournament, confronts fallen detective Shinobu Sensui, and brokers peace among the Three Kings of the Demon Plane.

02World & SettingEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Three parallel planes—the Human World, Spirit World (Reikai), and Demon Plane (Makai)—regulated by King Enma's barrier and shaped by Spirit, Demon, and Sacred Energy.

Human World (Sarayashiki)

Yusuke's hometown and site of early Spirit Detective cases.

Spirit World (Reikai)

Bureaucratic afterlife governed by King Enma and Koenma.

Hanging Neck Island

Arena for the brutal Dark Tournament funded by the Black Book Club.

Demon Plane (Makai)

Vast subterranean realm ruled by the Three Kings: Raizen, Mukuro, and Yomi.

03Story & ChronologyEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Chronological Progression

Adapts all four major sagas across 112 episodes: Spirit Detective (Ep. 1–25), Dark Tournament (Ep. 26–66), Chapter Black (Ep. 67–94), and Three Kings (Ep. 95–112), with 2018 OVAs adapting the manga's final hostage-crisis epilogue.

ANIME VS. SOURCE CONTINUITY DIFFERENCES: The anime adds a Shizuru–Sakyo subplot in the Dark Tournament, expands the Three Kings tournament fights, keeps Genkai alive in the finale (she dies in the manga's epilogue), and originally omitted the manga's final Spirit World hostage crisis (later adapted in the 2018 OVA All or Nothing).

06Battle & Combat SystemEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Combat balances Spirit Energy (Reiki), Demon Energy (Yōki), and rare Sacred Energy (Seikōki) alongside rule-bound Territory psychic fields.

Spirit, Demon & Sacred Energy

Aura projects as Yusuke's Reigun or Kuwabara's Spirit/Dimensional Sword; Sensui's golden Sacred Energy represents the rarest tier.

Territory Abilities

Chapter Black psychics project rule-bound spatial fields (e.g., Taboo, Gamemaster, Sniper) forcing opponents to win by wits.
