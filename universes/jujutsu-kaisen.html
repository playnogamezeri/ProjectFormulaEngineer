Synopsis & Production Context

Becoming the vessel for the King of Curses, Ryomen Sukuna, Yuji Itadori joins Megumi Fushiguro and Nobara Kugisaki at Tokyo Jujutsu High to collect Sukuna's fingers before execution. When Kenjaku seals Satoru Gojo in Shibuya and ignites the Culling Game, sorcerers wage an all-out war culminating in the Shinjuku Showdown.

02World & SettingEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Contemporary Japan (Tokyo and Kyoto Jujutsu High, Shibuya, Sendai, Shinjuku) shadowed by a secretive sorcerer hierarchy and Cursed Spirits born from leaked negative human emotion.

Tokyo & Kyoto Jujutsu High

Twin academies guarding Master Tengen's Tombs of the Star Corridor.

Shibuya (Oct 31, 2018)

Site of Gojo's sealing and Sukuna's catastrophic rampage.

Culling Game Colonies

Ten barrier-enclosed arenas across Japan (Tokyo, Sendai, Sakurajima).

Shinjuku

Battleground of the final showdown against Sukuna.

06Battle & Combat SystemEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Cursed Energy fueled by negative emotion powers Innate Techniques, Reverse Cursed Technique healing, guaranteed-hit Domain Expansions, Binding Vows, and Heavenly Restrictions.

Domains & Reverse Cursed Technique

RCT multiplies negative energy into healing; Domain Expansions guarantee hits at the cost of technique burnout.

Binding Vows & Heavenly Restriction

Strict self-imposed limits or zero-energy physical trades (Toji/Maki) yield overwhelming power.

12PurposesEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Moral of the Story: Strength Means Raising Allies So No One Has to Stand Alone

Jujutsu Kaisen challenges the myth of the untouchable lone savior by showing that real change requires comrades.

Even after devastating losses, Yuji, Megumi, and Nobara carry Gojo's hope forward—proving empathy outlasts curses.
