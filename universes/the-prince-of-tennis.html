Synopsis & Production Context

Fresh off four consecutive American Junior Tennis titles, 12-year-old Ryoma Echizen enters Seishun Academy (Seigaku) in Tokyo, where his father Nanjiro once played. Earning a rare freshman starting spot under captain Kunimitsu Tezuka, Ryoma helps Seigaku conquer Fudomine, St. Rudolph, Hyotei, and Rikkai Dai on the road to the National Championship—before advancing to Japan's U-17 Training Camp and the U-17 World Cup.

03Story & ChronologyEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Chronological Progression

Original TV adapts Tokyo Preliminaries through Kanto Finals (Eps. 1–128, with Josei Shonan replacing Midoriyama in Eps. 76–85), diverges into an anime-original US West Coast arc (Eps. 129–178), then returns to manga canon in the 26-ep National Tournament OVAs and New Prince of Tennis.

ANIME VS. SOURCE CONTINUITY DIFFERENCES: The 2001 TV series inserts Tezuka's departure filler (Eps. 69–75), replaces the manga's brief Midoriyama match with the 10-episode Josei Shonan arc (Eps. 76–85), and creates an anime-original finale (Eps. 129–178: Junior Selection vs. US West Coast & Ryoma vs. Tezuka). The 2006–2009 National Tournament OVAs skip back to canon immediately after the Kanto Finals.

06Battle & Combat SystemEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Framed as competitive tennis without fantasy magic: extreme biomechanical control, spin physics, probabilistic Data Tennis, and peak athletic flow states (Muga no Kyōchi).

Muga no Kyōchi & The Three Doors

Subconscious flow state unlocking Hyakuren Jitoku (arm power), Saiki Kanpatsu (rally prediction), and Ten'imuhō no Kiwami (effortless joy).

Data Tennis, Spin & Doubles Synchro

Probabilistic shot tracking, court-bending spin physics (Tezuka Zone, Twist Serve), and synchronized doubles coverage.
