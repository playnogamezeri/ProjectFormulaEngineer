Synopsis & Production Context

In a world where 80% of humanity wields Quirks, Quirkless boy Izuku Midoriya earns All Might's torch—One For All—and enrolls at U.A. High School alongside Katsuki Bakugo, Shoto Todoroki, and Ochaco Uraraka. As All Might retires and All For One grooms Tomura Shigaraki, Class 1-A fights to save both society and the broken souls behind its villains.

02World & SettingEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Near-future Japan centered on Musutafu's U.A. High School, regulated by the Hero Public Safety Commission (HPSC) and supported by high-tech Support Course equipment.

U.A. High School (Musutafu)

Japan's premier hero academy with Hero, Support, General, and Management courses.

Kamino Ward & Deika City

Sites of All Might's last stand and the Meta Liberation Army clash.

Tartarus Prison

Maximum-security facility breached after the Liberation War.

Final War Battlegrounds

Floating U.A. Coffin, Gunga Mountain, and Kamino.

03Story & ChronologyEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Chronological Progression

Spans U.A. Beginnings, Kamino Hideout Raid, Shie Hassaikai, Meta Liberation Army, Paranormal Liberation War, Dark Hero solo arc, and the Final War + 8-year epilogue.

ANIME VS. SOURCE CONTINUITY DIFFERENCES: Season 5 famously swapped the Meta Liberation Army and Endeavor Agency arcs to align with World Heroes' Mission, trimming Re-Destro and Spinner scenes, while Horikoshi added original scenes to Season 8 and Ep. 171 adapts bonus Ch. 431.

12PurposesEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Moral of the Story: Anyone Can Be a Hero When They Refuse to Look Away from Someone in Need

My Hero Academia widens the definition of heroism from one untouchable idol to an entire community willing to share the weight.

Even without a Quirk, Izuku's impulse to step forward inspires classmates, teachers, and civilians alike to build a kinder world.
