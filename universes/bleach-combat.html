01 / COSMOLOGICAL FOUNDATION

Draft: The Origin of Spirit Power

Bleach does not present spiritual power as a single technique invented by one faction. It is part of the nature of souls and spiritual existence. The history of the Soul King explains the formation of the present worlds and the cycle of life and death; the spiritual energy used by individuals is expressed through their souls, bodies, inherited traits, training and relationship with spiritual matter.

Before the division of the worlds

According to the history disclosed in the postwar novel Bleach: Can't Fear Your Own World, the world in the distant past existed in a primordial state before the current division between life and death. The Soul King, later known as the Reio, was an extraordinary being whose power could alter the structure of existence. The five ancestors of the great noble families came to regard the undivided world as a condition that had to be changed. Their actions established the foundation of the cosmology known in the main story.

The Soul King and the creation of the current order

The five ancestors used the Soul King's power to establish the foundations of the new world: the Human World, Soul Society and Hueco Mundo. The division between life and death and the circulation of souls became part of this arrangement. The Soul King was sealed and mutilated, becoming the lynchpin that sustains the separated realms. This history is not a simple creation myth: it is also a concealed political crime, and the stability of the current worlds rests on the treatment of the being whose power made their structure possible.

Important distinction: The Soul King is the cosmological lynchpin of the present world order and is connected to the origins of major spiritual lineages. That does not establish that every particle of Reishi or every individual's Reiryoku was personally manufactured by him. The source material distinguishes the world's restructuring from the everyday mechanics of spiritual power.

From the world order to individual spiritual power

Within the established cosmology, souls and spiritual matter have properties that living humans do not ordinarily perceive. Reishi are the spiritual particles that compose souls and spiritual environments. Reiryoku is the spiritual power a being possesses. When that power is released, it produces Reiatsu, the spiritual pressure that others can sense and that may affect the surrounding environment or weaker opponents. These are related concepts, not interchangeable names for the same thing.

Different lineages, different ways of using power

The major groups do not all fight by using spiritual energy in the same manner. Soul Reapers cultivate power from within the soul and express it through Zanpakutō and trained arts. Hollows develop through the transformation and consumption of souls, while Arrancar gain Soul Reaper-like traits and weaponized release forms. Quincy draw in ambient Reishi and shape it through their own power. Fullbringers manipulate the souls residing in physical matter, with their abilities tied to a distinctive Hollow-related inheritance. These systems share a spiritual foundation while retaining different methods, risks and consequences.

What this origin explains — and what remains unresolved

  • Why the present realms are separated and why the cycle of life and death matters.
  • Why the Soul King is essential to the stability of the current cosmology.
  • Why Reishi, Reiryoku and Reiatsu must be defined separately.
  • Why different spiritual lineages can interact while retaining distinct combat systems.
  • It does not provide a complete scientific account of the first origin of all souls, the ultimate origin of all Reishi, or a single numerical law governing every power.

Continuity note: the detailed account of the primordial world and the five ancestors is drawn from the postwar novel Can't Fear Your Own World, which VIZ identifies as a novel with original concept by Tite Kubo and text by Ryohgo Narita. It should be labeled as novel continuity, not misrepresented as a full exposition in the original manga chapters.

02 / FOUNDATIONAL VOCABULARY

Core Spiritual Terms

Reishi · 霊子

Spirit particles. The spiritual material composing souls and spiritual environments such as Soul Society and Hueco Mundo. Quincy can gather and shape ambient Reishi.

Reiryoku · 霊力

Spiritual power. The energy or power possessed by a soul or spiritually capable being. Its quantity and control affect the abilities a person can use.

Reiatsu · 霊圧

Spiritual pressure. The pressure produced when Reiryoku is released. It can be sensed and may exert force or suppress weaker spiritual beings.

Konpaku · 魂魄

Soul. The spiritual individual or soul-body that persists through the series' life-and-death system. The exact metaphysical anatomy is more complex than a simple energy container.

Kishi · 器子

Physical matter. A term used in contrast with Reishi when distinguishing the material composition of the living Human World from spirit-particle matter.

Reiraku · 霊絡

Spirit ribbons. Visible or traceable manifestations used by spiritually aware characters to follow spiritual signatures and locate beings.

03 / FUNCTIONAL MODEL

How Spiritual Power Functions

01 · SOUL / BEINGA spiritual individual has a soul and a level of spiritual capacity.
02 · REIRYOKUInternal spiritual power is cultivated, inherited, awakened or developed.
03 · APPLICATIONPower is shaped through lineage-specific abilities and trained techniques.
04 · REIATSUReleased power produces spiritual pressure that can be sensed or felt.

External spiritual matter

Reishi is the material field that certain combat systems directly manipulate. It is especially important to Quincy, whose weapons and many techniques depend on gathering ambient particles. Soul Reapers generally express their own spiritual power through their Zanpakutō, rather than constructing a weapon from surrounding particles in the Quincy manner.

Capacity is not the same as skill

A large amount of Reiryoku does not automatically guarantee victory. Control, technique, speed, tactical intelligence, matchup, stamina, information and the special conditions of an ability all matter. Reiatsu can create overwhelming pressure, but Bleach repeatedly shows that specialized abilities and tactical openings can change a fight.

04 / SOUL REAPER SYSTEM

Shinigami / Soul Reapers

Shinigami are souls with substantial spiritual power who train to protect the living, guide souls and combat Hollows. Their principal weapon is the Zanpakutō, a blade linked to the wielder's soul and to a distinct spirit identity.

Zanpakutō

A soul-linked weapon. Its identity, spirit, name and release are personal to the wielder, rather than a generic weapon class.

Asauchi

The foundational blank Zanpakutō supplied to Shinigami. Through imprinting and training, it becomes the individual's personal blade.

Shikai

The first release. The wielder calls the blade's name and release command to access its defined ability and form.

Bankai

The advanced release, generally requiring extensive mastery and manifestation of the Zanpakutō spirit. Its effect varies widely and is not always a simple power increase.

Four foundational combat disciplines

  • Zanjutsu: swordsmanship and Zanpakutō combat.
  • Hakuda: unarmed hand-to-hand combat.
  • Hohō: movement and high-speed footwork, including Shunpo.
  • Kidō: spiritual spellcraft, including Hadō and Bakudō; Kaidō is associated with healing practice.

Advanced and exceptional expressions

Hollowfication, forbidden techniques, specialized Zanpakutō abilities and high-level Kidō can alter the usual combat profile. Each ability should be documented by its activation, demonstrated effects, limitations, counters and the user's condition at the time.

05 / HOLLOW SYSTEM

Hollows & Arrancar

Hollows are souls transformed after the Chain of Fate corrodes and the soul becomes consumed by destructive hunger. Their forms and abilities vary. The Menos progression includes Gillian, Adjuchas and Vasto Lorde classes, each associated with a different scale and degree of individuality.

Hollow abilities

Common expressions include Cero, Bala, Sonído, Pesquisa, Hierro and regeneration. Not every Hollow possesses every ability.

Menos evolution

Gillian, Adjuchas and Vasto Lorde describe major Menos classes. Evolution and loss of individuality are important distinctions; size alone is not a complete combat ranking.

Arrancar

Hollows that break or remove their masks and gain Soul Reaper-like traits, including humanoid forms and Zanpakutō.

Resurrección

An Arrancar's release, returning aspects of its original Hollow nature and power. The release name and form are individual.

Core techniques

  • Cero: concentrated destructive spiritual blast.
  • Bala: a faster, generally less destructive projectile compared with Cero.
  • Hierro: hardened spiritual skin used for defense.
  • Sonído: high-speed movement technique.
  • Pesquisa: spiritual sensing and analysis.
  • Garganta: dimensional passage used by Hollows and Arrancar to travel between realms.
06 / QUINCY SYSTEM

Quincy

Quincy are spiritually aware living humans who gather Reishi from their surroundings and shape it using their own spiritual power. Their approach is often described as using power from the outside, contrasting with the Soul Reaper's expression of power from within the soul through a Zanpakutō.

Spirit Weapons

Weapons formed by gathering ambient Reishi, commonly bows and arrows but not limited to one shape.

Blut

A Quincy technique that circulates spiritual power through the blood to enhance defense (Blut Vene) or offense (Blut Arterie). The two modes are not simply interchangeable.

Hirenkyaku

High-speed movement achieved by riding or controlling Reishi beneath the user's feet.

Vollständig

An advanced Quincy transformation that manifests wings and a halo-like structure and greatly amplifies abilities.

Letzt Stil

An extreme Quincy power-release method associated with the loss of powers after use, distinct from Vollständig.

Schrift

A lettered ability associated with Yhwach's power and the Sternritter. Each Schrift has a distinct rule or conceptual effect.

Balance consequence

Quincy destroy Hollows rather than purifying them and returning their souls to the cycle. Large-scale destruction of souls disrupts the balance between the worlds. This conflict is a central reason the Quincy and Soul Reapers have a long and violent history.

07 / FULLBRING SYSTEM

Fullbring

Fullbringers are spiritually aware humans whose ability manipulates the souls residing in physical matter. Their powers are connected to Hollow Reiryoku and commonly emerge through a strong attachment to a particular object. Fullbring is not simply telekinesis: it acts on the soul of matter and draws out or changes its properties.

Object affinity

A Fullbringer's attachment to an object can serve as the focus and personal expression of their ability.

Substance manipulation

Users can alter how matter behaves, move through or reshape it, and produce effects defined by the individual Fullbring.

Combat expression

Fullbring abilities are highly individual. Their conditions, range and limits should be documented separately rather than reduced to one standard technique.

Power transfer

Fullbring power can interact with and be transferred or shared under special circumstances, as shown during Ichigo's Lost Agent story.

08 / HYBRID & SPECIAL SYSTEMS

Hybrid and Special Power Systems

Visored

Soul Reapers who gained Hollow powers and can manifest a Hollow mask, combining Soul Reaper training with Hollow-derived enhancement.

Hōgyoku

An artificial device associated with breaking boundaries between spiritual categories and accelerating evolution. Its effects are exceptional and should not be treated as an ordinary training method.

Modified Souls

Artificial souls designed for specialized functions. Their existence raises questions about constructed identity and the ethics of weaponizing souls.

Ichigo's hybrid nature

Ichigo's powers involve Human, Soul Reaper, Hollow and Quincy origins. His case is exceptional and should not be used as the standard template for every hybrid.

Hell-related phenomena

The special chapter No Breaths from Hell introduces a further dimension of spiritual danger and postwar uncertainty. Its mechanics should remain a separate research track.

Soul King fragments

Body parts and fragments associated with the Soul King can possess extraordinary spiritual properties. Their specific abilities and roles differ; they should not be generalized into a single uniform power.

10 / ANALYTICAL STANDARD

Combat Evaluation Framework

Every character entry and simulated matchup should use the same evidence categories. The database will avoid declaring a universal power ranking from one feat or title.

Spiritual capacity

Known Reiryoku, control, pressure, stamina and evidence of exhaustion or recovery.

Physical performance

Strength, speed, reaction, durability, mobility and endurance, with source context.

Technique

Weapon mastery, spell skill, precision, tactical judgment and adaptability.

Ability conditions

Activation requirements, range, duration, costs, counters and vulnerabilities.

Matchup

How one ability interacts with another; compatibility can matter more than raw output.

Evidence confidence

Direct feat, explicit explanation, reliable inference or unresolved interpretation.

Recommended dossier record

Ability name → system → user → activation → effect → range → duration → cost → limitations → counters → demonstrated feats → chapter / episode reference → confidence.
