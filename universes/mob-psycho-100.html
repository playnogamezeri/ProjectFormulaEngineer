Synopsis & Production Context

Terrified of hurting others with his overwhelming telekinesis, quiet middle schooler Shigeo 'Mob' Kageyama bottles up his feelings until they hit 100%—or worse, ???%. Assisted by fraudulent yet fiercely caring boss Arataka Reigen and the wholesome Body Improvement Club, Mob confronts evil spirit Dimple, rival espers, and the supremacist organization Claw.

02World & SettingEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Set in modern Seasoning City, Japan, where ordinary civilians coexist unknowingly with urban-legend yōkai, minor psychic unions, and militant espers like Claw.

Seasoning City & Salt Middle School

Home of Mob, the Body Improvement Club, and Telepathy Club.

Spirits and Such Consultation Office

Reigen's humble paranormal odd-jobs agency.

Black Vinegar Middle School & Seasoning Tower

Sites of Teru's clash and Toichiro Suzuki's World Domination climax.

Divine Tree

Giant broccoli sprouted from Mob and Toichiro's psychic overload.

03Story & ChronologyEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Chronological Progression

Follows Mob's Body Improvement Club entry, (LOL) Cult, Teru, Claw's 7th Division, Keiji Mogami, Reigen's TV exposure, Claw's World Domination, Dimple's Divine Tree, and ???%'s final integration.

ANIME VS. SOURCE CONTINUITY DIFFERENCES: Bones adapts ONE's 101-chapter manga faithfully, only toning down Minori's physical abuse/cat scene in the Mogami arc, altering Kuchisake-onna to 'The Dragger' for TV rules, and expanding action choreography.

12PurposesEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Moral of the Story: You Are Not Defined by Special Powers—You Are Defined by How You Live

Mob Psycho 100 dismantles power fantasies to celebrate everyday kindness, honest effort, and emotional growth.

Mob's real victory isn't defeating Claw or ???%—it's learning to laugh, cry, and run alongside his friends without hiding who he is.
