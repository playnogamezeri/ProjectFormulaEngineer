01 / SERIES PROFILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Akame ga Kill! is a dark-fantasy action series based on the manga written by Takahiro and illustrated by Tetsuya Tashiro. It follows Tatsumi, a young swordsman who reaches the Imperial Capital hoping to earn money for his impoverished village. After discovering the cruelty concealed behind the Capital's wealth, he joins Night Raid, an assassination unit working with the Revolutionary Army to overthrow the corrupt regime.

Original creators

Takahiro (writer) and Tetsuya Tashiro (manga artist)

Anime director

Tomoki Kobayashi

Animation studio

WHITE FOX

Anime release

July–December 2014

Format

Television anime · 24 episodes

Central conflict

Night Raid and the Revolutionary Army versus the Empire's corrupt ruling structure

Synopsis

Tatsumi travels to the Capital with childhood companions Ieyasu and Sayo, intending to secure resources for their struggling home. Separated from them and robbed, he accepts shelter from a wealthy household. He soon learns that the household has tortured and murdered his friends and other visitors. Night Raid intervenes, and Tatsumi chooses to join the assassins after seeing the scale of the Capital's corruption.

Night Raid's members wield Teigu—rare Imperial Arms with extraordinary abilities. Their targets include officials, nobles and military figures whose actions sustain the regime. But the Empire has its own elite fighters, including General Esdeath and the Jaegers. As the conflict escalates, personal loyalties, ideological conviction and the cost of revolution collide.

Adaptation note

The 2014 anime follows the manga's broad premise and many major events, but its final stretch diverges into an anime-original conclusion. Character outcomes and the final resolution must therefore be labelled by continuity rather than treated as interchangeable.

02 / GEOGRAPHY & CIVILIZATION

World & Setting

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

The Empire

The story takes place in a vast imperial nation with a medieval-fantasy aesthetic and advanced relic weapons. The young Emperor occupies the throne, but the real political power is manipulated by Prime Minister Honest. Corruption, exploitation and inequality have spread through the government and its institutions.

The Capital

The seat of imperial authority, wealth and political influence. Its outward grandeur hides poverty, abuse and institutional violence.

Rural Provinces

Communities such as Tatsumi's village endure poverty and depend on resources and security controlled by the Empire.

Night Raid's hideout

A concealed base used by the assassins to plan operations, recover and coordinate with the Revolutionary Army.

Danger Beasts and ancient technology

The wider world contains dangerous creatures and rare materials. In an earlier age, the Empire commissioned the creation of the Teigu from extraordinary resources, including Danger Beast remains. These weapons became strategic instruments whose power could alter the balance of a battle.

Key locations

  • Imperial Capital: The centre of government and the main theatre of Night Raid's early operations.
  • Tatsumi's village: His impoverished home and the reason he initially travels to the Capital.
  • Night Raid headquarters: The hidden base from which the assassins organize missions.
  • Imperial Palace: The seat of the Emperor and the political centre guarded by the Empire's strongest forces.
  • Esdeath's military theatres: Fronts where the Empire's expansionist campaigns and her command are shown.
03 / COMPLETE NARRATIVE

Story & Chronology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

The anime's narrative moves from Tatsumi's arrival in the Capital to Night Raid's covert campaign, then into open conflict between the Revolutionary Army and the Empire. The sequence below follows the 2014 anime continuity.

Before the story — The Empire's decline

Over generations, the imperial state becomes increasingly corrupt. Prime Minister Honest consolidates influence around the young Emperor, while ordinary citizens suffer under abusive officials, criminal networks and military exploitation.

Tatsumi leaves his village

Tatsumi, Ieyasu and Sayo travel toward the Capital to earn money for their community. They are separated on the road, and Tatsumi arrives alone after losing his funds.

The truth about the Capital

Tatsumi is taken in by Aria's wealthy household. Night Raid attacks the estate and reveals that the family has tortured and killed visitors, including Ieyasu and Sayo. Tatsumi kills Aria and is recruited by Night Raid.

Training and the first missions

Tatsumi adapts to life with Akame, Leone, Mine, Sheele, Lubbock, Bulat and their leader Najenda. He learns that Night Raid's work is part of a larger revolutionary campaign.

The Imperial Arms revealed

Tatsumi learns about Teigu and the lethal compatibility required to wield them. Night Raid's operations bring them into conflict with Imperial Arm users, including the executioner Zank and the Empire's increasingly capable enforcers.

Night Raid suffers its first losses

Sheele is killed in a confrontation with Seryu Ubiquitous and her biological Teigu, Koro. Bulat is mortally wounded after fighting General Esdeath's forces and entrusts Incursio to Tatsumi.

Esdeath and the Jaegers

Esdeath returns to the Capital and forms an elite unit known as the Jaegers. The team includes powerful Imperial Arm users and brings Akame's estranged younger sister, Kurome, into direct opposition with Night Raid.

New allies and escalating operations

Susanoo and Chelsea join Night Raid. Their abilities strengthen the group, but the Empire's counteroffensive intensifies. Chelsea's attempt to eliminate Kurome fails and costs her life.

Wild Hunt and the revolutionary crisis

Prime Minister Honest's son Syura leads the Wild Hunt, whose abuses destabilize the Capital further. Night Raid and other forces move against the unit as the Empire's internal conflict approaches open rebellion.

The final campaign

The Revolutionary Army advances on the Capital. Night Raid confronts the Empire's remaining elite fighters while Tatsumi's Incursio continues to evolve under extreme strain.

Fall of the Empire

The anime's final battles bring Night Raid, the Jaegers, Esdeath and the imperial leadership into a decisive confrontation. Tatsumi and other characters pay the ultimate price, while Akame faces Esdeath and the old regime collapses.

Continuity warning

The anime's final events are not a scene-for-scene adaptation of the manga's ending. The manga continues with different character outcomes and a distinct aftermath. This page separates the anime ending in its ending section and flags manga differences where relevant.

05 / CAST & INDIVIDUAL PROFILES

Character Database

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Search the principal cast by name, role or affiliation. These are concise encyclopedia profiles; dedicated individual pages can be added as the archive expands.

NIGHT RAID · MAIN PROTAGONIST

Akame

A highly skilled assassin who wields the cursed Teigu Murasame. Raised by the Empire alongside her sister Kurome, she defects after encountering Najenda and becomes one of Night Raid's most formidable fighters. Her calm exterior conceals deep loyalty to her comrades.

NIGHT RAID · PROTAGONIST

Tatsumi

A young swordsman from a poor village who joins Night Raid after discovering the Capital's cruelty. He begins with ordinary weapons, later inherits Bulat's Incursio and grows into a central combatant in the revolution.

NIGHT RAID · LEADER

Najenda

A former Imperial general who defects and becomes the leader of Night Raid. Strategic, disciplined and committed to the Revolutionary Army, she directs the group's operations and carries the consequences of her former service.

NIGHT RAID · ASSASSIN

Leone

A confident and physically powerful assassin who first encounters Tatsumi in the Capital. Her Teigu, Lionel, grants beast-like enhancements that improve her strength, senses and close-combat ability.

NIGHT RAID · SNIPER

Mine

A sharp-tongued marksman who wields Pumpkin, a gun-type Teigu whose destructive output increases in response to danger. Her abrasive manner hides a strong commitment to her comrades and the revolutionary cause.

NIGHT RAID · ASSASSIN

Sheele

A gentle and absent-minded woman who becomes a ruthless fighter during missions. Her Teigu, Extase, is a massive pair of scissors capable of cutting through almost anything. She dies protecting Mine from Seryu.

NIGHT RAID · SUPPORT

Lubbock

A resourceful and playful assassin who uses Cross Tail, a Teigu that creates and controls durable threads for traps, restraint and combat. He is deeply loyal to Najenda and supports Night Raid's operations.

NIGHT RAID · FORMER IMPERIAL GENERAL

Bulat

A powerful and principled fighter who wields the armor Teigu Incursio. He mentors Tatsumi and entrusts him with the Imperial Arm after suffering a fatal wound in battle.

NIGHT RAID · BIOLOGICAL TEIGU

Susanoo

A sentient biological Teigu who joins Night Raid under Najenda. He is exceptionally capable in combat and domestic work, and can sacrifice his own life force to empower his master.

NIGHT RAID · INFILTRATION

Chelsea

A pragmatic assassin and master of disguise who wields Gaea Foundation, a Teigu that lets her transform her appearance. She targets the Jaegers and attempts to eliminate Kurome.

IMPERIAL ARMY · GENERAL

Esdeath

The Empire's strongest general and a ruthless battlefield commander. Her Teigu, Demon's Extract, grants her control over ice. She is drawn to Tatsumi while remaining committed to the Empire's hierarchy and her own doctrine of strength.

IMPERIAL ARMY · ASSASSIN

Kurome

Akame's younger sister, trained by the Empire as an assassin. She wields Yatsufusa, a blade that can turn those it kills into controllable corpses. Her bond with Akame is shaped by separation, conditioning and conflicting loyalties.

IMPERIAL POLICE · ENFORCER

Seryu Ubiquitous

A fanatical enforcer who believes she embodies justice. Her body has been modified and she fights alongside Koro, a biological Teigu capable of changing size and form. She becomes one of Night Raid's most personal enemies.

IMPERIAL ARMY · JAEGERS

Wave

A young Imperial soldier assigned to the Jaegers. He values comradeship and a sense of justice, and gradually becomes troubled by the cruelty and moral compromises around him.

IMPERIAL ARMY · JAEGERS

Run

A composed and intelligent member of the Jaegers with a personal history tied to the Empire's treatment of civilians. He operates with restraint and carries motives beyond simple loyalty to the regime.

IMPERIAL ARMY · JAEGERS

Dr. Stylish

A flamboyant scientist who conducts human experimentation and modifies soldiers. His Teigu, Perfector, grants extraordinary precision and supports his surgical work.

IMPERIAL ARMY · JAEGERS

Bols

A masked member of the Empire's Incineration Squad who wields the flamethrower Teigu Rubicante. Despite his frightening appearance and violent occupation, he is devoted to his wife and daughter.

IMPERIAL GOVERNMENT · PRIME MINISTER

Honest

The manipulative Prime Minister who exercises power behind the young Emperor. His corruption and exploitation are central causes of the revolution.

IMPERIAL GOVERNMENT · EMPEROR

The Emperor

A young ruler whose authority is manipulated by Honest. He is sheltered from the full scale of the Empire's suffering and becomes central to the final conflict.

IMPERIAL NOBILITY · WILD HUNT

Syura

Honest's son and leader of Wild Hunt. He uses his position to indulge in cruelty and destabilizes the Capital through the unit's abuses.

NIGHT RAID TARGET · FORMER NOBLE

Aria

The wealthy young woman who offers Tatsumi shelter after he reaches the Capital. Her household's cruelty exposes the reality behind the city's privileged class.

Imperial Arms (Teigu)

Teigu are rare weapons and artifacts created in the Empire's past using extraordinary materials and Danger Beast components. Each has a distinct ability and often imposes specific tactical conditions. A person must be compatible with a Teigu to use it effectively. The story establishes a deadly risk when two Teigu users clash.

Combat principles

Compatibility

Teigu use depends on whether a wielder can synchronize with the weapon's nature and demands.

Trump cards

Many Imperial Arms possess a hidden or ultimate technique that can turn a battle but may carry a cost or limitation.

Biological Teigu

Some Imperial Arms are living entities with independent behavior, needs and combat capabilities.

Adaptation

Incursio's living armor can change in response to danger, but its evolution places increasing strain on its user.

07 / POLITICAL & MILITARY STRUCTURES

Nations & Factions

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

The Empire

The ruling state whose institutions have been captured by corrupt elites and whose military enforces imperial power.

Night Raid

A covert assassination unit affiliated with the Revolutionary Army. Its members target individuals whose actions sustain the corrupt regime.

Revolutionary Army

A broad resistance movement seeking to overthrow Honest's government and replace the existing political order.

Jaegers

An elite Imperial unit assembled by Esdeath to hunt Night Raid and defend the Empire.

Wild Hunt

A brutal special unit led by Syura. Its abuses create enemies inside and outside the Imperial establishment.

Imperial Police

Law-enforcement forces that protect the regime and pursue those labelled enemies of the state.

Imperial Army

The Empire's conventional military, deployed in campaigns and in defense of the Capital.

Danger Beast hunters

Combatants and military personnel who confront dangerous creatures and protect settlements and routes.

08 / CHARACTER CONNECTIONS

Relationships

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Akame · Kurome

Sisters separated and trained by the Empire. Their opposing paths turn family affection into a painful conflict between duty, survival and the hope of saving one another.

Tatsumi · Mine

Partners whose rivalry and mutual respect develop into affection. Their relationship becomes one of the story's emotional anchors.

Tatsumi · Bulat

Bulat becomes Tatsumi's mentor, teaching him the responsibilities of a fighter and passing Incursio to him.

Najenda · Esdeath

Former comrades in the Imperial military who choose opposing sides. Their strategic and ideological conflict reflects the fracture within the Empire.

Akame · Najenda

Najenda gives Akame a path away from the Empire's assassin program. Akame's loyalty to Night Raid grows from shared purpose and trust.

Wave · Kurome

Wave develops a close bond with Kurome and tries to protect her, even as her role in the Jaegers places them on the opposite side of Akame's struggle.

Leone · Tatsumi

Leone is the first Night Raid member to bring Tatsumi into the group's world and becomes a protective senior presence.

Night Raid · Revolutionary Army

The assassins are one specialized instrument of a larger rebellion. Their operations serve a political campaign whose outcome depends on forces beyond the hideout.

10 / INTERPRETATION

Themes & Analysis

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Corruption and power

The Empire demonstrates how concentrated authority can become detached from the welfare of the people it claims to govern.

Revolution and moral cost

Night Raid fights an abusive system through assassination, forcing the story to examine whether violent methods can produce a just society.

Justice and vengeance

Characters define justice through personal loss, ideology or institutional loyalty. Their competing definitions often make conflict unavoidable.

Family and chosen bonds

Akame and Kurome's separation contrasts with the loyalty Night Raid builds as a chosen family.

Power and sacrifice

Teigu grant extraordinary strength, but their use does not remove vulnerability. Victory often demands a permanent sacrifice.

Humanity within enemies

Several Imperial fighters are given personal attachments and private doubts, complicating a simple division between heroes and villains.

11 / FULL SPOILER CONCLUSION

Ending Explained

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL THE ANIME ENDING

The anime's final campaign brings Night Raid and the Revolutionary Army into open conflict with the Empire. Tatsumi's Incursio undergoes a final transformation as he confronts the Emperor's immense Imperial Arm. Tatsumi dies after preventing the weapon's destruction from killing civilians. Mine survives but is left in a coma, and the revolution continues toward victory.

Akame faces Esdeath in the decisive duel. Akame uses Murasame's curse against Esdeath, while Esdeath's power and determination make the fight exceptionally dangerous. Esdeath ultimately dies, and the old imperial regime collapses. Akame survives and leaves to confront the remaining consequences of Murasame's curse and the violence she has endured.

The anime's conclusion is an original adaptation ending. It should not be used as a substitute for the manga's distinct final events and character outcomes.

Anime and manga differences

The anime's final arc departs from the manga's later plot. The manga continues with a different sequence of battles, character fates and aftermath. In particular, Tatsumi's ultimate fate differs: the manga does not end with the same death shown in the anime. Any character database or chronology should label which version it describes.

What the anime resolves

  • The corrupt imperial leadership is defeated and the revolution succeeds in overthrowing the regime.
  • Akame survives the final confrontation with Esdeath.
  • The story closes with the personal and political costs of the revolution still visible.
  • The final outcome of several characters differs from the manga continuity.
