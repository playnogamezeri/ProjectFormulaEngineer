01 · Identity & Background

NAMEDavid Swallow
ROLEGolden Dawn Magic Knight
ORIGINClover Kingdom
MAGICDice Magic

History

David is a nobleman and Golden Dawn member. During the elf reincarnation, he becomes the human host of the elf Baval. He is present during the conflict at Clover Castle and later survives the attack on the Golden Dawn headquarters, where William's healing magic saves many injured squad members.

02 · Personality

David is relaxed and affable, and is shown caring about the well-being of his squadmates.

03 · Magic & Abilities

Dice Magic manifests dice whose results determine the power produced by a spell. The magic's effectiveness depends on the roll and the spell being used.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

Golden Dawn; William Vangeance; Yuno Grinberryall; elf host Baval.

05 · Story Chronology

Appears during the elf reincarnation conflict as Baval's human host. Later serves with the Golden Dawn and is present during Zenon's attack on the squad headquarters.

06 · Achievements & Contributions

His Dice Magic can scale an effect according to the number rolled. During the Golden Dawn attack, he is among those injured but saved after William's magic activates.

07 · Current Status & Continuity

Survives the attack on the Golden Dawn headquarters. His later story role is limited in the material covered here.
