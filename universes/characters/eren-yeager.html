01 / PERSONAL RECORD

Identity & Background

EXPAND IDENTITY DETAILS

Eren Yeager (エレン・イェーガー, Eren Yēgā) is the central protagonist of Attack on Titan. He is the son of Grisha Yeager and Carla Yeager, the younger paternal half-brother of Zeke Yeager, and a childhood friend of Mikasa Ackerman and Armin Arlert.

Full name

Eren Yeager (also rendered Eren Jaeger in some English materials)

Birthday

March 30

Birthplace

Shiganshina District, on Paradis Island

Family

Grisha Yeager (father), Carla Yeager (mother), Zeke Yeager (half-brother)

Military affiliation

104th Cadet Corps; Survey Corps; later aligned with the Yeagerists

Former roles

Soldier, Titan shifter, inheritor of the Attack, Founding and War Hammer Titans

Age

15 during the 850 military period; 19 during the 854 conflict

Japanese voice

Yūki Kaji

English voice

Bryce Papenbrook

Final status

Deceased; killed by Mikasa during the final battle

Name note: “Yeager” and “Jaeger” are alternate English renderings of the Japanese surname. This archive uses “Yeager” consistently.

02 / VISUAL IDENTITY

Appearance

EXPAND APPEARANCE DETAILS

Childhood and cadet years

As a child, Eren has short dark hair, expressive eyes and a lean build. During his cadet and early Survey Corps years, he is usually seen in the standard military uniform, with the green Survey Corps cloak marking his later service.

After the time skip

By the Marley and final-season period, Eren is taller and more physically mature, with long dark hair. He often wears a dark shirt and trousers, and later ties his hair back. His appearance becomes more restrained and severe, reflecting the distance he creates between himself and his former companions.

Titan forms

Attack Titan

A roughly 15-metre humanoid Titan with a muscular, combat-oriented build, long dark hair, exposed teeth and a distinctive exposed-muscle appearance.

Founding Titan manifestation

During the Rumbling, Eren's transformed body becomes an enormous skeletal, spine-dominated structure extending across the battlefield. This form is visually and physically unlike his earlier humanoid Titan.

03 / PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Motivations

EXPAND PERSONALITY DETAILS

Early traits

Eren is intense, outspoken, stubborn and quick to anger. He resents the resignation of people who accept life inside the Walls as an unavoidable condition. Armin's book about the outside world strengthens his desire to explore beyond the Walls. Eren's determination can inspire others, but his impulsiveness and willingness to endanger himself often create problems for his comrades.

Core motivations

  • Freedom: He wants to live without being confined by walls, enemies or inherited restrictions.
  • Protection: He wants his friends and the people of Paradis to survive.
  • Retaliation: His early hatred of Titans is rooted in the destruction of Shiganshina and Carla's death.
  • Self-determination: He resists being controlled by governments, military leaders or the plans of others.

Contradictions

Eren's desire to protect a small circle of loved ones ultimately leads him to commit mass slaughter on a global scale. He speaks of freedom while acting in ways that remove the freedom and lives of countless others. His later calmness does not mean that his decisions are emotionally uncomplicated; the final story reveals fear, grief, selfish longing and an inability to reconcile his desires with the consequences of his actions.

04 / LIFE CHRONOLOGY

Complete Biography

EXPAND BIOGRAPHY & STORY EVENTS
Childhood in Shiganshina

Eren grows up with his parents, Grisha and Carla, in Shiganshina District. He forms a close friendship with Armin and becomes especially close to Mikasa after helping rescue her from kidnappers. The three share a fascination with the world beyond the Walls, although Eren is the most openly impatient with the restrictions of their society.

845 — Fall of Wall Maria

The Colossal Titan appears and breaches the outer gate of Shiganshina; the Armored Titan destroys the inner gate. During the evacuation, Eren sees the Smiling Titan kill and eat Carla while Hannes carries him and Mikasa away. Eren vows to eliminate every Titan.

Inheritance from Grisha

After the fall, Grisha takes Eren into the forest, injects him with Titan serum and gives him the basement key. In his Pure Titan form, Eren consumes Grisha and inherits both the Attack Titan and the Founding Titan. Eren has no conscious memory of this transfer for years.

850 — 104th Cadet Corps

Eren trains alongside Mikasa, Armin, Jean, Sasha, Connie, Reiner, Bertolt, Annie and others. He graduates fifth in the class. His drive to join the Survey Corps is rooted in his desire to fight Titans and see the world outside the Walls.

Battle of Trost — First transformation

During the battle, Eren is swallowed while saving Armin. He transforms into the Attack Titan for the first time and fights other Titans. The revelation makes him a military asset and a perceived threat. After a tribunal, he is placed under the Survey Corps, with Levi's squad responsible for supervising him.

Female Titan and the Warriors

Eren encounters the Female Titan during the expedition beyond the Walls and later learns that Annie Leonhart is its inheritor. Reiner Braun and Bertolt Hoover reveal themselves as the Armored and Colossal Titans and abduct Eren. During the rescue, Eren comes into contact with Dina Fritz's Pure Titan, a royal-blooded Subject of Ymir, and briefly activates the Founding Titan's ability to direct nearby Titans.

Uprising and the Reiss family

Eren learns that the Reiss family holds the royal lineage connected to the Founding Titan. Rod Reiss attempts to have Historia consume Eren and inherit the Founder. Historia rejects the plan and helps Eren escape. The political order inside the Walls is overturned and Historia becomes queen.

Return to Shiganshina and the basement

Eren joins the operation to retake Shiganshina. The battle kills many Scouts, including Erwin Smith; Armin is saved with the Titan serum and inherits the Colossal Titan from Bertolt. The basement reveals Grisha's records: humanity exists beyond the sea, and the people inside the Walls are Eldians whose history is bound to Marley and the Titan conflict.

851 — The ocean

The surviving Scouts reach the sea. While his companions celebrate, Eren looks toward the distant enemy and recognizes that reaching the ocean has not delivered the freedom he imagined.

854 — Infiltration of Marley

Eren separates himself from his former companions and enters Marley undercover as a wounded Eldian soldier. He communicates with his allies through Falco Grice and waits for the moment to strike during Willy Tybur's declaration of war in Liberio.

Raid on Liberio and the War Hammer Titan

Eren transforms during the festival, killing civilians and military personnel as he attacks the gathering. He defeats Lara Tybur's War Hammer Titan and eventually obtains its power. The Survey Corps extracts him, but the raid deepens the conflict and Sasha Blouse is killed during the return.

Yeagerist conflict and the Paths

Eren's actions divide Paradis. The Yeagerists support him, while former comrades and military leaders oppose his methods. Eren and Zeke reach the Paths, where Eren reveals that he has been pursuing his own objective rather than Zeke's plan to sterilize the Eldian population. Eren appeals directly to Ymir Fritz and gains access to the Founding Titan's full power.

The Rumbling

Eren releases the Wall Titans and announces to all Subjects of Ymir that he will destroy the world beyond Paradis. The Rumbling advances across the continents and kills approximately 80 percent of humanity outside the island before Eren is stopped.

Final battle and death

Former enemies form an alliance to stop Eren. At the final confrontation, Mikasa reaches Eren inside the Founding Titan's mouth and kills him by severing his head. The immediate Rumbling ends, the power of the Titans disappears, and the surviving characters face an uncertain future.

05 / TITAN INHERITANCE

Three Titan Powers

EXPAND TITAN POWER DETAILS

Eren is the known inheritor of three of the Nine Titans. He first receives two from Grisha and later acquires the third during the Liberio raid.

Attack Titan

Inherited from: Grisha Yeager, who received it from Eren Kruger.

The Attack Titan is associated with resistance and the pursuit of freedom. Its distinctive memory connection allows inheritors to receive memories from future inheritors. Eren's access to future memories becomes central to his later decisions.

Founding Titan

Inherited from: Grisha Yeager, who took it from Frieda Reiss.

The Founder can influence Subjects of Ymir, including their memories and bodies, and can command Titans. Eren initially cannot use its full abilities because he lacks royal blood. Contact with royal-blooded Titans enables limited activation; later, reaching Ymir in the Paths gives him access to the Founder's power.

War Hammer Titan

Inherited from: Lara Tybur during the Liberio battle.

The War Hammer can create weapons and structures from hardened Titan material. Its inheritor can remain protected in a crystal while controlling the Titan body through a hardened cable. Eren uses its hardening abilities and learns to exploit its connection to the crystal.

Combined inheritance

Holding three Titan powers does not mean Eren can use every ability without limits or conditions. The Founder remains tied to the Paths and royal-blood mechanics; the Attack Titan's memory ability shapes his perception of time; and the War Hammer's material creation consumes resources and requires tactical use.

Curse of Ymir

As a Titan shifter, Eren is subject to the thirteen-year lifespan limit after inheriting a Titan power. The story's final events occur within that shortened lifespan.

06 / CAPABILITIES

Combat Skills & Tactics

EXPAND COMBAT DETAILS

Human combat

Eren receives military training in hand-to-hand combat, sword use and three-dimensional maneuvering equipment. He is not the most technically polished cadet, but he is persistent, physically capable and willing to continue fighting after serious injury.

Titan combat

  • Close-range striking, grappling and throws.
  • Hardening to reinforce fists and create defensive structures.
  • Adaptation through repeated combat against other shifters.
  • Use of the War Hammer's hardened material to create weapons and restraints.
  • Use of the Founder's power to command the Wall Titans during the Rumbling.

Strategic limitations

Eren's impulsiveness and emotional fixation can undermine teamwork. He becomes more calculating over time, but secrecy and unilateral decision-making isolate him from allies and contribute to the political fracture on Paradis.

07 / PERSONAL CONNECTIONS

Relationships & Affiliations

EXPAND RELATIONSHIP DETAILS

Mikasa Ackerman

Mikasa is Eren's closest childhood companion and one of the people he most wants to protect. Their bond includes deep affection, conflict and dependence. Eren's final conversation with Armin reveals his jealousy and fear of losing Mikasa, while Mikasa's love does not prevent her from killing him to stop the Rumbling.

Armin Arlert

Armin is Eren's childhood friend and the person who first shares the dream of seeing the world beyond the Walls. Their relationship is built on trust and imagination, but Eren's later choices place them on opposing sides. Their final conversation exposes Eren's vulnerability and Armin's grief.

Grisha Yeager

Eren's father passes the Attack and Founding Titans to him. Through the Paths and future memories, Eren influences Grisha's actions in the past, creating a disturbing connection between father and son that complicates ordinary cause and effect.

Carla Yeager

Eren's mother represents his childhood home and the loss that initially gives his hatred of Titans its focus. Her death becomes central to his early vow and, through the story's time-related revelations, to the tragedy of his later actions.

Zeke Yeager

Eren's older half-brother shares his connection to Grisha but develops a radically different response to Eldian suffering. Zeke seeks to end Eldian reproduction; Eren uses Zeke to reach the Paths, then rejects his plan.

Historia Reiss

Historia's royal blood is politically important to the military's plans for the Founder. Eren opposes the idea of sacrificing her to maintain Titan power and later withholds much of his plan from her.

Reiner Braun

Reiner is both Eren's former comrade and an enemy Warrior. Their confrontation in Marley reveals how both have been shaped by duty, trauma and the belief that they have no acceptable alternative.

Survey Corps

The Corps gives Eren comrades, purpose and a path beyond the Walls. His later unilateral actions rupture that bond, even as many former comrades risk their lives to stop him.

08 / CHARACTER ARC

Character Development

EXPAND DEVELOPMENT ANALYSIS
The avenger

Eren defines freedom as escaping the Walls and destroying the Titans that threaten humanity.

The weapon of humanity

After discovering his Titan power, he becomes a military asset and learns that survival requires cooperation and sacrifice.

The seeker of truth

The basement revelation changes the identity of the enemy. The conflict is no longer simply humanity versus Titans; it is a human conflict across nations and generations.

The isolated actor

Future memories and his view of the coming conflict push Eren toward secrecy. He separates himself from friends and chooses to act without their consent.

The destroyer

Eren unleashes the Rumbling, committing mass murder in pursuit of his objectives and the future he believes he has seen.

The defeated friend

His final conversation with Armin reveals a frightened, emotionally dependent young man beneath the certainty he projected. His friends stop him, but they cannot undo the destruction.

Interpretive note: The story does not reduce Eren to a single motive. His actions involve protection, freedom, anger, future memories, personal desire and destructive impulses. These explanations help interpret his choices; they do not excuse the harm he causes.

09 / FINAL FATE

Death, Legacy & Ending

EXPAND FINAL FATE DETAILS
REVEAL THE COMPLETE FINAL REVELATIONS

In the final battle, the alliance reaches Eren's Founding Titan form. Mikasa enters its mouth and decapitates him. She carries his head away and gives him a final farewell. The immediate Rumbling stops, and the Titan power vanishes from the world; those who had been transformed return to human form.

In the Paths conversation with Armin, Eren admits that he wanted his friends to survive and become people who could be regarded as heroes for stopping him. He also reveals selfish and confused feelings, including his inability to accept the thought of Mikasa loving someone else. The conversation does not offer a clean moral justification for the Rumbling.

The story indicates that the Rumbling kills approximately 80 percent of humanity beyond Paradis. The surviving world and Paradis remain politically unstable. The distant-future epilogue shows the possibility of renewed conflict long after Eren's death; the final image of a boy approaching a tree resembling the origin site of Titan power leaves the future deliberately open.

Legacy

Eren becomes both the person who sought to free his people and the person responsible for catastrophic destruction. His actions end the Titan power but do not resolve the political, historical or ethical conflicts that produced the story's violence.

10 / CRITICAL INTERPRETATION

Psychological & Thematic Analysis

EXPAND ANALYSIS

Freedom as an absolute

Eren's idea of freedom begins as a response to confinement. As the story expands, he encounters restrictions created not only by walls but by history, power, fear and the freedom of other people. His pursuit becomes contradictory when he tries to secure his own freedom by destroying the lives and choices of others.

Trauma and fixation

The fall of Shiganshina gives Eren a clear target for grief and rage. Later revelations complicate that target, but his intense need to move forward remains. His trauma is part of his characterization, not a complete explanation or an excuse for his later decisions.

Memory and agency

The Attack Titan's memory connection and the Founding Titan's Paths create a narrative in which past and future influence one another. Eren experiences memories that make the future appear fixed, yet the story still presents him as making choices and bearing responsibility for them.

Protection and moral collapse

Eren repeatedly frames his actions around protecting friends and Paradis. The scale of the Rumbling reveals the moral collapse of treating the survival of one group as permission to annihilate others. The narrative invites examination of that contradiction rather than a simple celebration of strength or determination.

12 / PURPOSES & REASONING

Purposes & Reasoning

EXPAND PURPOSES & REASONING

Eren’s purpose in the story is to turn the promise of freedom into the central moral crisis. He begins as a child who wants to escape confinement and destroy the Titans, but every revelation expands the meaning of the walls around him: history, fear, inherited memory and political hatred. His choices ask whether freedom can remain moral when pursued without limits. The story uses Eren to examine the transformation of a victim into a person capable of inflicting catastrophic harm. His reasoning changes as he gains knowledge of the future and becomes increasingly convinced that the path before him is unavoidable. That conviction is part of the tragedy, not proof that his actions are justified. The audience is asked to distinguish understanding his fear, love for his friends and desire for freedom from endorsing the Rumbling. Eren’s narrative purpose is to make the desire for liberation collide with responsibility toward people beyond one’s own community.
