Mikasa Ackerman

An elite soldier devoted to protecting Eren. Her Ackerman heritage gives her extraordinary combat ability. Despite her love for Eren, she ultimately kills him to stop the Rumbling; her choice helps Ymir release her fixation on King Fritz.

01 / IDENTITY

Identity

EXPAND IDENTITY DETAILS

Mikasa Ackerman is a central protagonist, born to an Ackerman father and a mother descended from the Hizuru Azumabito line. After human traffickers murder her parents, Eren Yeager rescues her; Grisha and Carla welcome her into their household. She later graduates first in the 104th Cadet Corps and joins the Survey Corps. Birthday: February 10. Japanese voice: Yui Ishikawa; English voice: Trina Nishimura.

02 / APPEARANCE

Appearance

EXPAND APPEARANCE DETAILS

Mikasa's dark hair changes length across the story, from long childhood hair to shorter cadet and Scout styles. Her red scarf, given to her by Eren after he rescues her, is her most recognizable personal object. Her clothing shifts from cadet uniform to Survey Corps equipment and later the darker combat gear used during the Liberio operation.

03 / PERSONALITY

Personality

EXPAND PERSONALITY DETAILS

Disciplined, observant, protective and decisive, Mikasa is emotionally guarded with strangers but deeply attached to Eren and Armin. Her instinct to protect Eren can narrow her perspective, yet she gradually learns that loyalty does not require surrendering moral judgment. Her compassion remains present even when she must oppose someone she loves.

04 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography

EXPAND BIOGRAPHY DETAILS

After the fall of Wall Maria, Mikasa enters the 104th Cadet Corps and graduates at the top of her class. She fights at Trost, joins the Survey Corps and takes part in the Female Titan pursuit, the Uprising and the return to Shiganshina. She later participates in the conflict with Marley. Eren's growing distance and his decision to initiate the Rumbling force her to reconsider what protecting him can mean.

05 / ABILITIES

Abilities

EXPAND ABILITIES DETAILS

Mikasa is an Ackerman, a lineage associated with exceptional physical capability. She is not a Titan shifter and has no Titan regeneration. Her strengths come from reflexes, conditioning, coordination, spatial judgment, weapon proficiency and extensive battlefield experience. Her maternal ancestry also connects her to the Azumabito family of Hizuru.

06 / COMBAT & SKILLS

Combat & Skills

EXPAND COMBAT & SKILLS DETAILS

Among the Survey Corps' strongest human fighters, Mikasa uses vertical maneuvering equipment with exceptional speed and precision. She is highly effective with dual blades and later uses thunder spears and firearms. Her combat ability remains human: injuries, exhaustion and equipment limits still apply.

07 / RELATIONSHIPS

Relationships

EXPAND RELATIONSHIPS DETAILS

Eren is the person who rescued her and the central emotional bond of her life. Armin is her other childhood companion and trusted friend. Grisha and Carla become her family after the rescue. Her comrades include Jean, Connie and Sasha. Her maternal connection to Kiyomi Azumabito reveals her family's political history.

08 / DEVELOPMENT

Development

EXPAND DEVELOPMENT DETAILS

Mikasa begins with a survival bond to Eren and a desire to protect her remaining family. Military service gives her a wider community and purpose. The revelations about the outside world and Eren's choices force her to distinguish the boy who saved her from the person choosing mass destruction. Her final decision asserts that love cannot require complicity.

09 / FINAL FATE

Final Fate

EXPAND FINAL FATE DETAILS

Mikasa kills Eren during the final battle, ending the Rumbling. Her decision is connected to Ymir Fritz finally releasing her bond to King Fritz, after witnessing Mikasa choose love without obedience. Mikasa survives, gives Eren a burial and continues to remember him in the years that follow.

10 / ANALYSIS

Analysis

EXPAND ANALYSIS DETAILS

Mikasa's central conflict is attachment versus ethical independence. Her defining strength is not only combat skill but the capacity to make a devastating moral decision. She demonstrates that caring for someone does not mean accepting every action they take. Stopping harm does not erase grief, love or memory.

11 / CHRONOLOGY

Chronology

EXPAND CHRONOLOGY DETAILS

Childhood outside Shiganshina; murder of her parents and rescue by Eren; life with the Yeager family; 104th Cadet Corps and first-place graduation; Battle of Trost; Survey Corps operations; Uprising; return to Shiganshina; Marley and Liberio; alliance against Eren; final battle and postwar life.
