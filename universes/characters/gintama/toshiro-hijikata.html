02Biography, Personality & MotivationsEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Strict 'Demonic Vice-Commander' and Gintoki's foil.

World & Story Context: Anachronistic late-Edo Japan blending wooden samurai districts and kimono with skyscrapers, spaceports (the Terminal), scooters, and *Weekly Shōnen Jump*, ruled by a puppet Bakufu under the alien Tendoshu.

03Abilities, Equipment & LimitationsEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Documented Arsenal: Master katana duelist, tactical command

System Rules: Lethal samurai swordplay and Yato superhuman martial arts collide with Altana planetary immortality and Amanto sci-fi weaponry.

04Chronology, Decisions & Final StatusEXPAND / COLLAPSE
SPOILER STATUS: Continues leading the Shinsengumi.

Continuity Context: Closely adapts Hideaki Sorachi's 704-lesson manga with ~20–25 anime-original episodes (including the Ep. 1–2 pilot). In 2017, Gintama. aired Rakuyou first and then inserted the skipped comedic arcs in Porori-hen (Ep. 329–341) before Silver Soul.

05Ensemble RelationshipsEXPAND / COLLAPSE
  • Gintoki Sakata (Yorozuya (Ex-Shiroyasha)) — Lazy, sweet-toothed samurai carrying the trauma of executing his teacher Shouyou.
  • Shinpachi Shimura (Yorozuya) — Timid dojo heir who matures into a steadfast samurai.
  • Kagura (Yorozuya / Yato Clan) — Fierce, rice-loving Yato girl who rejects her clan's bloodlust.
  • Isao Kondo (Shinsengumi Commander) — Beloved commander who anchors the Shinsengumi.
  • Sougo Okita (Shinsengumi 1st Division) — Deadly prodigy masking loyalty behind a sadistic grin.
  • Kotaro Katsura (Joui Patriots) — Eccentric rebel ('Runaway Kotaro') turned pragmatic protector of Edo.
  • Shinsuke Takasugi (Kiheitai Commander) — Gintoki's estranged comrade driven to destroy the world that killed Shouyou.
  • Kamui (Harusame 7th Division (Yato)) — Kagura's battle-hungry older brother.
  • Utsuro (Yoshida Shouyou) (Tendoshu / Naraku) — Nihilistic immortal sharing the body of Gintoki's kind teacher.
