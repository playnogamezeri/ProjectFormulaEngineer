History and background

Morgen is Nacht Faust's twin brother and a former member of the Grey Deer squad. His values and outlook contrast with Nacht's early conduct. During the Faust family's attempt to bind a supreme devil, Morgen intervenes to stop the ritual and is fatally wounded. In the final arc, Lucius resurrects him as a Paladin.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Morgen is remembered for his kindness, optimism and strong desire to protect others. His relationship with Nacht is central to both brothers' character histories.
