02 / IDENTIFICATION

Identity & Overview

Name: Mask De Masculine

Role: Quincy; Sternritter S — The Superstar

A theatrical wrestler-like Sternritter whose power grows through the support of his devoted companion James.

03 / CHARACTERIZATION

Personality & Motivations

Mask's strength is inseparable from performance and audience approval. The relationship with James complicates the boundary between a fighter's own power and power sustained by another.

04 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography & Story Development

Mask fights several Soul Reapers, including Renji Abarai and Kensei Muguruma. James cheers him on and appears to restore or amplify his strength after apparent defeats. Renji ultimately overcomes Mask with his upgraded Bankai and destroys the source of his repeated recovery.

05 / COMBAT

Powers & Abilities

The Superstar strengthens Mask through James's cheering. Mask uses wrestling-style physical attacks and a Vollständig that greatly increases his power.

Combat assessments distinguish demonstrated feats from reputation and inference. Matchups, preparation, injuries, and technique interactions can change outcomes.
