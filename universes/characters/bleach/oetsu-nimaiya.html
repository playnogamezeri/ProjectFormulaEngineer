04 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography & Story Development

Ōetsu resides in the Royal Palace and oversees the creation and restoration of Zanpakutō. During the Wandenreich invasion of the Royal Palace, he confronts the Schutzstaffel and initially defeats them with overwhelming sword skill. Their later return forces the Royal Guard conflict to escalate.

05 / COMBAT

Powers & Abilities

Ōetsu is a master swordsman and creator of Zanpakutō. His sword Sayafushi is exceptionally sharp and difficult to store in a conventional sheath.

Combat assessments distinguish demonstrated feats from reputation and inference. Matchups, preparation, injuries and technique interactions can change outcomes.

06 / CONNECTIONS

Relationships & Affiliations
