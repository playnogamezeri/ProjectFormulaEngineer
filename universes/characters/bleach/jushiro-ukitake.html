04 / BIOGRAPHY & CHRONOLOGY

Biography & Story Development

Ukitake's illness begins in childhood and is connected to Mimihagi, the right arm of the Soul King, which preserves his life. He supports Rukia and Ichigo during the Soul Society crisis and later participates in the defense against the Wandenreich. He ultimately offers himself in an attempt to preserve the world's balance.
