History and background

Nacht is the Black Bulls' vice-captain and a former associate of the Faust family who returns after years operating as a spy and devil host. His twin brother Morgen's death shapes his guilt and his harsh judgment of himself and others. He infiltrates the Spade Kingdom and brings critical knowledge about devils and the Qliphoth.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Nacht is cynical, severe and openly critical of people he considers foolish. His harshness is rooted in guilt and grief. He gradually recognizes the value of the Black Bulls' bonds, even while struggling to forgive himself.

Character-reading note: This describes traits demonstrated in the narrative; it does not assign a clinical diagnosis.

03 · Magic, Abilities & Equipment

Shadow Magic allows him to manipulate shadows for movement, restraint, concealment and attacks. He has contracts with four devils—Gimodelo, Slotos, Plumede and Walgner—and uses relics to access different Unite forms. Each form changes his capabilities and combat options. His power has contract conditions and does not make him invulnerable.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

Morgen is his twin brother and defining loss. Yami and the Black Bulls are his squad and chosen family. His devils are contracted partners, not ordinary pets.

Family, friendship, rivalry, mentorship, squad membership, political allegiance and romantic interest are distinct relationship types. Shared affiliation does not automatically establish friendship.

05 · Story Chronology & Development

He becomes central during the Spade Kingdom raid, training Asta and leading the anti-devil strategy. His personal conflict with Morgen's memory reaches its climax in the final arc.

Flashbacks, reincarnation memories and later revelations are placed in the character's personal chronology and not mistaken for present-day events.

06 · Achievements, Battles & Contributions

His infiltration, devil knowledge and multiple Unite forms make him one of the key tactical specialists in the battle against the Underworld threat.
