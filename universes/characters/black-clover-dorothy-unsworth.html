History and background

Dorothy is the captain of the Coral Peacocks, a Magic Knight squad. She is often seen asleep, but her apparent inactivity conceals a highly capable mage. Her Dream Magic creates Glamour World, a dream dimension in which she can shape the environment and manifest imagined things.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Dorothy is whimsical, playful and difficult to read. Her relaxed demeanor contrasts with her formidable magical ability. She is observant and capable of serious judgment when the situation demands it.

Character-reading note: This section describes traits demonstrated in the narrative. It does not assign a clinical diagnosis or infer motives beyond the character's depicted actions.

03 · Magic, Abilities & Equipment

Dream Magic allows her to draw targets into Glamour World, where the environment is under her control. The dimension's rules create powerful advantages, but its effectiveness depends on her maintaining control and on opponents finding ways to challenge the dream space.

05 · Story Chronology & Development

She contributes to the Royal Knights era and the elf reincarnation crisis, where Glamour World becomes a major tactical arena. Her later involvement reflects the continuing need for captains to defend the kingdom.
