History and background

Yami is a foreigner who arrives in the Clover Kingdom and becomes a Magic Knight before founding and leading the Black Bulls. He recruits people rejected by other squads and pushes them to exceed their limits. His dark attribute is unusual and becomes strategically important in conflicts involving devils and the Underworld.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Yami is blunt, intimidating, irreverent and demanding. He trusts people to overcome their limits, often through harsh training and pressure. His leadership is built on accepting misfits and judging them by what they do rather than their status.

Character-reading note: This describes traits demonstrated in the narrative; it does not assign a clinical diagnosis.

03 · Magic, Abilities & Equipment

Dark Magic is slow but exceptionally potent and can interact with otherworldly powers. Yami uses a katana to channel dark spells and combines his magic with ki sensing to read movement and intent. His techniques include Dark Cloaked attacks, Dimension Slash and later advanced applications. His physical strength and battle instincts are exceptional.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

Charlotte develops romantic feelings for him. Nacht is his vice-captain. The Black Bulls are his chosen family. His homeland and family history become important through the Hino Country storyline.

Family, friendship, rivalry, mentorship, squad membership, political allegiance and romantic interest are distinct relationship types. Shared affiliation does not automatically establish friendship.

05 · Story Chronology & Development

He leads the Black Bulls through the elf crisis and Spade Kingdom raid, where he is targeted because of Dark Magic's role in the Qliphoth. His past and homeland are explored later.

Flashbacks, reincarnation memories and later revelations are placed in the character's personal chronology and not mistaken for present-day events.

06 · Achievements, Battles & Contributions

His leadership transforms a rejected squad into a family of capable fighters. His Dark Magic and katana techniques are decisive in several major conflicts.
