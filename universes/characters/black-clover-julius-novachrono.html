History and background

Julius is the Clover Kingdom's Wizard King and one of its most powerful and respected mages. He is fascinated by unusual magic and believes the kingdom can become fairer by recognizing talent beyond inherited status. His apparent death and later return in a younger body are central to the series' political and supernatural developments. The later revelation of his connection to Lucius Zogratis complicates the meaning of his identity and agency.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Julius is curious, energetic and genuinely interested in people and magic. He is approachable despite his office and seeks to reform the kingdom's class hierarchy. His optimism coexists with the burdens of leadership and the consequences of the hidden Zogratis plot.

Character-reading note: This describes traits demonstrated in the narrative; it does not assign a clinical diagnosis.

03 · Magic, Abilities & Equipment

Time Magic allows him to store, steal, accelerate, decelerate and restrain time through specialized spells. His grimoire and accumulated time are tied to his longevity and combat capabilities. His younger reincarnated state has reduced reserves compared with his former peak; the later Lucius connection must be distinguished from Julius's public persona.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

He is a mentor and authority figure to many Magic Knights, including Yami and William. Marx François serves as his close aide. His relationship with Lucius is the central identity conflict of the final arc.

Family, friendship, rivalry, mentorship, squad membership, political allegiance and romantic interest are distinct relationship types. Shared affiliation does not automatically establish friendship.

05 · Story Chronology & Development

He leads the kingdom through the Eye of the Midnight Sun crisis, the elf reincarnation conflict and the Spade threat. His apparent death and return reshape the Wizard King's role. The final arc reveals the deeper connection between Julius and Lucius.

Flashbacks, reincarnation memories and later revelations are placed in the character's personal chronology and not mistaken for present-day events.

06 · Achievements, Battles & Contributions

His leadership, protection of the capital and use of Time Magic make him a defining figure in Clover's modern history. His identity is central to the final conflict.
