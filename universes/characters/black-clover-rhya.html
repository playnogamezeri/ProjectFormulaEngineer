History and background

Rhya is one of the Third Eye and a reincarnated elf. He is associated with the elf tribe's revenge campaign and is capable of copying the magic of other mages through his Copy Magic. His casual demeanor hides a capable and experienced fighter whose history is bound to the tragedy of the elves.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Rhya is laid-back, sarcastic and often appears uninterested, but he is loyal to his companions and deeply affected by the loss of the elf tribe. His apparent laziness should not be mistaken for a lack of resolve.

Character-reading note: This describes traits demonstrated in the narrative; it does not assign a clinical diagnosis.

03 · Magic, Abilities & Equipment

Copy Magic allows him to reproduce other people's magic, generally through contact with the relevant grimoire or magical source. The copied abilities are constrained by the rules of his magic and the techniques he can effectively use; copying does not automatically grant the original user's full experience.

Evaluation dimensions

  • Attribute and source: separate innate magic from spirit, devil, contract, reincarnation or external power.
  • Technique: record range, precision, area coverage, defensive utility and combinations.
  • Resources: mana, stamina, preparation, grimoire development and transformation conditions.
  • Limitations: retain demonstrated costs, counters and activation requirements; do not assume unlimited power.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

He is connected to Patolli and the Third Eye, and to the reincarnated elf group. His human host is distinct from his elf identity.

Family, friendship, rivalry, mentorship, squad membership, political allegiance and romantic interest are distinct relationship types. Shared affiliation does not automatically establish friendship.

05 · Story Chronology & Development

He fights the Magic Knights during the elf conflict and later participates in the events surrounding the truth about Zagred and the elves' restoration.

Flashbacks, reincarnation memories and later revelations are placed in the character's personal chronology and not mistaken for present-day events.

06 · Achievements, Battles & Contributions

His versatility through Copy Magic makes him unpredictable and valuable to the elf group. His eventual cooperation contributes to the resolution of the crisis.
