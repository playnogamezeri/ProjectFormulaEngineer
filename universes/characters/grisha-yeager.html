Grisha Yeager

Eren and Zeke's father. A former restorationist from Marley, he inherits the Attack Titan, reaches the Walls and later takes the Founding Titan from Frieda Reiss. Under future Eren's influence through the Paths, he kills most of the Reiss family and passes both Titans to young Eren.

01 / IDENTITY

Identity

EXPAND IDENTITY DETAILS

Grisha Yeager is a doctor, an Eldian Restorationist and the father of Zeke and Eren Yeager. His life connects Marley’s persecution of Eldians, the hidden history of the Walls and the inheritance of the Attack Titan. Grisha’s decisions shape the central conflict across generations, but his motives are complicated by trauma, political conviction, family failure and the extraordinary burden of inherited memories.

02 / APPEARANCE

Appearance

EXPAND APPEARANCE DETAILS

Grisha is a broad-shouldered adult man with dark hair, facial hair and a serious expression. In his life in Marley, his clothing and posture reflect the restrictions placed on an Eldian living in an internment zone. After reaching the Walls, he adopts the appearance and habits of a physician and family man. His visual presentation shifts between a public healer and a secretive revolutionary, reflecting the divided life he leads.

03 / PERSONALITY

Personality

EXPAND PERSONALITY DETAILS

Grisha is intelligent, determined and capable of deep affection, but he is also driven by guilt and ideological urgency. His early desire to restore Eldian dignity becomes entangled with the expectations of the Restorationists. As a father, he fails to understand Zeke’s emotional needs and places political hopes onto his son. Later, he builds a loving life with Carla and Eren, yet the past and the mission he carries never fully release him. His compassion and his capacity for terrible decisions coexist.

04 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography

EXPAND BIOGRAPHY DETAILS

As a child in Liberio, Grisha loses his sister Faye after she is taken outside the internment zone and killed. The injustice radicalizes him. He joins the Eldian Restorationists, marries Dina Fritz and has a son, Zeke. When Zeke reports the group, Grisha and the other Restorationists are sentenced to be transformed into Titans on Paradis. Eren Kruger saves Grisha and passes the Attack Titan to him, directing him toward the Walls. Grisha reaches Paradis, marries Carla, has Eren and becomes a doctor. After Wall Maria falls, he obtains the Founding Titan from the Reiss family and eventually passes both Titan powers to Eren.

05 / ABILITIES

Abilities

EXPAND ABILITIES DETAILS

Grisha inherits the Attack Titan from Eren Kruger and later takes the Founding Titan from Frieda Reiss. His Titan abilities include the Attack Titan’s physical combat capacity and its unusual connection to memories across inheritors. As a physician, he also has medical knowledge that allows him to serve the people of the Walls. His greatest influence is not a single technique but the knowledge and power he transfers to Eren, including the consequences of future memory.

06 / COMBAT & SKILLS

Combat & Skills

EXPAND COMBAT & SKILLS DETAILS

Grisha’s combat experience is limited compared with trained soldiers and later Titan shifters, but he can fight effectively as the Attack Titan. His confrontation with the Reiss family is driven by desperation and the pressure of the mission Kruger gave him. The story reveals that this act is shaped by Eren’s influence through the Paths, complicating any simple account of Grisha as either an independent aggressor or a powerless instrument. His human medical work contrasts sharply with the violence of his Titan inheritance.

07 / RELATIONSHIPS

Relationships

EXPAND RELATIONSHIPS DETAILS

Zeke is Grisha’s first son, and their relationship is damaged by Grisha’s attempt to make him carry the Restorationists’ cause. Dina is Grisha’s first wife and a royal-blooded Eldian whose fate becomes a devastating part of the story. Carla and Eren form his second family, and Grisha’s love for them is genuine, even as he burdens Eren with a mission. Kruger becomes his rescuer and predecessor, while the Reiss family becomes the target of the task he is ordered to complete.

08 / DEVELOPMENT

Development

EXPAND DEVELOPMENT DETAILS

Grisha’s story is revealed in fragments, first as a mysterious father and then as a child shaped by state violence. The basement revelations expose his past and force the audience to reinterpret earlier events. His relationship with Zeke shows how victimhood can be reproduced as harm within a family. His later interactions with Zeke through the Paths reveal remorse and an awareness of his failures. He is not simply redeemed by regret; the consequences of his actions remain.

09 / FINAL FATE

Final Fate

EXPAND FINAL FATE DETAILS

Grisha dies after passing the Attack and Founding Titans to Eren. Through the Paths, his memories and actions are revisited, and Zeke witnesses the emotional reality behind his father’s choices. Grisha’s final legacy is therefore both the power Eren inherits and the unresolved damage left within his family. His remorse does not undo the violence he committed or the chain of events he helped set in motion.

10 / ANALYSIS

Analysis

EXPAND ANALYSIS DETAILS

Grisha embodies the way historical oppression can shape family life and political action across generations. His childhood trauma helps explain his radicalization, but it does not make every later decision justified. His failure with Zeke demonstrates how a parent can turn a child into a vessel for a cause. His later tenderness toward Eren complicates the picture, while the Paths reveal how individual agency can become entangled with memory, destiny and manipulation.

11 / CHRONOLOGY

Chronology

EXPAND CHRONOLOGY DETAILS

Grisha grows up in Liberio, loses Faye and joins the Restorationists. He marries Dina and fathers Zeke, is betrayed and sentenced to exile on Paradis, then inherits the Attack Titan from Eren Kruger. He enters the Walls, marries Carla and fathers Eren. After Wall Maria falls, he takes the Founding Titan from the Reiss family and passes both powers to Eren before his death.

13 / PURPOSES & REASONING

Purposes & Reasoning

EXPAND PURPOSES & REASONING

Grisha’s purpose is to connect public history with private family damage. His childhood under Marleyan oppression helps explain his political awakening, while his treatment of Zeke shows how a person harmed by an ideology can reproduce its pressures at home. His later life with Carla and Eren demonstrates that sincere love can coexist with secrecy, guilt and decisions that place enormous burdens on children. Grisha’s reasoning is repeatedly shaped by competing duties: the Restorationist cause, Kruger’s mission, his family’s safety and the memories that connect Titan inheritors. The Paths reveal that his agency is complicated by future memory and Eren’s influence, but this does not make every consequence disappear. His role asks how historical trauma travels through generations and whether a parent can protect a child without turning that child into an instrument of a cause.
