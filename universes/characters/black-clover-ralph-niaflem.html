History

Ralph is a loyal servant of House Grinberryall, the former royal family of the Spade Kingdom. After the Dark Triad overthrows the royal house, he joins the Resistance. He crosses the dangerous Grand Magic Zone to reach Hage and reveal Yuno's royal origins, carrying the history of the coup and the survival of the Grinberryall line.

02 · Personality

Ralph is devoted, courageous and deeply loyal to the Grinberryall family. His commitment to restoring the rightful royal line motivates his dangerous journey.

03 · Magic & Abilities

Ralph possesses Fire Magic. His narrative importance is primarily as a messenger, witness and member of the Spade Kingdom Resistance rather than as a prominently featured combatant.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

House Grinberryall; Yuno Grinberryall; Ciel and Loyce Grinberryall; Spade Kingdom Resistance.

05 · Story Chronology

Serves the Grinberryall royal family, joins the Resistance after the coup, and travels to Clover Kingdom to find Yuno and disclose his identity.

06 · Achievements & Contributions

Successfully crosses the Grand Magic Zone and reaches Hage despite exhaustion and injury, enabling Yuno's past and royal heritage to be revealed.

07 · Current Status & Continuity

Alive in the story material covered here. Remains associated with the Spade Kingdom Resistance and the restored Grinberryall family.
