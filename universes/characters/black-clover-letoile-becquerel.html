History

Letoile is a noblewoman and member of the Golden Dawn. During the elf reincarnation, she becomes the host of the elf Kivn. After the elves are exorcised, she returns to the Golden Dawn. During Zenon's assault on the headquarters, she and Klaus combine defensive magic but are overwhelmed and seriously injured before William's healing spell saves them.

02 · Personality

Letoile is serious and selfless, prioritizing the safety of her comrades.

03 · Magic & Abilities

Compass Magic creates and manipulates compasses. Her spells can redirect trajectories, orient targets and guide attacks toward designated opponents.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

Golden Dawn; Klaus Lunettes; William Vangeance; elf host Kivn.

05 · Story Chronology

Introduced as a Golden Dawn member and elf host during the reincarnation conflict. Later fights alongside Klaus during Zenon's assault and survives after being healed.

06 · Achievements & Contributions

Her Compass Magic provides battlefield control by changing trajectories and guiding attacks. Her defensive teamwork with Klaus is notable during the Golden Dawn's defeat.

07 · Current Status & Continuity

Alive after the attack on the Golden Dawn headquarters. The canonical surname is Becquerel; the uploaded image filename uses the shorter spelling 'letoile-becca'.
