Gabi Braun

Reiner's cousin and a fiercely patriotic Marleyan candidate. After killing Sasha, she confronts the reality of the hatred she has inherited and becomes part of the effort to stop the Rumbling.

01 / IDENTITY

Identity

EXPAND IDENTITY DETAILS

Gabi Braun is an Eldian child from Marley’s Liberio internment zone and one of the leading Warrior candidates. She is introduced as exceptionally capable, fiercely patriotic and convinced that military distinction can improve her family’s position. Her story challenges the certainty with which she initially views the world: the people she has been taught to regard as devils become individuals whose lives resemble her own.

02 / APPEARANCE

Appearance

EXPAND APPEARANCE DETAILS

Gabi is a young girl with dark hair, sharp eyes and an alert, determined expression. Her compact build and military clothing emphasize her place among the Warrior candidates. She is frequently shown moving with confidence and urgency, and her expressions can shift quickly from triumph to anger or confusion. Her appearance gradually becomes less defined by the uniform and more by her relationships with people outside the world she was taught to understand.

03 / PERSONALITY

Personality

EXPAND PERSONALITY DETAILS

Gabi is bold, competitive, disciplined and outspoken. She has internalized Marley’s propaganda so thoroughly that she treats its claims as unquestionable truth. Her courage is genuine, but it is initially directed through a narrow worldview that equates obedience and military success with moral worth. She is also intelligent, perceptive and capable of revising her beliefs when confronted with evidence and personal relationships. Her flaws are not erased by her age; they are shown as learned and therefore capable of being challenged.

04 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography

EXPAND BIOGRAPHY DETAILS

Raised in Liberio, Gabi competes in the Warrior candidate program and is expected to inherit a Titan power. During the Marley Mid-East conflict she distinguishes herself through daring action. The attack on Liberio by Eren and the Survey Corps destroys the boundaries of her understanding: she witnesses violence against her community and kills Sasha Blouse while escaping. Gabi later reaches Paradis with Falco, where her captivity and contact with the Blouse family force her to confront the people she had been taught to hate. She becomes involved in the struggle against the Rumbling and the effort to stop it.

05 / ABILITIES

Abilities

EXPAND ABILITIES DETAILS

Gabi has exceptional marksmanship, tactical awareness and physical courage for her age. She is trained to use Marleyan military equipment and can remain composed in dangerous situations. Her ability to identify opportunities and act quickly makes her effective in small-scale combat. She does not possess a Titan power in the story; her significance comes from human skill, determination and the capacity to change her understanding.

06 / COMBAT & SKILLS

Combat & Skills

EXPAND COMBAT & SKILLS DETAILS

Gabi’s combat style is based on precision, initiative and the willingness to exploit an opening. Her use of a rifle and explosive tactics demonstrates training beyond that of an ordinary child. She is capable of lethal action, but her success does not make her invulnerable: she is still physically young and dependent on circumstances, allies and weapons. Her later role is less about defeating opponents and more about helping prevent further destruction.

07 / RELATIONSHIPS

Relationships

EXPAND RELATIONSHIPS DETAILS

Falco is her closest companion, and his concern for her often reveals emotional truths she is unwilling to acknowledge. Her family’s status in Liberio shapes her sense of duty. Sasha’s family becomes central to her transformation: Kaya’s anger and the Blouses’ eventual decision not to answer violence with revenge force Gabi to confront the humanity of those she harmed. Her relationship with the alliance also develops from distrust toward cooperation.

08 / DEVELOPMENT

Development

EXPAND DEVELOPMENT DETAILS

Gabi’s arc is a process of ideological unlearning. At first she repeats Marley’s language and sees military achievement as the route to dignity. The Liberio attack intensifies her hatred, but her experience on Paradis undermines the simple division between devils and innocent people. She must acknowledge that her actions have consequences and that the people she was taught to despise have families, grief and moral choices of their own. Her change is gradual and contested, not an instant conversion.

09 / FINAL FATE

Final Fate

EXPAND FINAL FATE DETAILS

Gabi survives the final conflict and remains with Falco and the other survivors. Her role in the alliance contributes to the effort to stop the Rumbling. The ending places her among the younger people who may help build a future less governed by inherited hatred. It does not guarantee that prejudice disappears, but her changed understanding gives the possibility of a different response to conflict.

10 / ANALYSIS

Analysis

EXPAND ANALYSIS DETAILS

Gabi is a study of how propaganda can shape a child’s identity before she has the freedom or experience to question it. The narrative does not excuse her violence, especially Sasha’s death, but it asks the audience to examine the system that taught her to see violence as righteous. Her transformation depends on encounters with people who refuse to reduce her to her worst action. She becomes a symbol of the difficult work of recognizing another person’s humanity without denying harm.

11 / CHRONOLOGY

Chronology

EXPAND CHRONOLOGY DETAILS

Gabi grows up in Liberio and rises through the Warrior candidate program. She fights in Marley’s war, survives the attack on Liberio and kills Sasha while escaping. She travels to Paradis with Falco, is confronted by the Blouse family and gradually revises her beliefs. She later assists the alliance against the Rumbling and survives the final battle.
