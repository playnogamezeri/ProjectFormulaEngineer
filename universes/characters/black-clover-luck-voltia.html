History and background

Luck is a Black Bulls member known for his love of fighting strong opponents. His childhood was shaped by a difficult relationship with his mother and the pressure to appear happy and powerful. The squad gives him a place where his combat enthusiasm can be accepted and directed toward protecting others.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Luck is cheerful, energetic and battle-hungry. His smile can conceal unresolved pain, and his development involves learning that strength is not only about finding the next opponent but also about protecting his companions.

Character-reading note: This describes traits demonstrated in the narrative; it does not assign a clinical diagnosis.

03 · Magic, Abilities & Equipment

Lightning Magic gives him high speed, electrical attacks and close-range combat options. His fighting style combines fast movement, reinforcement and aggressive spellcasting. He develops advanced techniques through training and later mana-control methods.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

Magna is his closest sparring partner and rival within the Black Bulls. The squad becomes his family. His past with his mother remains a key emotional element.

Family, friendship, rivalry, mentorship, squad membership, political allegiance and romantic interest are distinct relationship types. Shared affiliation does not automatically establish friendship.

05 · Story Chronology & Development

He appears in early Black Bulls missions, the Royal Knights selection and the Heart Kingdom training period. His later fights demonstrate improved technique and control.

Flashbacks, reincarnation memories and later revelations are placed in the character's personal chronology and not mistaken for present-day events.

06 · Achievements, Battles & Contributions

His speed and aggressive offense make him a dangerous close-range fighter. His growth includes advanced rune and mana techniques and better teamwork.
