History and background

Loyce is the former king of the Spade Kingdom and a member of House Grinberryall. He rules with Queen Ciel before the Dark Triad seizes power. He and other members of the royal family are killed during the coup, while the infant Yuno is carried to the Clover Kingdom.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Loyce is remembered as a kind ruler who presided over a peaceful period before the coup.
