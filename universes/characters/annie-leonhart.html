Annie Leonhart

A reserved Marleyan Warrior who infiltrates the military and possesses the Female Titan. After years in crystal stasis, she returns and eventually joins the alliance to stop Eren.

01 / IDENTITY

Identity

EXPAND IDENTITY DETAILS

Annie Leonhart is an Eldian Warrior from Marley and inheritor of the Female Titan. She infiltrates Paradis alongside Reiner Braun and Bertolt Hoover and joins the 104th Cadet Corps. Her central personal motivation is to return to her father in Marley. Japanese voice: Yū Shimamura; English voice: Lauren Landa.

02 / APPEARANCE

Appearance

EXPAND APPEARANCE DETAILS

Annie has pale blond hair, blue eyes and a reserved expression. Her human fighting stance is compact and controlled. The Female Titan is athletic and muscular, with a distinctive feminine facial structure and the ability to harden parts of its body.

03 / PERSONALITY

Personality

EXPAND PERSONALITY DETAILS

Annie is stoic, guarded, pragmatic and often blunt. She distances herself from others to protect herself emotionally, yet her attachment to her father is powerful. Her choices reveal a person who has learned to prioritize survival and mission completion in a system that trained her as a child soldier.

04 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography

EXPAND BIOGRAPHY DETAILS

Annie trains as a Warrior and is sent to Paradis with Reiner and Bertolt. She joins the cadets and later operates as the Female Titan, pursuing Eren and fighting the Survey Corps. When her identity is exposed, she encases herself in crystal and remains inaccessible for years. She awakens during the Rumbling crisis and is drawn into the alliance against Eren.

05 / ABILITIES

Abilities

EXPAND ABILITIES DETAILS

The Female Titan is a versatile shifter with strong physical performance, hardening and a scream that can attract nearby Pure Titans under specific conditions. Annie's martial arts training makes her especially effective in hand-to-hand combat. Titan inheritance also grants regeneration and transformation.

06 / COMBAT & SKILLS

Combat & Skills

EXPAND COMBAT & SKILLS DETAILS

Annie is an exceptional hand-to-hand fighter. Her training includes throws, joint control, kicks and efficient strikes, techniques that carry into her Female Titan form. She uses hardening defensively and offensively, and can adapt to opponents through timing and precision rather than relying only on brute force.

07 / RELATIONSHIPS

Relationships

EXPAND RELATIONSHIPS DETAILS

Her father is the central emotional bond motivating her desire to return home. Reiner and Bertolt are fellow Warriors and fellow infiltrators. Armin's willingness to keep thinking about her during her crystal confinement becomes significant when she returns. Her later cooperation with former enemies is shaped by the shared need to stop the Rumbling.

08 / DEVELOPMENT

Development

EXPAND DEVELOPMENT DETAILS

Annie begins as a mission-focused infiltrator who tries to keep emotional distance from the people she is ordered to deceive and kill. Her long absence does not erase her actions. Her return forces her to confront the possibility of cooperating with people she once fought and to decide what kind of future she can still choose.

09 / FINAL FATE

Final Fate

EXPAND FINAL FATE DETAILS

Annie emerges from crystal stasis during the Rumbling and joins the alliance against Eren. She survives the final battle and is reunited with her father after the Titan power disappears.

10 / ANALYSIS

Analysis

EXPAND ANALYSIS DETAILS

Annie's guardedness reflects a life shaped by coercive training, isolation and the pressure to complete a state mission. Her arc asks whether someone who has committed harm can choose a different course without pretending the past never happened. Accountability and the possibility of change coexist.

11 / CHRONOLOGY

Chronology

EXPAND CHRONOLOGY DETAILS

Warrior training; infiltration of Paradis; 104th Cadet Corps; Female Titan operations; crystal stasis; awakening during the Rumbling; alliance against Eren; final battle and reunion with her father.
