01 / CHARACTER RECORD

Identity & background

Alaindelon is a demon who becomes part of the extended household around Beel. His ability to move people between locations makes him an important logistical link in a story where travel between worlds becomes increasingly significant.

02 / CHARACTER RECORD

Personality

Flamboyant, theatrical and eccentric, Alaindelon often behaves in ways that surprise the human characters. His comic presentation contrasts with the seriousness of his demon-world knowledge and usefulness.

03 / CHARACTER RECORD

Abilities & combat

A supernatural transportation ability that allows him to carry people across distances and between dimensions. He also has demon physiology and knowledge of the worlds he travels between.

04 / CHARACTER RECORD

Limitations

His role is not primarily that of a frontline combatant. His eccentric behaviour can make him difficult for the human characters to take seriously, even when his abilities are essential.

05 / CHARACTER RECORD

Story chronology

Alaindelon is involved in Beel's arrival and the early explanation of the demon-human arrangement. He remains a recurring presence as Oga's circle becomes more involved in the Demon World.

06 / CHARACTER RECORD

Relationships

Beel — the prince whose household he serves. Hilda — fellow demon attendant. Oga and Furuichi — humans who repeatedly rely on or react to his transport ability.

07 / CHARACTER RECORD

Character analysis

Alaindelon is a functional bridge between settings as well as a comic character. His presence makes the supernatural geography of the series accessible without turning every journey into a separate plot device.

08 / CHARACTER RECORD

Anime & manga continuity

The anime includes his central transportation and comic roles. Later manga developments involving the extended cast continue beyond the anime's endpoint.

09 / CHARACTER RECORD

Archive status

Recurring supporting character in both continuities.
