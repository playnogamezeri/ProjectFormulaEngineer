History and background

Undine is the water spirit who protects the Heart Kingdom and traditionally serves its queen. Her power is linked to the kingdom's natural mana and the royal line. She is a guardian and mentor figure, but her role shifts when the curse on Loropechika and the Spade Kingdom threat disrupt their bond.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Undine is proud, protective and serious about her duty. She can be stern with those she considers inexperienced, but her loyalty to the Heart Kingdom and its queen is unwavering.

Character-reading note: This describes traits demonstrated in the narrative; it does not assign a clinical diagnosis.

03 · Magic, Abilities & Equipment

As the water spirit, Undine greatly amplifies Water Magic and can support Spirit Dive. Her power is connected to the Heart Kingdom's mana and to the person she chooses as host. The spirit's availability can change when the bond or the queen's condition changes.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

Loropechika is her queen and traditional partner. Noelle becomes an important temporary partner during the Spade Kingdom conflict. She is connected to the other elemental spirits Salamander and Sylph.

Family, friendship, rivalry, mentorship, squad membership, political allegiance and romantic interest are distinct relationship types. Shared affiliation does not automatically establish friendship.

05 · Story Chronology & Development

She supports the Clover-Heart alliance and later becomes involved in the transfer of spirit power as the battle against Megicula unfolds.

Flashbacks, reincarnation memories and later revelations are placed in the character's personal chronology and not mistaken for present-day events.

06 · Achievements, Battles & Contributions

Her spirit power and strategic choice of host help preserve the Heart Kingdom and empower Noelle during a decisive conflict.
