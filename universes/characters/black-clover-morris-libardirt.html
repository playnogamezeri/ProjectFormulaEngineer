History and background

Morris is a scientist whose experiments pursue the advancement of magic with little regard for the people harmed. He manipulates political power in the Diamond Kingdom before aligning with the Spade Kingdom's Dark Triad. He later becomes involved in the Qliphoth conflict and is transformed by Lucius.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Morris is portrayed as obsessive, calculating and ethically ruthless. His scientific curiosity is repeatedly separated from moral responsibility.
