02Biography, Personality & MotivationsEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Nine-times-reincarnated Magi guarding the Great Rift.

World & Story Context: An alternate ancient Old World inspired by One Thousand and One Nights—spanning Balbadd, Sindria, the Kou Empire, Reim Empire, Magnostadt, and the Dark Continent—sustained by Rukh and Magoi.

03Abilities, Equipment & LimitationsEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Documented Arsenal: External Rukh, Dungeon creation

System Rules: Rukh and Magoi fuel elemental Magic, Magi-tier external Rukh channeling, Metal Vessels (Djinn Equip & Extreme Magic), Household Vessels, and Borg/Isolation Barriers.

04Chronology, Decisions & Final StatusEXPAND / COLLAPSE
SPOILER STATUS: Helps stop David in the finale.

Continuity Context: Season 1 adjusts the Zagan expedition's motive (framing it around curing Black Rukh curses) and moves Al-Thamen's ambush to Sindria. Crucially, the final 171 chapters (Alma Torran, Kou Empire, Sacred Palace) remain manga-only.

05Ensemble RelationshipsEXPAND / COLLAPSE
  • Aladdin (Magi (Son of Solomon)) — Cheerful Magi uncovering Alma Torran's truth to stop David.
  • Alibaba Saluja (Balbadd / Amon King Vessel) — Outcast prince turned diplomat who stabilizes Kou.
  • Morgiana (Fanalis Tribe / Alibaba's Household) — Former slave who embraces her heritage and protects her friends.
  • Sinbad (King of Sindria / International Alliance) — Charismatic conqueror hosting David's Rukh who seizes the Sacred Palace.
  • Hakuryuu Ren (Kou 4th Prince / Emperor) — Vengeful prince who stages Kou's civil war before abdicating.
  • Judar (Kou / Black Magi) — Cynical Magi raised by Al-Thamen who defects with Hakuryuu.
  • Kouen Ren (Kou 1st Prince) — Scholar-general seeking world unification.
  • Kougyoku Ren (Kou 5th Empress) — Manipulated by Sinbad before rebuilding Kou with Alibaba.
  • Arba (Gyokuen Ren) (Al-Thamen Founder) — Betrayer of Solomon possessing Hakuei.
