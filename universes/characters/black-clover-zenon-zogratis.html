History and background

Zenon is one of the Zogratis siblings and a central commander in the Spade Kingdom takeover. His childhood relationship with Allen Fiarain and his military experiences shape his belief that power and ruthless decisions are necessary. His devil contract with Beelzebub grants Spatial Magic and, later, a devil heart that greatly increases his power.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Zenon is cold, controlled and utilitarian. He values results and believes weakness endangers everyone. His apparent lack of emotion is shaped by trauma and a rigid philosophy of strength, which is challenged by Yuno and the Golden Dawn.

Character-reading note: This describes traits demonstrated in the narrative; it does not assign a clinical diagnosis.

03 · Magic, Abilities & Equipment

Bone Magic creates and controls bone weapons, armor and structures. Beelzebub grants Spatial Magic, allowing him to dominate space and restrict opponents' movement. His devil-heart transformation increases his power and enables stronger combined techniques. His spatial control has specific conditions and can be countered by exceptional magic and teamwork.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

Dante and Vanica are his siblings. Beelzebub is his devil. Yuno and the Golden Dawn are his principal opponents. Allen's death is central to his past.

Family, friendship, rivalry, mentorship, squad membership, political allegiance and romantic interest are distinct relationship types. Shared affiliation does not automatically establish friendship.

05 · Story Chronology & Development

His conflict includes the assault on the Golden Dawn, the Spade Kingdom raid and the battle against Yuno. His personal history is revealed as the raid progresses.

Flashbacks, reincarnation memories and later revelations are placed in the character's personal chronology and not mistaken for present-day events.

06 · Achievements, Battles & Contributions

His combination of Bone and Spatial Magic makes him one of the most dangerous Dark Triad members. His defeat is a major milestone in Yuno's development.

Official character directory confirms his Dark Triad membership. Bone Magic is his own attribute; Spatial Magic is Beelzebub's.
