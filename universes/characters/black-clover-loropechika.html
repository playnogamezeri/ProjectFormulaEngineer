History and background

Loropechika is the queen of the Heart Kingdom and inherits centuries of knowledge through the royal line. Her kingdom's mana-rich environment and protective systems are tied to its queen and the water spirit Undine. She becomes a central ally to Clover during the threat posed by the Spade Kingdom.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Loropechika is kind, earnest and emotionally expressive. She is burdened by responsibility and the knowledge of the curse threatening her life, but she continues to care for her people and allies.

Character-reading note: This describes traits demonstrated in the narrative; it does not assign a clinical diagnosis.

03 · Magic, Abilities & Equipment

Her Water Magic is enhanced by the Heart Kingdom's natural mana and her bond with Undine. She uses Mana Method and runes as part of the kingdom's magical tradition. Her curse and spirit separation affect her available power and condition.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

Undine is her guardian spirit. Gadjah and the Heart Kingdom's guardians serve her. Noelle and the Clover mages become important allies.

Family, friendship, rivalry, mentorship, squad membership, political allegiance and romantic interest are distinct relationship types. Shared affiliation does not automatically establish friendship.

05 · Story Chronology & Development

She guides the Clover Kingdom's training and alliance against Spade. Her capture and curse become major stakes in the Spade Kingdom raid.

Flashbacks, reincarnation memories and later revelations are placed in the character's personal chronology and not mistaken for present-day events.

Her leadership and inherited knowledge are crucial to the Clover-Heart alliance and the defense against the Qliphoth plan.
