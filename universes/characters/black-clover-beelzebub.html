History and background

Beelzebub is one of the supreme devils whose power becomes central to the Spade Kingdom's Qliphoth plan. Zenon Zogratis makes a contract with him, gaining Spatial Magic and the ability to dominate space. Beelzebub's power is a strategic component of the Dark Triad's attempt to open the gates of the Underworld.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Beelzebub is portrayed as calculating and transactional. His relationship with Zenon is a devil contract rather than a human friendship; the devil's interests and the host's ambitions overlap because of the power each can obtain.

Character-reading note: This section describes traits demonstrated in the narrative. It does not assign a clinical diagnosis or infer motives beyond the character's depicted actions.

03 · Magic, Abilities & Equipment

Spatial Magic manipulates space, enabling spatial domination and control over areas. The precise limits and full spell inventory should be kept tied to the manga's demonstrated feats rather than assumed to be limitless. His power is accessed through a devil-host arrangement and the Qliphoth conflict.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

Zenon is his human host. He is opposed by the Clover and Heart Kingdom alliance and the Magic Knights seeking to prevent the Underworld's gates from opening.

Relationship standards

Family, friendship, rivalry, mentorship, squad membership, political allegiance and romantic interest are recorded as separate relationship types. A shared organization does not automatically mean two characters are friends or allies in every circumstance.

05 · Story Chronology & Development

His role becomes critical during the Spade Kingdom raid, particularly through Zenon's devil power and the battle over the Qliphoth. The consequences of the contract extend into the later conflict.

Chronology is presented in narrative order at a high level. Flashbacks, reincarnation memories and later revelations are identified by their place in the character's history rather than being mistaken for events that occurred during the present-day plot.
