02Biography, Personality & MotivationsEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Carefree martial arts prodigy revealed as the Monkey King.

World & Story Context: Divided into the Human Realm, Sage/Demon Realm, and Heavenly Realm, the world is governed by an ancient divine Taboo and contracts called Borrowed Power (Charyeok), pitted against pure Genuine Martial Arts.

03Abilities, Equipment & LimitationsEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Documented Arsenal: Renewal Taekwondo, Nabong Needle Ryu, Yeoui, Geundoowun

System Rules: Combat pits pure Genuine Martial Arts (like Renewal Taekwondo) against Borrowed Power (Charyeok) contracts forged with gods, demons, and historical heroes.

04Chronology, Decisions & Final StatusEXPAND / COLLAPSE
SPOILER STATUS: Achieves Nirvana as Maitreya, defeats Mujin, and maintains cosmic harmony (Webtoon).

Continuity Context: MAPPA's 13-episode anime heavily compresses 112 Webtoon chapters—especially Part 2 (71 chapters in 8 episodes)—trimming preliminary fights, character backstories, and Charyeok exposition while streamlining Jin Taejin's rescue arc. Parts 3–6 (Oraeguk, World Competition, Ragnarok, and the 17-year timeskip RE: A Match with God) remain unadapted.

05Ensemble RelationshipsEXPAND / COLLAPSE
  • Han Daewi (Team Seoul / The Six) — Stoic fighter who inherits the Jade Emperor's Wisdom to protect humanity.
  • Yoo Mira (Team Seoul / The Six / Neo Nox) — 25th heir of Moonlight Sword Style seeking to revive her family's school.
  • Park Ilpyo (South Jeolla Team / The Six) — Tactical rival who becomes Mori's trusted ally and World Government leader.
  • Jin Taejin (RE Taekwondo Force) — Mori's adoptive grandfather who rejects Charyeok entirely.
  • Park Mujin (Park Mubong) (GOH Committee / The Six / World President) — Ambitious tournament founder who betrays Mori to usurp divine power.
  • Jegal Taek (South Gyeongsang Team) — Ruthless Part 1–2 antagonist who consumes the Key to ascend.
  • Commissioner Q (GOH Judges / The Six) — Eccentric judge who trains Daewi in Charyeok.
  • Okhwang (Jade Emperor) (Heavenly Realm / NOX Founder) — Ancient ruler of Heaven seeking the Key.
  • Lee Sujin (NOX -> Balhae / The Six) — Former NOX Bishop who leads the Balhae resistance.
