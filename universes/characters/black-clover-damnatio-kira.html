History and background

Damnatio serves as a judge within the Clover Kingdom's political and judicial system. He investigates and prosecutes cases involving threats to the kingdom, including the aftermath of the elf reincarnation crisis. His position gives him authority to determine guilt and impose sentences, making him a representative of institutional power rather than a frontline squad captain.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Damnatio is severe, formal and convinced that order must be preserved. He tends to prioritize the kingdom's legal structure over personal sympathy, although his later story places his loyalty and judgment under extreme pressure.

Character-reading note: This section describes traits demonstrated in the narrative. It does not assign a clinical diagnosis or infer motives beyond the character's depicted actions.

03 · Magic, Abilities & Equipment

Scale Magic creates scales that can weigh and suppress magical power. Its use is especially suited to judgment and restraint, reflecting his institutional role. It should not be described as an automatic measure of moral truth; the spell's effect is magical suppression, not an infallible ethical verdict.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

He is associated with the Kira royal family and the Magic Parliament. Asta and the Black Bulls become central to his judicial actions after the elf crisis.

Relationship standards

Family, friendship, rivalry, mentorship, squad membership, political allegiance and romantic interest are recorded as separate relationship types. A shared organization does not automatically mean two characters are friends or allies in every circumstance.

05 · Story Chronology & Development

He presides over the prosecution of Asta and Secre following the elf conflict. Later developments place him within the larger Lucius crisis and reveal the danger of the political order he serves.

Chronology is presented in narrative order at a high level. Flashbacks, reincarnation memories and later revelations are identified by their place in the character's history rather than being mistaken for events that occurred during the present-day plot.
