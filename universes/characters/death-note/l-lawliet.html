02Biography, Personality & MotivationsEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Eccentric, sweet-loving prodigy who deduces Light is Kira.

World & Story Context: Grounded in early-2000s Tokyo/Kanto and international intelligence agencies (NPA, FBI, Interpol), shadowed by the barren Shinigami Realm and the strict supernatural laws of the Death Note.

03Abilities, Equipment & LimitationsEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Documented Arsenal: World's greatest detective

System Rules: Intellectual warfare governed by strict supernatural rules: knowing a target's true face and name kills via heart attack in 40 seconds or scripted actions up to 23 days.

04Chronology, Decisions & Final StatusEXPAND / COLLAPSE
SPOILER STATUS: Killed by Rem to save Misa.

Continuity Context: Anime Ep. 26–37 compress 49 manga chapters (Ch. 60–108), cutting US President David Hoope's subplot and much of Mello's deduction. Crucially, Manga Ch. 107–108 show Light begging Ryuk and dying inside the warehouse followed by a 1-year epilogue introducing Matsuda's Theory, whereas Anime Ep. 37 has Mikami stab himself and Light die quietly on a staircase.

05Ensemble RelationshipsEXPAND / COLLAPSE
  • Light Yagami (Kira / 2nd L) — Brilliant student corrupted into a self-styled god of a 'new world.'
  • Ryuk (Shinigami Realm) — Bored Death God who drops the Note for amusement and stays neutral.
  • Misa Amane (Second Kira) — Idol blindly devoted to Light after he avenged her parents.
  • Near (Nate River) (SPK / 3rd L) — L's calm younger successor who exposes Light's warehouse trap.
  • Mello (Mihael Keehl) (Wammy's House / Mafia) — L's volatile successor whose kidnapping of Takada breaks Light's plan.
  • Teru Mikami (X-Kira (4th Kira)) — Fanatical prosecutor whose deviation to kill Takada seals Light's defeat.
  • Soichiro Yagami (NPA / Task Force Chief) — Principled chief who dies believing Light is innocent.
  • Touta Matsuda (Task Force) — Earnest detective who idolized Light until the warehouse betrayal.
  • Rem (Shinigami Realm) — Shinigami devoted to Misa who sacrifices her life to kill L.
