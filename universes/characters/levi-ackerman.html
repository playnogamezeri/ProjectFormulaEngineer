Levi Ackerman

Humanity's strongest soldier, known for exceptional precision and discipline. He survives the final battle, fulfills his promise to help end the Beast Titan's role in the conflict and sees the fallen Scouts in his final salute.

01 / IDENTITY

Identity

EXPAND IDENTITY DETAILS

Levi Ackerman is a captain in the Survey Corps and one of the story's most formidable human soldiers. He is born in the Underground to Kuchel Ackerman. After his mother's death, his uncle Kenny Ackerman raises him for a time and teaches him survival. Erwin Smith later recruits him into the Survey Corps. Japanese voice: Hiroshi Kamiya; English voice: Matthew Mercer. Birthday: December 25.

02 / APPEARANCE

Appearance

EXPAND APPEARANCE DETAILS

Levi is short, compact and muscular, with straight black hair usually styled in an undercut and narrow, intense eyes. His movements are economical and controlled. He wears the Survey Corps uniform and often a white cravat. His insistence on cleanliness is a recurring trait, even in the violence and disorder of military life.

03 / PERSONALITY

Personality

EXPAND PERSONALITY DETAILS

Blunt, severe, disciplined and impatient with careless behavior, Levi is not indifferent to his subordinates. He remembers fallen soldiers and treats their sacrifices seriously. His pragmatism comes from understanding that every choice can cost lives, not from an absence of emotion.

04 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography

EXPAND BIOGRAPHY DETAILS

Levi grows up in the Underground, where poverty and violence shape his early life. Kenny teaches him survival after Kuchel's death. Levi later forms a group with Isabel Magnolia and Furlan Church, but an encounter with Erwin's Survey Corps changes his path. He joins the regiment and becomes a leading fighter, serving through the Female Titan, Uprising, Shiganshina and Marley conflicts. His pursuit of Zeke becomes a personal promise after repeated losses.

05 / ABILITIES

Abilities

EXPAND ABILITIES DETAILS

Levi is an Ackerman, a lineage associated with exceptional physical capabilities. He is not a Titan shifter. His performance combines conditioning, reflexes, coordination, spatial awareness, weapon mastery and experience. His limits remain human: severe injuries, exhaustion and impaired mobility affect him, especially after the Thunder Spear explosion involving Zeke.

06 / COMBAT & SKILLS

Combat & Skills

EXPAND COMBAT & SKILLS DETAILS

Levi is an elite user of vertical maneuvering equipment and blades. He changes direction rapidly, exploits blind spots and attacks Titan weak points with precision. He is also capable in close combat against humans. His battles demonstrate technique and tactical timing rather than unlimited strength. Later injuries significantly constrain his abilities.

07 / RELATIONSHIPS

Relationships

EXPAND RELATIONSHIPS DETAILS

Erwin Smith is Levi's commander and the person whose final decision he must make at Shiganshina. His bond with subordinates is shaped by the responsibility of leading them into danger. Hange becomes a trusted fellow officer and commander. Zeke is the target of Levi's vow. Kenny is his uncle and a formative, complicated influence. Levi and Mikasa share Ackerman lineage but not a close upbringing.

08 / DEVELOPMENT

Development

EXPAND DEVELOPMENT DETAILS

Levi's early life teaches him to survive by relying on himself. Service changes the meaning of strength: he must trust others, lead them and carry the consequences when they die. Repeated losses do not make him abandon the mission, but sharpen his understanding that the dead cannot be replaced by slogans. His final salute to fallen Scouts is remembrance rather than triumph.

09 / FINAL FATE

Final Fate

EXPAND FINAL FATE DETAILS

Levi survives the final battle despite severe injuries and lasting physical impairment. He fulfills his promise by helping create the opportunity for Zeke's death, contributing to the halt of the Rumbling. In the aftermath he is shown among survivors, honoring comrades who did not return.

10 / ANALYSIS

Analysis

EXPAND ANALYSIS DETAILS

Levi's character is built around the burden of survival. He is celebrated as humanity's strongest soldier, yet skill cannot prevent every death or undo every loss. His discipline is a way to continue serving others while carrying grief. His respect for the fallen gives the military struggle a human measure beyond victory and defeat.

11 / CHRONOLOGY

Chronology

EXPAND CHRONOLOGY DETAILS

Childhood in the Underground; raised by Kenny; association with Isabel and Furlan; encounter with Erwin's Survey Corps; recruitment; Special Operations Squad; Female Titan and Uprising operations; return to Shiganshina; pursuit of Zeke; severe injury; alliance against Eren; final battle and aftermath.
