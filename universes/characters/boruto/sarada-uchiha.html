Sarada Uchiha

Aliases: Sarada; daughter of Sasuke and Sakura

ARTWORK SLOT — GENERATED ARTWORK DEFERRED UNTIL THE FINAL ART PHASE

Sarada is Boruto's teammate and the daughter of Sasuke Uchiha and Sakura Haruno. She seeks to become Hokage, making her ambition a deliberate continuation of Naruto's ideal rather than a simple desire for status.

Biography & development

Sarada's early search for her family history and her relationship with Sasuke shape her understanding of belonging. Her commitment to the village and to her friends develops alongside her Sharingan and her leadership within Team 7.

Personality & motivations

Disciplined, intelligent, direct and compassionate. She balances high expectations with a strong sense of fairness and responsibility.

Abilities & limitations

Uses the Sharingan, fire-style ninjutsu, precise chakra control and the superhuman strength associated with Sakura's training. Her Mangekyō Sharingan is a major later manga development; its exact costs and limitations should be documented with the relevant continuity and chapter context.

Affiliations & relationships

Konohagakure; Team 7; daughter of Sasuke and Sakura; close friend and teammate of Boruto and Mitsuki.

Character significance

Sarada represents leadership grounded in service, and challenges the idea that inherited clan history must dictate an individual's future.
