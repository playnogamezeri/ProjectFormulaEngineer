Naruto Uzumaki

Aliases: Seventh Hokage; former jinchūriki of Kurama

ARTWORK SLOT — GENERATED ARTWORK DEFERRED UNTIL THE FINAL ART PHASE

Naruto is the Seventh Hokage and Boruto's father. He represents the previous generation's achievement of peace, but his office creates tensions between public duty and private family life.

Biography & role

After the Fourth Shinobi World War, Naruto becomes Hokage and helps maintain cooperation among the Five Great Shinobi Countries. In Boruto, his leadership is tested by threats that emerge beyond conventional village security.

Personality & values

Empathetic, persistent, direct and committed to reconciliation. His determination to protect the village sometimes leaves him with too little time for his family.

Abilities & status

His legacy includes mastery of shadow clones, Rasengan, Sage Mode and Six Paths-era abilities. Kurama's role and Naruto's later power/status changes are major spoilers and must be tied to the correct point in the chronology.

Relationships

Husband of Hinata; father of Boruto and Himawari; mentor and counterpart to the Five Kage; a major parental figure to Kawaki.

Significance

Naruto embodies the achievements and costs of peace-building, while the sequel asks whether institutions built for war can adapt to a peaceful age.
