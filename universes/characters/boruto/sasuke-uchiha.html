Sasuke Uchiha

Aliases: Shadow Hokage; last surviving Uchiha of his generation

ARTWORK SLOT — GENERATED ARTWORK DEFERRED UNTIL THE FINAL ART PHASE

Sasuke is a wandering shinobi who works outside the ordinary village structure to investigate threats and protect the world. He becomes Boruto's mentor and a crucial link between generations.

Biography & role

After the Fourth Shinobi World War, Sasuke pursues atonement and investigates the Ōtsutsuki. He trains Boruto and supports Konoha from beyond its formal chain of command.

Personality & motivations

Reserved, pragmatic and intensely focused. His past gives him a particular sensitivity to the consequences of hatred, isolation and inherited conflict.

Abilities & limitations

Sharingan and Rinnegan-era abilities, space-time techniques, lightning and fire release, swordsmanship and high-level tactical skill. Later losses and limitations must be placed accurately in the timeline.

Relationships

Husband of Sakura; father of Sarada; mentor to Boruto; former rival and lifelong counterpart to Naruto.

Significance

Sasuke serves as a bridge between the old conflicts and the new generation's responsibilities.
