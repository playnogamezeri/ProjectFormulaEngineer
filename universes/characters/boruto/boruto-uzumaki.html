Boruto Uzumaki

Aliases: Boruto; son of the Seventh Hokage

ARTWORK SLOT — GENERATED ARTWORK DEFERRED UNTIL THE FINAL ART PHASE

Boruto is the central protagonist of the sequel, the son of Naruto and Hinata. Raised in a peaceful, modernizing Konoha, he initially resents the demands of Naruto's office and the distance it creates within the family. His journey tests his assumptions about strength, responsibility, loyalty and what it means to inherit a legacy.

Biography & development

Boruto enters the Academy already skilled and confident, but his early identity is shaped by frustration with his father's public duties. The Chūnin Exams expose the ethical danger of relying on scientific ninja tools to manufacture victory. His later missions with Team 7 deepen his bonds with Sarada and Mitsuki, while encounters with Momoshiki and the Karma mark force him to confront Ōtsutsuki power and the possibility of losing control of his own body.

Personality & motivations

Quick-witted, mischievous, observant and protective. He wants recognition as himself rather than as Naruto's son. Over time, his priorities shift toward protecting others and accepting consequences.

Abilities & limitations

Uses shadow clones, Rasengan variants, nature transformation and advanced close combat. His abilities include the inherited Hyūga connection and the Karma/Ōtsutsuki complications. The Jougan is principally an anime continuity element and should not be treated as fully explained manga canon.

Affiliations & relationships

Konohagakure; Team 7; family ties to Naruto, Hinata and Himawari; close bonds and rivalries with Sarada, Mitsuki and Kawaki; mentorship from Sasuke.

Character significance

Boruto embodies generational change: he inherits a world made safer by the previous generation, but must decide what peace is worth defending when its foundations are challenged.
