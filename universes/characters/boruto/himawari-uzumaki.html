Himawari Uzumaki

Aliases: Himawari; daughter of Naruto and Hinata

ARTWORK SLOT — GENERATED ARTWORK DEFERRED UNTIL THE FINAL ART PHASE

Himawari is Boruto's younger sister. Her gentle disposition coexists with the Hyūga family's inherited potential and a growing role in the later story.

Biography & development

Himawari grows up in Naruto's household and is affected by the emotional and political upheavals surrounding her family. Later manga developments give her a much more consequential connection to the world's power system.

Personality & abilities

Kind, perceptive and emotionally grounded. She has demonstrated the Byakugan and Hyūga taijutsu foundations; later power revelations must be distinguished from the anime's earlier portrayal.

Affiliations & relationships

Konohagakure; daughter of Naruto and Hinata; sister of Boruto; close family connection to Kawaki.

Significance

Himawari's role challenges assumptions about who inherits power and how gentleness can coexist with formidable capability.
