Konohamaru Sarutobi

Aliases: Team 7's original jōnin instructor

ARTWORK SLOT — GENERATED ARTWORK DEFERRED UNTIL THE FINAL ART PHASE

Konohamaru is a Konoha jōnin from the Sarutobi clan and the original leader of the new Team 7. He represents continuity between the previous generation and the young shinobi.

Biography & role

Having once admired Naruto as a child, Konohamaru becomes a capable shinobi and instructor. He leads Boruto, Sarada and Mitsuki through early missions and supports their growth.

Personality & abilities

Earnest, competitive and responsible. His toolkit includes Rasengan, fire and wind techniques, shadow clones and the tactical skills of a jōnin.

Affiliations & relationships

Konohagakure; Sarutobi clan; Team 7 instructor; grandson of the Third Hokage and former student of Naruto.

Significance

Konohamaru illustrates how admiration can mature into mentorship and institutional responsibility.
