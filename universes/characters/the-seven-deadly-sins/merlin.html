02Biography, Personality & MotivationsEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Britannia's greatest mage who secretly orchestrates Chaos's awakening.

World & Story Context: A medieval fantasy Britannia shared by Humans, Fairies, Giants, Goddesses, and Demons, shaped by the Supreme Deity, Demon King, and primordial Chaos.

03Abilities, Equipment & LimitationsEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Documented Arsenal: Morning Star Aldan, Infinity

System Rules: Power is quantified via Balor's Magical Eye (Magic + Strength + Spirit) and shaped by Sacred Treasures, Demon King Commandments, and Supreme Deity Graces.

04Chronology, Decisions & Final StatusEXPAND / COLLAPSE
SPOILER STATUS: Departs with Arthur Pendragon after Chaos awakens.

Continuity Context: Season 1 alters Gowther's introduction (omitting Pelliot's arc), cuts Oslo's Capital of the Dead debut, and spares Howzer and Dawn Roar from Dreyfus's attacks—changes Season 2 largely ignores to realign with manga canon. Signs of Holy War (4 eps) is anime-original written by Nakaba Suzuki, and Prisoners of the Sky (2018) is non-canon, while Cursed by Light (2021) is canon.

05Ensemble RelationshipsEXPAND / COLLAPSE
  • Meliodas (Seven Deadly Sins (Dragon's Sin of Wrath)) — Captain of the Sins and former Commandment leader cursed with eternal life.
  • Elizabeth Liones (Liones / Goddess Clan) — Third Princess of Liones and reincarnation of Goddess Elizabeth.
  • Diane (Seven Deadly Sins (Serpent's Sin of Envy)) — Giant Clan warrior who overcomes memory erasures.
  • Ban (Seven Deadly Sins (Fox's Sin of Greed)) — Former immortal bandit who sacrifices his immortality in Purgatory.
  • King (Harlequin) (Seven Deadly Sins (Grizzly's Sin of Sloth)) — Third Fairy King who matures into the strongest Fairy ruler.
  • Gowther (Seven Deadly Sins (Goat's Sin of Lust)) — Doll created by Demon Gowther who reclaims his sealed heart.
  • Escanor (Seven Deadly Sins (Lion's Sin of Pride)) — Human chosen by Sunshine who reaches invincible peak power at high noon.
  • Zeldris (Ten Commandments (Piety)) — Meliodas's younger brother fighting to free his vampire lover Gelda.
  • Mael (Estarossa) (Four Archangels) — Strongest Archangel brainwashed into believing he was Meliodas's brother Estarossa.
