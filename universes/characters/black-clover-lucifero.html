History and background

Lucifero is one of the highest-ranking devils and the supreme devil associated with gravity. He seeks to manifest in the human world through the Qliphoth Advent Ritual and uses Dante Zogratis as a host. His incomplete manifestation becomes one of the central threats of the Spade Kingdom raid.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Lucifero is arrogant, domineering and contemptuous of humans. He treats other beings as subordinate to his power.
