Complete Biography

PROFILE DETAILS ＋
Before Night Raid's campaign

Chelsea joins Night Raid after serving in another revolutionary unit that was devastated by the Jaegers. She understands how quickly a team can be destroyed by overconfidence and urges her new comrades to take survival seriously.

Conflict with the revolution

Gaea Foundation allows her to transform her appearance into other people or creatures. She uses disguise to approach targets and strike vital points with concealed needles. Her method depends on patience, deception and precise execution.

Final campaign

Chelsea is killed by Kurome's corpse puppets after her assassination attempt fails in both continuities. The display of her remains is intended to break Night Raid's morale.
