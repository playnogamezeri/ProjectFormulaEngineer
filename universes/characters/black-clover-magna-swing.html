History and background

Magna is a peasant from the Clover Kingdom who joins the Black Bulls. He initially struggles to keep pace with mages who possess much larger mana reserves. Rather than accept that limitation, he develops a spell that redistributes mana between two combatants, allowing skill and preparation to challenge a stronger opponent.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Magna is loud, rough-edged, loyal and proud. He values effort and refuses to be dismissed because of his low mana. His rivalry with Luck is affectionate and helps both characters grow.

Character-reading note: This describes traits demonstrated in the narrative; it does not assign a clinical diagnosis.

03 · Magic, Abilities & Equipment

Fire Magic gives him offensive spells and a rough, direct combat style. His signature Soul Chain Death Match divides the mana of two participants and prevents an easy escape, allowing him to fight opponents whose raw reserves greatly exceed his own. The spell depends on conditions and tactical commitment.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

Luck is his closest friend and rival. Yami and the Black Bulls give him a squad and family. His peasant background informs his perspective on class inequality.

Family, friendship, rivalry, mentorship, squad membership, political allegiance and romantic interest are distinct relationship types. Shared affiliation does not automatically establish friendship.

05 · Story Chronology & Development

He participates in squad missions and training before developing his decisive mana-redistribution technique during the Spade Kingdom conflict. His fight against Dante's subordinate becomes a landmark in his growth.

Flashbacks, reincarnation memories and later revelations are placed in the character's personal chronology and not mistaken for present-day events.

06 · Achievements, Battles & Contributions

His defeat of an overwhelmingly stronger opponent through a carefully designed spell is one of the series' clearest examples of strategy overcoming a raw power gap.
