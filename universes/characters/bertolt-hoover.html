Bertolt Hoover

The quiet Warrior who inherits the Colossal Titan and participates in the fall of Wall Maria. Armin inherits his Titan after the Scouts capture him during the return to Shiganshina.

01 / IDENTITY

Identity

EXPAND IDENTITY DETAILS

Bertolt Hoover is one of Marley’s Warrior candidates and the inheritor of the Colossal Titan. Within the 104th Cadet Corps, he appears quiet, capable and reluctant to draw attention to himself. His reserved manner conceals the enormous responsibility placed on him: he was sent to Paradis as a child and became one of the people responsible for breaching its defenses. His story is inseparable from Reiner Braun and Annie Leonhart, whose shared mission binds them together even as their lives among the island’s people complicate their loyalty.

02 / APPEARANCE

Appearance

EXPAND APPEARANCE DETAILS

Bertolt is exceptionally tall and lean, with a long face, dark hair and a composed expression. His height makes him visually distinctive even before the Colossal Titan is revealed. His posture often appears hesitant or withdrawn, and his expressions communicate discomfort more readily than confidence. In Titan form, his identity is transformed into an immense, skinless body whose scale and heat make him a weapon of mass destruction.

03 / PERSONALITY

Personality

EXPAND PERSONALITY DETAILS

Bertolt is quiet, observant and cautious. He rarely asserts himself, often allowing Reiner or others to take the lead. His reserve should not be mistaken for a lack of conviction: he can act decisively when he believes the mission requires it. He experiences conflict between personal attachment and military duty, but repeatedly chooses the mission even when that choice causes suffering. His tragedy lies partly in his awareness that the people he harms are individuals rather than faceless enemies.

04 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography

EXPAND BIOGRAPHY DETAILS

Raised in Marley, Bertolt was selected for the Warrior program and inherited the Colossal Titan. He crossed the sea with Reiner and Annie as part of the operation against the Walls. On the day Wall Maria fell, he transformed at Shiganshina and destroyed the outer gate, beginning the catastrophe that shaped the series. He later lived among the people of the Walls under a false identity, trained with the 104th and formed genuine bonds. When the Warriors’ identities were exposed, he returned to open conflict and ultimately became central to the battle to retake Shiganshina.

05 / ABILITIES

Abilities

EXPAND ABILITIES DETAILS

The Colossal Titan gives Bertolt extraordinary size, destructive transformation force and the ability to release immense heat and steam. Its transformation can create a catastrophic blast, making the moment of transformation itself a strategic weapon. The Titan’s size grants reach and intimidation but also makes it slow and difficult to conceal. Bertolt’s control of steam can repel attackers, though sustained use consumes the Titan’s body and limits movement.

06 / COMBAT & SKILLS

Combat & Skills

EXPAND COMBAT & SKILLS DETAILS

Bertolt’s greatest combat advantage is scale rather than agility. At Shiganshina, the Colossal Titan’s explosive transformation and steam output reshape the battlefield and deny opponents a safe approach. He can use heat as a defensive barrier and his enormous body to destroy fortifications. His limitations are equally important: the Titan is slow, its movements are predictable, and skilled opponents can exploit the time and energy required to act. His final confrontation demonstrates that strategic planning can overcome overwhelming physical power.

07 / RELATIONSHIPS

Relationships

EXPAND RELATIONSHIPS DETAILS

Reiner is Bertolt’s closest companion and the person who most often speaks or acts for their group. Their partnership is built on shared training, fear and the burden of their mission. Annie is their fellow Warrior, and their connection is shaped by the same assignment and the risks of discovery. Bertolt develops bonds with the 104th cadets, particularly through ordinary shared life, making his betrayal painful for both sides. Armin becomes his direct opponent and exposes the human cost of Bertolt’s choices.

08 / DEVELOPMENT

Development

EXPAND DEVELOPMENT DETAILS

Bertolt begins as a seemingly passive cadet whose true allegiance is hidden. As the story reveals his identity, his quietness takes on a different meaning: he has spent years suppressing doubt while preparing to carry out violence. In the Return to Shiganshina conflict, he becomes more forceful and openly committed to completing the mission. His development is not a simple redemption arc; it is the unveiling of a person who recognizes the horror of his actions yet cannot bring himself to abandon the cause he was raised to serve.

09 / FINAL FATE

Final Fate

EXPAND FINAL FATE DETAILS

During the battle of Shiganshina, Armin devises a plan that forces Bertolt to expend the Colossal Titan’s steam while Eren approaches from the opposite direction. Bertolt is defeated and captured. Armin is given the Titan serum and inherits the Colossal Titan by consuming Bertolt. Bertolt’s death ends his role in the conflict, while his memories and the consequences of his actions continue through Armin’s inheritance.

10 / ANALYSIS

Analysis

EXPAND ANALYSIS DETAILS

Bertolt represents the moral cost of turning children into instruments of state policy. He is both responsible for terrible violence and shaped by a system that trained him to see that violence as necessary. The narrative does not erase the victims by explaining his fear, nor does it make his choices uncomplicated. His quiet presence also challenges the assumption that the most dangerous person must appear aggressive: enormous destructive capacity can be carried by someone who seems almost invisible.

11 / CHRONOLOGY

Chronology

EXPAND CHRONOLOGY DETAILS

Bertolt is raised in Marley and selected as a Warrior candidate. He inherits the Colossal Titan, travels to Paradis with Reiner and Annie, and participates in the fall of Wall Maria. He joins the 104th Cadet Corps under a false identity, remains embedded in the military community, and is exposed as a Warrior. He fights at Shiganshina, is defeated by the combined strategy of Armin and Eren, and is consumed by Armin after the serum decision.

13 / PURPOSES & REASONING

Purposes & Reasoning

EXPAND PURPOSES & REASONING

Bertolt’s purpose in the story is to reveal how an ordinary-seeming person can become an agent of enormous destruction through indoctrination, duty and fear. His quietness contrasts with the scale of the Colossal Titan, creating a deliberate tension between outward restraint and devastating capacity. His reasoning is shaped by the Warrior mission and the belief that completing it is necessary, even as life among the people of the Walls makes that certainty harder to sustain. Bertolt is not presented as innocent simply because he is conflicted; the deaths caused by his actions remain real. Nor is he a one-dimensional monster. His role asks the audience to consider how systems recruit children into violence and how personal bonds complicate obedience. His final confrontation with Armin also turns the conflict into a contest of strategy, sacrifice and competing visions of survival.
