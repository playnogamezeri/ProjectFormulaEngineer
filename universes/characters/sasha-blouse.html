01 / IDENTITY

Identity

EXPAND IDENTITY DETAILS

Sasha Blouse is a member of the 104th Cadet Corps who becomes a Survey Corps soldier. She comes from a hunting community in the forested region of Dauper and is known for her appetite, quick instincts and skill with a bow. Her humor makes her memorable, but she is also a brave and observant soldier whose compassion becomes central to her place in the story.

02 / APPEARANCE

Appearance

EXPAND APPEARANCE DETAILS

Sasha has long brown hair, expressive eyes and an athletic build shaped by life in a rural hunting community. Her early appearance is often associated with food and her informal manner, while her Survey Corps uniform marks her transition into military life. She carries herself with the alertness of someone accustomed to reading the forest and tracking movement. Her expressions move easily between comic delight and serious concern.

03 / PERSONALITY

Personality

EXPAND PERSONALITY DETAILS

Sasha is cheerful, impulsive and strongly motivated by food, but those traits are only part of her character. She is perceptive, resourceful and courageous under pressure. Her upbringing gives her a practical understanding of hunting and survival, and she can act decisively when someone is in danger. She is also compassionate toward vulnerable people, even when helping them requires her to face a threat alone.

04 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography

EXPAND BIOGRAPHY DETAILS

Sasha joins the 104th Cadet Corps and initially stands out for her unconventional behavior, including eating during a formal address. She graduates and joins the Survey Corps, taking part in operations against the Titans. During the attack on a rural village, she returns to her home region and rescues a young girl, Kaya, from a Titan. Her family’s values and her father’s wish to move beyond old divisions become important to her later characterization. During the Liberio raid, Sasha is shot by Gabi Braun and dies after returning to the airship.

05 / ABILITIES

Abilities

EXPAND ABILITIES DETAILS

Sasha is an accomplished archer and hunter, with strong tracking instincts and practical fieldcraft. She is skilled with the standard equipment used by the Survey Corps and can respond quickly to danger. Her abilities are grounded in observation, aim and knowledge of terrain rather than unusual strength. She is particularly effective in situations where careful movement and an accurate shot matter.

06 / COMBAT & SKILLS

Combat & Skills

EXPAND COMBAT & SKILLS DETAILS

Sasha’s combat style combines ranged precision with quick reactions. Her hunting background allows her to use a bow and rifle effectively, and she can assess movement in forested or rural environments. She is brave enough to act independently when a civilian is threatened, as shown by her rescue of Kaya. Her skill does not make her invulnerable; her death in the Liberio aftermath underscores how quickly a soldier can be lost even after surviving major battles.

07 / RELATIONSHIPS

Relationships

EXPAND RELATIONSHIPS DETAILS

Connie is one of Sasha’s closest friends, and their shared humor creates a sense of normal life amid military trauma. The 104th cadets and Survey Corps become her chosen community. Her family, particularly her father, represents the rural values she carries with her. Kaya’s rescue creates a bond that extends Sasha’s influence beyond her own lifetime. Gabi becomes connected to Sasha through the fatal encounter, and the Blouse family’s response to Gabi shapes the story’s treatment of revenge.

08 / DEVELOPMENT

Development

EXPAND DEVELOPMENT DETAILS

Sasha’s development moves from comic relief toward a fuller portrait of courage and compassion. Her rescue of Kaya reveals the seriousness beneath her playful habits. Her father’s reflections on making room for children to leave the forest deepen the theme of breaking inherited cycles. Her death does not function only as a shock; it becomes a moral turning point for Gabi, the Blouse family and the Survey Corps survivors.

09 / FINAL FATE

Final Fate

EXPAND FINAL FATE DETAILS

Sasha is shot by Gabi during the return from the Liberio operation and dies aboard the airship. Her final words refer to meat, recalling the appetite that often provided comic relief. The simplicity of the moment contrasts with the enormous political conflict around her. Her memory continues to affect Connie, the Blouse family, Gabi and the other survivors, especially as they decide whether grief will become revenge.

10 / ANALYSIS

Analysis

EXPAND ANALYSIS DETAILS

Sasha represents the value of ordinary life inside a story dominated by war and ideology. Her love of food, humor and family is not trivial; it reminds the audience what the soldiers are trying to protect. Her rescue of Kaya shows compassion crossing social boundaries, while her death demonstrates how political violence reaches individuals who are not symbols or military leaders. The response of her family challenges the idea that grief must be answered with further harm.

11 / CHRONOLOGY

Chronology

EXPAND CHRONOLOGY DETAILS

Sasha grows up in Dauper, joins the 104th Cadet Corps and becomes a Survey Corps soldier. She survives the fall of Wall Maria’s aftermath and several major operations. She returns to her home region during the uprising and saves Kaya. She participates in the attack on Liberio, is shot by Gabi during the retreat and dies aboard the airship. Her memory shapes the choices of the survivors.

13 / PURPOSES & REASONING

Purposes & Reasoning

EXPAND PURPOSES & REASONING

Sasha’s purpose is to preserve the value of ordinary life within a narrative dominated by war, political systems and Titan powers. Her appetite, humor, hunting skills and love for her community make her recognizably human. Her rescue of Kaya reveals that her instincts extend beyond her own group, while her death during the Liberio operation demonstrates how political violence reaches individual people and families. The reasoning around Sasha’s legacy is carried forward by the Blouse family, whose response to Gabi challenges the expectation that grief must become revenge. Sasha is not simply comic relief or a plot device whose only purpose is to die. She represents the everyday joys and relationships that conflict destroys, and the possibility of choosing compassion even after devastating loss.
