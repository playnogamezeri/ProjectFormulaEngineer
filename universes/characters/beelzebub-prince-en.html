Character overview

Another son of the Great Demon Lord, Prince En brings royal rivalry and the ambitions of the Demon World into Oga's life.

01 / CHARACTER RECORD

Identity & background

En is a young demon prince and Beel's older brother. His presence expands the story's focus from Beel's immediate guardianship to the larger royal family and the competing expectations placed on the Demon Lord's children.

02 / CHARACTER RECORD

Personality

Confident and accustomed to royal privilege, En can be demanding and competitive. His behaviour reflects both his status and his position within a family whose children are tied to the Demon Lord's plans.

03 / CHARACTER RECORD

Abilities & combat

A demon prince with supernatural power and access to royal attendants and resources. His exact capabilities and their limits should be read in the context of the specific manga and anime events rather than inferred solely from his title.

04 / CHARACTER RECORD

Limitations

En is still a young character whose decisions are shaped by family expectations and the people around him. His status does not make him independent of the royal household.

05 / CHARACTER RECORD

Story chronology

En becomes involved in the human-world storyline and the conflict surrounding Beel. His arrival introduces a new dynamic for Oga's household and leads to further developments involving the Demon Lord's family.

06 / CHARACTER RECORD

Relationships

Beel — younger brother and central royal counterpart. The Great Demon Lord — father. His attendants and household — the people who accompany him and carry out his wishes.

07 / CHARACTER RECORD

Character analysis

En's role highlights that Beel's mission is part of a family and political arrangement, not an isolated event. His rivalry with Beel adds a sibling dimension to the series' supernatural premise.

08 / CHARACTER RECORD

Anime & manga continuity

The anime adapts the Prince En storyline within its 60 episodes, while the manga continues the wider family conflict and later events.

09 / CHARACTER RECORD

Archive status

Major arc character in both anime and manga.
