Porco Galliard

01 / IDENTITY

Identity

EXPAND IDENTITY DETAILS

Porco Galliard is a Marleyan Warrior and inheritor of the Jaw Titan. He is Marcel Galliard’s younger brother and a rival of Reiner Braun, whom he blames for Marcel’s death and for the course of his own life. Porco’s pride, aggression and combat ability make him a formidable soldier, but his story is also about the emotional cost of being judged against a sibling and being used by a military system.

02 / APPEARANCE

Appearance

EXPAND APPEARANCE DETAILS

Porco is a young man with short blond hair, sharp features and an intense expression. His posture often communicates impatience or defiance, especially when he confronts Reiner. As the Jaw Titan, he becomes a compact, fast-moving Titan with a powerful jaw and claws. The Titan’s armored mouth and agility distinguish it from the larger, slower shifters.

03 / PERSONALITY

Personality

EXPAND PERSONALITY DETAILS

Porco is proud, combative and quick to anger. He is highly conscious of status and often expresses contempt for Reiner, whom he sees as unworthy of the Armored Titan. His confidence can become recklessness, but beneath it is unresolved grief and the belief that he should have inherited a different fate. He is not without loyalty: his concern for fellow Warriors and his final decision reveal a capacity for sacrifice that his abrasive manner tends to hide.

04 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography

EXPAND BIOGRAPHY DETAILS

Porco grows up in Marley alongside his older brother Marcel, who is selected for the Warrior program. Marcel influences the selection process that results in Reiner receiving the Armored Titan, partly to protect Porco from inheriting a shortened and dangerous life. Porco later inherits the Jaw Titan after Ymir returns to Marley and is consumed. He participates in Marley’s military operations, including the Liberio conflict and the battle on Paradis, where he confronts Reiner and Eren.

05 / ABILITIES

Abilities

EXPAND ABILITIES DETAILS

The Jaw Titan is known for exceptional speed, agility, powerful claws and jaws capable of breaking hardened Titan material. Porco’s version is especially suited to rapid attacks and close-quarters combat. Its smaller size makes it more mobile than many of the Nine Titans, although it is less suited to absorbing heavy punishment. Porco uses these traits aggressively, relying on speed and precision to overwhelm opponents.

06 / COMBAT & SKILLS

Combat & Skills

EXPAND COMBAT & SKILLS DETAILS

Porco is an aggressive close-range fighter. He uses the Jaw Titan’s speed to strike quickly, climb and reposition, while its jaws and claws can damage hardened defenses. His encounters with Eren and Reiner demonstrate the Titan’s offensive potential but also its vulnerability when opponents understand its movement and exploit openings. His pride sometimes drives him to take risks, while his final actions show tactical awareness and deliberate sacrifice.

07 / RELATIONSHIPS

Relationships

EXPAND RELATIONSHIPS DETAILS

Marcel is the central figure in Porco’s personal history. His brother’s decision to protect him shaped the inheritance he eventually received, though Porco initially misunderstands the reasons. Reiner is his long-standing rival and the focus of his resentment. Pieck and the other Warriors are comrades within a system that demands loyalty and sacrifice. His final understanding of Reiner changes the emotional meaning of their rivalry.

08 / DEVELOPMENT

Development

EXPAND DEVELOPMENT DETAILS

Porco begins as a hostile critic of Reiner, convinced that his rival is inferior and undeserving. As memories and events reveal the truth about Marcel’s decision, Porco’s resentment becomes more complicated. He recognizes that Reiner’s survival and suffering were not simply signs of weakness. His arc is brief but decisive: pride gives way to a clearer understanding of sacrifice, and he chooses to use his remaining strength to help another person.

09 / FINAL FATE

Final Fate

EXPAND FINAL FATE DETAILS

During the battle on Paradis, Porco is badly damaged and learns more about Marcel’s past through inherited memories. He realizes that Marcel deliberately influenced the Warrior selection to keep Porco from inheriting the Armored Titan. Porco then allows Falco to consume him, passing the Jaw Titan to the younger candidate and saving Reiner from being eaten. His death becomes an act of protection rather than rivalry.

10 / ANALYSIS

Analysis

EXPAND ANALYSIS DETAILS

Porco’s story examines sibling comparison, pride and the distortions caused by incomplete knowledge. He spends much of his life resenting Reiner for a decision Marcel made to protect him. Once he understands the truth, he can choose differently. His sacrifice does not erase his hostility or the violence he committed, but it shows how understanding another person’s burden can transform the meaning of a rivalry.

11 / CHRONOLOGY

Chronology

EXPAND CHRONOLOGY DETAILS

Porco is raised in Marley and follows his brother Marcel into the Warrior program. Marcel’s intervention results in Reiner receiving the Armored Titan. Porco later inherits the Jaw Titan after Ymir’s return to Marley. He fights in Marley’s campaigns and the battle on Paradis, learns the truth behind Marcel’s choice and sacrifices himself so Falco can inherit the Jaw Titan.

13 / PURPOSES & REASONING

Purposes & Reasoning

EXPAND PURPOSES & REASONING

Porco’s purpose is to explore pride, sibling comparison and the harm caused by judging another person without knowing the full story. He resents Reiner and believes his rival received a power he did not deserve, but his understanding changes when he learns what Marcel did to protect him. Porco’s reasoning begins in anger and wounded status, yet his final choice is guided by recognition and sacrifice. His role also demonstrates the Jaw Titan’s distinct combat function: speed and precision can matter as much as size or armor. The story does not turn his death into a simple redemption; he remains a soldier in a system that sends children to inherit weapons. His arc asks how new knowledge can change the meaning of an old rivalry and whether understanding another person’s burden can lead to a different final decision.
