History and background

Secre served the first Wizard King, Lumiere, and helped him investigate the ancient magic stones and the threat that became Zagred. She uses forbidden magic to seal Lumiere and herself, becoming a small bird known as Nero for centuries. In the present era, she joins the Black Bulls and eventually regains her human form.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Secre is reserved, observant and pragmatic. Her long life and burden of guilt make her guarded, but she is loyal and deeply committed to protecting the people connected to Lumiere and the truth of the past.

Character-reading note: This describes traits demonstrated in the narrative; it does not assign a clinical diagnosis.

03 · Magic, Abilities & Equipment

Sealing Magic can lock away magic, objects or beings under specific conditions. Her forbidden spell transforms her and seals Lumiere, but the act carries a severe cost. Her bird form is a consequence of the sealing magic rather than her natural species.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

Lumiere is the first Wizard King and her closest historical companion. Asta and the Black Bulls become her modern companions. She is connected to the ancient elf-human tragedy and Zagred's scheme.

Family, friendship, rivalry, mentorship, squad membership, political allegiance and romantic interest are distinct relationship types. Shared affiliation does not automatically establish friendship.

05 · Story Chronology & Development

Her history is revealed during the elf reincarnation arc. She plays a critical role in the battle against Zagred and later becomes a full member of the Black Bulls.

Flashbacks, reincarnation memories and later revelations are placed in the character's personal chronology and not mistaken for present-day events.

06 · Achievements, Battles & Contributions

Her sealing magic preserves Lumiere and helps expose the truth of the ancient tragedy. Her centuries-long loyalty is central to the resolution of the elf conflict.
