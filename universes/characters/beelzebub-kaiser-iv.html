PROFILE SUMMARY

Character overview

Known as Baby Beel, the infant demon prince bonds with Tatsumi Oga and becomes the catalyst for the series' human and demonic conflicts.

01 / CHARACTER RECORD

Identity & background

Beel is the youngest prince in the Great Demon Lord's family and is sent to the human world as part of a plan involving humanity's destruction. Despite his royal status and immense potential, he is an infant whose immediate needs, emotions and attachment to Oga shape most of his early appearances.

02 / CHARACTER RECORD

Personality

Beel communicates largely through infant behaviour, facial expressions and crying. He is strongly attached to Oga and reacts intensely to separation, fear and frustration. His apparent simplicity should not obscure the enormous demonic power he carries.

03 / CHARACTER RECORD

Abilities & combat

Immense demonic energy, most visibly expressed through electrical discharges. His emotional state can affect the release of that power. His bond with Oga enables the human guardian to access and channel aspects of Beel's power.

04 / CHARACTER RECORD

Limitations

Beel is physically an infant and depends on caregivers. His power is not consistently under conscious control, and emotional distress can make it dangerous to people nearby. His development is gradual rather than an immediate mastery of royal abilities.

05 / CHARACTER RECORD

Story chronology

Beel is carried into the human world and encounters Oga, whom he selects as his human parent. Hilda oversees his care. His illness and power instability lead the group toward the Demon World and reveal more about his royal family. The manga continues his development beyond the anime's endpoint.

06 / CHARACTER RECORD

Relationships

Tatsumi Oga — chosen human parent and central attachment. Hilda — maid and caretaker. The Great Demon Lord — father. Prince En — older brother and rival within the royal family. Lamia — medical support during a critical period.

07 / CHARACTER RECORD

Character analysis

Beel is both a source of comedy and the emotional centre of the story. His dependence forces Oga to practise care, patience and protection; the contrast between infant vulnerability and demonic scale drives the series' central irony.

08 / CHARACTER RECORD

Anime & manga continuity

The anime follows Beel's early life and the guardian bond but ends before the manga's complete late-story developments. The manga provides further context for Beel's family, origins and future.

09 / CHARACTER RECORD

Archive status

Central character in all 60 anime episodes and the complete manga.
