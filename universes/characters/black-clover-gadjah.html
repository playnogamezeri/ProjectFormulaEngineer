History and background

Gadjah is one of the Heart Kingdom's Spirit Guardians and serves Queen Loropechika. He is a highly capable mage who helps train Clover Kingdom knights as part of the alliance against the Spade Kingdom. His devotion to the queen and kingdom shapes his role in the conflict.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Gadjah is disciplined, loyal and serious about his responsibilities. His conduct reflects the duty expected of a guardian serving the Heart Kingdom.
