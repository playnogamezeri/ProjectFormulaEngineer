History

Zora is the son of Zara Ideale, a commoner who becomes the first commoner to join the Magic Knights. Zara's death at the hands of corrupt Magic Knights shapes Zora's distrust of the institution. Zora adopts a disguise and operates independently, targeting arrogant and abusive knights through traps before eventually joining the Black Bulls.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Zora is cynical, sarcastic and openly contemptuous of hypocrisy. His hostility toward corrupt Magic Knights comes from personal loss and a belief that the institution should protect people rather than exploit them. Beneath his abrasive behavior, he values fairness and respects people who demonstrate integrity through action.

03 · Magic, Abilities & Equipment

Ash Magic allows Zora to create ash-based traps and arrays that punish opponents for triggering them. His combat style emphasizes preparation, misdirection and counterattacks rather than overwhelming mana. His traps can reflect or amplify magical attacks according to their construction, making opponent behavior part of the battlefield. His effectiveness depends on setup, placement, timing and understanding the enemy's spell.

Combat strengths

  • Trap construction and battlefield preparation.
  • Countering stronger mages through their own attacks.
  • Reading opponents and exploiting arrogance.
  • Survival through misdirection rather than direct power contests.

Limitations

Trap-based tactics require conditions, positioning and preparation. A surprise attack or opponent who avoids triggering the traps can reduce his advantage.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

Zara Ideale is his father and the central influence on his values. The Black Bulls become his squad and a group whose unconventional nature he gradually accepts. His interactions with Asta and other Magic Knights test his belief that the institution is beyond reform.

05 · Story Chronology & Development

Zora's childhood and his father's death establish his distrust of the Magic Knights. He operates as a masked vigilante, enters the Royal Knights selection under a disguise and later becomes part of the Black Bulls. His story explores the difference between an institution's ideals and the conduct of the people who claim to represent it.

06 · Achievements, Battles & Contributions

Zora's key contribution is tactical: he uses traps to neutralize stronger opponents and expose corrupt behavior. During the Royal Knights selection, his methods demonstrate that intelligence and preparation can defeat raw magical superiority. His knowledge of trap magic later supports the Black Bulls in larger conflicts.

07 · Current Status & Continuity

Zora's profile follows the manga continuity. The exact limits of every trap configuration are not reduced to a single power rating; the spell's effect depends on its design and the opponent's response. Unconfirmed personal details are left unspecified.
