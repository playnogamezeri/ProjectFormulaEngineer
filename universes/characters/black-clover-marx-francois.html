History and background

Marx serves as a close aide to Julius Novachrono and helps manage the Wizard King's administrative and investigative responsibilities. His Memory Magic allows him to access and share memories, making him valuable for communication, evidence and coordination. He often acts as a practical counterbalance to Julius's enthusiasm.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Marx is conscientious, formal and responsible. He is often exasperated by Julius's impulsiveness but remains loyal and committed to the kingdom's welfare.

Character-reading note: This describes traits demonstrated in the narrative; it does not assign a clinical diagnosis.

03 · Magic, Abilities & Equipment

Memory Magic can extract, examine and communicate memories. It is useful for investigation and information transfer, but should not be treated as unrestricted mind control or proof that every memory is complete and infallible.

Evaluation dimensions

  • Attribute and source: separate innate magic from spirit, devil, contract, reincarnation or external power.
  • Technique: record range, precision, area coverage, defensive utility and combinations.
  • Resources: mana, stamina, preparation, grimoire development and transformation conditions.
  • Limitations: retain demonstrated costs, counters and activation requirements; do not assume unlimited power.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

Julius is his closest superior and colleague. He works within the Wizard King's administration and interacts with Magic Knight captains.

Family, friendship, rivalry, mentorship, squad membership, political allegiance and romantic interest are distinct relationship types. Shared affiliation does not automatically establish friendship.

05 · Story Chronology & Development

He appears in the kingdom's response to the Eye of the Midnight Sun, the elf crisis and the political aftermath. His work supports the Wizard King's leadership and the kingdom's investigations.

Flashbacks, reincarnation memories and later revelations are placed in the character's personal chronology and not mistaken for present-day events.

06 · Achievements, Battles & Contributions

His information-gathering and memory-sharing abilities help the kingdom coordinate and understand threats.
