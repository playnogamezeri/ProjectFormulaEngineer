History and background

Henry is born with a condition that causes him to absorb the mana of people nearby. To avoid harming others, he lives in isolation until Yami's squad takes him in. The Black Bulls' hideout becomes both his home and a magical construct he can reshape. His family background and condition explain his unusual role within the squad.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Henry is gentle, reserved and grateful for companionship. He speaks slowly and values the Black Bulls deeply because they accept him despite the danger of his condition.

Character-reading note: This section describes traits demonstrated in the narrative. It does not assign a clinical diagnosis or infer motives beyond the character's depicted actions.

03 · Magic, Abilities & Equipment

Recombination Magic allows Henry to rearrange and combine the structure of the Black Bulls' base, turning it into a mobile fortress or combat construct. His mana-draining condition can make prolonged proximity dangerous, but the squad's unusual mana characteristics allow them to coexist with him.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

Yami and the Black Bulls give him a family and a home. His connection to the squad is central to his willingness to fight.

He becomes active during the elf crisis and later battles, using the base itself as a strategic weapon and defensive platform.
