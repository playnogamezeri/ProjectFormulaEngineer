History and background

Vanessa is a witch who escapes the Witch Queen's control and joins the Black Bulls. Her magic initially focuses on creating and manipulating threads, but her relationship with her squad and desire to protect them lead to the emergence of the Red Thread of Fate, a spell that can alter the outcome of events for her companions under specific conditions.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Vanessa is relaxed, flirtatious and fond of drinking, but she is loyal and emotionally perceptive. Her independence is hard-won after growing up under the Witch Queen's control. She values the Black Bulls as the family she chose.

Character-reading note: This describes traits demonstrated in the narrative; it does not assign a clinical diagnosis.

03 · Magic, Abilities & Equipment

Thread Magic creates and controls threads for binding, movement and manipulation. Red Thread of Fate is a powerful protective spell that changes a harmful outcome for a chosen ally, but it is not an unrestricted ability and depends on the conditions established in the story.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

The Witch Queen is her mother and former controller. The Black Bulls are her chosen family. Yami's acceptance is central to her freedom.

Family, friendship, rivalry, mentorship, squad membership, political allegiance and romantic interest are distinct relationship types. Shared affiliation does not automatically establish friendship.

05 · Story Chronology & Development

Her story includes the Witches' Forest conflict, the discovery of her fate-altering magic and the major battles in which she protects the Black Bulls.

Flashbacks, reincarnation memories and later revelations are placed in the character's personal chronology and not mistaken for present-day events.

06 · Achievements, Battles & Contributions

Red Thread of Fate can prevent a decisive defeat for her allies and demonstrates how emotional bonds can shape magical development.
