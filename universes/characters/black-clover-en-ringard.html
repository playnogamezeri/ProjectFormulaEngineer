01 · Identity & Background

NAMEEn Ringard
ROLEGreen Mantis Magic Knight; Royal Knights member
ORIGINClover Kingdom
MAGICFungus Magic

History

En is a Green Mantis Magic Knight who joins the Royal Knights after the selection exam. He is the eldest sibling in a large family and takes the principle of protecting younger siblings seriously. During the elf conflict, he uses mushrooms to support concealment and weaken opponents, then helps with rescue work after the fighting.

02 · Personality

En appears physically frail, shy and frequently shivering, but his protective instincts are strong. He becomes unusually forceful when defending younger siblings or confronting mistreatment of them.

03 · Magic & Abilities

Fungus Magic creates mushrooms with varied functions, including communication, concealment, weakening effects and unusual mobility. His talking mushroom acts as a proxy voice with a much more fiery manner than En's usual demeanor.

Abilities are described from demonstrated material. No unsupported numerical power ranking is assigned.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

Green Mantis; Royal Knights; his younger siblings; Noelle Silva, whom he encourages when Solid mistreats her.

05 · Story Chronology

Appears during the Royal Knights Selection Exam, joins the Royal Knights, participates in the conflict involving the Eye of the Midnight Sun, and later assists in protecting and evacuating Clover Kingdom civilians.

06 · Achievements & Contributions

Uses support-oriented fungus spells to conceal people and interfere with enemies rather than relying on direct destructive force. His protective conduct toward Noelle is a defining character moment.

07 · Current Status & Continuity

Clover Kingdom Magic Knight. His story emphasizes support magic and protective responsibility.
