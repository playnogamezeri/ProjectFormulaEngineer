01 / CHARACTER RECORD

Identity & background

Himekawa is one of the four principal Tōhōshinki. His family's wealth gives him access to resources and influence unusual among the school's delinquent population. He often approaches conflict as a problem to manipulate rather than simply overpower.

02 / CHARACTER RECORD

Personality

Arrogant, calculating and image-conscious, Himekawa enjoys using money and planning to control situations. Beneath the vanity, he can be pragmatic and loyal when circumstances demand it.

03 / CHARACTER RECORD

Abilities & combat

A capable fighter who supplements physical skill with technology, financial resources, information gathering and tactical planning. His methods make him dangerous in situations where brute force is not enough.

04 / CHARACTER RECORD

Limitations

His dependence on money and elaborate plans can fail when opponents act unpredictably or when a direct physical confrontation removes his advantages.

05 / CHARACTER RECORD

Story chronology

Himekawa first appears as a major figure in Ishiyama's delinquent hierarchy. After conflict with Oga, he becomes part of the shifting alliance among former rivals and is repeatedly involved in the group's wider adventures.

06 / CHARACTER RECORD

Relationships

Oga — initially an opponent, later part of the same circle. Kanzaki and Tōjō — fellow Tōhōshinki. His family background and financial network shape his influence.

07 / CHARACTER RECORD

Character analysis

Himekawa broadens the series' idea of strength. He demonstrates that information, resources and planning can matter alongside physical power, while his vanity creates comic vulnerability.

08 / CHARACTER RECORD

Anime & manga continuity

His core school-rival and ally material is adapted in the anime. The manga continues the ensemble's story beyond the television ending.

09 / CHARACTER RECORD

Archive status

Recurring supporting character in anime and manga.
