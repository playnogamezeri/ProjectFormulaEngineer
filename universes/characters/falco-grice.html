Falco Grice

01 / IDENTITY

Identity

EXPAND IDENTITY DETAILS

Falco Grice is a young Eldian living in Marley’s Liberio internment zone and a candidate in the Warrior program. He is the younger brother of Colt Grice and a central point-of-view character in the Marley and Final Season storyline. Unlike many of the children around him, Falco is not driven primarily by national hatred or personal glory. His empathy and instinct to protect others become increasingly significant as he discovers how much of his world has been built on inherited fear.

02 / APPEARANCE

Appearance

EXPAND APPEARANCE DETAILS

Falco is a youthful, slight-built boy with short light-colored hair and a gentle, attentive expression. His appearance emphasizes his age and vulnerability in a military system that expects children to compete for a Titan inheritance. As the story progresses, his posture and expression reflect the emotional burden of events beyond his control. His later Titan form is markedly different from his human appearance and becomes one of the most unusual transformations in the series.

03 / PERSONALITY

Personality

EXPAND PERSONALITY DETAILS

Falco is compassionate, observant and unusually considerate. He recognizes Gabi’s strengths while also understanding the danger of the expectations placed on her. He is willing to risk his own prospects to protect someone else, even when he knows that doing so may cost him. His kindness is not ignorance: he gradually understands the cruelty of the conflict and still resists adopting its hatred. He can be anxious and uncertain, but he repeatedly acts with moral clarity under pressure.

04 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography

EXPAND BIOGRAPHY DETAILS

Falco grows up in Liberio under Marleyan rule and enters the Warrior candidate program. He competes alongside Gabi and other children, while his brother Colt is already positioned as a favored candidate. During the Liberio operation, Falco encounters Eren Yeager and becomes caught in the events that expose the scale of the conflict. He is injured and imprisoned, then drawn into the struggle surrounding the Yeagerists and the alliance. After being turned into a Pure Titan through Zeke’s scream, he inherits the Jaw Titan and becomes a key participant in the final battle.

05 / ABILITIES

Abilities

EXPAND ABILITIES DETAILS

Falco’s human strengths are observation, empathy and a willingness to act for others. As the Jaw Titan inheritor, he gains a compact, fast and powerful Titan body with formidable jaws and claws. His inheritance is unusual because his transformation is influenced by spinal fluid from Zeke, and his Titan develops avian characteristics that allow flight. The precise mechanics are exceptional to his story and should not be generalized as a standard Jaw Titan ability.

06 / COMBAT & SKILLS

Combat & Skills

EXPAND COMBAT & SKILLS DETAILS

Falco initially has little battlefield experience compared with trained soldiers and veteran Warriors. His Titan form changes his role dramatically: speed, aerial mobility and powerful jaws make him valuable in the final confrontation. His ability to fly gives the alliance a means of reaching the battlefield and extracting survivors. Falco’s combat contribution is strongest when used in coordination with others; his inexperience makes trust and guidance essential.

07 / RELATIONSHIPS

Relationships

EXPAND RELATIONSHIPS DETAILS

Gabi is Falco’s closest companion and the person he most consistently tries to protect. His feelings for her are expressed through patience and concern rather than demands. Colt is his older brother and a source of familial support, though Colt’s duty as a Warrior candidate places pressure on the family. Falco also forms connections with people from Paradis, including the former enemies who become allies. His compassion toward them helps undermine the categories that Marley taught him to accept.

08 / DEVELOPMENT

Development

EXPAND DEVELOPMENT DETAILS

Falco begins as a candidate trying to survive a system that measures children by military usefulness. He becomes increasingly aware that the enemy he was taught to hate is made up of ordinary people. His relationship with Gabi gives this realization emotional force, while his transformation into a Titan makes him part of the very machinery he once hoped to escape. He matures by choosing protection and cooperation over inherited hostility.

09 / FINAL FATE

Final Fate

EXPAND FINAL FATE DETAILS

Falco survives the final battle and retains the Jaw Titan’s power through the conflict’s conclusion. His flying Titan form helps the alliance reach the battlefield and rescue people. In the aftermath, he remains with Gabi and the surviving group, representing a younger generation that may have an opportunity to build relationships beyond the old divisions. The story does not promise that peace will be easy, but Falco’s choices demonstrate that inherited hatred is not inevitable.

10 / ANALYSIS

Analysis

EXPAND ANALYSIS DETAILS

Falco is a counterexample to the claim that children must reproduce the beliefs of the society that raises them. He is shaped by Marley’s propaganda and militarization, yet his empathy allows him to question its assumptions. His Titan inheritance also reverses the usual meaning of power: it becomes most valuable when used to protect and rescue rather than dominate. His story offers a limited but meaningful image of how a different future might begin.

11 / CHRONOLOGY

Chronology

EXPAND CHRONOLOGY DETAILS

Falco grows up in Liberio and enters the Warrior candidate program. He meets Eren during the Liberio events, is caught in the ensuing conflict and later becomes involved with the alliance. Zeke’s scream turns him into a Pure Titan; he then inherits the Jaw Titan. His Titan develops flight, which he uses to support the alliance during the final battle. He survives and remains with Gabi in the aftermath.
