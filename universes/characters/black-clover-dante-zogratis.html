History and background

Dante is the eldest of the Zogratis siblings and a leader of the Dark Triad, the group that seizes control of the Spade Kingdom. His devil contract with Lucifero grants immense power. Dante's ideology centers on human malice and the belief that people reveal their true nature through evil. He seeks to use the Qliphoth plan to bring devils into the human world.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Dante is arrogant, theatrical and sadistic. He treats conflict as a way to expose what he considers humanity's essential evil. His confidence and cruelty mask a worldview that reduces people to impulses and power.

Character-reading note: This section describes traits demonstrated in the narrative. It does not assign a clinical diagnosis or infer motives beyond the character's depicted actions.

03 · Magic, Abilities & Equipment

Body Magic allows him to alter and manipulate his body, including regeneration and physical restructuring. Through Lucifero he wields Gravity Magic, creating overwhelming pressure and battlefield control. His devil power greatly amplifies his physical strength and endurance, though his arrogance and opponents' teamwork expose vulnerabilities.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

He is the elder brother of Zenon and Vanica and the host of Lucifero. He is opposed by the Black Bulls and the allied Magic Knights.

05 · Story Chronology & Development

His main conflict occurs during the Spade Kingdom raid, where he fights the Black Bulls and is challenged by Asta, Magna and others. His role is part of the Dark Triad's failed attempt to open the Underworld.

Chronology is presented in narrative order at a high level. Flashbacks, reincarnation memories and later revelations are identified by their place in the character's history rather than being mistaken for events that occurred during the present-day plot.
