History and background

Licht is the leader of the ancient elf tribe and the original owner of the four-leaf grimoire that later becomes Asta's five-leaf grimoire. He loves Tetia, a human royal, and seeks peaceful coexistence between elves and humans. The massacre of the elves, manipulated by the devil Zagred, turns his story into one of the central tragedies of the series.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Licht is gentle, idealistic and compassionate, believing coexistence is possible despite prejudice. His grief and the manipulation of his memories reveal how even a peaceful leader can be turned into an instrument of revenge.

Character-reading note: This describes traits demonstrated in the narrative; it does not assign a clinical diagnosis.

03 · Magic, Abilities & Equipment

Sword Magic allows him to create, wield and control magical swords. His immense mana and elven power make him exceptionally formidable. His later state as a giant demon is a consequence of forbidden magic and despair, not his ordinary form.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

Tetia is his beloved. The elf tribe and Patolli are central to his history. He is connected to the first Wizard King Lumiere and the origins of the five-leaf grimoire.

Family, friendship, rivalry, mentorship, squad membership, political allegiance and romantic interest are distinct relationship types. Shared affiliation does not automatically establish friendship.

05 · Story Chronology & Development

His past is gradually revealed through the elf reincarnation arc. The truth of the massacre and Zagred's manipulation reframes the conflict between the elves and Clover Kingdom.

Flashbacks, reincarnation memories and later revelations are placed in the character's personal chronology and not mistaken for present-day events.

06 · Achievements, Battles & Contributions

His legacy shapes the grimoire, the elf conflict and the possibility of reconciliation between humans and elves.

07 · Current Status & Continuity

The name Licht is sometimes transliterated differently in older sources. Keep his identity distinct from Patolli, who impersonates him.

Anime coverage and manga-confirmed material are distinguished. Exact dates, ages, ranks, spell names or outcomes not established by reliable sources are left unspecified rather than guessed.
