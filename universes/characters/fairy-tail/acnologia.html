02Biography, Personality & MotivationsEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Mad former Slayer immune to magic.

World & Story Context: Earth-land centers on the Kingdom of Fiore (Magnolia, Crocus) regulated by the Magic Council, alongside the western Alvarez Empire, northern Guiltina, and parallel worlds Edolas and Elentear.

03Abilities, Equipment & LimitationsEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Documented Arsenal: Arcane Dragon Slayer immunity, dragonification

System Rules: Magic powered by internal Ethernano (Dragon Slayer, Celestial Spirit, Requip, Fairy Law) alongside Tartaros's emotion-fueled Etherious Curses.

04Chronology, Decisions & Final StatusEXPAND / COLLAPSE
SPOILER STATUS: Body sealed by Fairy Sphere; spirit slain by Natsu.

Continuity Context: The anime adds three filler arcs—Daphne (Ep. 69–75), Key of the Starry Sky (Ep. 125–150), and Eclipse Celestial Spirits (Ep. 204–226)—and phases out Season 1's spell-casting Magic Circles starting in Season 2.

  • Natsu Dragneel (Fairy Tail (Team Natsu)) — Fiery mage raised by Igneel who defeats Zeref and Acnologia.
  • Lucy Heartfilia (Fairy Tail (Team Natsu)) — Novice wizard turned master strategist who unites Fiore via Fairy Sphere.
  • Gray Fullbuster (Fairy Tail (Team Natsu)) — Stoic ice mage carrying Silver's Devil Slayer legacy.
  • Erza Scarlet (Fairy Tail (S-Class)) — Titania, Tower of Heaven survivor and daughter of Irene Belserion.
  • Wendy Marvell (Fairy Tail (Team Natsu)) — Young Dragon Slayer providing vital healing and Dragon Force support.
  • Happy (Fairy Tail / Exceed) — Natsu's loyal winged blue feline companion from Edolas.
  • Gajeel Redfox (Fairy Tail (Ex-Phantom Lord)) — Reformed rival who becomes fiercely loyal to Fairy Tail and Levy.
  • Makarov Dreyar (Fairy Tail Guild Master) — Beloved patriarch who risks his lifespan casting Fairy Law in Alvarez.
  • Zeref Dragneel (Alvarez Emperor Spriggan) — Natsu's cursed immortal brother seeking release through Natsu or Fairy Heart.
