History and background

Gordon is a quiet member of the Black Bulls whose family is associated with curses and poison-based magic. His upbringing leaves him socially isolated, and he initially struggles to communicate with others. Through the squad, he develops stronger friendships and a clearer sense of his family's abilities and history.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Gordon is gentle, loyal and deeply affectionate, although his low voice and habit of muttering make him difficult for others to understand. His apparent gloom conceals a sincere desire to be accepted and to help his friends.

Character-reading note: This section describes traits demonstrated in the narrative. It does not assign a clinical diagnosis or infer motives beyond the character's depicted actions.

03 · Magic, Abilities & Equipment

Poison Magic can produce and manipulate toxic substances. His family knowledge of curses gives him a specialized support role in identifying, understanding and addressing curse-related effects. His utility depends on preparation and the nature of the curse or poison involved.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

His father and the Agrippa family are central to his inherited knowledge. The Black Bulls become his chosen family and help him overcome isolation.

Relationship standards

Family, friendship, rivalry, mentorship, squad membership, political allegiance and romantic interest are recorded as separate relationship types. A shared organization does not automatically mean two characters are friends or allies in every circumstance.

05 · Story Chronology & Development

His development includes reconnecting with his family, learning more about curses and contributing to the squad's response to threats affecting its members.
