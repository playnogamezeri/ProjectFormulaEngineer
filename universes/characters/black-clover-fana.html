History and background

Human Fana is a mage associated with the Diamond Kingdom's experimentation and military program. She becomes connected to Mars and the Third Eye, and her body is later used as the host for the reincarnated elf Fana. She is also associated with the fire spirit Salamander in the series' larger spirit conflicts.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Human Fana's history is marked by experimentation, suffering and a desire for connection. Her identity should be separated from the elf soul that temporarily occupies her body; the two are related through the reincarnation plot but are not the same person.

Character-reading note: This section describes traits demonstrated in the narrative. It does not assign a clinical diagnosis or infer motives beyond the character's depicted actions.

03 · Magic, Abilities & Equipment

Her magic is associated with Fire Magic and, in later developments, Salamander's immense fire power. The exact ability set changes with the host/spirit and reincarnation context; profiles should specify which state is being described.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

Mars is a central companion from her Diamond Kingdom history. The elf Fana is the reincarnated soul inhabiting her body for part of the story.

Relationship standards

Family, friendship, rivalry, mentorship, squad membership, political allegiance and romantic interest are recorded as separate relationship types. A shared organization does not automatically mean two characters are friends or allies in every circumstance.

05 · Story Chronology & Development

She is introduced through the Diamond Kingdom and Third Eye conflicts, then becomes part of the elf reincarnation crisis and later spirit-related developments.

Chronology is presented in narrative order at a high level. Flashbacks, reincarnation memories and later revelations are identified by their place in the character's history rather than being mistaken for events that occurred during the present-day plot.
