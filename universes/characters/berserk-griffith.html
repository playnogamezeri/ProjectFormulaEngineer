PROFILE SUMMARY

Character overview

A charismatic commander whose extraordinary ambition and pursuit of a kingdom transform the lives of everyone who follows him.

CONTINUITY NOTE: This profile distinguishes established story information from interpretation. Manga developments beyond the animated adaptations are identified as such.
01 / CHARACTER RECORD

Identity & background

Griffith rises from humble circumstances through military talent, strategic judgment and the loyalty of the Band of the Hawk. His dream of possessing a kingdom shapes his decisions. After his capture, torture and physical ruin, the Eclipse presents him with the choice that leads to his rebirth as Femto.

02 / CHARACTER RECORD

Personality

Griffith is composed, persuasive, strategic and intensely ambitious. He can inspire devotion while maintaining emotional distance. His relationship with Guts unsettles his sense of control, exposing the tension between his dream and his dependence on another person.

03 / CHARACTER RECORD

Abilities & combat

As a human commander, Griffith is an exceptional strategist and skilled swordsman who directs the Hawks in battle. As Femto, he is a member of the God Hand with supernatural power far beyond ordinary human combat; his post-transformation abilities should not be equated with his former human skill.

04 / CHARACTER RECORD

Limitations

His human body is catastrophically damaged before the Eclipse. His ambition and need to preserve his dream shape his choices, while his transformation changes the scale and nature of his power. The narrative's metaphysical rules should not be reduced to a simple numerical power ranking.

05 / CHARACTER RECORD

Story chronology

Griffith forms and leads the Band of the Hawk, rises in Midland's military and becomes close to Guts. Following Guts's departure, Griffith's actions lead to imprisonment and torture. During the Eclipse he sacrifices the Hawks and is reborn as Femto. Later he returns to the physical world and establishes Falconia.

06 / CHARACTER RECORD

Relationships

Guts — the person whose departure destabilizes Griffith. Casca — loyal commander and member of the Hawks. Charlotte — Midland princess whose relationship with Griffith affects the political story. The God Hand — the supernatural group into which he is reborn.

07 / CHARACTER RECORD

Character analysis

Griffith embodies the danger of making a personal dream the supreme measure of value. His leadership creates genuine loyalty, but the Eclipse reveals the devastating point at which he treats those lives as expendable in pursuit of his ambition.

08 / CHARACTER RECORD

Anime & manga continuity

The anime adaptations portray different portions of Griffith's story. The Golden Age adaptations cover his rise and the Eclipse; later manga developments concerning Femto and Falconia extend well beyond the 1997 series.

09 / CHARACTER RECORD

Archive status

Principal antagonist and central figure in the manga's political and supernatural conflicts.
