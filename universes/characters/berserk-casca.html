PROFILE SUMMARY

Character overview

A capable warrior and commander whose history, agency, trauma and recovery are central to the emotional story of Berserk.

CONTINUITY NOTE: This profile distinguishes established story information from interpretation. Manga developments beyond the animated adaptations are identified as such.
01 / CHARACTER RECORD

Identity & background

Casca rises through the Band of the Hawk to become one of Griffith's trusted commanders. Her early life and the circumstances that bring her to Griffith shape her fierce commitment to the Hawks. Her bond with Guts develops during the Golden Age. She survives the Eclipse, but experiences profound trauma.

02 / CHARACTER RECORD

Personality

Casca is disciplined, courageous, proud and fiercely committed to her responsibilities. She can be sharp and guarded, especially when her authority or competence is challenged. Her feelings and decisions are shaped by loyalty, love, duty and the struggle to retain agency.

03 / CHARACTER RECORD

Abilities & combat

An experienced battlefield fighter and tactician. Casca's skill comes from training, command experience, discipline and judgment rather than supernatural powers. She leads troops and fights effectively within the military world of the Golden Age.

04 / CHARACTER RECORD

Limitations

Casca is human and vulnerable to injury, exhaustion and the consequences of war. The Eclipse leaves her with severe psychological trauma and a long period in which she cannot safely navigate ordinary life without support. Recovery is difficult and does not erase what happened.

05 / CHARACTER RECORD

Story chronology

Casca's story follows her rise within the Hawks, her changing relationship with Guts and Griffith, and her survival of the Eclipse. In later manga arcs, companions seek ways to help her recover. Her restored memories bring new emotional and narrative challenges.

06 / CHARACTER RECORD

Relationships

Griffith — commander to whom she was devoted. Guts — fellow Hawk and deeply significant partner. Judeau — comrade who understands her feelings. Farnese and Schierke — important figures in her later care and recovery.

07 / CHARACTER RECORD

Character analysis

Casca's story concerns agency under hierarchy, gendered expectations, loyalty and the long aftermath of trauma. She should not be reduced to a device in Guts's story: her own history, leadership and recovery are essential parts of the narrative.

08 / CHARACTER RECORD

Anime & manga continuity

The 1997 anime and Golden Age films depict the Golden Age and Eclipse, but do not adapt the manga's full later recovery storyline. Later manga developments are therefore not shown in those adaptations.

09 / CHARACTER RECORD

Archive status

Principal character; her later manga storyline extends beyond the main Golden Age anime adaptations.
