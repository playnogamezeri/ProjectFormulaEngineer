Reiner Braun

A Marleyan Warrior who infiltrates the 104th Cadet Corps and inherits the Armored Titan. Torn between duty, guilt and fractured identity, he ultimately joins the alliance against Eren.

01 / IDENTITY

Identity

EXPAND IDENTITY DETAILS

Reiner Braun is an Eldian Warrior serving Marley and the inheritor of the Armored Titan. He is raised in the Liberio internment zone and selected for the Warrior program. He infiltrates Paradis with Bertolt Hoover and Annie Leonhart, later joining the 104th Cadet Corps under a false identity. Japanese voice: Yoshimasa Hosoya; English voice: Robert McCollum.

02 / APPEARANCE

Appearance

EXPAND APPEARANCE DETAILS

Reiner is tall and powerfully built, with blond hair and a strong jaw. His appearance matures from a broad-shouldered cadet to a visibly burdened adult. The Armored Titan is distinguished by hardened plates across much of its body, giving it a fortress-like silhouette.

03 / PERSONALITY

Personality

EXPAND PERSONALITY DETAILS

Reiner initially presents himself as dependable, protective and confident. The contradiction between his Warrior mission and the friendships he forms inside the Walls fractures his identity. He experiences guilt, depression and a divided sense of self, while continuing to feel responsibility toward the people he has harmed.

04 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography

EXPAND BIOGRAPHY DETAILS

Reiner participates in the fall of Wall Maria and spends years living among the people of Paradis. His identity as a Marleyan Warrior is revealed at Shiganshina. He returns to Marley and later fights in the Liberio and Paradis conflicts. As Eren's Rumbling begins, Reiner joins former enemies in an alliance to stop the destruction.

05 / ABILITIES

Abilities

EXPAND ABILITIES DETAILS

Reiner inherits the Armored Titan, whose hardened plates provide strong defense against blades and many conventional attacks. The armor is not invulnerable: powerful weapons, Titan abilities and strategic targeting can break or bypass it. He also has the regeneration and transformation capabilities common to Titan shifters.

06 / COMBAT & SKILLS

Combat & Skills

EXPAND COMBAT & SKILLS DETAILS

Reiner is a durable frontline combatant, combining physical strength, endurance and close-quarters Titan fighting. His Armored Titan can absorb punishment and force openings, but its protection can limit mobility and can be defeated through coordinated tactics. His human military training also makes him effective with standard equipment.

07 / RELATIONSHIPS

Relationships

EXPAND RELATIONSHIPS DETAILS

Bertolt and Annie are fellow Warriors and partners in the Paradis mission. Marcel Galliard's sacrifice influences the Warrior group and Reiner's history. Eren becomes both an enemy and a mirror of the burdens Reiner carries. Gabi is his cousin and a younger Warrior candidate whose future he wants to protect.

08 / DEVELOPMENT

Development

EXPAND DEVELOPMENT DETAILS

Reiner's story exposes the psychological damage of turning children into weapons of state. He begins with a mission framed as heroic duty, then discovers that the people he was ordered to destroy are individuals with families and dreams. His guilt does not erase responsibility, but it helps him recognize the false divisions that shaped him.

09 / FINAL FATE

Final Fate

EXPAND FINAL FATE DETAILS

Reiner joins the alliance opposing Eren and survives the final battle. After the Titan power disappears, he returns to a world whose political future remains uncertain. He must live with the consequences of his actions rather than being granted a simple absolution.

10 / ANALYSIS

Analysis

EXPAND ANALYSIS DETAILS

Reiner is both responsible for serious harm and shaped by an abusive militarized system. His character requires holding those truths together. Understanding trauma does not excuse violence; it reveals how governments can manufacture enemies and make children carry the consequences.

11 / CHRONOLOGY

Chronology

EXPAND CHRONOLOGY DETAILS

Warrior training in Marley; mission to Paradis; 104th Cadet Corps; fall of Wall Maria; revelation at Shiganshina; return to Marley; Liberio and later battles; alliance against Eren; final battle and aftermath.
