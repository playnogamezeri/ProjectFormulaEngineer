History and background

Grey initially appears as a shy, heavily disguised figure who avoids attention. Her magic allows her to transform appearances and objects, and later revelations show a more unusual ability connected to the transformation of matter. Her past includes hardship and an abusive family environment before she finds a place with the Black Bulls.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Grey is timid, self-conscious and compassionate. She gradually gains confidence through friendship and learns to participate more openly. Her care for Gauche and the squad becomes a key part of her emotional development.

Character-reading note: This section describes traits demonstrated in the narrative. It does not assign a clinical diagnosis or infer motives beyond the character's depicted actions.

03 · Magic, Abilities & Equipment

Transformation Magic changes appearances and forms. Her later transmutation-related ability has effects beyond ordinary disguise, allowing her to alter the nature or state of things in ways that can support healing and combat. The exact limits should be tied to demonstrated scenes.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

Gauche is important to her emotional story. The Black Bulls give her acceptance and a place where she can develop without hiding.

05 · Story Chronology & Development

Her arc moves from secrecy and fear to confidence and meaningful participation, especially in the elf conflict and later kingdom-threatening battles.
