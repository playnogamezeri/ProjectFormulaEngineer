Armin Arlert

A strategist whose curiosity and empathy shape the group. He inherits the Colossal Titan after the Return to Shiganshina battle and later becomes a leading voice in the effort to negotiate peace.

01 / IDENTITY

Identity

EXPAND IDENTITY DETAILS

Armin Arlert is one of the central protagonists and Eren and Mikasa's childhood friend. He grows up in Shiganshina and dreams of seeing the world beyond the Walls after learning about it from a book associated with his grandfather. He joins the 104th Cadet Corps and later the Survey Corps. Birthday: November 3. Japanese voice: Marina Inoue; English voice: Jessie James Grelle.

02 / APPEARANCE

Appearance

EXPAND APPEARANCE DETAILS

Armin is initially slight in build, with blond hair and blue eyes. His appearance matures after the time skip, and he wears the Survey Corps uniform during his service. His Colossal Titan form is enormous, with exposed musculature and the immense scale and heat associated with that power.

03 / PERSONALITY

Personality

EXPAND PERSONALITY DETAILS

Curious, imaginative, empathetic and strategically minded, Armin often doubts his courage because he is physically weaker than many soldiers. Nevertheless, he repeatedly acts when others hesitate. He tries to understand opponents and imagine alternatives, while remaining capable of making ruthless decisions when he believes they are necessary.

04 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography

EXPAND BIOGRAPHY DETAILS

Armin joins the 104th Cadet Corps with Eren and Mikasa and becomes an important planner during the Battle of Trost. His intelligence helps expose the Female Titan's identity. At Shiganshina he is gravely burned while helping defeat Bertolt. The decision is made to save him with the Titan serum; he consumes Bertolt and inherits the Colossal Titan. He later joins the expedition to Marley and becomes central to the alliance opposing Eren.

05 / ABILITIES

Abilities

EXPAND ABILITIES DETAILS

Armin's human strengths are analysis, observation, strategic planning, persuasion and recognizing patterns in an opponent's behavior. As inheritor of the Colossal Titan, he gains enormous size, destructive physical power and the ability to release intense heat and steam. Transformation can cause catastrophic collateral damage, so judgment is essential.

06 / COMBAT & SKILLS

Combat & Skills

EXPAND COMBAT & SKILLS DETAILS

Armin is not a specialist in direct physical combat. His battlefield value usually comes from planning, timing, deception and identifying weaknesses. His Colossal Titan form gives him immense destructive capacity, but its scale makes it difficult to deploy without harming surrounding people and infrastructure.

07 / RELATIONSHIPS

Relationships

EXPAND RELATIONSHIPS DETAILS

Eren and Mikasa are his closest childhood friends. He shares Eren's dream of seeing the outside world, though their ideas of freedom diverge. He respects Erwin and carries the burden of being chosen to live in his place. Bertolt's memories and Titan power become part of his life after inheritance. His bond with Annie and trust in Jean, Connie, Hange and the alliance matter in the final conflict.

08 / DEVELOPMENT

Development

EXPAND DEVELOPMENT DETAILS

Armin begins as a boy who believes physical weakness makes him a burden. Repeated crises show that his mind, empathy and willingness to act can change battles. Inheriting the Colossal Titan gives him immense power and forces him to confront its cost. His childhood dream matures into a commitment to understand people beyond inherited enemy labels.

09 / FINAL FATE

Final Fate

EXPAND FINAL FATE DETAILS

Armin survives the final battle and becomes one of the representatives seeking peace after the Rumbling. He speaks with Eren through the Paths before the end and carries the memory of their friendship and disagreement. He continues toward a future in which diplomacy may prevent another cycle of war, though lasting peace is not guaranteed.

10 / ANALYSIS

Analysis

EXPAND ANALYSIS DETAILS

Armin represents curiosity and perspective-taking. His dream begins with a desire to see landscapes beyond the Walls and matures into a commitment to understand people whose histories differ from his own. He is not a perfect pacifist; he uses violence and accepts terrible costs. His distinctive contribution is the continued search for alternatives and recognition of humanity across enemy lines.

11 / CHRONOLOGY

Chronology

EXPAND CHRONOLOGY DETAILS

Childhood friendship with Eren and Mikasa; discovery of the outside-world book; 104th Cadet Corps; Battle of Trost; Female Titan operations; Uprising; Shiganshina and inheritance of the Colossal Titan; journey to Marley; Rumbling crisis; alliance and final battle; postwar diplomatic role.
