02Biography, Personality & MotivationsEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Steadfast homeroom teacher coordinating Final War logistics.

World & Story Context: Near-future Japan centered on Musutafu's U.A. High School, regulated by the Hero Public Safety Commission (HPSC) and supported by high-tech Support Course equipment.

03Abilities, Equipment & LimitationsEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Documented Arsenal: Erasure

System Rules: Genetic Quirks (Emitter, Transformation, Mutant) alongside the twin stockpiling/stealing Quirks One For All and All For One, Quirk Awakenings, and Support Items.

04Chronology, Decisions & Final StatusEXPAND / COLLAPSE
SPOILER STATUS: Continues teaching at U.A.

Continuity Context: Season 5 famously swapped the Meta Liberation Army and Endeavor Agency arcs to align with World Heroes' Mission, trimming Re-Destro and Spinner scenes, while Horikoshi added original scenes to Season 8 and Ep. 171 adapts bonus Ch. 431.
