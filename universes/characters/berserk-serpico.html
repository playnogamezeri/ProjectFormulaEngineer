PROFILE SUMMARY

Character overview

A controlled and observant swordsman whose loyalty to Farnese is rooted in a complicated family history and a protective promise.

CONTINUITY NOTE: This profile distinguishes established story information from interpretation. Manga developments beyond the animated adaptations are identified as such.
01 / CHARACTER RECORD

Identity & background

Serpico is revealed to be Farnese's half-brother, born from her father's relationship with a servant. He grows up in difficult circumstances and becomes Farnese's attendant and protector. His life is shaped by the promise to keep her safe.

02 / CHARACTER RECORD

Personality

Serpico is calm, courteous, perceptive and strategically minded. He often hides his own feelings behind a composed manner. His loyalty is deep, but it is also complicated by resentment, sacrifice and the burden of his promise.

03 / CHARACTER RECORD

Abilities & combat

Serpico is an agile and technically skilled swordsman who favors speed, positioning and tactical use of terrain. His Sylph Sword and associated equipment allow him to exploit wind and mobility in combat. He is dangerous through precision rather than brute force.

04 / CHARACTER RECORD

Limitations

Serpico's strength depends on skill, timing and equipment; he is not invulnerable. His devotion to Farnese can compromise his independent judgment, and his reserved nature makes it difficult for him to express his own needs.

05 / CHARACTER RECORD

Story chronology

Serpico first appears as Farnese's attendant and accompanies the Holy Iron Chain Knights. His family connection to Farnese is later revealed. He joins Guts's traveling party and remains a key protector and tactical fighter.

06 / CHARACTER RECORD

Relationships

Farnese — half-sister and the person he has sworn to protect. Guts — initially a dangerous rival, later a companion with whom he maintains a complex relationship. Isidro and the traveling party — fellow companions.

07 / CHARACTER RECORD

Character analysis

Serpico's story explores loyalty when it becomes both a source of meaning and a personal constraint. His competence and restraint conceal the costs of organizing his life around another person's safety.

08 / CHARACTER RECORD

Anime & manga continuity

Serpico's story is primarily part of the Conviction and later manga material. He appears in the 2016–2017 anime, while the manga provides greater context for his history and relationships.

09 / CHARACTER RECORD

Archive status

Major supporting character in the manga's later arcs.
