History and background

Mars is a soldier raised through the Diamond Kingdom's brutal magical experimentation program. His body and mind are altered through the fusion of two children's magic, connecting him to Fana. His early encounter with Asta and Yuno reveals the human cost of Diamond's military system. He later becomes a figure seeking a different future for the kingdom.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Mars begins emotionally suppressed and conditioned to obey. His memories and connection to Fana help him recover compassion and question the system that shaped him. He becomes more thoughtful and protective as he gains agency.

Character-reading note: This describes traits demonstrated in the narrative; it does not assign a clinical diagnosis.

03 · Magic, Abilities & Equipment

Crystal Magic creates and controls crystal structures for offense and defense. Through the experimental fusion with Fana's magic, he also gains access to fire-based abilities. His combat style combines hard constructs with elemental offense.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

Fana is central to his identity and emotional history. He is connected to Ladros and the Diamond Kingdom's military leadership and experiments.

Family, friendship, rivalry, mentorship, squad membership, political allegiance and romantic interest are distinct relationship types. Shared affiliation does not automatically establish friendship.

05 · Story Chronology & Development

He is introduced during the dungeon exploration conflict and later reappears as the Diamond Kingdom's political situation changes. His path reflects the possibility of reform after military abuse.

Flashbacks, reincarnation memories and later revelations are placed in the character's personal chronology and not mistaken for present-day events.

06 · Achievements, Battles & Contributions

His survival, change of allegiance and movement toward reform make him an important bridge between the Diamond Kingdom and Clover's allies.
