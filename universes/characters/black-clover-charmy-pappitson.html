History and background

Charmy initially appears as a small, food-loving member of the Black Bulls. Her heritage is later revealed to include dwarf ancestry, explaining a distinct transformation and the extraordinary nature of her food-related magic. Her magic can create cotton constructs and food-based effects that restore or consume magical energy.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Charmy is cheerful, affectionate and intensely devoted to food. She may appear carefree, but she becomes fiercely protective when friends or meals are threatened. Her dwarf heritage adds depth to her identity and power.

Character-reading note: This section describes traits demonstrated in the narrative. It does not assign a clinical diagnosis or infer motives beyond the character's depicted actions.

03 · Magic, Abilities & Equipment

Cotton Magic creates sheep and cotton constructs for defense, support and offense. Food Magic manifests food-related effects and can replenish allies' mana by feeding them. Her larger dwarf-associated form dramatically changes her combat capabilities.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

The Black Bulls are her found family. Her connection to food is a defining personal trait; her family heritage is relevant to her later development.

Relationship standards

Family, friendship, rivalry, mentorship, squad membership, political allegiance and romantic interest are recorded as separate relationship types. A shared organization does not automatically mean two characters are friends or allies in every circumstance.

05 · Story Chronology & Development

Her heritage is disclosed as the story expands beyond the early Black Bulls missions. She participates in the major kingdom conflicts and demonstrates the strategic value of recovery and support magic.

Chronology is presented in narrative order at a high level. Flashbacks, reincarnation memories and later revelations are identified by their place in the character's history rather than being mistaken for events that occurred during the present-day plot.

06 · Achievements, Battles & Contributions

Her ability to restore allies' mana makes her more than comic relief: she can alter the endurance of a battle and support high-level fighters.
