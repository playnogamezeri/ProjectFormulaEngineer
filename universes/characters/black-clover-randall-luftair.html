01 · Identity & Background

ROLEVice-Captain, Crimson Lion Kings
ORIGINClover Kingdom
MAGICAir Magic; Reinforcement Magic

History

Randall serves as vice-captain of the Crimson Lion Kings. During the elf reincarnation conflict, an elf spirit takes control of his body and attacks the squad's base. Fuegoleon and Salamander defeat the possessed Randall, and the reincarnated elves are later released. Randall subsequently resumes his service with the Crimson Lions.

02 · Personality

His ordinary personality is only lightly explored in the available story material. The hostility shown while possessed belongs to the elf spirit and should not be treated as Randall's own settled personality.

03 · Magic & Abilities

Air Magic lets him manipulate air for barriers and offensive blasts. He also uses Reinforcement Magic to strengthen his physical abilities. His grimoire contains air-based spells.

Abilities are described from demonstrated material. No unsupported numerical power ranking is assigned.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

Crimson Lion Kings; Fuegoleon Vermillion; Leopold Vermillion; Clover Kingdom Magic Knights.

05 · Story Chronology

Introduced as a senior Crimson Lion. Becomes an elf host during the elf reincarnation arc, is freed after the conflict, and later serves with the squad during subsequent threats.

06 · Achievements & Contributions

As an elf host, his body overpowers several Crimson Lion members. This is a possession-assisted event, not a clean measure of Randall's independent combat level.

07 · Current Status & Continuity

Alive after the elf reincarnation conflict. Distinguish his own actions from the actions of the elf who possessed him.
