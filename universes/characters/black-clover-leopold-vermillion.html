History and background

Leopold is the younger Vermillion sibling and a Magic Knight who admires his older brother Fuegoleon and sister Mereoleona. He is determined to become stronger in his own right rather than remain in their shadow. His rivalry with Asta pushes him to train harder and define his own standards.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Leopold is energetic, earnest, competitive and courageous. He respects effort and has a strong sense of honor. His enthusiasm sometimes outruns his experience, but his determination is sincere.

Character-reading note: This describes traits demonstrated in the narrative; it does not assign a clinical diagnosis.

03 · Magic, Abilities & Equipment

Fire Magic gives him direct offensive spells and control of flame. He develops through training and combat experience, improving the scale and precision of his fire-based techniques.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

Fuegoleon and Mereoleona are his older siblings. Asta becomes a friendly rival and source of motivation. He belongs to the Crimson Lion Kings.

Family, friendship, rivalry, mentorship, squad membership, political allegiance and romantic interest are distinct relationship types. Shared affiliation does not automatically establish friendship.

05 · Story Chronology & Development

He participates in the Royal Knights selection and later kingdom-wide conflicts. His development is marked by training, rivalry and his effort to stand independently as a Vermillion.

Flashbacks, reincarnation memories and later revelations are placed in the character's personal chronology and not mistaken for present-day events.

06 · Achievements, Battles & Contributions

His strongest achievement is his continued growth and service as a Magic Knight despite the immense reputations of his siblings.
