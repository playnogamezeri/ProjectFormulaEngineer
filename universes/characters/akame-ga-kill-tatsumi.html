03 / CHARACTER FILE

Complete Biography

PROFILE DETAILS ＋
Before Night Raid's campaign

After being separated from childhood companions Ieyasu and Sayo, Tatsumi is deceived by the Capital's apparent generosity. Discovering that Aria's household tortured and murdered them destroys his faith in the ruling class. He joins Night Raid and gradually becomes a skilled revolutionary fighter.

Conflict with the revolution

Tatsumi first relies on ordinary swordsmanship. Bulat's Incursio later gives him armor, enhanced strength, speed, defense and adaptive capabilities. The living Danger Beast within the armor continues to evolve, but that evolution increasingly threatens the user's body.

Final campaign

The continuities sharply diverge. In the 2014 anime, Tatsumi dies after using Incursio's evolved power to stop the Imperial superweapon and save the Capital. In the manga, he survives but is transformed into a dragon-like being through Incursio's evolution; his relationship with Mine continues into the aftermath.

Relationships & Affiliations

PROFILE DETAILS ＋

Bulat is his mentor and the source of Incursio. Akame becomes a trusted comrade; Mine develops from rival and teammate into his closest romantic bond. His village and the memory of Ieyasu and Sayo remain the emotional reasons behind his commitment.

Faction context

Psychological & Thematic Analysis

Tatsumi is the audience's entry into the Empire's moral collapse. His growth tests whether an ordinary person's courage can survive a world where every victory demands sacrifice.

The character's story contributes to the series' wider examination of violence, political legitimacy and the difference between personal conviction and moral responsibility. Their actions can be understood in context without automatically being justified by that context.

The contrast between private relationships and public allegiance is especially important: the Empire and the Revolutionary Army both demand sacrifice, while individual characters must decide what they are willing to defend and what they refuse to become.
