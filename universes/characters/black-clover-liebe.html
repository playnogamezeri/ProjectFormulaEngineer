History and background

Liebe is a devil born without magic, an unusual condition that leaves him vulnerable in the Underworld. Licita finds him and raises him as her son. After Lucifero attempts to possess him, Licita sacrifices herself and seals Lucifero in the five-leaf grimoire. Liebe's hatred of devils and his bond with Licita shape his eventual partnership with Asta.

02 · Personality & Psychology

Liebe is initially bitter, distrustful and consumed by revenge against devils, especially Lucifero. His relationship with Asta forces him to confront grief and isolation and gradually develop trust. He is not simply a weapon; he is a person with his own agency and memories.

Character-reading note: This describes traits demonstrated in the narrative; it does not assign a clinical diagnosis.

03 · Magic, Abilities & Equipment

Anti-Magic negates magical phenomena and is channelled through Asta's swords and their shared power. Liebe's lack of mana is central to why Licita's condition does not harm him. Devil Union combines their power, but it has conditions and a finite duration.

04 · Relationships & Affiliations

Licita is his adoptive mother. Asta becomes his partner and brotherly counterpart. Lucifero is his principal enemy and the source of his trauma.

Family, friendship, rivalry, mentorship, squad membership, political allegiance and romantic interest are distinct relationship types. Shared affiliation does not automatically establish friendship.

05 · Story Chronology & Development

His history is revealed during the Spade Kingdom conflict. He and Asta form a contract based on mutual partnership rather than domination and confront Lucifero together.

Flashbacks, reincarnation memories and later revelations are placed in the character's personal chronology and not mistaken for present-day events.

06 · Achievements, Battles & Contributions

His Anti-Magic is one of the most unusual powers in the series. His personal achievement is moving from isolation and vengeance toward a chosen bond with Asta.
