PROFILE SUMMARY

Character overview

An elf who accompanies Guts and offers healing, emotional contrast and occasional humor amid the violence of the Black Swordsman journey.

CONTINUITY NOTE: This profile distinguishes established story information from interpretation. Manga developments beyond the animated adaptations are identified as such.
01 / CHARACTER RECORD

Identity & background

Puck is an elf from the astral world who encounters Guts during the Black Swordsman period. He chooses to travel with him, despite Guts's hostile and solitary manner. His presence gives the story a recurring point of compassion and a connection to the supernatural ecology of the setting.

02 / CHARACTER RECORD

Personality

Puck is expressive, curious, compassionate and irreverent. He often responds to danger with humor, but his concern for suffering is genuine. He challenges Guts's emotional isolation even when Guts resists his companionship.

03 / CHARACTER RECORD

Abilities & combat

Puck is not primarily a frontline fighter. As an elf, he can provide healing through elf dust and has abilities associated with his nature and small size. His greatest contribution is often support, observation and emotional intervention.

04 / CHARACTER RECORD

Limitations

Puck is physically small and not suited to overpowering major human or supernatural enemies. His healing support has limits and does not make him capable of reversing every injury or trauma.

05 / CHARACTER RECORD

Story chronology

Puck meets Guts during the Black Swordsman storyline and becomes an early traveling companion. He remains part of the wider journey as Guts gradually gathers a group of companions.

06 / CHARACTER RECORD

Relationships

Guts — traveling companion whose emotional defenses Puck repeatedly challenges. Isidro — fellow companion with whom he shares comic exchanges. The wider group — Puck provides levity and support during dangerous travels.

07 / CHARACTER RECORD

Character analysis

Puck's humor is not merely tonal relief. His compassion provides a counterpoint to the cruelty of the setting and repeatedly reminds the reader that small acts of care remain possible in a brutal world.

08 / CHARACTER RECORD

Anime & manga continuity

Puck appears in the manga and in the 1997 anime's Black Swordsman material. Adaptations focused only on the Golden Age omit him or give him little or no role.

09 / CHARACTER RECORD

Archive status

Recurring supporting character in the manga and selected adaptations.
