Synopsis & Production Context

Hired by the Einzbern family to secure the omnipotent Holy Grail, disillusioned mercenary Kiritsugu Emiya enters the 1994 Fourth Holy Grail War alongside his homunculus wife Irisviel and King Arthur (Saber). Standing against them are traditionalist Tokiomi Tohsaka, King of Conquerors Iskander, Clock Tower lecturer Kayneth, and the hollow priest Kirei Kotomine.

02World & SettingEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Set in 1994 Fuyuki City, Japan, where a hidden magus society governed by London's Clock Tower and moderated by the Holy Church wages a secret war with modern firearms and ancient Magecraft.

Fuyuki City & Mion River

Commercial Shinto and residential Miyama districts hosting the Fourth War.

Einzbern Castle

Forest stronghold fortified with Kiritsugu's traps and Kayneth's siege.

Tohsaka Manor & Fuyuki Church

Command centers of Tokiomi Tohsaka and Holy Church Overseer Risei Kotomine.

Fuyuki Bridge

Site of Iskander's final charge against Gilgamesh's Enuma Elish.

11Ending ExplainedEXPAND / COLLAPSE
SPOILER DISCLOSURE: Detailed resolution of Fate/Zero across anime and source material.

Kiritsugu rejects Angra Mainyu's rigged wish and orders Saber to destroy the Grail, inadvertently spilling black mud that ignites the Great Fuyuki Fire. He rescues and adopts young Shirou Emiya, passing on his dream before dying five years later.

12PurposesEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Moral of the Story: Utilitarian Math Cannot Save Everyone—Only Compassion Can

Fate/Zero exposes the tragedy of a man who treated human lives as arithmetic until the Grail forced him to see the ledger.

Though Kiritsugu's methods fail, rescuing a single boy from the ashes proves that saving one real person still matters.
