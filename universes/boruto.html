01 / SERIES PROFILE

Overview

Set after the Fourth Great Ninja War, the story follows Boruto Uzumaki and a new generation growing up in a more technologically connected era. Their lives are shaped by the legacy of the previous generation and threats that challenge the shinobi world's assumptions.

Creator

Original concept by Masashi Kishimoto; manga story by Ukyo Kodachi and later Masashi Kishimoto, with art by Mikio Ikemoto.

Publication & release

Manga began in 2016; television anime began in 2017. The manga continues under the Two Blue Vortex title after the first part.

The hidden villages are navigating peace, modernization and new forms of danger. Scientific ninja tools, inter-village cooperation and Ōtsutsuki-related mysteries expand the older Naruto setting.

10 / EXPANDED RESEARCH DOSSIER

Series analysis & world reference

The sequel follows Boruto Uzumaki and his generation in a world transformed by the end of the Fourth Shinobi World War. Peace, technology and inter-village cooperation create opportunities that did not exist for Naruto's generation, but they also produce new conflicts around identity, inherited expectations and threats that exceed familiar shinobi institutions. Boruto's relationship with Naruto is a central personal tension within the larger transition between generations.

World structure and institutions

The Five Great Shinobi Countries remain important, but the postwar era is marked by diplomatic cooperation, scientific ninja tools and changing security priorities. Konoha's urban modernization contrasts with the older village structure. The Ōtsutsuki history and Kara's activities reveal that the shinobi world is connected to a larger cosmic conflict.

Abilities, technology and operating rules

Chakra natures, ninjutsu, taijutsu, genjutsu and dōjutsu continue from Naruto, while scientific ninja tools reproduce or store techniques through technology. Karma is a distinct Ōtsutsuki-related mark with consequences for the host's body and identity. The Jougan's status and capabilities require careful canon labeling because anime and manga treatment differ. Do not conflate technological replication with natural chakra mastery.

Organizations, factions and relationships

The new generation of Konoha shinobi, the Five Kage, the Scientific Ninja Weapons Team, Kara and the Ōtsutsuki are separate institutional or adversarial groups. Kara's inner and outer structures matter to understanding its operations. The parent generation remains influential but should not displace the younger cast as the sequel's narrative center.

Major themes and adaptation continuity

Boruto examines generational change, the cost of peace, parental absence, technological progress, identity and the burden of living under a famous predecessor's legacy. The manga and television anime have substantial differences in pacing and material, including anime-original arcs; these should be catalogued as distinct continuity layers rather than blended without labels.
