Synopsis & Production Context

Born into an era of unprecedented shinobi peace, Boruto Uzumaki resents his father Naruto's Hokage duties and seeks his own path under Sasuke Uchiha. When extraterrestrial Ōtsutsuki invaders and the cyborg syndicate Kara target Earth's chakra, Boruto is branded with Momoshiki's Karma seal—culminating in Kawaki sealing Naruto away and Eida's Omnipotence casting Boruto as a global fugitive.

02World & SettingEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Set roughly 15 years after the Fourth Shinobi World War across the Five Great Shinobi Nations, centered on a modernized Konohagakure featuring skyscrapers, rail networks, and Scientific Ninja Tools alongside ancient Ōtsutsuki dimensions.

Konohagakure (Hidden Leaf)

Modernized metropolis in the Land of Fire governed by the Seventh (later Eighth) Hokage.

Kara Hideouts & Dimensions

Hidden subterranean bases and pocket dimensions linked via Karma space-time portals.

Daikokuten Dimension

Timeless storage dimension where Kawaki seals Naruto and Hinata.

Sunagakure & Kirigakure

Allied hidden villages navigating post-war modernization and Shinju threats.

03Story & ChronologyEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Chronological Progression

The anime opens with supervised Academy-era prequel arcs before adapting the Momoshiki invasion, Mujina Bandits, Ao/Vessel, and Kawaki arcs up to Code's Assault (Part I Ch. 67). Unadapted chapters (Ch. 68–80) trigger Omnipotence and a three-year timeskip into Two Blue Vortex.

ANIME VS. SOURCE CONTINUITY DIFFERENCES: Of 293 anime episodes, 74 are direct manga canon, 179 are supervised anime-canon (such as Kara Actuation and Mitsuki's Disappearance), 12 are mixed, and 28 are standalone filler. Sumire Kakei debuted in the anime's Academy Entrance Arc before becoming a canon manga scientist, while Deepa and Urashiki Ōtsutsuki are anime-original antagonists.

11Ending ExplainedEXPAND / COLLAPSE
SPOILER DISCLOSURE: Detailed resolution of Boruto: Naruto Next Generations across anime and source material.

Part I of the TV anime concludes at Episode 293 right after Kawaki kills and revives Boruto, leaving the Omnipotence twist (Ch. 68–80) and Two Blue Vortex unadapted. In the ongoing manga, Boruto operates as a lone protector alongside Koji Kashin, Shikamaru, and Sarada against the Shinju.

12PurposesEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Moral of the Story: Who You Are Endures Even When the World Forgets Your Name

Boruto challenges a young hero to prove his convictions when his entire history, family, and reputation are rewritten overnight.

By contrasting Kawaki's fear-driven control with Boruto's willingness to shoulder exile, the series argues that true shinobi resolve is defined by selfless action rather than public acclaim.
