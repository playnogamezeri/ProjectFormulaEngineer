01 / READING THE RECORD

Catalogue Rules

Scope: This is the first structured edition, not a claim that every named blade in Bleach has been catalogued. It prioritizes major manga and TYBW wielders and records only established information. A missing Bankai entry means “not revealed or not established here,” not proof that the wielder cannot possess one.

Shikai is the initial release of a Zanpakutō; the release call is commonly spoken before the blade's name. Bankai is the advanced release and is individually named. Release commands have variant translations across official editions, so the English command below is a readable reference rendering, not a claim that every translation uses identical wording.

Ability summaries describe demonstrated functions, not an exhaustive list of every named move. Exact limits are included only where the story establishes them. “Unknown” and “not disclosed” are intentionally retained.

02 / CENTRAL CHARACTERS

Main Wielders

ICHIGO KUROSAKI · SUBSTITUTE SOUL REAPER

Zangetsu

Shikai: Zangetsu — no standard spoken release command is used in his familiar released state.

Bankai: Tensa Zangetsu.

Demonstrated function: Ichigo's releases concentrate his combat power into high-speed, high-impact sword combat and Getsuga Tenshō attacks. His blade and power presentation change as the truth of his mixed heritage is revealed.

Evidence / limits: His early oversized blade is not a simple indicator of raw output. The later dual-blade revelation and reforged Bankai are distinct stages; do not treat every visual form as a separate Bankai.

MANGATYBW
RUKIA KUCHIKI · SQUAD 13

Sode no Shirayuki

Shikai command: “舞え” — “Dance.”

Bankai: Hakka no Togame.

Demonstrated function: A cold-based Zanpakutō that lowers Rukia's body temperature to extreme levels; her Shikai forms use precise temperature control. Bankai releases an immense field of freezing cold.

Limits: Rukia must carefully manage the temperature state and restore her body gradually; sudden movement or poor control during the return to normal temperature can be dangerous.

MANGATYBW
RENJI ABARAI · SQUAD 6

Zabimaru

Shikai command: “咆えろ” — “Howl.”

Bankai: Sōō Zabimaru. An earlier incomplete/incorrectly understood Bankai name was Hihiō Zabimaru.

Demonstrated function: Shikai extends and segments into a bladed, flexible weapon. The true Bankai reveals a different structure and more complete expression of the Zanpakutō spirit.

Evidence / limits: The distinction between Hihiō Zabimaru and Sōō Zabimaru is a major canon correction; catalogue the earlier form as a partial understanding, not an unrelated second Bankai.

MANGATYBW
URYŪ ISHIDA · QUINCY

No Zanpakutō

Weapon system: Quincy spirit weapons, not a Zanpakutō.

Catalogue note: Uryū belongs in the wider combat database but should not be assigned a Shikai or Bankai. His bow, Schrift and Quincy techniques are recorded separately.

03 / CAPTAINS & OFFICERS

Established Shikai and Bankai Records

BYAKUYA KUCHIKI · SQUAD 6

Senbonzakura

Shikai: “散れ” — “Scatter.” The blade disperses into countless tiny blade fragments that resemble cherry-blossom petals.

Bankai: Senbonzakura Kageyoshi. Creates vast formations of blade fragments, controlled in large-scale patterns and concentrated attacks.

Combat note: The petals are blades, not harmless flowers; Byakuya can alter their density, formation and control for offense and defense.

TŌSHIRŌ HITSUGAYA · SQUAD 10

Hyōrinmaru

Shikai: “霜天に坐せ” — “Sit upon the frozen heavens.”

Bankai: Daiguren Hyōrinmaru.

Demonstrated function: Ice and water manipulation, dragon-shaped ice manifestations and large-area freezing. The mature form shown in TYBW changes the scale and precision of its power.

Limits: The Bankai's maturation and remaining ice petals are relevant to the duration and completeness of its adult form.

GENRYŪSAI YAMAMOTO · FORMER HEAD CAPTAIN

Ryūjin Jakka

Shikai: “万象一切灰燼と為せ” — “Reduce all creation to ash.”

Bankai: Zanka no Tachi.

Demonstrated function: The most destructive fire-type Zanpakutō shown. Bankai concentrates its flames into a blade and manifests effects associated with east, west, south and north.

Combat note: Its heat threatens the surrounding Soul Society; prolonged release has catastrophic environmental consequences.

SŌI FŌNG · SQUAD 2

Suzumebachi

Shikai: “尽敵螫殺” — “尽敵螫殺” commonly rendered “Sting all enemies to death.”

Bankai: Jakuhō Raikōben.

Demonstrated function: Shikai marks a target with a two-hit death condition at the same location. Bankai becomes a large missile-like explosive weapon.

Limits: The Shikai's mark condition requires repeated strikes at the same point; the Bankai is conspicuous and poorly suited to stealth.

GIN ICHIMARU · FORMER SQUAD 3

Shinsō

Shikai: “射殺せ” — “Shoot to kill.”

Bankai: Kamishini no Yari.

Demonstrated function: The blade extends and retracts at extraordinary speed. Gin's Bankai conceals a fragment that carries a cell-destroying poison.

Combat note: The blade's true threat is not simply its maximum length or speed; the hidden poison is the decisive mechanism.

SŌSUKE AIZEN · FORMER SQUAD 5

Kyōka Suigetsu

Shikai: “砕けろ” — “Shatter.”

Bankai: Not revealed.

Demonstrated function: Complete hypnosis manipulates the five senses of those who have witnessed its release, enabling Aizen to control their perception of targets and events.

Limits: The conditions for entering the hypnosis and the possibility of avoiding or countering it are more important than raw force. Do not invent a Bankai for Aizen.

SHUNSUI KYŌRAKU · HEAD CAPTAIN

Katen Kyōkotsu

Shikai: “花風紊れて花神啼き、天風紊れて天魔嗤う” — a poetic release call with translation variations.

Bankai: Katen Kyōkotsu: Karamatsu Shinjū.

Demonstrated function: Shikai imposes children's games as combat rules. Bankai unfolds a staged tragedy that affects those caught within its area.

Limits: Its effects are not a simple direct attack; allies and nearby participants may be endangered, making the choice of when and where to release it critical.

JŪSHIRŌ UKITAKE · FORMER SQUAD 13

Sōgyo no Kotowari

Shikai: “波悉く我が盾となれ、雷悉く我が刃となれ” — “All waves become my shield; all lightning become my blade.”

Bankai: Not revealed.

Demonstrated function: The paired blades and connecting cord redirect or return energy-based attacks through a system of plates.

Limits: Its known function is tied to incoming energy and redirection; do not assume it can reflect every physical or conceptual attack.

RETSU UNOHANA · FORMER SQUAD 4 / FIRST KENPACHI

Minazuki

Shikai: “捩れよ” is not established as a reliable universal release rendering in all references; use the name and demonstrated release rather than overstate a translation.

Bankai: Minazuki (皆尽).

Demonstrated function: Shikai manifests a large manta-like creature used for transport and healing. Bankai releases a blood-like substance and forms a lethal close-combat environment.

Evidence note: The precise mechanics are shown briefly and remain open to interpretation.

KENPACHI ZARAKI · SQUAD 11

Nozarashi

Shikai: “飲め” — “Drink.”

Bankai: Bankai is demonstrated, but no distinct spoken Bankai name is confirmed.

Demonstrated function: Shikai transforms the blade into a massive cleaver capable of extraordinary cutting power. Bankai dramatically amplifies Kenpachi's physical destructive capacity.

Limits: The Bankai's power places severe strain on Kenpachi's body; his own strength can exceed what his body can safely withstand.

MAYURI KUROTSUCHI · SQUAD 12

Ashisogi Jizō

Shikai: “掻き毟れ” — “Rip.”

Bankai: Konjiki Ashisogi Jizō; later modified forms appear.

Demonstrated function: Shikai attacks the nervous system and inhibits movement while preserving sensation. Bankai manifests a giant poisonous creature; Mayuri modifies its properties for specific opponents.

Limits: Its toxins and configuration can be altered; do not treat one appearance as the only possible Bankai design.

SAJIN KOMAMURA · FORMER SQUAD 7

Tenken

Shikai: “轟け” — “Roar.”

Bankai: Kokujō Tengen Myō’ō.

Demonstrated function: A giant armored warrior mirrors Komamura's movements and attacks. Its later Dangai Jōe form is tied to the Humanization Technique.

Limits: Damage to the giant is reflected onto Komamura; the humanization transformation carries a severe, irreversible cost in the story.

KISUKE URAHARA · FORMER SQUAD 12

Benihime

Shikai: “起きろ” — “Awaken.”

Bankai: Kannonbiraki Benihime Aratame.

Demonstrated function: Shikai produces versatile red spiritual-energy techniques, barriers and cutting effects. Bankai restructures what it touches within its range, including living bodies and surrounding material.

Limits: Its restructuring is range- and contact-dependent; the effect is not an unrestricted wish-granting power.

CHŌJIRŌ SASAKIBE · FORMER LIEUTENANT SQUAD 1

Gonryōmaru

Shikai: “穿て” — “Pierce.”

Bankai: Kōkō Gonryō Rikyū.

Demonstrated function: Lightning-based blade techniques; Bankai summons and controls a large lightning formation over the battlefield.

Continuity note: This Bankai belongs to Sasakibe, not Yamamoto.

04 / SPECIAL CASES

Unusual and Additional Zanpakutō

SHŪHEI HISAGI · SQUAD 9

Kazeshini

Shikai: “刈れ” — “Reap.” Dual scythes connected by chains, used for versatile mid-range attacks.

Bankai: Fushi no Kōjyō is revealed in the novel Can't Fear Your Own World; label it novel continuity. Its chain-and-recovery effect binds the combatants and prevents a straightforward death while spiritual power remains.

MANGA SHIKAINOVEL BANKAI
IKKAKU MADARAME · SQUAD 11

Hōzukimaru

Shikai: “延びろ” — “Extend.” A segmented spear/staff weapon.

Bankai: Ryūmon Hōzukimaru.

Demonstrated function: A three-part weapon whose dragon crest accumulates power as the fight continues.

Limits: The Bankai's parts can be damaged; its increasing destructive power is not instant or without risk.

YUMICHIKA AYASEGAWA · SQUAD 11

Fuji Kujaku / Ruri’iro Kujaku

Shikai: “咲け” — “Bloom” is the public release; Yumichika can use the blade's true name, Ruri’iro Kujaku, to reveal its actual ability.

Bankai: Not revealed.

Demonstrated function: The true Shikai drains spiritual energy through vine-like tendrils and blooms.

Limits: Yumichika conceals the blade's true nature because its Kidō-like ability conflicts with Squad 11's combat culture.

RANGIKU MATSUMOTO · SQUAD 10

Haineko

Shikai: “唸れ” — “Growl.” The blade disperses into ash-like particles that cut under Rangiku's control.

Bankai: Not revealed.

Continuity note: Anime-original Zanpakutō spirit material expands Haineko's characterization, but those episodes should be tagged separately from manga evidence.

IZURU KIRA · SQUAD 3

Wabisuke

Shikai: “面を上げろ” — “Raise your head.” Its hooked blade doubles the weight of anything it strikes.

Bankai: Not revealed.

Combat function: Repeated strikes burden the target and force it down; its strength is tactical control rather than a simple cutting contest.

MOMO HINAMORI · SQUAD 5

Tobiume

Shikai: “弾け” — “Snap.” Produces explosive fire-based attacks.

Bankai: Not revealed.

Evidence note: Her official character profile identifies Tobiume as her Zanpakutō; detailed power entries should be cross-checked against manga and anime depictions.

ROJŪRŌ ŌTORIBASHI · SQUAD 3

Kinshara

Shikai: “奏でろ” — “Play.” A golden whip-like weapon associated with musical combat techniques.

Bankai: Kinshara Butōdan.

Demonstrated function: Music creates illusions or sensory effects that influence the opponent's perception.

Limits: The opponent's experience of the music and the conditions for hearing it matter; avoid assuming universal mind control.

KENSEI MUGURUMA · SQUAD 9

Tachikaze

Shikai: “吹っ飛ばせ” — “Blow it away.” A compact blade that channels explosive force through strikes.

Bankai: Tekken Tachikaze.

Demonstrated function: A close-range Bankai that amplifies destructive impact through its gauntlet-like form.

SHINJI HIRAKO · SQUAD 5

Sakanade

Shikai: “倒れろ” — “Collapse.” Its scent and release effect reverse an opponent's sense of direction and orientation.

Bankai: Sakashima Yokoshima Happōfusagari.

Demonstrated function: The Bankai reverses friend/enemy perception in a surrounding area, causing affected opponents to attack former allies.

Limits: Its Bankai is dangerous to allies and unsuitable for ordinary group combat; its effects and use are shown in the novel/anime-expanded material and should be tagged by source.

NEMU KUROTSUCHI · SQUAD 12

No confirmed Zanpakutō

Nemu is an artificial soul created through Mayuri's research. Do not assign her a Zanpakutō merely because she serves as a lieutenant.
