Kara

Aliases: Kara; the Kara organization

ARTWORK SLOT — GENERATED ARTWORK DEFERRED UNTIL THE FINAL ART PHASE

Kara is a clandestine organization connected to Jigen and the Ōtsutsuki project. It uses covert networks, modified bodies and advanced technology to pursue its goals.

History & purpose

Kara operates behind the visible political order. Its central project is tied to the vessel process, the Divine Tree and Ōtsutsuki ambitions. Its precise origins and internal history are revealed gradually.

Structure & leadership

The organization distinguishes the Inner members from the Outer network. Jigen acts as its central authority; the Inners include powerful modified individuals with specialized abilities. Membership and leadership change as the organization is exposed and dismantled.

Operations & resources

Covert surveillance, recruitment, scientific experimentation, transport, combat operations and the use of advanced prosthetics and modifications.

Important members

Jigen/Isshiki Ōtsutsuki, Amado, Koji Kashin, Delta, Boro, Code, Victor and other Inners/Outer associates. Their status and allegiance vary across the chronology.

Conflicts

Primary adversarial force for Konoha and the new Team 7 during the Vessel and Kawaki developments.
