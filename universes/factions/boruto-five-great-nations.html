Five Great Shinobi Countries & Villages

Aliases: Five Great Nations; Five Kage system

ARTWORK SLOT — GENERATED ARTWORK DEFERRED UNTIL THE FINAL ART PHASE

The five major shinobi countries and their hidden villages form the central political order of the Naruto world. Their postwar cooperation shapes the relatively peaceful environment in which Boruto's generation grows up.

Members

Land of Fire / Konohagakure; Land of Wind / Sunagakure; Land of Water / Kirigakure; Land of Lightning / Kumogakure; Land of Earth / Iwagakure.

Governance

Each village is led by a Kage: Hokage, Kazekage, Mizukage, Raikage or Tsuchikage. The Five Kage coordinate on threats that exceed one nation's capacity.

History & relationships

The alliance formed during the Fourth Shinobi World War becomes a foundation for the sequel's more cooperative political climate. Rivalries and strategic interests remain, but large-scale war is no longer the default condition.

Significance

The Five Kage system embodies the difficult maintenance of peace among states with histories of conflict.
