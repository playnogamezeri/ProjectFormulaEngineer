Ōtsutsuki Clan

Aliases: Ōtsutsuki

ARTWORK SLOT — GENERATED ARTWORK DEFERRED UNTIL THE FINAL ART PHASE

The Ōtsutsuki are an ancient extraterrestrial clan whose members travel between worlds, cultivate Divine Trees and harvest chakra fruits. Their activities underpin the sequel's largest cosmological conflict.

Origins & purpose

The clan's known members pursue chakra cultivation through the Divine Tree cycle. Their history connects Kaguya, the origin of chakra on Earth, and later threats such as Momoshiki, Kinshiki, Isshiki and Code's inherited mission.

Structure & methods

Members operate in pairs or assigned roles in the broader harvesting system. Their abilities include dōjutsu, space-time movement, chakra absorption, transformation and extraordinary longevity.

Known members

Kaguya, Momoshiki, Kinshiki, Isshiki and other named figures. Do not treat every person with an Ōtsutsuki-related mark as a clan member by birth.

Conflicts & significance

The clan reframes the shinobi world's history and places local village conflicts within a much larger struggle over life, power and planetary resources.
