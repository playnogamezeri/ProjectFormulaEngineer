Konohagakure (Hidden Leaf Village)

Aliases: Hidden Leaf; Konoha

ARTWORK SLOT — GENERATED ARTWORK DEFERRED UNTIL THE FINAL ART PHASE

The principal shinobi village of the Land of Fire and the home institution of Boruto's generation. In the sequel it is modernized, economically active and governed by the Seventh Hokage before later political upheavals.

History & purpose

Founded by Hashirama Senju and Madara Uchiha as an attempt to end the era of warring clans. By Boruto's era, Konoha is a major center of postwar diplomacy, education, technology and shinobi administration.

Structure & leadership

The Hokage leads the village, supported by advisers, mission administration, shinobi ranks, the Academy, intelligence and medical services. The village's structure has evolved from a wartime military settlement toward a modern civic center.

Operations & resources

Mission assignments, border security, intelligence gathering, training, medical care, scientific research and inter-village cooperation. Scientific ninja tools increasingly affect its security and defense.

Relationships & conflicts

Member of the Five Great Shinobi Villages and allied with the other major villages after the Fourth War. Kara and Ōtsutsuki-related threats challenge its conventional defenses.

Members

Linked principal characters include Boruto, Sarada, Mitsuki and Naruto.
