Team 7 (Boruto era)

Aliases: New Team 7

ARTWORK SLOT — GENERATED ARTWORK DEFERRED UNTIL THE FINAL ART PHASE

The young shinobi team initially consisting of Boruto Uzumaki, Sarada Uchiha and Mitsuki under jōnin instructor Konohamaru Sarutobi. Its composition and leadership change as the story progresses.

Purpose & history

Team 7 carries forward the name associated with Naruto's original team while establishing a distinct identity for the new generation. Its missions build teamwork, judgment and field experience.

Structure & members

Original core: Boruto, Sarada and Mitsuki; instructor: Konohamaru. Later changes, including Kawaki's involvement, should be dated to the relevant story period rather than treated as the original formation.

Operations & relationships

Undertakes village missions, investigations and high-risk responses. The members' different backgrounds create both friction and complementary strengths.

Narrative significance

The team explores friendship, inherited expectations and the development of leadership among young shinobi.

References & continuity

Primary reference: NARUTO Official Site — Boruto. Manga continuity and television-anime continuity are identified separately where their presentation differs. Exact age, dates, status and disputed details are not inferred when the source does not establish them.
