Synopsis & Production Context

Confined behind three concentric 50-meter walls—Maria, Rose, and Sina—humanity battles mindless Titans using gas-powered ODM gear. Joining the Survey Corps alongside Mikasa Ackerman and Armin Arlert, Eren Yeager discovers his own Titan-shifting power and uncovers a geopolitical war across the sea.

02World & SettingEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Paradis Island (home to Eldians behind the three Walls) exists in an inverted-Earth world dominated by the mainland empire of Marley and nations like Hizuru.

Wall Maria / Shiganshina

Outermost wall and Eren's hometown, breached in Year 845.

Wall Rose & Wall Sina

Middle and inner walls housing Trost District, Stohess, and the royal capital.

Paradis Island

Madagascar-modeled island where King Karl Fritz isolated Eldians.

Marley & Liberio

Mainland empire confining Eldians in internment zones and deploying Warrior Titans.

Chronological Progression

From the Fall of Shiganshina and the Battle of Trost to uncovering the Royal Government, retaking Shiganshina, invading Marley's Liberio, and the global Rumbling.

06Battle & Combat SystemEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Paradis relies on gas-propelled ODM gear and Thunder Spears against Pure Titans and the Nine Titans—all linked through the atemporal Paths dimension to Ymir Fritz.

ODM Gear & Thunder Spears

3D grappling equipment with ultrahard blades targeting a Titan's nape.

The Nine Titans & 13-Year Curse

Founding, Attack, Colossal, Armored, Female, Beast, Jaw, Cart, and War Hammer Titans; shifters die 13 years after inheritance.

The Paths & Coordinate

Atemporal dimension connecting all Subjects of Ymir; Founding Titan's full power requires royal blood contact.

11Ending ExplainedEXPAND / COLLAPSE
SPOILER DISCLOSURE: Detailed resolution of Attack on Titan across anime and source material.

Eren unleashes the Rumbling, trampling 80% of humanity outside Paradis. The Alliance stops him: Levi kills Zeke and Mikasa decapitates Eren, freeing Ymir Fritz from her 2,000-year attachment to King Fritz and erasing the Power of the Titans forever. Armin and survivors serve as peace ambassadors, while a distant-future epilogue shows Shiganshina rebuilt, bombed in a new war, and a boy approaching the tree over Eren's grave.

12PurposesEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Moral of the Story: Leaving the Forest of Hatred Requires Empathy Before Retaliation

Attack on Titan evolves from monster survival horror into a warning about how inherited trauma, tribalism, and dehumanizing propaganda perpetuate endless war.

Neither Eren's apocalypse nor Zeke's erasure brings lasting peace; only ordinary people choosing dialogue across enemy lines—like the Alliance—create a chance to step out of the forest.
