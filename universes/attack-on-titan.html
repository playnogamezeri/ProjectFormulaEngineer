01 / SERIES PROFILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Attack on Titan (進撃の巨人, Shingeki no Kyojin) is a Japanese dark-fantasy action series adapted from the manga written and illustrated by Hajime Isayama. The story begins as a desperate struggle for human survival inside enormous defensive walls, then expands into a conflict about history, nationalism, inherited violence, memory and the meaning of freedom.

Creator

Hajime Isayama

Original manga

Serialized 2009–2021 in Bessatsu Shōnen Magazine; collected in 34 main volumes.

Animation studios

WIT Studio (Seasons 1–3); MAPPA (The Final Season).

Anime broadcast

2013–2023, with the concluding television special episodes released in 2023.

Primary setting

Paradis Island, Marley and the wider world.

Core premise

Humanity's conflict with Titans and the truth behind their origin.

Synopsis

For generations, the people of Paradis Island have lived inside three concentric walls, believing that humanity beyond them has been wiped out by man-eating Titans. When the Colossal Titan breaches Wall Maria, Eren Yeager witnesses the destruction of his home and the death of his mother. He joins the military with Mikasa Ackerman and Armin Arlert, determined to eradicate every Titan.

The discovery that humans can transform into Titans changes the nature of the war. Political conspiracies inside the walls lead to buried royal history; the basement of Eren's childhood home reveals that humanity outside the island survived, and that the islanders are Eldians feared and oppressed by the nation of Marley. The conflict grows from survival against monsters into a global struggle shaped by imperial history, racial persecution, military power and cycles of revenge.

02 / GEOGRAPHY & CIVILIZATION

World & Setting

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Paradis Island and the Walls

The central human population initially lives within three concentric walls: Maria, Rose and Sheena (also rendered Sina). The districts protruding from the walls attract Titans and serve as defensive choke points. The capital and royal government occupy the innermost territory. The Walls are not simply masonry: they contain countless Colossal Titans hardened in place by the Founding Titan's power.

Wall Maria

The outermost wall. Its fall at Shiganshina begins the central story.

Wall Rose

The middle defensive ring, whose apparent breach triggers the second major crisis.

Wall Sheena / Sina

The innermost ring, protecting the royal centre and capital.

The world beyond the Walls

Marley is a major military power across the sea. Its government uses Eldian subjects as a persecuted population and deploys Eldian Titan shifters as weapons. Other nations exist beyond Marley and Paradis; their politics and attitudes toward Eldians vary, but fear of the island and the strategic value of Titan power shape international relations.

Key locations

  • Shiganshina District: Eren, Mikasa and Armin's home district at the southern edge of Wall Maria.
  • Trost District: Site of the first major counteroffensive after the initial breach.
  • Utgard Castle: A refuge during the crisis involving the apparent breach of Wall Rose.
  • Underground Chapel: The Reiss family's hidden chamber, where the Founding Titan's succession is contested.
  • Basement of the Yeager home: The evidence that overturns the islanders' understanding of the world.
  • Liberio: Marleyan internment zone and site of Eren's attack during the festival.
  • Fort Salta: The final battlefield during the attempt to stop the Rumbling.
03 / COMPLETE NARRATIVE

Story & Chronology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

The chronology below follows the major historical and narrative developments. Dates inside the fictional calendar are given where they are central to the story; not every event has a precisely established date.

Approximately 2,000 years before the main story — Ymir Fritz

Ymir obtains the power of the Titans after coming into contact with an unexplained source of life. King Fritz enslaves her and uses her power to expand Eldia. After her death, her power is divided among her descendants and eventually becomes the Nine Titans. Ymir remains bound in the Paths, continuing to shape Titan bodies.

Rise and fall of the Eldian Empire

Eldia uses Titan power to dominate rival peoples, including Marley. Internal conflict among the royal families and Titan inheritors weakens the empire.

Great Titan War and the retreat to Paradis

King Karl Fritz withdraws to Paradis with many Eldians, constructs the Walls using Colossal Titans and threatens the outside world with the Rumbling as a deterrent. Marley gains control of several of the Nine Titans.

845 — Fall of Wall Maria

The Colossal Titan and Armored Titan breach the outer defenses. Eren's mother Carla is killed. Eren, Mikasa and Armin flee, and the displaced population is forced into the remaining territory.

850 — Cadet training and the Battle of Trost

Eren, Mikasa, Armin and their peers graduate from the 104th Cadet Corps. Eren's Titan transformation is revealed during the battle for Trost, making him both a possible weapon and a threat to the military.

850 — Female Titan and Clash of the Titans

The Survey Corps encounters the Female Titan, later revealed as Annie Leonhart. Reiner Braun and Bertolt Hoover are exposed as the Armored and Colossal Titans. Ymir's Titan identity and the royal bloodline mystery also emerge.

850 — Royal Government and Uprising

The Survey Corps challenges the false monarchy. Historia Reiss is revealed as the legitimate royal heir and becomes queen. The Reiss family's connection to the Founding Titan and the transfer of power from Frieda Reiss to Grisha Yeager are uncovered.

850 — Return to Shiganshina

The Survey Corps retakes the district and reaches the Yeager basement. Erwin Smith dies in the operation; Armin survives after receiving the Colossal Titan from Bertolt. Grisha's journals reveal the outside world and Marley.

851 — The sea

The surviving Scouts reach the ocean. Eren recognizes that the people across the sea are not simply the final obstacle to freedom; they are human beings who may see Paradis as an enemy.

854 — Marley and the Liberio raid

The narrative shifts to Marley and introduces Gabi Braun, Falco Grice and the Warrior candidates. Eren infiltrates Liberio and attacks during Willy Tybur's declaration of war. The Survey Corps extracts him, but Sasha is killed during the return.

854 — Yeagerist uprising and the Rumbling

Eren and Zeke pursue their separate plans involving royal blood and the Founding Titan. Eren reaches Ymir in the Paths and gains access to the Founder's full power. He releases the Wall Titans and begins the Rumbling.

854 — Alliance and final battle

Former enemies from Paradis and Marley form an alliance to stop Eren. At Fort Salta, the alliance confronts Eren's transformed Founding Titan. Mikasa kills Eren, ending the immediate Rumbling and freeing Ymir from her attachment to King Fritz.

After the battle — Titan power disappears

Those transformed by the Titan curse return to human form. The power of the Titans vanishes. The surviving characters attempt diplomacy, while Paradis later develops militarily and the conflict between nations remains unresolved.

04 / STORY STRUCTURE

Major Story Arcs

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Fall of Shiganshina

The Colossal and Armored Titans breach Wall Maria; Eren's motivation is forged by loss.

104th Training & Battle of Trost

The new cadets enter military service and Eren's transformation changes humanity's strategy.

Female Titan

The Scouts investigate a Titan shifter operating inside their ranks; Annie is exposed and crystallizes herself.

Clash of the Titans

Wall Rose's crisis reveals Reiner and Bertolt; Ymir's past and the Beast Titan become central.

Uprising

The Scouts overthrow the corrupt royal government and restore Historia to the throne.

Return to Shiganshina

The battle against the Warriors leads to the basement revelation and a devastating choice over the serum.

Marley

The story reframes the conflict through the lives of Eldians in Marley and Eren's infiltration.

War for Paradis

The attack on Liberio, the Yeagerists' rise and the struggle over Zeke's plan divide former allies.

Rumbling / Final Battle

The alliance attempts to stop Eren's global destruction and end the Titan power.

05 / CAST & INDIVIDUAL PROFILES

Character Database

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Browse the principal cast below. Each entry provides a concise profile on this pilot page; the architecture is designed to support dedicated character pages as the archive expands.

PROTAGONIST · SURVEY CORPS

Eren Yeager

Initially driven by revenge against Titans, Eren becomes the inheritor of the Attack, Founding and War Hammer Titans. After learning the world's history, he chooses the Rumbling, killing most of humanity outside Paradis. Mikasa kills him, ending his life and the immediate Titan curse.

PROTAGONIST · SURVEY CORPS

Mikasa Ackerman

An elite soldier devoted to protecting Eren. Her Ackerman heritage gives her extraordinary combat ability. Despite her love for Eren, she ultimately kills him to stop the Rumbling; her choice helps Ymir release her fixation on King Fritz.

PROTAGONIST · COMMANDER / COLOSSAL TITAN

Armin Arlert

A strategist whose curiosity and empathy shape the group. He inherits the Colossal Titan after the Return to Shiganshina battle and later becomes a leading voice in the effort to negotiate peace.

SURVEY CORPS · CAPTAIN

Levi Ackerman

Humanity's strongest soldier, known for exceptional precision and discipline. He survives the final battle, fulfills his promise to help end the Beast Titan's role in the conflict and sees the fallen Scouts in his final salute.

SURVEY CORPS · COMMANDER

Erwin Smith

The Survey Corps commander who pursues the truth beyond the Walls despite immense losses. He leads the suicidal charge against the Beast Titan and dies after Levi chooses to save Armin instead.

SURVEY CORPS · RESEARCHER / COMMANDER

Hange Zoë

A researcher fascinated by Titans who becomes commander after Erwin. Hange sacrifices themself to delay the Rumbling and allow the flying boat to take off.

ROYAL HEIR · QUEEN OF PARADIS

Historia Reiss

Born Historia Reiss and raised under the name Christa Lenz, she rejects the role others prescribe for her and becomes queen. She refuses to inherit the Beast Titan from Zeke during the final political crisis.

WARRIOR · ARMORED TITAN

Reiner Braun

A Marleyan Warrior who infiltrates the 104th Cadet Corps and inherits the Armored Titan. Torn between duty, guilt and fractured identity, he ultimately joins the alliance against Eren.

WARRIOR · COLOSSAL TITAN

Bertolt Hoover

The quiet Warrior who inherits the Colossal Titan and participates in the fall of Wall Maria. Armin inherits his Titan after the Scouts capture him during the return to Shiganshina.

WARRIOR · FEMALE TITAN

Annie Leonhart

A reserved Marleyan Warrior who infiltrates the military and possesses the Female Titan. After years in crystal stasis, she returns and eventually joins the alliance to stop Eren.

WARRIOR · BEAST TITAN

Zeke Yeager

Eren's half-brother and a Marleyan Warrior who inherits the Beast Titan. He supports an euthanasia plan intended to end Eldian reproduction; Eren betrays that plan and uses Zeke's royal blood to reach the Founder.

WARRIOR · JAW TITAN

Porco Galliard

Marleyan Warrior who inherits the Jaw Titan from Ymir. He dies during the battle for Shiganshina after allowing Falco to consume him and inherit the Jaw Titan.

WARRIOR CANDIDATE · JAW TITAN

Falco Grice

A compassionate Warrior candidate who becomes a Pure Titan after ingesting Zeke's spinal fluid, then inherits the Jaw Titan by consuming Porco. His Titan develops the ability to fly.

WARRIOR CANDIDATE

Gabi Braun

Reiner's cousin and a fiercely patriotic Marleyan candidate. After killing Sasha, she confronts the reality of the hatred she has inherited and becomes part of the effort to stop the Rumbling.

104TH CADET · SURVEY CORPS

Jean Kirstein

A practical soldier who initially wants a safe life in the Military Police. He becomes a dependable leader and joins the alliance against Eren despite his attachment to Paradis.

104TH CADET · SURVEY CORPS

Connie Springer

A loyal soldier from Ragako whose village is transformed by Zeke. His mother remains a Titan until the Titan power disappears after Eren's defeat.

104TH CADET · SURVEY CORPS

Sasha Blouse

A skilled archer and hunter whose warmth makes her a beloved member of the group. Gabi kills her during the return from Liberio.

ELDIAN RESTORATIONIST · FORMER SCOUT

Grisha Yeager

Eren and Zeke's father. A former restorationist from Marley, he inherits the Attack Titan, reaches the Walls and later takes the Founding Titan from Frieda Reiss. Under future Eren's influence through the Paths, he kills most of the Reiss family and passes both Titans to young Eren.

ORIGIN OF TITAN POWER

Ymir Fritz

The first human to obtain the Titan power. Enslaved by King Fritz, she remains psychologically bound to him after death and continues shaping Titan bodies in the Paths. Mikasa's decision to kill Eren helps Ymir let go of that bond.

ELDIAN ROYAL FAMILY · FOUNDER

King Karl Fritz

The 145th King of Eldia who retreats to Paradis, creates the Walls and imposes a vow renouncing war on the Founding Titan's inheritors. His ideology shapes the false peace inside the Walls.

TYBUR FAMILY · WAR HAMMER TITAN

Willy Tybur

A Marleyan noble who publicly reveals the truth of the island threat and declares war on Paradis. Eren kills him during the Liberio attack; his sister Lara is revealed as the War Hammer Titan.

104TH CADET · FEMALE TITAN SUPPORT

Ymir

A former Eldian orphan who lived as a cult figure before being transformed into a Pure Titan and wandering for decades. She inherits the Jaw Titan by consuming Marcel Galliard and later sacrifices herself after returning to Marley with Reiner and Bertolt.

06 / SUPERNATURAL & BIOLOGICAL SYSTEM

Titan Powers

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

The Nine Titans

Intelligent Titan powers pass between Eldian inheritors. A shifter's term is limited by the Curse of Ymir, which gives an inheritor thirteen years after receiving a Titan power. The Nine are connected through the Paths and ultimately originate from Ymir Fritz.

Other Titan categories and mechanics

Pure Titans

Mindless Eldians transformed through Titan spinal fluid. They instinctively seek humans and can be restored only by consuming a shifter.

Abnormal Titans

Pure Titans whose behavior differs from the usual pattern, sometimes moving unpredictably or targeting unusual groups.

Paths

A metaphysical network connecting Subjects of Ymir across time and space, through which memories and Titan bodies are transmitted.

Hardening

A Titan ability that creates hardened material for armor, weapons, structures and the Walls.

Ackerman abilities

The Ackermans possess extraordinary combat instincts and physical capability connected to Titan science, without becoming ordinary Titan shifters.

Curse of Ymir

A shifter's lifespan is limited to thirteen years after inheriting one of the Nine Titans.

07 / POLITICAL & MILITARY STRUCTURES

Nations & Factions

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Paradis Island

Home of the Walls and the Eldian population central to the story. Its military includes the Survey Corps, Garrison Regiment and Military Police Brigade.

Marley

A powerful state that controls most of the Nine Titans and uses Eldian Warriors as military assets while segregating Eldian civilians.

Survey Corps

The branch that conducts expeditions beyond the Walls and investigates the Titans. Its emblem is the Wings of Freedom.

Garrison Regiment

Responsible for defending the Walls, maintaining their defenses and responding to Titan incursions.

Military Police Brigade

Maintains order in the interior and protects the royal government; its leadership becomes deeply implicated in the regime's corruption.

Marleyan Warrior Unit

Trains Eldian candidates and deploys Titan shifters in Marley's wars.

Eldian Restorationists

A clandestine movement seeking to restore Eldian sovereignty. Grisha and Dina are involved before their capture.

Yeagerists

A radical movement supporting Eren's plan and advocating Paradis-first nationalism through military takeover.

Alliance

Former enemies from Paradis and Marley who cooperate to stop the Rumbling.

Relationships

Eren · Grisha · Zeke

A family shaped by inherited ideology. Eren manipulates Grisha's past through the Paths, while Zeke tries to impose his own solution on Eldian suffering.

10 / INTERPRETATION

Themes & Analysis

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Freedom and confinement

The Walls first represent physical protection and later expose the limits of a freedom built on ignorance, inherited memory and exclusion.

Inherited hatred

Political narratives teach each generation to fear and punish the next. Individuals struggle to escape histories they did not create.

Memory and identity

Memories can be altered, inherited and manipulated. The series asks how much identity depends on personal experience versus constructed history.

War and dehumanization

Both sides describe the other as monstrous, allowing violence against civilians to be rationalized as necessary survival.

Cycles of violence

Revenge can feel like justice to those who suffer, but it reproduces the conditions that make future revenge seem inevitable.

Responsibility and choice

Characters act under coercive histories and terrible constraints, but the story continues to ask what responsibility remains when choices are limited.

11 / FULL SPOILER CONCLUSION

Ending Explained

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL THE COMPLETE ENDING

Eren uses the Founding Titan to initiate the Rumbling, unleashing the Colossal Titans hidden within the Walls. His stated objective and his private motivations are complex: he wants to protect his friends and Paradis, but he also acts on a destructive drive toward the freedom he imagined. The Rumbling kills approximately 80 percent of humanity outside the island before the alliance stops him.

Armin, Mikasa, Levi, Reiner, Annie, Jean, Connie, Pieck, Falco, Gabi and their allies confront Eren at Fort Salta. Mikasa enters the Titan's mouth and decapitates Eren, ending his life. In the Paths, Ymir witnesses Mikasa choosing to kill the person she loves rather than allow mass destruction to continue. This helps Ymir release her attachment to King Fritz. The power of the Titans disappears, and people transformed into Titans return to human form.

Armin and the survivors become part of a diplomatic effort to prevent another war. Paradis, however, becomes increasingly militarized under the Yeagerists. The closing images show conflict returning in later generations; much later, a boy and dog approach a huge tree resembling the one where Ymir first encountered the source of Titan power. The ending does not confirm that the same Titan curse returns, but it deliberately leaves open the possibility that the cycle of power and conflict may begin again.

What the ending resolves

  • Eren is stopped and the Rumbling ends.
  • The Titan power disappears from the world at that moment.
  • Ymir's long obedience to King Fritz ends.
  • The surviving Eldians and Marleyans face the difficult work of rebuilding trust.
  • The political future of Paradis and the wider world remains unstable.

What the ending does not promise

It does not guarantee permanent peace, erase the consequences of the Rumbling, or establish that humanity has escaped violence. The distant-future imagery emphasizes that historical cycles can recur even after a supernatural power disappears.
