01 / SERIES PROFILE

Overview

In a world where gates connect Earth to monster-filled dungeons, low-ranked hunter Sung Jinwoo survives a deadly double dungeon and gains access to a mysterious leveling system. He grows stronger while uncovering the origin of the gates and the larger conflict behind them.

Creator / source

Chugong (original web novel); webtoon adaptation illustrated by DUBU (REDICE Studio)

02 / SETTING

World & setting

Hunters awaken supernatural abilities and form guilds to raid dungeons. Governments and ranking systems manage the threat, while gates and monarch-level entities reveal that the crisis is part of a much older war.

EXPANDED RESEARCH / SERIES DOSSIER

World, mechanics and critical analysis

Chugong’s fantasy action story follows Sung Jinwoo, initially known as the weakest hunter, who survives a catastrophic double dungeon and gains access to a system that allows him to grow through quests and level progression. His transformation changes the balance of power among hunters and nations, but it also draws him into a conflict whose scale exceeds the public understanding of gates and dungeons.

World structure and institutions

The modern world is disrupted by gates connecting Earth to monster-filled dungeons. Hunters are awakened individuals who can enter these spaces and are ranked by measured ability. Guilds, national hunter associations and international interests organize the response to dungeon threats, while dungeon breaks expose the limits of institutional preparation.

Systems, rules and limitations

The System presents Jinwoo with quests, levels, stats, skills, inventory and class progression. Most hunters do not share his ability to continuously level up. Mana, ranks, dungeon grades, artifacts and shadow extraction operate under different rules. The System’s origin and purpose become increasingly important, so it should not be treated as a neutral game interface.

Factions and organizational dynamics

Korean hunter associations, major guilds, international hunter organizations and powerful individuals compete over resources and influence. Jinwoo’s relationship with the Korean Hunter Association changes as his strength becomes difficult to classify. The Monarchs and Rulers introduce a higher cosmological conflict beyond ordinary hunter institutions.

Themes and adaptation continuity

Solo Leveling explores growth, isolation, responsibility, family, power and the cost of becoming indispensable. Its game-like progression is a narrative mechanism, but the emotional stakes come from relationships and the consequences of overwhelming strength. The web novel, webtoon, anime and sequel material should be catalogued separately; the anime’s pacing and visual adaptation should not be mistaken for the full source text.
