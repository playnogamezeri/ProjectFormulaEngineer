Synopsis & Production Context

In Arthurian-inspired Britannia, Princess Elizabeth Liones seeks the Seven Deadly Sins—seven legendary knights framed for treason a decade earlier—starting with Boar Hat tavern owner Meliodas. After liberating Liones from corrupt Holy Knights, the Sins face the unsealed Ten Commandments of the Demon Clan, unraveling Meliodas and Elizabeth's 3,000-year divine curses in a New Holy War.

Purgatory

Harsh temporal wasteland where Ban rescues Meliodas's emotions.

03Story & ChronologyEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Chronological Progression

Covers the Holy Knight Saga (S1), anime-original Signs of Holy War bridge (4 eps), Ten Commandments Saga (S2), New Holy War & Demon King Saga (S3–4), Cursed by Light (canon film), Grudge of Edinburgh, and Four Knights of the Apocalypse.

ANIME VS. SOURCE CONTINUITY DIFFERENCES: Season 1 alters Gowther's introduction (omitting Pelliot's arc), cuts Oslo's Capital of the Dead debut, and spares Howzer and Dawn Roar from Dreyfus's attacks—changes Season 2 largely ignores to realign with manga canon. Signs of Holy War (4 eps) is anime-original written by Nakaba Suzuki, and Prisoners of the Sky (2018) is non-canon, while Cursed by Light (2021) is canon.

06Battle & Combat SystemEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Power is quantified via Balor's Magical Eye (Magic + Strength + Spirit) and shaped by Sacred Treasures, Demon King Commandments, and Supreme Deity Graces.

Power Levels & Sacred Treasures

Balor's Eye sums Magic, Strength, and Spirit; seven bespoke Sacred Treasures unlock each Sin's full magical output.

Ten Commandments & Four Graces

Absolute Demon King decrees (Piety, Love, Truth, etc.) balanced by the Archangels' four Graces (Sunshine, Flash, Tornado, Ocean).

Moral of the Story: No Sin Is Too Heavy to Redeem When You Fight for Someone You Love

The Seven Deadly Sins pairs Arthurian spectacle with a story about outcasts earning a second chance.

Even curses written by gods can be broken by comrades who refuse to abandon one another.
