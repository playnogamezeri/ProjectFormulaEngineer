02 / SETTING

World & setting

A modern world conceals the Noblesse, an ancient society of powerful beings, alongside human organizations conducting experiments and pursuing power. The Union and noble clans influence the balance between humans and supernatural factions.

EXPANDED RESEARCH / SERIES DOSSIER

World, mechanics and critical analysis

The supernatural action series follows Cadis Etrama Di Raizel, a powerful noble who awakens after centuries of sleep and attempts to adapt to modern life. His connection with Frankenstein and the students around them draws him into conflicts involving modified humans, secret organizations and rival nobles. The contrast between Raizel’s quiet dignity and the modern world’s casual routines is a recurring source of humor and character development.

World structure and institutions

The setting is contemporary, but hidden noble society and covert human organizations operate behind ordinary institutions. The Union conducts experiments and manipulates human subjects, while noble houses maintain ancient hierarchies and political traditions. Modern technology and old supernatural authority coexist, often uneasily.

Systems, rules and limitations

Nobles possess extraordinary physical and spiritual abilities, with power shaped by lineage, individual traits and authority. Blood-based techniques, soul weapons, contracts and specialized abilities have distinct rules. Modified humans and Union experiments represent a different path to power, often involving artificial enhancement and physical cost.

Factions and organizational dynamics

The noble houses, the Union, modified human teams and the protagonists’ human allies form the main faction structure. Frankenstein’s loyalty and Raizel’s status create a relationship between personal service and political authority. The Union’s internal ranks and experiments change as its leadership is challenged.

Themes and adaptation continuity

Noblesse explores loyalty, dignity, sacrifice, friendship across social boundaries and the responsibility of overwhelming power. Its central figures often choose restraint even when capable of domination. The webtoon is the primary narrative source; the anime adaptations include an OVA and television series, and their coverage and sequencing should be distinguished from the full comic chronology.
