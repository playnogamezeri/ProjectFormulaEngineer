Synopsis & Production Context

Passed among reluctant relatives for seeing spirits invisible to others, Takashi Natsume finally finds warmth with foster parents Shigeru and Tōko Fujiwara. After accidentally unsealing wolf-daiyōkai Madara ('Nyanko-sensei') and inheriting Reiko's Book of Friends, Natsume spends his days returning bound names while navigating exorcist clans and human friendships.

02World & SettingEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Set in a rural Kyushu town modeled on Hitoyoshi and Kuma District in Kumamoto Prefecture, where forests, shrines, and rivers teem with yōkai unseen by most humans, policed secretly by exorcists (haraiya).

Fujiwara Residence (Hitoyoshi-inspired)

Loving foster home of Shigeru, Tōko, Takashi, and Nyanko-sensei.

Yowake High School

School where Natsume befriends Tanuma, Taki, Nishimura, and Kitamoto.

Mountain Shrines & Eastern Forest

Habitats of the Dog's Circle and regional yōkai.

Matoba Estate

Headquarters of the ruthless Matoba exorcist clan.

03Story & ChronologyEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Chronological Progression

Episodic healing stories of returning names interweave with recurring arcs featuring actor-exorcist Shūichi Natori, Seiji Matoba's coercion, Natsume's childhood house, the Sealed Room, and Soranome—the very first yōkai Reiko bound.

ANIME VS. SOURCE CONTINUITY DIFFERENCES: Faithful adaptation that occasionally reorders chapters (e.g., S6 Ep. 3 occurs chronologically after Ep. 11), changes Natsume's eye color from green to golden-brown, softens his early irritability, and expands Jun Sasada's recurring appearances.

12PurposesEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Moral of the Story: Kindness Turns Even Fleeting Encounters into Lifelong Warmth

Natsume's Book of Friends shows that empathy—not dominance—heals the loneliness of being different.

By returning names rather than hoarding power, Natsume proves that letting go with respect builds deeper bonds than possession ever could.
