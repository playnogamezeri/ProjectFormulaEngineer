Synopsis & Production Context

Known as the 'Glory Textbook,' pro captain Ye Xiu refuses to commercialize his image and is forced to retire from Team Excellent Era, surrendering his iconic Battle Mage account One Autumn Leaf. Working nights at Happy Internet Cafe, he logs into Glory's fresh 10th Server with an Unspecialized character, Lord Grim, and an unfinished Silver weapon—the Myriad Manifestation Umbrella—assembling a new roster to fight back to the Glory Pro Alliance summit.

02World & SettingEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Set in China's hyper-competitive professional esports ecosystem built around the PC MMORPG Glory, where guilds farm Wild Boss materials in-game to fund 20-team Pro Alliance and Challenger League franchises.

Cafe owned by Chen Guo that becomes Team Happy's grassroots headquarters.

Glory 10th Server & Heavenly Domain

In-game servers where guilds compete for dungeon records and Wild Bosses.

Glory Pro Alliance Arenas

Professional esports stadiums hosting regular-season and playoff matches.

Zurich World Invitational (Novel)

International tournament stage led by Ye Xiu in the novel's finale.

06Battle & Combat SystemEXPAND / COLLAPSE

All combat occurs strictly inside the PC MMORPG Glory via mouse-and-keyboard execution—no real-world magic exists outside the game.

24 Subclasses & Unspecialized Play

Six base classes branch into 24 specializations; Unspecialized skips Level 20 advancement to combine 120 low-level skills via the 12-form Myriad Manifestation Umbrella.

Silver Equipment & Pro APM

Custom Silver gear forged from Wild Boss drops meets high-APM animation canceling and Master Tactician macro-strategy.

12PurposesEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Moral of the Story: Glory Is Not a Solo Game—It's Never Too Late to Start Over

The King's Avatar captures the discipline, camaraderie, and joy of competitive gaming without a single shortcut.

When the spotlight fades, a true champion simply logs back in at Level 1 and builds a team that belongs to the game.
