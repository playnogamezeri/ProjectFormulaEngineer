Synopsis & Production Context

In an alternate 2017 a.t.b., Japan has been conquered by the Holy Britannian Empire and renamed Area 11. Exiled prince Lelouch—sworn to destroy Britannia for his mother's murder and sister Nunnally's future—gains the eye-power Geass and becomes the masked revolutionary Zero, clashing with his childhood friend Suzaku Kururugi.

02World & SettingEXPAND / COLLAPSE

An alternate-history Earth divided among three superpowers—the Holy Britannian Empire, the Chinese Federation, and the E.U.—fueled by Japan's Sakuradite mineral reserves and Knightmare Frame mecha.

Area 11 (Tokyo Settlement & Shinjuku)

Conquered Japan under Britannian viceroyalty, rich in Sakuradite mines.

Ashford Academy

Britannian school serving as Lelouch and Nunnally's civilian cover.

Kamine Island & C's World

Ancient Thought Elevator ruins linking to humanity's collective unconsciousness.

Pendragon & Damocles

Imperial Britannian capital and Schneizel's F.L.E.I.J.A.-armed sky fortress.

03Story & ChronologyEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Chronological Progression

R1 charts Zero's rise, the Black Knights' formation, the tragic SAZ Massacre, and the First Black Rebellion. R2 escalates to the Chinese Federation coup, the United Federation of Nations, the Second Battle of Tokyo, Charles's Ragnarök Connection, and the Zero Requiem.

ANIME VS. SOURCE CONTINUITY DIFFERENCES: The 2017–2018 compilation film trilogy alters TV continuity for Lelouch of the Re;surrection (2019) and Rozé of the Recapture (2024): Mao's arc is cut, Diethard shoots Villetta instead of Shirley, and Shirley Fenette survives to smuggle Lelouch's body to C.C. for resurrection.

10Themes & AnalysisEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Means vs. Ends in Revolution

Questions whether peace built on deception, terrorism, and self-sacrifice can ever justify its human cost.

Collective Memory vs. Forward Motion

Rejects Charles's stagnant world of truth in favor of humanity's imperfect, forward-looking hope.

11Ending ExplainedEXPAND / COLLAPSE
SPOILER DISCLOSURE: Detailed resolution of Code Geass across anime and source material.

In the Zero Requiem, Lelouch conquers the world as a tyrannical Emperor to absorb all global hatred, then has Suzaku—wearing Zero's mask—publicly assassinate him. Lelouch's death frees the world to unite under Empress Nunnally, while Suzaku bears Zero's identity as lifelong atonement.

12PurposesEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Moral of the Story: Those Who Shoot Must Be Prepared to Be Shot

Code Geass refuses to let its antihero wield absolute power without paying the full moral price.

From Lelouch's opening maxim to the Zero Requiem, the series insists that leadership demands accountability—and that true peace cannot be coerced through fear alone, but must be entrusted to those willing to live and rebuild.
