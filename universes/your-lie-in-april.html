Synopsis & Production Context

Drilled relentlessly by his abusive, terminally ill mother Saki, 14-year-old Kōsei Arima earned the nickname 'Human Metronome' before her death left him psychologically unable to hear his own piano. Two years later, childhood friend Tsubaki introduces him to violinist Kaori Miyazono, whose audacious performances pull Kōsei back to music—even as Kaori hides a terminal illness and a secret motive.

02World & SettingEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Set in contemporary Nerima Ward, Tokyo, blending everyday junior-high life and sports clubs with the high-pressure world of Japanese classical music competitions.

Hospital Rooftop & Courtyard

Quiet sanctuary where Kōsei visits Kaori as her health declines.

04Major Story ArcsEXPAND / COLLAPSE
Spring Violin Preliminaries (Ep. 1–4 · Vols. 1–2)

Kaori recruits Kōsei as her accompanist; he finishes Saint-Saëns despite losing hearing mid-piece.

Maihou Piano Competition (Ep. 5–11 · Vols. 3–5)

Kōsei faces rivals Takeshi and Emi, dedicating Chopin's Wrong Note Étude to Kaori.

Summer Gala & Saki's Ghost (Ep. 12–14 · Vols. 6–7)

Mentored by Hiroko Seto, Kōsei plays Love's Sorrow solo and makes peace with his mother.

Autumn Festival & Nagi's Duet (Ep. 15–18 · Vols. 8–9)

Kōsei teaches Nagi Aiza and performs a four-hands duet that inspires Kaori to attempt surgery.

Winter Finals & Spring Breeze (Ep. 19–22 · Vols. 10–11)

Kōsei performs Chopin's First Ballade as Kaori passes away, leaving behind her confession letter.

06Battle & Combat SystemEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Zero supernatural magic: the series centers on classical piano and violin repertoire, competition judging vs. emotional expression, and musical dialogue.

Canonical Classical Repertoire

Real works by Chopin (Ballade No. 1, Études), Saint-Saëns, Beethoven (Kreutzer), Rachmaninoff (Love's Sorrow), and Debussy anchor every arc.

Competition Orthodoxy vs. Expression

Juries penalize Kaori for breaking tempo markings while audiences are moved—teaching Kōsei to transcend robotic 'Human Metronome' perfection.
