01 / SERIES PROFILE

Overview

Kōsei Arima is a piano prodigy who loses the ability to hear his own playing after his mother's death. His life begins to change when he meets violinist Kaori Miyazono, whose expressive approach to music challenges his fear, grief and relationship with performance.

Creator / source

Naoshi Arakawa

02 / SETTING

World & setting

A contemporary Japanese setting centered on school life, music lessons, recitals and youth competitions. The story treats classical performance as both a technical discipline and an emotional form of communication.
