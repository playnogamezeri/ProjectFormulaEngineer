01 / ORGANIZATION PROFILE

Profile & Purpose

The Imperial Army is the Empire's conventional military power, responsible for defending territory, conducting campaigns and suppressing threats to imperial authority. It includes ordinary troops as well as specialized commands and elite formations.

Purpose

Defend the Empire, maintain territorial control and enforce the Emperor's authority through military power.

Mission

Deploy soldiers across the Empire, guard strategic locations, fight external and internal enemies and support the regime's security operations.

02 / HISTORICAL FOUNDATION

Origin

The army is part of the Empire's long-standing state structure. Its history is tied to the Empire's centuries of expansion and its reliance on military strength and the Imperial Arms.

03 / ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE

Structure & Members

The Empire's military includes senior generals, regional forces, garrisons, palace defense and specialized units. General Budo is identified as a leading military commander and head of the army; Esdeath commands major forces and later leads the Jaegers. The precise chain of command varies by assignment.

Notable members / associated figures

Notable military figures include General Budo, General Esdeath, Najenda before her defection, and numerous unnamed officers and soldiers. The list is illustrative rather than exhaustive.

04 / NARRATIVE RECORD

Story History

The army sustains the Empire's territorial authority and fights rebels and other threats. Defections by experienced officers weaken the regime. As the Revolutionary Army advances, Imperial forces are drawn into the final defense of the Capital and are ultimately defeated with the old regime.

05 / CONTEXT & INTERPRETATION

Story Significance

The army represents the Empire's institutional capacity for organized force. Its ordinary soldiers should be distinguished from Teigu-wielding special squads and the police-like Capital Guard.

Continuity and classification note

The Jaegers and Wild Hunt are separate special formations; they should not be used as synonyms for the conventional Imperial Army. The Capital Guard has policing duties within the wider security structure.
