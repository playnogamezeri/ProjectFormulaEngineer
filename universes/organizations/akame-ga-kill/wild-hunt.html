01 / ORGANIZATION PROFILE

Profile & Purpose

Wild Hunt is a secret-police group formed under Syura, the Prime Minister's son. Although presented as an Imperial security force, its members abuse their authority and become a threat to civilians and even other Imperial factions.

Purpose

Officially reinforce internal security and compensate for the Empire's losses among its special forces.

Mission

Operate as a secret-police unit and pursue perceived enemies of the Empire. In practice, Syura and his associates exploit their position to terrorize people and indulge in violence.

02 / HISTORICAL FOUNDATION

Origin

Syura establishes Wild Hunt during the Empire's escalating conflict with Night Raid, presenting it as a response to the losses and pressure faced by the Jaegers.

03 / ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE

Structure & Members

Syura is the founder and leader. The unit is composed of dangerous Teigu users and operates under the protection of the Prime Minister's political network. Its official status becomes untenable after its abuses are exposed.

Notable members / associated figures

Named members include Syura, Champ, Cosmina, Dorothea, Enshin and Izou. The story's anime adaptation reduces the group's presence compared with the manga.

04 / NARRATIVE RECORD

Story History

Wild Hunt's abuses provoke opposition. Run investigates Syura's activities and reports them to Esdeath, who pushes for the unit's disbandment. Despite the official order, members continue operating separately. Night Raid later confronts the remaining group and kills its key members.

05 / CONTEXT & INTERPRETATION

Story Significance

Wild Hunt shows how a regime's security apparatus can become a source of danger when authority is unchecked. It also creates conflict within the Empire, demonstrating that the state's factions do not share a single moral code.

Continuity and classification note

Wild Hunt is a specific secret-police group, not a generic name for all Imperial police. Keep its manga and anime appearances distinct.
