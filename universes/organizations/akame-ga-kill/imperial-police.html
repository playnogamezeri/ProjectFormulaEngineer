01 / ORGANIZATION PROFILE

Profile & Purpose

The Imperial Police label refers to law-enforcement personnel serving the Empire, particularly the forces tasked with maintaining security in the Capital. The story shows these duties embedded within a corrupt political order.

Purpose

Maintain public security, enforce imperial law and protect the Capital's institutions.

Mission

Patrol streets, investigate crimes, capture suspects and assist with security operations. Under corrupt leadership, law enforcement can also be directed against political opponents and vulnerable civilians.

02 / HISTORICAL FOUNDATION

Origin

The police and Capital Guard form part of the Empire's established administrative and military security structure. The narrative depicts their personnel and functions but does not provide a complete institutional founding history.

03 / ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE

Structure & Members

The Capital Guard is shown as a police-like force. Ogre serves as its captain before his death; Seryu Ubiquitous is a member before transferring to the Jaegers. Other policing and security bodies operate under the Empire's broader authority.

Notable members / associated figures

Named figures associated with the Capital Guard include Ogre and Seryu Ubiquitous. This is not a complete police roster.

04 / NARRATIVE RECORD

Story History

The Capital Guard's role is visible in the Empire's daily enforcement system. Ogre is killed by Night Raid, and Seryu later joins the Jaegers. The Empire's police and security functions are overtaken by political corruption and increasingly militarized responses to rebellion.

05 / CONTEXT & INTERPRETATION

Story Significance

This entry distinguishes ordinary law enforcement from elite assassination and special-police formations. It illustrates how public-safety institutions can lose legitimacy when they serve the ruling elite rather than the public.

Continuity and classification note

The source material does not establish a single, fully detailed organization formally named 'Imperial Police' with a complete roster. This page treats the label as the encyclopedia's category for Imperial law-enforcement forces, especially the Capital Guard.
