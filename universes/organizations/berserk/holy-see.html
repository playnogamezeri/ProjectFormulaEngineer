01 / ORGANIZATION PROFILE

Profile & Purpose

The Holy See is a major religious institution in the world of Berserk. Its doctrines influence public understanding of evil, heresy and supernatural events, while its authorities exercise political and social power through the clergy and the Inquisition.

Purpose

A powerful religious institution whose doctrine, clergy and inquisitorial authority shape the Conviction arc.

Classification

RELIGIOUS INSTITUTION · FAITH & AUTHORITY. This archive distinguishes political institutions, mercenary forces and later supernatural military formations.

02 / HISTORICAL FOUNDATION

Origin

The institution predates the Conviction storyline and is already influential across the human world. The narrative reveals its structure and beliefs through the characters and events encountered during that arc; a complete founding chronology is not established in the story.

03 / ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE

Structure & Members

The Holy See includes senior religious authorities, clergy, local churches and inquisitorial personnel. Mozgus is a prominent inquisitor encountered in the Conviction arc. The institution's teachings should not be treated as a complete or reliable explanation of the world's actual supernatural cosmology.

Notable members and associated figures

Notable figures associated with the Conviction storyline include Mozgus and his six executioner attendants, as well as other clergy and believers. The page does not claim a complete roster of the global institution.

04 / NARRATIVE RECORD

Story History

The Holy See's influence is visible in the fear and religious fervor surrounding the Tower of Conviction and the refugee crisis. The Inquisition's methods and the public's response to perceived heresy become central to the arc's examination of faith and cruelty.

05 / CONTEXT & INTERPRETATION

Story Significance

The Holy See explores the relationship between belief, institutional power, fear and persecution. The story contrasts the institution's claims about evil with the more complicated supernatural reality revealed to the reader.

Continuity note

This dossier follows the manga's broader narrative. The 1997, Golden Age film/Memorial Edition and 2016–2017 anime adaptations cover different portions and do not provide a single complete screen continuity.
