01 / ORGANIZATION PROFILE

Profile & Purpose

The Band of the Hawk is a mercenary company led by Griffith. Its members fight as a disciplined force in the conflicts surrounding the Kingdom of Midland and become known for their battlefield effectiveness and loyalty to their commander.

Purpose

Griffith's celebrated mercenary company, whose rise through Midland's wars forms the emotional and political centre of the Golden Age.

Classification

MERCENARY COMPANY · MIDLAND. This archive distinguishes political institutions, mercenary forces and later supernatural military formations.

02 / HISTORICAL FOUNDATION

Origin

The company grows under Griffith's leadership from a comparatively small mercenary force into a major military power. Guts joins after a duel with Griffith, and the group expands through successive campaigns.

03 / ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE

Structure & Members

Griffith serves as commander. The company includes a core leadership group and specialized units; Casca becomes a leading commander, while Guts commands a raiding unit during the Golden Age. The organization changes as its victories attract new recruits and political attention.

Notable members and associated figures

Key members include Griffith, Guts, Casca, Judeau, Pippin, Corkus, Rickert and Gaston. This is a principal named roster, not every soldier who served in the company.

04 / NARRATIVE RECORD

Story History

The Band wins major campaigns for Midland and helps Griffith rise from mercenary captain to noble status. After Guts leaves, Griffith's personal crisis leads to imprisonment and torture. The surviving Hawks attempt to rescue him; during the Eclipse, most are sacrificed, and the original company is effectively destroyed.

05 / CONTEXT & INTERPRETATION

Story Significance

The Band of the Hawk represents chosen family, shared purpose and the human cost of ambition. Its rise and destruction define the Golden Age tragedy and shape Guts and Casca's lives.

Continuity note

This dossier follows the manga's broader narrative. The 1997, Golden Age film/Memorial Edition and 2016–2017 anime adaptations cover different portions and do not provide a single complete screen continuity.
