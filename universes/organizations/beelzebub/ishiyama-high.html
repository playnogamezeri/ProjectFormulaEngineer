Ishiyama High

The delinquent school at the centre of the human-world storyline. Its informal hierarchy, rivalries and student alliances form the social environment in which Oga first becomes Beel's guardian.

01 / PROFILE & PURPOSE

Identity and function

Ishiyama High is portrayed as a rough, disorderly school whose student culture is dominated by delinquent groups and individual strength. It is not a conventional school society: reputation, loyalty, fighting ability and personal alliances shape its internal order.

Primary purpose

As an educational institution, it is the students' school. As a story setting, it is the arena where Oga's reputation and the main human cast are established.

Operating mission

No formal organizational mission is stated. Its practical social order is maintained informally by powerful students and their groups.

Authority

Influence rests largely on reputation and strength, especially the Tōhōshinki and prominent gang leaders.

Location

The human world, in the fictional city of Ishiyama.

The school’s exaggerated delinquent culture is a fictional comedy-action setting, not a realistic model of education or a recommendation to resolve conflict through violence.

02 / ORIGIN

Origin story

The manga introduces Ishiyama High already known for its delinquent population and entrenched student hierarchy. The series does not provide a detailed founding history for the school. Its relevant origin is therefore narrative: it is the environment Oga inhabits before Beel enters his life, and the place where his reputation makes him a natural focal point for other fighters.

The Tōhōshinki and Red Tails are established groups within this school culture before Oga's guardian role begins.

04 / STORY HISTORY

Development through the story

  1. Before Beel: Ishiyama's student order is already established, with the Tōhōshinki and other groups exercising influence.
  2. Oga's guardian assignment: Oga's attempts to find a replacement guardian bring him into conflict with the school's strongest students and deepen his ties to them.
  3. Destruction: Beel's uncontrolled demonic power destroys the original Ishiyama High building during the conflict with Tōjō.
  4. Dispersal and rebuilding: The students are displaced to other schools before the original school is eventually rebuilt.
  5. Return: The rebuilt Ishiyama becomes the setting for the Killer Six Elements conflict, as a new generation challenges the established fighters.

The school persists as a social identity even when its physical campus is unavailable. That continuity helps reunite the cast.

05 / NARRATIVE ROLE

Significance to Beelzebub

Ishiyama is the story's human-world foundation. It establishes the rules Oga understands—strength, reputation and loyalty—before the demon world introduces a much larger hierarchy. The school also supplies the friendships and rivalries that become Oga's support network.

Its destruction is a turning point: a consequence of Beel's power that forces the cast into a new institutional setting. Its eventual rebuilding provides a return to the series' original social centre and allows a new generation to challenge the old hierarchy.

Core significance: Ishiyama is more than a backdrop. It is the community that transforms Oga's isolated reputation into a network of allies.
