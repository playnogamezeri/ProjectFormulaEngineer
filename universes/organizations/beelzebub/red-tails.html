Red Tails

An all-girl delinquent group at Ishiyama High, originally founded to protect female students. Its leadership changes across generations, with Aoi Kunieda among its defining leaders.

01 / PROFILE & PURPOSE

Identity, purpose and rules

The Red Tails are an all-female delinquent group at Ishiyama High. Unlike groups defined only by a leader's fighting reputation, the Red Tails have an explicitly protective founding purpose: the safety of girls at the school.

Founding purpose

Protect female students within Ishiyama High's rough delinquent environment.

Mission in practice

Provide collective strength, group identity and leadership for girls who might otherwise be vulnerable to harassment or intimidation.

Tradition

The group has a succession of female leaders and a recognizable membership identity.

Reported rule

A traditional rule says a member who has or gains a boyfriend must leave. The story later treats this rule as questionable and not consistently enforced.

02 / ORIGIN

Founding story

The Red Tails were founded by Misaki Oga, Tatsumi Oga's older sister, before the current Tōhōshinki leadership. They are described as one of Ishiyama's oldest gangs. The group was created to protect girls at a school where delinquent violence and group intimidation are common.

The series does not provide a complete account of the founding membership or the exact date of formation.

04 / STORY HISTORY

Leadership and story development

  1. Misaki's founding: The group begins as a protective organization for girls at Ishiyama.
  2. Leadership succession: Ringo Hōjō and then Aoi Kunieda occupy the leadership role.
  3. Aoi's involvement: Aoi's position links the Red Tails directly to the Tōhōshinki and to Oga's early story.
  4. Nene's leadership: Nene Ōmori succeeds Aoi and leads the group through later events.
  5. Later generation: The Reborn Red Tails and later leadership developments show that the group identity continues beyond one leader.

The manga also uses the group's dating restriction as a comedic point of discussion, showing that a rule can persist as tradition even when members and former leaders do not treat it as an absolute principle.

05 / NARRATIVE ROLE

Significance to Beelzebub

The Red Tails provide a female-led power structure within Ishiyama. Aoi's membership and leadership prevent the school's hierarchy from being represented only by male delinquent fighters. The group's protective origin also gives its strength a different stated purpose from domination or personal status.

Through Aoi, the Red Tails connect school politics, the Tōhōshinki and the emotional complications around Oga. Through its succession, the group also demonstrates continuity beyond its best-known leader.

Core significance: The Red Tails show that strength and organization can be used for protection and collective security, not only for control.
