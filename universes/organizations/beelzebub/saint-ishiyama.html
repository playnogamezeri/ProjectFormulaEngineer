Saint Ishiyama Academy

The sister school that receives Ishiyama's students after their original campus is destroyed. Its formal school culture and elite Six Knights create a new institutional conflict.

01 / PROFILE & PURPOSE

Identity and institutional role

Saint Ishiyama Academy is the sister school to Ishiyama High. It becomes the temporary school environment for Oga and the displaced Ishiyama students after Beel's power destroys their original campus.

Institutional purpose

A school operating with formal rules, clubs, sports teams, student leadership and disciplinary authority.

Story function

It contrasts Ishiyama's informal strength-based order with a more structured school hierarchy.

Central conflict

Its students and authorities initially reject the incoming delinquents and seek to enforce school rules against them.

Authority structure

School administration and the Six Knights, who exercise a powerful student-level disciplinary role.

02 / ORIGIN

How it enters the story

Saint Ishiyama is introduced after the destruction of Ishiyama High. It is identified as Ishiyama's sister school and becomes the receiving institution for the displaced students. Its own student body is not initially welcoming, and the arrival of the delinquents exposes tensions between institutional order and informal power.

The manga does not provide a detailed founding history for the academy; its narrative origin is tied to the relocation following Ishiyama's destruction.

04 / STORY HISTORY

Development through the story

  1. Arrival: The Ishiyama students transfer after their school is destroyed and face hostility from Saint Ishiyama students.
  2. Six Knights opposition: The Knights use the academy's rules and their authority to challenge the newcomers.
  3. Volleyball contest: Oga's group defeats the Six Knights in a school sports contest, changing how the academy's students view them.
  4. Threat from outside: The conflict with Teimou brings the two student populations into a common danger and encourages cooperation.
  5. Saotome's arrival: The teacher's presence reveals that the academy storyline is also a gateway into Spellmaster training and demon conflict.
05 / NARRATIVE ROLE

Significance to Beelzebub

Saint Ishiyama is a structural contrast to Oga's original school. Its formal authority allows the story to question whether rules are fair when those who enforce them also control the process of judgment. It also forces Ishiyama's students to operate in a new environment and develop relationships outside their familiar hierarchy.

The academy is where the story moves from school rivalry toward organized supernatural conflict through Saotome and the Spellmasters.

Core significance: Saint Ishiyama tests whether an established group can earn a place in a new institution rather than relying only on its old reputation.
