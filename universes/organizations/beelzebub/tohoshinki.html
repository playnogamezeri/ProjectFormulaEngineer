Tōhōshinki

The four most powerful delinquent leaders of Ishiyama High: Kanzaki, Himekawa, Kunieda and Tōjō. They begin as obstacles in Oga's search for a replacement guardian and become major allies.

01 / PROFILE & PURPOSE

Identity and purpose

Tōhōshinki is the name given to Ishiyama High's four dominant fighters. The name is formed from the characters in the members' surnames. They function as the school's top delinquent leadership circle rather than a formal club or student-government body.

Purpose

To represent and maintain the standing of the strongest four student fighters within Ishiyama's informal hierarchy.

Mission

No written mission or charter is established. Their shared role is to command respect and influence through individual strength and their personal followings.

Leadership

Four prominent leaders; Kanzaki was closest to dominating the school before Oga's arrival.

Later function

They become part of Oga's core circle and receive King's Emblems that allow them to access Beel's demonic power.

02 / ORIGIN

How the group formed

The manga presents the Tōhōshinki as an already established group before the main story begins. They are recognized as the four strongest students at Ishiyama. Their exact founding date, original agreement and first meeting are not detailed in the story.

Oga targets them because he believes the strongest delinquent might be able to take Beel from him. This practical objective turns the established hierarchy into the opening sequence of the series.

03 / STRUCTURE & MEMBERS

The four leaders

Hajime Kanzaki

One of the most influential Ishiyama fighters and the first Tōhōshinki member Oga confronts. His circle includes Takeshi Shiroyama, Sōken Chabō and Tetsuhito Kajiura.

Tatsuya Himekawa

A wealthy, calculating fighter who uses money, planning and influence as well as combat ability. His past friendship with Kugayama becomes important in the Solomon Company storyline.

Aoi Kunieda

Leader of the Red Tails and one of the Tōhōshinki. Her strength and sense of responsibility make her a major independent figure within the group.

Hidetora Tōjō

One of the strongest fighters at Ishiyama. His strength and connection to Saotome make him central to the escalation into demon-contract conflicts.

Later allies and power marks

After their conflicts with Oga, the four become loyal allies. Each receives a King's Emblem, a mark that allows access to Beel's power in combat. Their individual emblems and growth are distinct; the group does not become a uniform fighting unit with identical abilities.

The four named leaders are the complete core membership. Their personal followers are affiliated circles, not additional Tōhōshinki members.

04 / STORY HISTORY

History through the story

  1. Established dominance: The four hold the highest standing among Ishiyama's delinquent fighters.
  2. Oga's challenge: Oga confronts them in sequence while searching for a stronger guardian candidate for Beel.
  3. Rivalry becomes alliance: Their defeats and subsequent experiences turn the four from obstacles into trusted allies.
  4. Beel's illness and Tōjō conflict: The group becomes involved in the events surrounding Beel's condition and Oga's rematch with Tōjō.
  5. Saint Ishiyama and beyond: They continue to support Oga through the Six Knights conflict, contractor battles and later manga arcs.
  6. Rebuilt Ishiyama: The Killer Six Elements challenge the established fighters' position, testing whether the old leadership can adapt.
05 / NARRATIVE ROLE

Significance to Beelzebub

The Tōhōshinki are the bridge between Oga's solitary reputation and his eventual network of allies. Each confrontation changes the social landscape around him. Their later King's Emblems also demonstrate that the story's human fighters can participate in supernatural conflicts without losing their individual identities.

They embody one of the series' recurring shifts: an opponent can become a dependable ally when conflict gives way to recognition and shared purpose.

Core significance: They turn Ishiyama's hierarchy from a sequence of rivals into a coalition that stands with Oga and Beel.
