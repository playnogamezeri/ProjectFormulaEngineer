Behemoth & the 34th Pillar Division

A major demon-world military power whose 34th Pillar Division operates under Prince En and is commanded by Jabberwock. It is central to the attempt to remove Oga and influence succession.

01 / PROFILE & PURPOSE

Organization and mission

Behemoth is a major demon-world power. Its 34th Pillar Division is a large military organization owned by Behemoth but, in the story's command structure, operating directly under Prince En and led by Jabberwock.

Strategic objective

Support En's claim in the succession conflict by eliminating Oga, the human contractor linked to Beel.

Command

Jabberwock leads the 34th Pillar Division; En is the political principal it serves.

Headquarters

Demon World.

Structure

Leader, Pillar Barons and Pillar Generals, with subordinate units.

The manga distinguishes Behemoth as the owning power from the 34th Pillar Division's operational command under En. These are related but not interchangeable names.

02 / ORIGIN

Origin and political background

Behemoth is established as a demon-world military power whose forces become involved in the struggle around the Demon Lord's heirs. The Great Demon Lord does not simply settle the succession question by declaring one son the winner. That uncertainty creates room for factions to act in support of En and against Beel's human contractor.

The series does not give a full foundation chronicle for Behemoth or a complete account of how its 34 divisions were first organized.

04 / STORY HISTORY

History through the story

  1. En's succession ambitions: The division becomes an instrument of En's effort to secure his position against Beel.
  2. Human-world operation: Its forces target Oga because he is Beel's contractor and guardian.
  3. Escalation: The Pillar officers confront Oga and his allies, exposing the scale of demon military organization.
  4. Internal instability: The story reveals disappearances and manipulation involving members of the Pillar force and the Solomon Company.
  5. Later conflicts: Pillar Division figures continue to intersect with the human fighters and the broader succession crisis.
05 / NARRATIVE ROLE

Significance to Beelzebub

Behemoth's forces turn the story from a school-level conflict into a military and political struggle. They show that Beel's identity is not merely a family secret: it is a matter with consequences for demon-world power and succession.

The 34th Pillar Division also gives the human cast a clear measure of the gap between ordinary delinquent fighting and organized demonic power. The conflict forces Oga to train, coordinate and rely on allies.

Core significance: Behemoth is the military pressure that makes the succession conflict tangible in the human world.
