Paradis Island

The island where the Walls shelter the Subjects of Ymir and where the story's political and military conflicts begin.

01 / ORGANIZATION PROFILE

Profile & Purpose

Paradis is both a geographic setting and the home of the walled Eldian society. For roughly a century, most residents live inside Walls Maria, Rose and Sheena under a monarchy shaped by the First King's vow renouncing war.

Purpose

Initially preserve the population inside the Walls and maintain the monarchy's manufactured peace; later defend the island from foreign powers and determine its political future.

Classification

ISLAND · ELDIAN POLITY. The Index 07 category includes nations and coalitions as well as formal military organizations.

02 / HISTORICAL FOUNDATION

Origin

King Karl Fritz leads a portion of the Eldian population to the island after the Great Titan War. He uses the Founding Titan to create the Walls from countless Colossal Titans and alters the memories of most Subjects of Ymir on the island.

03 / ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE

Structure & Members

Paradis has a royal government, civilian institutions and an armed force divided into the Survey Corps, Garrison Regiment and Military Police Brigade. The royal government is later exposed and replaced; Queen Historia becomes the legitimate monarch. The Yeagerists subsequently seize military and political power.

Notable members and associated figures

Key figures include Karl Fritz, the Reiss royal line, Rod Reiss, Historia Reiss, Darius Zackly, Dot Pixis, Erwin Smith, Hange Zoë, Eren Yeager and Floch Forster. These figures represent different periods and competing institutions, not one unified leadership team.

04 / NARRATIVE RECORD

Story History

The fall of Wall Maria in 845 destabilizes the island. The Survey Corps uncovers the royal conspiracy and the truth in the Yeager basement. Contact with the outside world reveals Marley and the global persecution of Eldians. In 854, the island is divided by the conflict over Eren and the Rumbling; the Yeagerists dominate the government. After the Rumbling, Paradis remains militarized and its long-term fate is shown across later generations.

05 / CONTEXT & INTERPRETATION

Story Significance

Paradis begins as the apparent last refuge of humanity and becomes a state caught between isolation, survival, nationalism and global retaliation. It is not itself a single organization; this dossier treats it as the central polity and geographic home of several separate factions.

Continuity and classification note

This dossier follows the Attack on Titan anime and manga story as summarized in the series encyclopedia. Where the source material does not establish a complete roster, precise founding date or full command chart, the page avoids presenting an invented complete record.
