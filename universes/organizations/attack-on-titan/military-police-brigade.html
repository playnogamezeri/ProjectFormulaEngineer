01 / ORGANIZATION PROFILE

Profile & Purpose

The Military Police Brigade is one of the three main branches of Paradis's military. It is stationed primarily in the interior, especially around Wall Sheena, and is responsible for policing and protecting the monarchy.

Purpose

Maintain internal order, guard the royal government and enforce the existing political structure. Its privileged position also makes it vulnerable to corruption and concealment of state secrets.

Classification

MILITARY BRANCH · PARADIS. The Index 07 category includes nations and coalitions as well as formal military organizations.

02 / HISTORICAL FOUNDATION

Origin

The Brigade forms part of the military system established under the monarchy. Entry is restricted to the top ten graduates of each training class, making it a prestigious and comparatively safe posting.

03 / ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE

Structure & Members

The Brigade has a formal command hierarchy and includes regular Military Police as well as secretive Interior Police units. Nile Dok is a prominent commander; Kenny Ackerman leads an anti-personnel squad operating under the Interior Police.

Notable members and associated figures

Notable figures include Nile Dok, Marlo Freudenberg, Hitch Dreyse, Annie Leonhart during her infiltration, Kenny Ackerman, Djel Sannes and the Interior Police personnel. These groups should not be treated as identical: the Interior Police is a covert arm within the broader institution.

04 / NARRATIVE RECORD

Story History

The Brigade initially attempts to contain Eren through military tribunal proceedings. The Uprising arc exposes its complicity in the royal regime's abuses and the Interior Police's suppression of dissent. After the monarchy is overthrown, the branch's authority is transformed; the Yeagerist takeover later displaces the old military order.

05 / CONTEXT & INTERPRETATION

Story Significance

The Brigade embodies the tension between public safety and state control. Its prestige and proximity to power make it a key instrument of the monarchy's secrecy and corruption, although individual members do not all share the same motives.

Continuity and classification note
