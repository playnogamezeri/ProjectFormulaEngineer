01 / ORGANIZATION PROFILE

Profile & Purpose

The Survey Corps is one of the three principal branches of Paradis's military. Its soldiers conduct expeditions outside the Walls, gather intelligence, study Titans and attempt to expand humanity's understanding and territory.

Purpose

Explore Titan territory, protect humanity through knowledge and military action, and ultimately uncover the history concealed by the monarchy.

Classification

MILITARY BRANCH · PARADIS. The Index 07 category includes nations and coalitions as well as formal military organizations.

02 / HISTORICAL FOUNDATION

Origin

The Corps develops as the outward-facing branch of the island's military. Its emblem, the Wings of Freedom, expresses the aspiration to reach beyond the confinement of the Walls.

03 / ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE

Structure & Members

The organization is led by a Commander and supported by captains, squad leaders and specialist teams. The Special Operations Squad and other expedition units operate under the Corps. Commanders across the main story include Keith Shadis, Erwin Smith and Hange Zoë; Armin succeeds Hange after the final battle.

Notable members and associated figures

Notable members include Erwin Smith, Hange Zoë, Levi Ackerman, Eren Yeager, Mikasa Ackerman, Armin Arlert, Jean Kirstein, Connie Springer, Sasha Blouse, Historia Reiss, Moblit Berner and Floch Forster. Membership changes sharply through casualties, transfers and political divisions.

04 / NARRATIVE RECORD

Story History

The Corps suffers catastrophic losses in expeditions and the battles for Trost and Shiganshina. It exposes the Female Titan, the Warriors, the royal conspiracy and the truth of the outside world. After the time skip, its surviving members are split by Eren's actions; former Scouts later form part of the alliance against the Rumbling.

05 / CONTEXT & INTERPRETATION

Story Significance

The Survey Corps is the story's principal institution of discovery. Its repeated sacrifices make knowledge possible, while its history also shows the human cost of pursuing truth under authoritarian and existential pressure.
