Garrison Regiment

01 / ORGANIZATION PROFILE

Profile & Purpose

The Garrison Regiment is one of Paradis's three main military branches. It maintains and mans the Walls, operates defensive artillery, protects populated districts and responds when Titans breach the defenses.

Purpose

Protect civilian life and infrastructure, maintain the defensive system and coordinate immediate responses to attacks.

Classification

MILITARY BRANCH · PARADIS. The Index 07 category includes nations and coalitions as well as formal military organizations.

02 / HISTORICAL FOUNDATION

Origin

The Garrison is established as the island's stationary defensive force after the construction of the Walls. It becomes the largest branch because most graduates do not qualify for the Military Police and do not choose the high-risk Survey Corps.

03 / ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE

Structure & Members

The Regiment operates through garrisons, wall stations, artillery crews and district commands. Dot Pixis commands the southern territory during the major early crises; subordinate officers include Kitz Weilman, Anka and Gustav.

Notable members and associated figures

Notable members include Dot Pixis, Hannes, Rico Brzenska, Ian Dietrich, Kitz Weilman, Anka and Gustav. The exact personnel roster changes over time and is not exhaustively enumerated in the story.

04 / NARRATIVE RECORD

Story History

The Garrison's defensive system fails when the Colossal and Armored Titans breach Wall Maria. During the Battle of Trost, its soldiers coordinate with the newly revealed Eren Titan to plug the gate. It later contributes to the defense of the island and is fractured by the Yeagerist coup.

05 / CONTEXT & INTERPRETATION

Story Significance

The Garrison represents the ordinary defensive burden of maintaining a society under constant threat. Its role shifts from wall protection to the defense of Paradis against foreign attack.

Continuity and classification note
