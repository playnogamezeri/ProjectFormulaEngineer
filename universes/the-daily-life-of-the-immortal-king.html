Synopsis & Production Context

In modern Songhai City where Daoist cultivation powers smartphones and high school tournaments, Wang Ling has advanced a cultivation realm every two years since infancy and casually slew a Demon King at age six. Wearing a sealing talisman to suppress his reality-warping Qi, Wang Ling enrolls at No. 60 High School alongside heiress Sun Rong, Chen Chao, and Guo Hao—repeatedly saving his friends and the cosmos while trying to keep his cover intact.

03Story & ChronologyEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Chronological Progression

Five seasonal arcs (63 episodes) spanning S1 (Shadow Stream), S2 (Demon Emperor & Wang Ying), S3 (Kyuumiya Family), S4 (Evil Sword God), and S5 (Wang Nuan & Heavenly Dao).

ANIME VS. SOURCE CONTINUITY DIFFERENCES: The donghua heavily diverges from Kuxuan's web novel starting in Season 1. In the novel, Wang Ling has perfect control over his powers without needing a sealing talisman, and key novel figures (such as cousin Wang Ming) are omitted or rewritten as the donghua builds original seasonal climaxes.
