Synopsis & Production Context

Rejected 50 times in middle school, red-haired delinquent Hanamichi Sakuragi joins Shohoku's basketball club purely to win over Haruko Akagi. Under Coach Anzai, captain Takenori Akagi, and stoic rival Kaede Rukawa, Sakuragi transforms from a foul-prone novice into Shohoku's rebounding engine—upsetting Shoyo and Ryonan to reach Nationals and challenge 3-time champion Sannoh Kogyo.

Grounded in Kanagawa Prefecture's Shōnan region and Hiroshima's National Inter-High Championship, governed by real-world high school basketball and academic eligibility rules.

03Story & ChronologyEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Chronological Progression

Covers Sakuragi's fundamentals, Ryonan practice match, Miyagi and Mitsui's return, Kanagawa preliminaries (Shoyo, Kainan, Ryonan), and the Hiroshima Inter-High (Toyotama & Sannoh).

ANIME VS. SOURCE CONTINUITY DIFFERENCES: The 1993 TV anime stops before Nationals with an anime-original Shoyo–Ryonan practice match (Ep. 97–101). Takehiko Inoue's 2022 film The First Slam Dunk adapts the manga's Sannoh climax from Ryota Miyagi's perspective, adding flashbacks about his brother Sota.

11Ending ExplainedEXPAND / COLLAPSE
SPOILER DISCLOSURE: Detailed resolution of Slam Dunk across anime and source material.

Injured Sakuragi catches Rukawa's pass to sink the 79–78 buzzer-beater over Sannoh. Exhausted, Shohoku falls in Round 3 to Aiwa Gakuin; in 10 Days After, Akagi and Kogure study for exams, Miyagi becomes captain, Mitsui stays for Winter, and Sakuragi rehabs on the beach.

12PurposesEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Moral of the Story: If You Refuse to Give Up, Even a Beginner Can Change the Scoreboard

Slam Dunk turns hustle, fundamentals, and honest love for a sport into something unforgettable.

Shohoku doesn't need a fairy-tale championship—beating Sannoh with every ounce left on the floor proves their summer was real.
