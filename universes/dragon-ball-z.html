Synopsis & Production Context

Five years after defeating Piccolo Jr., Son Goku's peaceful life shatters when his brother Raditz arrives and reveals Goku's alien Saiyan heritage. Dying to stop Raditz, Goku trains in the afterlife while his son Gohan, Piccolo, and Vegeta confront existential threats from Frieza, Dr. Gero's Cell, and Majin Buu.

Expands from Earth (West City, Capsule Corp, Kami's Lookout) to Planet Namek, Planet Vegeta, and the cosmic Other World (Snake Way, King Kai's planet, Sacred World of the Kais).

Planet Namek

Three-sunned homeworld of the Namekians and the wish-granting Porunga.

Other World & Kaioshin Realm

Afterlife governed by King Yemma, King Kai, and the Supreme Kais.

Planet Vegeta (Destroyed)

Former Saiyan homeworld obliterated by Frieza.

03Story & ChronologyEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Chronological Progression

Spans four main sagas—Saiyan, Namek/Frieza, Androids/Cell, and Majin Buu—ending with a 10-year timeskip to the 28th World Martial Arts Tournament.

ANIME VS. SOURCE CONTINUITY DIFFERENCES: Toei added anime-original filler sagas—Garlic Jr. (Ep. 108–117) and the Other World Tournament (Ep. 195–199)—plus standalone episodes (like Goku and Piccolo's driving lesson in Ep. 125) and extended fights. Dragon Ball Z Kai (2009–2015) trims most filler to match Toriyama's manga.

11Ending ExplainedEXPAND / COLLAPSE
SPOILER DISCLOSURE: Detailed resolution of Dragon Ball Z across anime and source material.

Ten years after Kid Buu's defeat, Goku enters the 28th World Martial Arts Tournament to meet Uub, Kid Buu's pure-hearted human reincarnation. Sensing Uub's potential, Goku leaves the match unfinished to train Earth's next protector.

12PurposesEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Moral of the Story: There Is Always Another Limit to Surpass—and Someone to Pass the Torch To

Dragon Ball Z celebrates the joy of testing yourself against impossible odds while believing anyone can choose a better path.

From low-class Saiyan to mentor of his enemy's reincarnation, Goku's journey insists that growth never ends and that compassion can turn yesterday's villain into tomorrow's guardian.
