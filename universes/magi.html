Synopsis & Production Context

Guided by his flute's Djinn Ugo, young Magi Aladdin teams with ambitious former Balbadd prince Alibaba Saluja and Fanalis warrior Morgiana to conquer mysterious Dungeons. Their travels entangle them in Sindria, Magnostadt's magician caste war, and the ancient tragedy of Alma Torran against Al-Thamen and King Sinbad.

02World & SettingEXPAND / COLLAPSE

An alternate ancient Old World inspired by One Thousand and One Nights—spanning Balbadd, Sindria, the Kou Empire, Reim Empire, Magnostadt, and the Dark Continent—sustained by Rukh and Magoi.

Balbadd & Kingdom of Sindria

Alibaba's mercantile homeland and Sinbad's prosperous island alliance.

Magnostadt & Reim Empire

Magician-caste academy state and the Roman-inspired western empire.

Kou Empire

Eastern superpower built on Dungeon Capturers and Djinn vessels.

Alma Torran & Sacred Palace

Lost ancient world of King Solomon and the source of the Rukh.

03Story & ChronologyEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Chronological Progression

Main TV covers Dungeon/Balbadd, Sindria/Pirates, and the Magnostadt War (Ch. 1–198). Unadapted manga chapters reveal Alma Torran (Ch. 199–241), the Kou Civil War (Ch. 242–282), and the Sacred Palace Final Arc (Ch. 283–369).

ANIME VS. SOURCE CONTINUITY DIFFERENCES: Season 1 adjusts the Zagan expedition's motive (framing it around curing Black Rukh curses) and moves Al-Thamen's ambush to Sindria. Crucially, the final 171 chapters (Alma Torran, Kou Empire, Sacred Palace) remain manga-only.

11Ending ExplainedEXPAND / COLLAPSE
SPOILER DISCLOSURE: Detailed resolution of Magi: The Labyrinth of Magic across anime and source material.

While the anime ends after Magnostadt's war (Ep. 50), Manga Ch. 369 concludes inside the Sacred Palace: Sinbad sacrifices himself to destroy David, dissolving the Magi and Dungeon systems so humanity can govern itself freely, and Alibaba marries Morgiana.

12PurposesEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Moral of the Story: Humanity Deserves the Freedom to Write Its Own Destiny

Magi questions even the kindest ruler who tries to decide the world's fate on everyone else's behalf.

By dismantling divine Dungeons and Magi, Aladdin and Alibaba entrust the future to ordinary people learning to live together.
