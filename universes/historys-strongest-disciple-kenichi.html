01 / SERIES PROFILE

Overview

Kenichi Shirahama is a bullied student who joins a dojo where several masters teach him different martial arts. His training develops from self-defense into increasingly dangerous confrontations with organized fighters and powerful martial artists.

Creator / source

Shun Matsuena

02 / SETTING

World & setting

A contemporary setting features dojos, martial-arts organizations and rival groups. The Ryozanpaku masters represent different disciplines, while opponents test the limits of Kenichi's training and resolve.
