Synopsis & Production Context

Mocked as 'Weak Legs,' Kenichi Shirahama befriends martial arts prodigy Miu Fūrinji and joins Ryōzanpaku Dojo. Training in Jujitsu, Karate, Muay Thai, Chinese Kenpo, and Weapons under the creed of Katsujinken (Life-Saving Fist), Kenichi unites former bullies into the Shinpaku Alliance to topple Ragnarök and the lethal Satsujinken syndicate Yami.

03Story & ChronologyEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Chronological Progression

The 50-episode TV series adapts the entire Ragnarök Saga (Ch. 1–143) culminating in Kenichi vs. Odin. The 11 OVAs adapt early YOMI clashes, while the manga continues through D of D, Tidat, Titan, and The Eternal Sunset (Ch. 583).

ANIME VS. SOURCE CONTINUITY DIFFERENCES: The TV anime stops cleanly at Ch. 143 (Ragnarök finale) and softens graphic violence, leaving most of the Yami/YOMI Saga (Ch. 144–583) unadapted outside the 11 Brain's Base OVAs.

06Battle & Combat SystemEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Martial arts philosophy dividing calm Sei (Silence) and aggressive Dou (Motion) Ki, governed by Katsujinken (Life-Saving Fist) vs. Yami's lethal Satsujinken (Killing Fist).

Sei, Dou & Dual Ki

Sei prioritizes counter-reading; Dou channels explosive aggression; combining both carries severe bodily strain.

Ryōzanpaku Master Styles

Synthesizes Jujitsu, Karate, Muay Thai, Chinese Kenpo, and Weapons.

11Ending ExplainedEXPAND / COLLAPSE

After thwarting Yami's Eternal Sunset plot (Ch. 583), both Yami and Ryōzanpaku disband. Years later, Kenichi is a successful novelist implied to have married Miu—confirming he met Hayato's challenge—with the story continuing in 2026's Kenichi 2: Tatsujin-hen.

12PurposesEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Moral of the Story: Strength Exists to Protect Life, Not Dominate Others

History's Strongest Disciple Kenichi champions stubborn perseverance and moral restraint for anyone starting from zero.

Through six relentless masters and a coward who refuses to run away, the series proves that how you use strength defines you more than raw talent.
