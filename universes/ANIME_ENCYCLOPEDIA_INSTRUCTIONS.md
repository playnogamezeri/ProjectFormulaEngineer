# MOTION4I — Anime Universe Encyclopedia Production Instructions

## 1. Master Reference
- **Master Template:** `universes/akame-ga-kill.html`
- **Main Directory:** `index.html` (`?view=anime`, 61 canonical titles)
- **Progress Tracker:** `universes/ANIME_ENCYCLOPEDIA_TRACKER.md`

## 2. Standard 14-Section Encyclopedia Index
Every anime page (`universes/<slug>.html`) must preserve these 14 numbered sections and anchor IDs:
- `00` Official Poster (`#series-poster`)
- `01` Overview (`#overview`)
- `02` World & Setting (`#world`)
- `03` Story & Chronology (`#story`)
- `04` Major Story Arcs (`#arcs`)
- `05` Character Database (`#characters`) — searchable & filterable with live count
- `06` Battle & Combat System (`#powers`) — adapted to non-combat genres without inventing powers
- `07` Nations & Factions (`#factions`)
- `08` Relationships (`#relationships`)
- `09` Anime/Manga Guide (`#episodes`)
- `10` Themes & Analysis (`#themes`)
- `11` Ending Explained (`#ending`)
- `12` Purposes (`#purposes`)
- `13` References (`#sources`)

## 3. Linked Dossiers & Continuity Standards
- **Character Dossiers:** `universes/characters/<anime-slug>/<character-slug>.html`
- **Organization/Faction Dossiers:** `universes/factions/<anime-slug>/<faction-slug>.html`
- Distinguish **manga canon**, **anime canon**, **anime-original/filler**, **film/novel continuity**, and **editorial interpretation**.
- Never invent episode counts, dates, or unverified artwork URLs. Use labeled placeholders until official artwork is verified.
