Synopsis & Production Context

In a technologically advanced alternate Japan secretly guided by Seven Kings empowered by the Dresden Slate, Yashiro Isana is hunted by both HOMRA and Scepter 4 after the Colorless King frames him for Tatara Totsuka's murder. Aided by Kuroh Yatogami and Strain illusionist Neko, Shiro reclaims his identity as Silver King Adolf K. Weismann—leading to an all-out war against Green King Nagare Hisui's <jungle>.

02World & SettingEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Set in Shizume City in Japan's Kanto region—featuring island-campus Ashinaka High School, Mihashira Tower, and the 100 km Kagutsu Crater—where the Dresden Slate grants Seven Kings and their Clansmen supernatural Auras.

Shizume City & Ashinaka High School

Futuristic Kanto metropolis and island school where students carry PDAs.

Mihashira Tower

Gold Clan headquarters guarding the Dresden Slate.

HOMRA Bar & Scepter 4 HQ

Bases of the Red brotherhood and Blue law-enforcement unit.

Kagutsu Crater & Yomito Gate

Site of the 1999 Damocles Down and Jungle's final stronghold.

03Story & ChronologyEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Chronological Progression

Spanning prequels in Seven Stories (Kagutsu Crater aftermath, Lost Small World, Memory of Red), Season 1's Academy Island Incident, Missing Kings' Red King succession, Return of Kings' Dresden Slate War, and Circle Vision's epilogue.

ANIME VS. SOURCE CONTINUITY DIFFERENCES: K is an anime-original canon; companion manga and light novels (such as K: Dog and Cat and K: Countdown) bridge gaps between seasons, while Seven Stories visually updated the Gold and Colorless Swords of Damocles.

06Battle & Combat SystemEXPAND / COLLAPSE

The Dresden Slate selects Seven Kings who share colored Auras with Clansmen, alongside irregular Strains, governed by Weismann Levels and the catastrophic Sword of Damocles.

Seven Kings, Aura & Strains

Kings bestow Aura insignias onto Clansmen; Strains manifest irregular powers from Slate leakage.

Weismann Level & Damocles Down

Overuse fractures a King's overhead Sword of Damocles, threatening mass-casualty impact like the 1999 Kagutsu Crater.

11Ending ExplainedEXPAND / COLLAPSE
SPOILER DISCLOSURE: Detailed resolution of K across anime and source material.

Shiro intentionally drives his Weismann Level past its limit to drop his Sword of Damocles directly onto the Dresden Slate at Yomito Gate, destroying the Slate. All Kings and Clansmen lose their powers, Munakata is saved, Nagare passes away, and Shiro returns to Ashinaka High School as a teacher.

12PurposesEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Moral of the Story: Power Borrowed from Fate Matters Less Than the Bonds You Keep Without It

K examines how extraordinary power isolates leaders until they trust others to share—or lay down—the crown.

When the Dresden Slate shatters, HOMRA, Scepter 4, and the Silver Clan prove their loyalty was never about Aura, but about each other.
