Synopsis & Production Context

17-year-old martial artist Jin Mori joins The God of High School, a nationwide tournament promising the winner any wish. Teaming up with Full-Contact Karate master Han Daewi and Moonlight Sword heir Yoo Mira, Mori battles through Seoul and the National Competition as tournament organizer Park Mujin, the cult NOX, and Charyeok-wielding fighters clash—culminating in Mori's awakening as the sealed Monkey King, Sun Wukong.

02World & SettingEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Divided into the Human Realm, Sage/Demon Realm, and Heavenly Realm, the world is governed by an ancient divine Taboo and contracts called Borrowed Power (Charyeok), pitted against pure Genuine Martial Arts.

Seoul & National GOH Arenas

High-tech tournament venues hosting metropolitan and national brackets.

Oraeguk (Sage Realm, Webtoon)

Ancient monkey kingdom where Mori confronts his Sun Wukong past.

Heavenly Realm (Webtoon)

Domain of Okhwang and gods clashing with humanity during Ragnarok.

Post-Ragnarok Earth (Webtoon)

World Government era ruled by World President Park Mujin.

03Story & ChronologyEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Chronological Progression

The 13-episode anime covers Part 1 (Ch. 1–41) in Eps. 1–5 and Part 2 (Ch. 42–112) in Eps. 6–13, while the Webtoon continues through Parts 3–6 and the Epilogue (Ch. 113–569).

ANIME VS. SOURCE CONTINUITY DIFFERENCES: MAPPA's 13-episode anime heavily compresses 112 Webtoon chapters—especially Part 2 (71 chapters in 8 episodes)—trimming preliminary fights, character backstories, and Charyeok exposition while streamlining Jin Taejin's rescue arc. Parts 3–6 (Oraeguk, World Competition, Ragnarok, and the 17-year timeskip RE: A Match with God) remain unadapted.
04Major Story ArcsEXPAND / COLLAPSE
Part 1: A Match with God (Ep. 1–5 · Ch. 1–41)

Mori, Daewi, and Mira form Team Seoul; Mori defeats Daewi in the metropolitan finals.

Part 2: The National Competition (Ep. 6–13 · Ch. 42–112)

NOX infiltrates Nationals; Park Ilpyo awakens the Nine-Tails Key; Mori awakens as Sun Wukong to stop Jegal Taek.

Parts 3–4: Adventure & World Competition (Unadapted · Ch. 113–216)

Mori journeys to Oraeguk while his fur clone Hui Mori fights in the GOH World Tournament.

Part 5: Ragnarok (Unadapted · Ch. 217–307)

Humanity wars with Heaven; Daewi inherits Okhwang's Wisdom; Mori beats 666:Satan; Mujin betrays Taejin.

Part 6 & Epilogue: RE: A Match with God (Unadapted · Ch. 308–569)

17 years later, Dan Mori achieves Nirvana as Maitreya to defeat Supreme God Park Mujin.

06Battle & Combat SystemEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Combat pits pure Genuine Martial Arts (like Renewal Taekwondo) against Borrowed Power (Charyeok) contracts forged with gods, demons, and historical heroes.

Borrowed Power (Charyeok)

Contracts drawing on violet bio-energy (GP) across Weapon, Hero, Parasitic, Direct, Lineal, and Key classifications.

Genuine Martial Arts & Recoilless Concept

Techniques like Re-Taekwondo, Nabong Needle Ryu, and Moonlight Sword Style—peaking in Recoilless strikes that bend space without Charyeok.

Human Agency vs. Divine Predestination

Humans reject divine Taboos and forged hierarchies through earned skill.

Earned Discipline vs. Borrowed Power

True mastery belongs to those who refine themselves rather than relying solely on external gifts.

11Ending ExplainedEXPAND / COLLAPSE
SPOILER DISCLOSURE: Detailed resolution of The God of High School across anime and source material.

The Webtoon concluded November 3, 2022 (Ch. 569) with Jin Mori achieving Nirvana as Maitreya to defeat Park Mujin, while Daewi and Mira marry and help Ilpyo rebuild Earth. The anime remains 1 season (13 episodes) with no Season 2 announced.

12PurposesEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Moral of the Story: Real Strength Is Forged Through Discipline, Not Borrowed

The God of High School celebrates the grit of martial arts standing toe-to-toe with gods.

Even when fate and divine power stack the deck, loyalty to your friends and mastery of your own craft break every Taboo.
