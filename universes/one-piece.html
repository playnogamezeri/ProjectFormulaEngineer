Synopsis & Production Context

Eating the Mythical Zoan Hito Hito no Mi, Model: Nika as a child, Monkey D. Luffy sets sail from East Blue to find the legendary treasure One Piece and become Pirate King. Recruiting Zoro, Nami, Usopp, Sanji, Chopper, Robin, Franky, Brook, and Jimbei, the Straw Hats liberate kingdoms, uncover the Void Century, and confront the Four Emperors and Five Elders.

02World & SettingEXPAND / COLLAPSE

The Blue Planet is quartered into Four Blues by the Red Line mountain range and the equatorial Grand Line (split into Paradise and the New World), ruled by the World Government from Mary Geoise.

Four Blues & Paradise

East Blue through Sabaody, Alabasta, Skypiea, Water 7, and Marineford.

The New World (Wano, Egghead, Elbaph)

Emperor-ruled seas housing Road Poneglyphs and Vegapunk's lab.

Mary Geoise & Empty Throne

Seat of the Celestial Dragons, Five Elders, and secret sovereign Imu.

Laugh Tale

Final island holding Roger's One Piece.

03Story & ChronologyEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Chronological Progression

Pre-Timeskip (East Blue through Marineford's tragedy) leads to a 2-year training hiatus, followed by the New World saga (Fish-Man Island, Dressrosa, Whole Cake Island, Wano) and the Final Saga (Egghead & Elbaph).

ANIME VS. SOURCE CONTINUITY DIFFERENCES: Toei's anime adds original filler arcs (such as Warship Island and G-8) and omits most canon manga Cover Stories (e.g., Eneru's Great Space Mission, Germa 66), while shifting in April 2026 to a 26-episode-per-year seasonal schedule.

12PurposesEXPAND / COLLAPSE

Moral of the Story: True Freedom Means Sharing the Feast with Everyone Beside You

One Piece defines the Pirate King not as the ruler of the seas, but as the person freest—and most generous—on the ocean.

Across 1,100+ chapters, Luffy's crew proves that liberating friends and refusing to forget history can topple any empire.
