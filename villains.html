CHARACTER DATABASE // ANTAGONISTS

Villains, rivals & relationships.

A cross-universe guide to major antagonists and the romantic relationships connected to them. Entries distinguish confirmed canon relationships from unconfirmed or one-sided attachments.

Research note: “Villain” is not a perfect fit for every series. Sports anime generally have rivals rather than villains, so those entries are labeled as competitive antagonists. “No confirmed partner” means the source story does not establish one; it does not prove the character has never had a relationship. Relationship labels describe canon evidence, not fan shipping.
