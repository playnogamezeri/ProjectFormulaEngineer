EVIDENCE-LED CHARACTER RESEARCH // DOSSIER SYSTEM

Character investigations.

A consistent research framework for protagonists, supporting characters and antagonists across all 30 ANIMARCHIVE universes. The aim is to understand each character—not to excuse them, condemn them in advance, or confuse interpretation with canon.

Scope and honesty: The current archive contains four full starter protagonist profiles and one featured antagonist or rival per universe. This page establishes the dossier standard for expanding those profiles. It does not claim that every character in every series has already been researched. Coverage will grow in documented batches, with sources and uncertainty recorded.

Every character receives the same investigation

01 · Identity & origin
Names, aliases, birthplace, family, ancestry, upbringing and the complete canon origin story.
02 · Life chronology
Major events in sequence, turning points, losses, victories, training and transformations.
03 · Psychological profile
Motivations, fears, needs, values, habits, coping strategies and internal contradictions.
04 · Forensic findings
Evidence from actions, dialogue, decisions, relationships, patterns and consequences. Findings must point to story evidence.
05 · Capabilities & limits
Abilities, techniques, resources, constraints, vulnerabilities, costs and demonstrated failure conditions.
06 · Relationships
Family, friends, mentors, rivals, victims, romantic relationships and meaningful changes over time.
07 · Problems & missing pieces
Knowledge gaps, emotional wounds, poor judgments, unmet needs, harmful habits and skills or support they lack.
08 · Ethics & accountability
What they get right and wrong, who is harmed, what they justify, and whether they accept responsibility.
09 · Completeness assessment
What growth, truth, repair, learning or change could help them become more whole—without assuming there is one perfect endpoint.
10 · Research record
Primary canon references, adaptation differences, reliable secondary sources, confidence level and unresolved questions.

Evidence labels

Canon fact

Explicitly shown or stated in the original work or an officially recognized continuation. Identify whether the evidence comes from manga, anime, film or another canon source.

Adaptation difference

Anime-only scenes, omissions, filler, altered chronology or differences between manga, anime and novel continuity. Never silently merge versions.

Interpretation

A reasoned reading of behavior or themes. Explain the evidence and keep it distinct from what the story explicitly confirms.

Unconfirmed / disputed

Missing, ambiguous or conflicting evidence. State what is unknown instead of filling gaps with fan theories.

Investigation safeguards

“Forensic” here means close examination of fictional evidence and behavior; it is not a clinical diagnosis or a claim that a fictional character can be medically assessed. Psychological language will be used cautiously. Real-world religious, historical, scientific and cultural comparisons will be contextualized and sourced independently.
