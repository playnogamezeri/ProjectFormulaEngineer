01 / FILM IDENTITY

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Love (愛 — the single character "Ai," love itself) is a 1955 Hong Kong Cantonese romantic drama, the first installment of a two-part release: Part One opened January 1, 1955 (per IMDb's listing), and Love Part 2 followed a week later, on January 8 — the era's diptych practice, in which a story too long or too popular for one sitting was issued as a pair of features in quick succession, the second part's audience pre-sold by the first. The cast records name the era's first rank: Ng Cho-fan, Pak Yin, Cheung Wood-yau and Sun Ma Si-tsang (the masquerade master of The Wise Guys Who Fool Around, in this Galaxy) among the principals — and Bruce Lee, fourteen, credited in HKMDB's filmography as "Ma Si-Hau's son," a role he carries through both parts: the diptych's child, present at the story's opening and its conclusion alike. No plot synopsis for either part survives in the English-language sources this archive could verify, and none is reconstructed here — the standing practice of this shelf's honest records. What the record does certify is the films' standing: the Hong Kong Film Archive's Lee retrospective names "Love (Part 1 & 2)" among the "several great Cantonese pictures" of his apprenticeship — "from straight roles to heavies, from comedy to tragedy, Lee proved that he could play well in any roles" — and files the pair between A Myriad Homes and An Orphan's Tragedy in its honor roll. A two-part romantic drama, headlined by the era's definitive romantic faces, praised by the Archive seventy years on, and lost to English plot summary entire: the archive files the pair as the 1955 group's diptych exhibit — a love story the record keeps the shape of (two parts, one week, one character for a title) and has surrendered the telling of.
