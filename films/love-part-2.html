01 / FILM IDENTITY

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Love Part 2 (愛下集 — "Love, the concluding installment") is the second panel of the 1955 Hong Kong Cantonese romantic diptych Love, released January 8, 1955 (per MyDramaList's record) — exactly one week after Part One opened on New Year's Day. Where Part One is the promise, Part Two is the settlement: the feature in which the divided story's accounts — whatever the interval left suspended — are closed, before an audience that had kept the story for seven days and returned, on the eighth, to a cinema that functioned as the city's appointed room for endings. The cast records for the second panel show the diptych deepening its bench, as serial stories do when the first part has sold: Pak Yin and Cheung Wood-yau continue; Hung Sin-nui — the opera diva of her generation — and Mui Yee join the principals; and Bruce Lee, fourteen, carries his credit of "Ma Si-Hau's son" through the conclusion, the household's through-line made whole. No plot synopsis for Part Two survives in the English-language sources this archive could verify, and none is reconstructed here; the Hong Kong Film Archive's retrospective files the pair together — "Love (Part 1 & 2)" — among the "several great Cantonese pictures" of Lee's apprenticeship, a joint verdict this file honors by keeping the panels as separate entries, exactly as 1955 issued them. The archive files Part Two as the diptych's settlement exhibit: the rarer artifact, in any era, of a conclusion that arrived on schedule — seven days, to the morning — while the promise was still warm.
