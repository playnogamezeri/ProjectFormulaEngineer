01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Sona is Ling's right hand: she meets him at the airport with the news that the stones are gone, she runs his intelligence, and when the collection turns physical it is Sona who takes Vanessa Fait. At the hangar she fights the finale's parallel duel — Daria, hand to hand, while Su and Ling settle the main account nearby. The Galaxy page's portrait is exact and this file keeps it: professionalism without a cause, the best employee in the film working for its worst man, with never a suggestion that she has noticed the discrepancy.

ROLE

The enforcer

Principal supporting antagonist; Ling's assistant, intelligence channel and kidnapping hand.

DEFINING TRAIT

Professionalism without a cause

Cold, capable, unhesitating — her loyalty is total and entirely unexamined, the film's quietest moral problem.

FATE

The parallel duel

She fights Daria at the hangar as the auction collapses; the documented plot record closes on the rescue and Ling's death, and her page records her fate no further than the duel the film gives her.

Place in the story

Sona is the film's study of competence in service — the counterpart to Daria, who serves a household, and to Su, who serves a mission. Her page is deliberately kept at the film's own scale: a complete portrait of the professional the plot uses hardest and explains least.

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

Sona serves as Ling's assistant and enforcer in his plutonium pipeline. She is the first of his organisation the film shows in motion: at San Francisco International Airport she informs Ling that Christophe has been attacked and that Fait's gang has taken the black diamonds — the report that starts her employer's collection campaign.

Through the hunt she is Ling's instrument of reach: his intelligence channel and, when the leverage is applied, his hand — it is Sona who takes Vanessa Fait, the kidnapping that converts the film's heist plot into a rescue. At the airport hangar, as Ling's auction opens and the crew storms it, Sona fights the finale's parallel duel against Daria, Fait's second — enforcer against enforcer, in the margins of the main account between Su and Ling. The documented plot record closes on Vanessa's rescue, Ling's death and the police arrival; it assigns Sona no further fate, and this page records the limit honestly.

04 / PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Psychology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation, grounded in the character's on-screen actions.

Cold capability

Interpretation: Sona's register never changes — briefing, kidnapping and combat are delivered at the same temperature. The film treats the flatness as professionalism; the archive records it as the character's defining chill.

Loyalty, unexamined

She serves a man who murdered his own team, and the film never gives her a moment of doubt. Interpretation: her silence on the subject is the character — the professional for whom the employer's nature is not a category.

The mirror of Daria

Both women are seconds to their principals, both fight the finale's undercard, and the film sets them against each other as the two kinds of service: hers to a paymaster, Daria's to a family.

09 / ANALYSIS & INVESTIGATION

Analysis & Investigation

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial analysis. Documented facts are cited to the sources in Section 13; readings of the character's function are the archive's own.

Function: professionalism without a cause

The analysis the archive records follows the Galaxy page's own verdict: Sona is the best employee in the film working for its worst man, and the film never suggests she has noticed. She exists to pose the question Su and Daria answer by their choices — what is competence worth, unexamined? — and her thin file is the question's form: the plot uses her constantly and knows her not at all.

An honest limit

The archive notes plainly that the documented plot record does not resolve Sona's fate beyond the hangar duel. Rather than import an ending, her page keeps the record's edge where the record puts it.
