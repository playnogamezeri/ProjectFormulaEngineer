01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Vanessa Fait is eight years old, and she is the reason the film exists. Her father's code — steal clean, get home for the goodnight — is built around her; when his score brings home a weapon disguised as diamonds, Ling collects the debt in the only currency Fait cannot refuse: Vanessa herself, taken and held in a van at the airport hangar as the auction's guarantee. What distinguishes her file is what the Galaxy page insists on: she is not a passive stake. Bound and gagged with duct tape, she frees herself, finds a cell phone, and calls her father with the clues that locate her — the rescue's decisive act is the victim's own.

ROLE

The stake made literal

The plot's object and its moral centre; the kidnapped daughter whose rescue organises every other arc.

DEFINING TRAIT

Resourcefulness under tape

Freed bonds, a found phone, a dying battery used exactly right: the film's youngest character performs its most efficient escape.

FATE

Rescued, by a team she joined

Freed at the hangar as the auction falls; her father promises, over her safety, that the career is over.

Place in the story

Vanessa's page is the film's centre of value: the Galaxy record states it plainly — she is what the entire machinery of agents, thieves and auctions is finally about, and the film measures every adult in it by their distance from her. Her file is kept honest about her screen time: she is a child in a handful of scenes, and the largest of them is her own escape.

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

Vanessa Fait is the eight-year-old daughter of Anthony Fait, and the centre of the home his professional code is built to protect — the goodnight he steals to get home for. When his crew's heist lifts the black diamonds and the stones' owner comes collecting, Ling takes the leverage that cannot be refused: Vanessa is kidnapped and held, bound and gagged with duct tape, in a van at the airport hangar where the auction will be held.

Her captivity is the film's hinge, and she works it herself: she frees herself from the tape, finds a cell phone in the van, and calls her father. Before the battery dies she gives him the clues to her location — enough for the crew to identify the hangar. The rescue that follows is therefore partly her own operation: Su, Fait and the crew storm the auction, Ling is killed, and Vanessa is freed. As the police arrive, her father promises to end his criminal career — the safe and happy life, pledged over the child who helped secure it.

09 / ANALYSIS & INVESTIGATION

Analysis & Investigation

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial analysis. Documented facts are cited to the sources in Section 13; readings of the character's function are the archive's own.

Function: the stake that acts

The analysis the archive records: kidnapped-child plots usually reduce the child to a photograph and a deadline. This film gives its stake the rescue's pivotal act — self-liberation and the locating call — and thereby keeps its moral arithmetic honest: Vanessa is the centre of value and a participant in her own recovery, and every adult's arc is graded against her standard.
