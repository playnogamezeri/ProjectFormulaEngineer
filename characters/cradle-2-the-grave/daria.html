01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Daria is Anthony Fait's second — in the crew's work and, as the Galaxy page frames it, his would-be partner in every sense. She runs the diamond heist at his side, escapes the SWAT blockade with the crew, and when the score recoils into a kidnapping, her place in the hunt is defined by what she is actually defending: not a bag of stones but the household — Fait, Vanessa, the family business of the crew. At the hangar she takes the finale's parallel duel, Sona, hand to hand, while the men settle the main account. The archive files her as the crew's adult: the one whose loyalty needs no converting.

ROLE

The second

Principal supporting; Fait's lieutenant in the crew and the household's other pillar.

DEFINING TRAIT

Loyalty without negotiation

Everyone else in the film is converted, coerced or paid; Daria is simply there from the first frame, for the family rather than the take.

FATE

Standing at the rescue

She fights Sona at the hangar and is present for Vanessa's rescue and the crew's survival of the night.

Place in the story

Daria carries the crew-as-family theme in person. Fait's page is the father's ledger; Daria's is the household's — the proof that what Ling kidnapped into was not a gang but a family business, with her as its second adult.

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

Daria is Fait's second in the crew that robs the diamond exchange for Christophe — Daria, Tommy and Miles under Fait's lead. She gathers the take with them, black diamonds included, and escapes the SWAT blockade when Su's warning goes unheeded and the police close in; Miles alone is captured. Through the appraisal and its unravelling — Archie's surrender of the stones, the gangsters, the jailed Chambers — Daria is the crew's constant.

When Ling kidnaps Vanessa, the crew's hunt becomes Daria's cause in its purest form: the stones were a job, but Vanessa is family. She is with the crew through the nightclub raid and the race to the airport, and at the hangar, while Su faces Ling and Fait fights for his daughter, Daria takes the parallel duel — Sona, Ling's enforcer, hand to hand — the two seconds of the two organisations settling their own account in the main event's margin. Vanessa is rescued; the crew survives the night whole.

The biography above is an editorial paraphrase of the documented plot accounts (Wikipedia and the cited sources), not a scene-by-scene transcript.

04 / PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Psychology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation, grounded in the character's on-screen actions.

The constant

Interpretation: Daria is the only principal whose allegiance the film never has to argue for. Su must be converted, Archie coerced, Fait provoked — Daria's loyalty is the story's fixed point, and the film uses her stillness the way it uses Su's: as a measure.

The household's adult

Her stake is stated by the Galaxy page and this archive adopts it: not the stones but the household. Interpretation: she is the proof that Fait's domesticity is shared, not solitary — the family the kidnapping threatens has two adults.

Competence, unadvertised

She runs a heist, survives a blockade and fights the finale's undercard without the film ever pausing to certify her — the archive records the economy as respect.

09 / ANALYSIS & INVESTIGATION

Analysis & Investigation

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial analysis. Documented facts are cited to the sources in Section 13; readings of the character's function are the archive's own.

Function: the family business, embodied

The analysis the archive records follows the Galaxy page: Daria's stake is not the stones but the household the stones endangered. She is the evidence that Fait's crew is a family with a trade rather than a gang with a leader — and therefore the evidence that Ling's kidnapping was aimed at a home, which is what raises the film's stakes above its MacGuffin.
