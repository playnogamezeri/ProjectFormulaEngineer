01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Anthony Fait runs a clean crew — Daria, Tommy and Miles — on a professional code: steal, don't shoot, get home. The heist that opens the film is commissioned by a French middleman, Christophe, and its real cargo is a bag of black diamonds nobody priced correctly. When the stones turn out to be a weapon, their owner turns out to be a rogue agent named Ling, and Ling's collection method turns out to be Fait's eight-year-old daughter, the film's two plots — the score and the family — collapse into one. Fait's story is the Galaxy page's portrait exactly: the professional criminal as working father, whose competence and vulnerability are the same fact — a man with something to lose.

ROLE

The thief

Co-protagonist; crew leader whose score triggers the plot and whose daughter becomes its stake.

DEFINING TRAIT

Professionalism as fatherhood

He steals without guns and keeps a home worth returning to; the film measures every danger against the goodnight he owes Vanessa.

FATE

Reformed, on a promise

Vanessa rescued, Ling dead, and Fait promises the arriving police — and himself — that the career is over.

Place in the story

Fait is the film's heart in the literal sense the plot enforces: every machine in the story — the heist, the intelligence hunt, the auction — is finally aimed at his household. His page carries the film's emotional ledger, against Su's procedural one.

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

Anthony Fait and his crew — Daria, Tommy and Miles — rob a diamond exchange for the French broker Christophe, lifting among the take a bag of black diamonds. Mid-heist, a Taiwanese agent named Su phones Fait and orders him to leave the stones; Fait refuses, and the crew escapes past a SWAT blockade, though Miles is captured and his share recovered. Fait takes the black diamonds to his friend Archie for appraisal.

That night the score turns: Fait meets Su in person; Ling — the stones' true owner, Christophe's employer — phones demanding them, and sends henchmen Fait and Su beat off together. Archie confesses that gangsters have already taken the stones from him at threat of his life. Then Ling's second call lands: he has kidnapped Vanessa. Fait's hunt for the stones is now a father's deadline. He confronts the jailed crime lord 'Jump' Chambers, whose gangsters robbed Archie; raids Chambers's nightclub; and learns with the crew that the stones sit in bubble bath in Chambers's office — only to arrive after Ling's men have taken them.

Vanessa, bound in a van, frees herself, finds a phone, and calls her father with clues to her location before the battery dies: an airport hangar. The crew reads the flight schedules, finds the airport where the private buyers are landing, and storms Ling's auction. While Su settles Ling, Fait and his crew fight through the hangar team and Vanessa is rescued. As the police arrive, Fait promises to end his criminal career and give his daughter the safe life the film has spent two hours pricing.

The biography above is an editorial paraphrase of the documented plot accounts (Wikipedia and the cited sources), not a scene-by-scene transcript.

04 / PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Psychology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation, grounded in the character's on-screen actions.

The code

Interpretation: Fait's professionalism — no guns, clean scores, home by bedtime — is not a thief's vanity but a father's risk management. The film's tension is that the code fails anyway: the danger comes home precisely because he was good at the work.

Refusal as first instinct

He refuses Su's warning, refuses Ling's demand, refuses Chambers's silence — Fait's character is a series of refusals that escalate his danger and define his dignity in the same gesture.

The father, underneath

Every negotiation he conducts is shadowed by Vanessa. Interpretation: the film never lets his competence detach from its purpose; he is the rare heist lead whose skill is entirely in service of a household.

08 / SYMBOLISM & THEMES

Symbolism & Themes

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation.

The goodnight

Interpretation: Fait's domestic ritual — home in time to say goodnight — is the film's recurring measure of what his work is for, and the kidnapping is staged as its violation: the one appointment the score makes him miss.

The no-guns code

Interpretation: his crew's refusal of firearms is the film's moral bookkeeping — the thieves are priced as professionals, the agents' world as the violent one — and the finale's plutonium settles the comparison absolutely.

09 / ANALYSIS & INVESTIGATION

Analysis & Investigation

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial analysis. Documented facts are cited to the sources in Section 13; readings of the character's function are the archive's own.

Function: the stake made literal

The analysis the archive records, following the Galaxy page: Vanessa is what the entire machinery of agents, thieves and auctions is finally about, and Fait is the man the machinery is aimed at. His dual role — the plot's trigger and its target — is the film's structural originality inside a standard hybrid: the MacGuffin's thief and the hostage's father are the same man.

DMX at the centre

Contemporary reviews were mixed on the film and kind to its lead's presence; the archive records the design plainly — Bartkowiak and Silver built three films on this pairing of a hip-hop lead's charisma with Li's discipline, and Fait is the fullest character DMX was given inside the formula.

11 / BEHIND THE CHARACTER

Behind the Character

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

DMX

DMX (Earl Simmons) takes his most substantial acting role as Fait; ScreenDaily's contemporary review called it a decent job with his brooding screen presence, at the centre of a film that opened at number one in North America.

The trilogy

Cradle 2 the Grave is the third of the Bartkowiak–Silver martial-arts/hip-hop films, after Romeo Must Die (2000) and Exit Wounds (2001), sharing cast, crew and locations.

Production

The soundtrack album, featuring DMX and others, reached the US top ten and was certified Gold — part of the film's documented commercial footprint.

12 / LEGACY & SIGNIFICANCE

Legacy & Significance

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Fait is the fullest screen character of DMX's acting career and the emotional template for the action era's working-father criminals. Within the Galaxy record, his page anchors the film's claim that its hybrid worked: a heist picture whose engine is a bedtime promise.

The archive also records the ending as the film leaves it — a promise made to police and daughter alike, its keeping left, properly, beyond the frame.
