01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Tommy drives for Fait's crew, and talks for them too — the film's comic engine, played by Anthony Anderson in the second of his three Bartkowiak pictures. He is in the vault for the heist, out past the blockade in the escape, and through the hunt for the stones and the child. But his signature is the ending the Galaxy page records: in the closing movement, Tommy is already pitching the adventure as a movie — the survivor as narrator, converting danger into anecdote while it is still warm. In a film about men who may not live to retire, he is the one who has already decided how the story will be told.

ROLE

The driver

Supporting principal; the crew's wheelman and voice.

DEFINING TRAIT

Danger, converted to anecdote

His comedy is a survival technology — and, in the end credits, a literary one: he is the crew's first historian.

FATE

The pitch

He survives the hangar and closes the film pitching it as a movie, opposite Archie, in the credits' running gag.

Place in the story

Tommy is the film's pressure valve and its memory. His page is kept at his true size in the story — a supporting principal whose function the Galaxy page names exactly: the survivor as narrator, the man already turning the worst night of their lives into the story they will tell.

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

Tommy is the driver of Fait's crew — with Daria and Miles — in the diamond-exchange heist for Christophe. He is at the wheel and in the talk through the robbery and the escape past the SWAT blockade, when Miles is captured and the crew scatters with the take. Through the stones' unravelling — Archie's surrender of them, the Chambers hunt, the nightclub — Tommy is the crew's constant motion and commentary.

When Vanessa is kidnapped, Tommy's comedy acquires its edge: the jokes continue, now as the crew's way of keeping a father's deadline bearable. He is with the crew at the hangar for the storming of the auction and Vanessa's rescue. And in the end credits, opposite Archie, Tommy does what the Galaxy page says defines him: he pitches the whole adventure as a movie, casting it as he goes — the survivor converting the danger into anecdote before the bodies are cold.

04 / PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Psychology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation, grounded in the character's on-screen actions.

The talker

Interpretation: Tommy's mouth is the crew's morale system. The film gives its fear and its wonder to him to say aloud, which lets the quieter characters stay quiet — a division of emotional labour he performs without being asked.

Loyalty, unceremonious

He never makes a speech about the family; he simply drives toward every danger the family faces. Interpretation: his constancy is Daria's, in a comic register — the two of them are the crew's fixed points.

The born narrator

The credits pitch is not a gag pasted on: it is the character completing himself. The man who narrated the danger all film finally narrates it as a story — the archive records it as his arc's true ending.

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Anderson plays Tommy in the second of his three films for Bartkowiak and Silver (Romeo Must Die, Exit Wounds, Cradle 2 the Grave), sharing much of the same cast and crew.

12 / LEGACY & SIGNIFICANCE

Legacy & Significance

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Tommy stands in the Galaxy record as the Bartkowiak trilogy's comic signature and the model for the era's driver-talker supporting role. His credits pitch remains one of the period's most affectionately remembered fourth-wall touches.
