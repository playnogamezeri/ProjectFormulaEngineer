01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Rico is the penguin unit's ordnance and warehousing in one body: a near-wordless specialist who produces dynamite, tools and weaponry by regurgitation, on demand, without inventory or explanation. The film makes his stomach the plot's evidence locker. Confronted by Dave in the submarine, Rico swallows a vial of the Medusa Serum and Dave's collection of snow globes — one from every zoo that discarded the octopus — and those two swallowed objects become, respectively, the sample that proves the threat and the collection Kowalski reads as a target list. The unit's least verbal member carries its most consequential cargo.

ROLE

Weapons officer and logistics

The unit's armoury, demolitions and storage — all internal.

DEFINING TRAIT

Capacity

Rico's gift is not any single weapon but unlimited, unquestioning room: whatever the mission needs swallowed, carried or produced, fits.

FATE

Restored and honoured

Mutated and restored in New York; ends the film jetpacked with the unit, his cargo having cracked the case.

Place in the story

Rico is the unit's body in the most literal sense — its stomach, its strongbox, its supply chain. The film's plot mechanics run through him: no swallowed globes, no deduction; no swallowed serum, no proof. His page records the specialist as infrastructure, and the one film where the infrastructure is also the evidence.

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

Rico is one of the three chicks who break from the colony in the Antarctic prologue, fighting leopard seals aboard the wrecked whaling ship to save the stray egg, and one of the three who adopt the hatchling as Private. A decade later he is the unit's demolitions and storage: present at the Fort Knox break-in, kidnapped with the team to Dave's submarine off Venice.

In the submarine comes his defining act of the film. As Dave displays his grievance — and his collection of snow globes, one from every zoo and aquarium that passed him on — Rico swallows the collection and a vial of the Medusa Serum before the unit fights its way out through Dave's octopus henchmen and the canals of Venice. Rescued by the North Wind and then deported by it, Rico produces the globes again on the escape plane's route, and Kowalski reads them as what they are: Dave's itinerary of rejection, and therefore his target list — Shanghai next.

At Shanghai Rico supports the trap that catches Dave in the dinosaur skeleton; after Dave's escape with Private, he joins the hijacking of the North Wind jet and the submarine infiltration, is captured with both teams, and is among the penguins mutated and unleashed on New York. Private restores him, Kowalski and Skipper to sanity with Dave's ray, and Rico fights in the final battle while Private powers the full reversal — ending the film restored, commended by association, and airborne on the North Wind's gifted jetpacks.

The biography above is an editorial paraphrase of the documented plot accounts (Wikipedia and the cited sources), not a scene-by-scene transcript.

04 / PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Psychology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation, grounded in the character's on-screen actions.

Enthusiasm as a complete psychology

Rico's inner life is conducted at the level of appetite and delight: explosions please him, swallowing is his reflex answer to valuables, and the film never asks him for a second register. Interpretation: he is the id of the unit, licensed and beloved.

Trust without audit

He swallows first and never asks; the serum vial goes down with the souvenirs. The unit's faith in Rico is the reciprocal of his faith in the unit — neither side inspects the arrangement, and the plot is built on its reliability.

08 / SYMBOLISM & THEMES

Symbolism & Themes

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation.

The body as archive

Rico literalises the film's theme: what a creature has swallowed — grievances, souvenirs, evidence — is what it carries into every later scene. Dave collects globes on shelves; Rico collects them in himself.

The specialist as infrastructure

He symbolises the unglamorous truth of every elite unit in the film, the North Wind included: operations run on the member who carries things.

09 / ANALYSIS & INVESTIGATION

Analysis & Investigation

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial analysis. Documented facts are cited to the sources in Section 13; readings of the character's function are the archive's own.

Plot mechanics made flesh

Rico is the rare character who is also the story's filing system. The serum sample establishes the threat's chemistry; the globes establish its geography; both exist in the plot because Rico ate them. The film's screenwriting trusts a gag — the penguin who swallows everything — to do the work other films give to laboratories and databases.

The recast and the register

DiMaggio's television Rico was more verbal; the film's Rico, in Vernon's near-wordless performance, is pushed toward pure appetite and reaction. The change suits the feature's economy: three penguins talk, one penguin acts, and the division of labour is the unit's comedy engine.

11 / BEHIND THE CHARACTER

Behind the Character

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

A change of voice

Wikipedia records the recast plainly: Conrad Vernon voices Rico in the film, replacing John DiMaggio, who voiced the character in the television series. The film keeps Rico at his least verbal — grunts, cackles and appetite — making the recast nearly seamless by design.

The obsession, billed

The cast listing's own description of Rico — 'obsessed with big booms' — is the character sheet the performance follows: demolitions as personality, storage as plot function.

12 / LEGACY & SIGNIFICANCE

Legacy & Significance

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Rico is the Madagascar penguins' purest gag given a body: the living equipment locker. This film is his most consequential outing — the entry where the locker holds the evidence and the joke becomes the investigation.

Within the ensemble he remains the fourth voice and first resort: when the unit needs something impossible carried, Rico has already swallowed it.
