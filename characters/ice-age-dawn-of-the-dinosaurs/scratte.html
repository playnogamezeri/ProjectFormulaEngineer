Scratte

A female saber-toothed squirrel — glamorous, acrobatic, armed with the full cartoon arsenal of the femme fatale — whose pursuit of Scrat's acorn (or of Scrat; the film keeps her motives at silent-film ambiguity) gives the saga's oldest subplot its first second character.

01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Scratte arrives in the franchise like a genre arriving: the eyelashes, the glide, the acrobatic theft performed as dance. She is the first creature in three films who operates at Scrat's level and in Scrat's grammar — the acorn is her object too, and the film never finally adjudicates whether she wants the nut, the squirrel, or the game. What it adjudicates is the ending: courted, wed in miniature, and then ranked second to an acorn in the open, Scratte answers with the subplot's most decisive act — she seals the door between the worlds with Scrat on the wrong side of it, keeping the lost world, the acorn and the last word.

ROLE

The subplot's second character

Principal of the Scrat sequences; rival, beloved and final judge of the saga's eternal chase.

DEFINING TRAIT

Ambiguity, weaponised

Disher plays her without a word, in eyelash and timing: the film never settles whether the acorn or the squirrel is the prize, because Scratte never settles it where anyone can see.

FATE

The door, sealed

She remains in the lost world with the acorn, having closed its entrance on the suitor who ranked her second: the subplot's only winner.

Place in the story

Scratte is the mirror the franchise's id had never faced: another appetite, better dressed. Her page is the record of the subplot's power shift — for the first time, Scrat's story has a second author, and she writes the ending.

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

Scratte enters the subplot as a descent: gliding down on the acorn like weather, and taking it with an acrobat's ease that announces the chase's new terms. The early movement is a duel of thefts between equals — the acorn passing paw to paw, rescues extended and robbed in the same gesture — with the film carefully refusing to price her motives: the nut, the squirrel and the sport are all in play, and her eyelashes give nothing away.

The duel settles, by degrees neither party announces, into the idyll: a home in the lost world, a shared life, the acorn retired to the status of furniture. Scratte in domesticity is the subplot's gentlest invention — the femme fatale at rest, the rivalry's weapons hung up. The film lets the idyll stand just long enough to be a life, and then stages the audit: the acorn calls Scrat back, and he chooses it, in the open, over her.

Her answer is the subplot's last word and its only act of state: as the worlds separate, Scratte seals the entrance to the lost world with Scrat stranded on the surface — keeping the acorn, the world and the ending. She exits the film as she entered it, in command of the terms: the chase's first winner, because she is the first player who was willing to end the game.

04 / PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Psychology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation, grounded in the character's on-screen actions.

The unreadable prize-fighter

Interpretation: Scratte's ambiguity is her armour and her honesty at once — she may genuinely not rank the nut and the squirrel herself, until the moment he ranks them for her, at which point her clarity is total.

The professional

Her thefts are cleaner than Scrat's, her glide is better than his leap, her composure survives defeats his never does: the subplot's competence, in eyelashes.

Scorn, as statecraft

The sealed door is not spite but policy: a border closed by the party who was ranked second, with the prize kept on her side of it. The film gives her the subplot's only decisive act and lets it stand as justified.

09 / ANALYSIS & INVESTIGATION

Analysis & Investigation

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial analysis. Documented facts are cited to the sources in Section 13; readings of the character's function are the archive's own.

Function: the second author

For three films Scrat's subplot had one character and one object; Scratte converts it into a relationship, and relationships — unlike chases — can be ended by either party. The archive records her as the franchise's first agent of closure in the margins: the herd's films end by gathering, hers ends by sealing, and the symmetry is the subplot's whole argument about what Scrat's appetite costs when someone finally sends it a bill.
