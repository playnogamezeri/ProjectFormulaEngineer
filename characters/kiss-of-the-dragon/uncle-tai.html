01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Tai is the infrastructure of decency in a captured city, and the film prices him accordingly. An old man keeping a seafood shop in a rough quarter of Paris, he is in truth a sleeper supporter of the Chinese service — Liu's base of operations, set before the officer ever lands: the safe room, the local knowledge, the quiet old pair of hands at the end of the line. Liu falls back on the shop twice, and the shop's scenes are the film's warmest: wounds sewn at the counter, Jessica's story heard under his roof, a pimp disciplined among his stock. Then the jurisdiction finds the address. Tai comes home with groceries and disinfectants for his wounded guest, and Richard's right-hand man Max strafes the shop with machine-gun fire — the keeper gunned down in his own doorway, the message about shelter delivered in the city's plainest language. Liu kills Max in the same doorway, and the film's errand changes key from that moment: the refuge's death is the point where the frame stops being a professional problem. The archive files Tai as the run's study of the support figure as the story's moral collateral — the man whose whole function is to make decency possible in Paris, and whose murder is how the film announces what decency costs there.

ROLE

The sleeper and the safe room

Liu's base in Paris, set before he lands: the shop is the film's one address where the framed man is a guest instead of a quarry, and its keeper is the reason.

DEFINING TRAIT

Decency as cover, cover as decency

A seafood shop, an old man, a neighbourhood that knows him: the archive files the ordinariness as the craft — the perfect sleeper is the one whose cover and whose character are the same man.

FATE

Gunned down in his doorway

Shot by Richard's man Max as he returns with groceries and disinfectants for his guest: the safe room's price, paid by its keeper, and avenged in the same doorway by the guest he died sheltering.

Place in the story

Tai's page is the archive's record of the film's smallest principal and its heaviest death: three scenes of shelter and one doorway. The archive files him beside the run's other infrastructure figures — the keepers of the safe rooms — with the distinction that his film makes the keeper's murder its hinge: everything Liu does after the shop is done in a dead host's city.

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

Tai's biography, as the record gives it, is a cover maintained for years and a function discharged in days. The sleeper's life before Liu's arrival is the shop itself — the archive follows the record in declining to furnish him a furnished past: he is an old Chinese man in Paris, a seafood keeper in a quarter the vice squad farms, known to the street, invisible to the office, which is precisely the biography a sleeper is supposed to have. When the service's officer is dispatched to Paris, the base is already standing: Liu reports to the shop on arrival, sets his operation there, and the film's first movement runs on Tai's infrastructure — the local ground, the quiet rooms, the old man's unremarkable face at the counter of the one address in Paris where the Chinese officer is expected and welcome.

His middle is the refuge, twice kept. After the frame and the Seine — the liaison dead, the tape retaken, the fugitive wounded and the whole jurisdiction hunting — Liu falls back on the shop, and the shop becomes the film's heart for its longest quiet passage: Jessica brought in off the street, her needlework closing the officer's wound at his counter, her story told under his roof, Lupo's intrusion answered among his stock. The archive records the scenes' design: Tai is mostly present as the room itself — the keeper whose premises make the film's only honest conversations possible — and his one errand out of it is the fatal one, the ordinary kindness of a host going out for disinfectants and groceries for the wounded man under his roof.

His death is the frame's price, itemised. He comes home through his own doorway with the bags in his arms, and Max — Richard's right-hand man, who has watched the shop since Lupo's beating advertised its guest — opens fire with a machine gun, strafing the premises and gunning the old man down where he stands. Liu kills Max in the same doorway, with the shop's own implements to hand — the archive follows the review record's plain account: chopsticks, at close quarters, in answer — and the refuge is finished as a fact of the city: the address is known, the keeper is dead, the safe room is a crime scene. The film's remaining movements have no shelter in them, and the archive files that as Tai's posthumous function: his death is the hinge on which Liu's errand turns from evidence to rescue, from the professional's patience to the endgame's arithmetic — the orphanage, the station, the forbidden point. The old sleeper's file closes where his shop does, with his guest standing in his doorway over his killer's body, in a city that has just been told, in machine-gun fire, what it charges for decency.

04 / PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Psychology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation, grounded in the character's on-screen actions.

The host's calm

Interpretation: Tai's manner is a neighbourhood's — unhurried, dry, unsurprised by anything the street brings through his door, including a framed intelligence officer and a prostitute with a needle and thread. The archive reads the calm as the sleeper's true tradecraft: a man who has made himself so ordinary that catastrophe has to announce itself with a machine gun before his street believes it.

Care as function

His one recorded errand is for disinfectants and groceries — the archive files the shopping list as the character: the support figure's whole ethic in two items, provisions for a wounded guest, bought with his life on the way home.

05 / ABILITIES & SKILLS

Abilities, Skills & Weaknesses

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
THE COVER

A life, maintained

Years of unremarkable shopkeeping as a foreign service's Paris base: the archive counts the endurance as the ability — anyone can be a sleeper for a week; Tai has been one long enough to be the street's furniture, which is the only kind of sleeper who survives until he is needed.

THE SAFE ROOM

Shelter as a system

Rooms, local knowledge, a counter where wounds get sewn and stories get heard: the shop shelters the fugitive twice and incubates the alliance that ends the frame. The archive files the premises themselves among the film's decisive assets.

WEAKNESS

The fixed address

A safe room is a place, and places can be watched: Lupo's beating advertises the guest, Max's surveillance finds the door, and the keeper's own errands take him through it unarmed. The archive records the weakness as the role's definition — infrastructure cannot run.

08 / SYMBOLISM & THEMES

Symbolism & Themes

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation.

The doorway

Interpretation: Tai lives behind a shop door and dies in it — the archive reads the threshold as the character's whole file in one image: the boundary between the captured city and the one safe room in it, held by an old man with shopping bags, and crossed at last by machine-gun fire. Everything after in the film is outside, in the weather Richard makes.

The groceries

Interpretation: the disinfectants in the dead man's arms are the film's smallest and heaviest props — the archive files them against Liu's needles and Jessica's stitches as the story's third medicine: the provisions of care, bought by the keeper, undelivered. The film's economy of care is completed by its cost.

09 / ANALYSIS & INVESTIGATION

Analysis & Investigation

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial analysis. Documented facts are cited to the sources in Section 13; readings of the character's function are the archive's own.

Function: the collateral that reprices the plot

Tai exists to be the frame's first innocent price. Before the shop, Liu's problem is professional — evidence, jurisdiction, procedure; after the doorway, it is personal, and the film's endgame (the solo station assault, the forbidden point) is authorised by the audience's arithmetic in a way the tape never could authorise it. The analysis the archive records is that the film spends its warmest minor character with full knowledge of the exchange rate: one old man's death buys the climax its moral licence.

The sleeper as the service's conscience

Against the delegation — which believes Liu and cannot act — Tai is the Chinese service as it should be: present, local, useful, and mortal. The archive notes the contrast as the film's institutional grading, applied to both services at once: Paris's police are an apparatus of crime, Beijing's local man is a shopkeeper who dies buying bandages.

11 / BEHIND THE CHARACTER

Behind the Character

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Burt Kwouk

Kwouk, beloved from the Pink Panther films and a fixture of British screens, brought the production an instantly trusted face for its safe room — the archive records the casting's function plainly: the audience must believe in the shop's safety at sight, so that the strafing lands as the film's desecration.

The shop set

The seafood shop was built as the film's one warm interior — wood, steam, stock and lamplight against the hotel's and station's glass and steel: the archive notes the design as the production's geography of the story, the captured city's two architectures and the old man's small third one between them.
