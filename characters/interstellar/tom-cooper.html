01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Tom is the film's Earth-bound tragedy in its plainest form: the son who inherited the farm instead of the sky, and who keeps the century's bargain — stay, work, endure — long after the bargain has stopped paying anyone. Chalamet plays the boy (competent, quiet, already half the farmer his father is training him to be) and Affleck the man: a husband and father in a poisoned house, who has buried one child from the dust in his lungs, whose wife and surviving son are coughing toward the same account, and who still will not leave — because leaving the farm means admitting the life was a mistake, and the life is all his father left him. His refusal, and Murph's desperate arson that breaks it, are the Earth story's climax: the stay-at-home son as the dying world's most faithful and most endangered citizen.

ROLE

The inheritor of the soil

Principal supporting; Cooper's son, the farm's keeper, the Earth story's domestic centre.

DEFINING TRAIT

Endurance curdled into refusal

His virtue and his danger are the same trait at different decades: he does not quit — not the crop, not the house, not the bargain.

FATE

Saved against his will

Murph's fire draws him from the farm while she evacuates his family; his fate beyond the evacuation the film leaves, pointedly, to the history his sister makes.

Place in the story

Tom is the film's counterweight to every mile flown: the proof that staying was also a heroism, and also a trap. His page is the record of the Galaxy's most grounded casualty — the son the century used up in place.

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

Tom at fifteen is his father's apprentice in the only trade the century permits: he drives, repairs, plants, and receives — in the truck, in the fields — Cooper's departing charge, to keep the farm and watch over his sister. The messages he sends the Endurance across the decades are the Earth story's ledger: the farm held, a marriage (to Lois), children, Donald's death reported in a farmer's few words — and then the entries darken: his son Jesse's lungs fail in the dust; the boy is buried by the farm he was born to inherit; Lois and the surviving child cough in a house the air is poisoning by degrees. Tom's creed does not bend. The farm is the family's meaning; the blight and the dust are weather; a Cooper does not run.

The confrontation comes when Murph — returned from Brand's deathbed with the truth and a plan — begs him to bring his family to the facility before the air finishes them. He refuses her at the farmhouse door with the whole weight of his life behind the refusal: she left for the sky's people and their equations; he stayed and buried his son in this ground; the ground is not negotiable. Her answer is the Earth story's most desperate act of love: she sets his corn alight, drawing him out to fight the fire, and uses his absence to get Lois and the boy into a truck and away. The film leaves Tom at the fire's edge — his sister's arson as his rescue, his creed burned as his salvation — and does not show his ending: the history Murph is about to make will lift the stations and empty the farms, and the archive records his fate as the film leaves it, unresolved by design: the stayer, overtaken by the future his family built without asking him.

04 / PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Psychology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation, grounded in the character's on-screen actions.

The creed of staying

Interpretation: Tom's refusal is his father's charge fossilised — keep the farm, keep the family — obeyed past the point where obedience serves anyone. The film treats the fossilisation as the dying world's characteristic tragedy: its virtues, continued into its poison, become its dangers.

Grief without a language

He buries a son and reports it in a message of a few flat sentences: the archive notes Affleck's restraint as the character's definition — Tom feels everything in the register of work, and the work is failing, and there is no second register available to him.

The brother of the sky-sister

His estrangement from Murph is the century's split in one family: the soil and the sky stopped being able to save each other, and the siblings' love survives only as her arson — help he would never have accepted, given in the only form left.

FARMING

The keeper's craft

He holds a farm productive decades into the blight: the Earth story's one unbroken professional record.

ENDURANCE

The stayer's stamina

Losses that break the film's other characters — a child, a father, a world — are absorbed by Tom into the next season's work.

LOYALTY

The charge, kept

His father's instructions are executed for decades at ruinous personal cost: the film's most literal promise-keeper, which is precisely his tragedy.

WEAKNESS

The unupdatable creed

He cannot revise the instruction when the world revises its terms: the virtue that kept the farm becomes the refusal that nearly keeps his family in the poison.

08 / SYMBOLISM & THEMES

Symbolism & Themes

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation.

The farmhouse

Interpretation: Tom's house — the same rooms as the bookcase, the ghost, the childhood — becomes in his keeping a fortress of the past: the film's sacred site defended as a tomb, until his sister burns the fields around it to prove that the past's house can still be escaped from.

The buried child

Interpretation: Jesse's grave by the farm is the dying Earth's smallest and heaviest fact — the future, interred in the soil it was meant to inherit — and the archive files it as the fact Tom's creed could not metabolise and Murph's fire finally answered.

09 / ANALYSIS & INVESTIGATION

Analysis & Investigation

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial analysis. Documented facts are cited to the sources in Section 13; readings of the character's function are the archive's own.

Function: the cost of staying

Tom exists so the film's celebration of leaving is honestly priced: for every Cooper who flies, a Tom keeps the farm, buries the child, and refuses the rescue. The analysis the archive records is that the film honours him even in his refusal — his creed was his father's gift and his century's demand — and that Murph's arson is staged not as his defeat but as the family's love changing tactics: when persuasion fails, the Coopers save each other by force of fire.

12 / LEGACY & SIGNIFICANCE

Legacy & Significance

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Tom became the reference figure for the left-behind in exploration stories — the stayer whose fidelity the future both honours and overruns — and his unresolved ending remains one of the film's most debated mercies: the archive records that the film simply does not tell us, and that the not-telling is the point.

Within the Galaxy, his file stands in the line of the grounded: characters whose heroism never leaves their own fields, filed as the price-list of every launch.
