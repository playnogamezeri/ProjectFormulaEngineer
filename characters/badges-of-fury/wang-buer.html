01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Wang Bu'er is the film's actual protagonist under its top-billed star: Wen Zhang carries the screen time, the investigation and the comedy, as a young detective whose name — Wang Bu'er, glossed by Wikipedia as literally 'not stupid Wang' — is the joke his conduct keeps telling on itself. He is impulsive, disobedient and accident-prone, partnered with the veteran Huang Feihong as a check on his brashness and supervised by Angela, with whom he bickers through the case. But the case is his: he is the one who states, shockingly and correctly, that the grinning deaths are a serial murder; he works the suspect list from Liu Jinshui to her sister; and when the trail needs a target, he supplies himself, posing as the actress's new boyfriend to draw the killer into the open.

ROLE

Young detective; the case's engine

Protagonist in function; Huang's partner, Angela's subordinate, and the investigation's theorist and bait.

DEFINING TRAIT

Zeal ahead of skill

Wang's confidence outruns his competence in every register except pattern-recognition — the one the case rewards.

FATE

In the trap, as the trap

The documented record ends with Wang posing as Liu Jinshui's boyfriend, the danger rising the closer he gets to the truth.

Place in the story

Wang is the film's thesis about detection in a comedy precinct: the buffoon is the only one reading the evidence correctly. His page records the double act's working half — the partner who does the labour the legend's camouflage declines — and the investigation's documented arc, which this archive is careful to end where its sources end: at the bait, with the killer still offstage.

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

Wang is introduced inside the unit's ordinary disgrace: the stakeout of the trafficker Chen Hu, whose case bookends the department's opinion of Angela's team. Wang's zeal is the unit's hazard — impulsive, disobedient to orders on the ground, and forever landing himself and his partner in hot water, from which Huang Feihong's concealed mastery extracts him. The partnership's terms are set early: Wang charges; Huang insures.

Three deaths in three days change the register. A movie star dies during filming, a ballroom dancer during a dance, a diver from his board — each corpse grinning. Where the deaths might read as grotesque accidents, Wang makes the shocking statement that defines the case: this is a serial murder. The pattern resolves around a person: every victim had dated the rising actress Liu Jinshui. A fourth victim, found embedded in a block of concrete with the signature smile, makes her the prime suspect — and Wang's investigation then clears her: she is found to be innocent, the pattern's centre rather than its author.

Suspicion shifts to the sister, Dai Yiyi — the former darts champion with the voodoo practice and the history of taking Jinshui's boyfriends — among others. With the trail stalling, Wang reaches the plan the synopses end on: he will pose as Liu Jinshui's boyfriend, making himself the next name on the killer's list, and lure the murderer out. The documented record closes there, on the warning that gives the plan its price: the closer he gets to the truth, the greater the danger he is in. This archive does not carry him past it.

04 / PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Psychology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation, grounded in the character's on-screen actions.

Arrogance as method

Wang's certainty is unearned in general and indispensable in particular: the same refusal to defer that makes him a disciplinary problem makes him the only detective willing to call three accidents a serial murder. Interpretation: the film argues that pattern-recognition and insubordination are the same trait at different addresses.

The clown's courage

His final documented act is not a gag: volunteering as bait is the case's bravest decision, taken by its least skilful officer. The comedy never cancels the fact that the buffoon is the one who steps into the pattern.

08 / SYMBOLISM & THEMES

Symbolism & Themes

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation.

The name

Wang Bu'er — "not stupid Wang" — symbolises the film's epistemology: the fool's reading of events is the correct one, and the name is the alibi the film gives him in advance.

The volunteer

He symbolises detection as exposure: when the evidence runs out, the detective becomes the evidence, offering himself to the pattern to make it show its hand.

09 / ANALYSIS & INVESTIGATION

Analysis & Investigation

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial analysis. Documented facts are cited to the sources in Section 13; readings of the character's function are the archive's own.

The protagonist in the passenger billing

Wang is the clearest case in this Galaxy of billing inverting function: the star is support, the support is the lead. The character is built to sustain that — he holds the investigation, the romance-adjacent bait plot and the comedy simultaneously — and the reviews' consensus that the film rises and falls on Wen Zhang's energy is a judgment on the character's construction as much as the performance.

What the bait plan says

Posing as the boyfriend is the investigation's logical end and its ethical exposure: the police convert a cleared suspect's romantic life into a trap and an officer into a target. The film plays it as caper; the archive notes the structure underneath — the pattern killed men for standing where Wang now volunteers to stand.

11 / BEHIND THE CHARACTER

Behind the Character

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

The third pairing

Wen Zhang's third film with Jet Li, after Ocean Heaven and The Sorcerer and the White Snake, and the first to invert their hierarchy: the younger actor leads, the legend supports. Reviewers singled out his revelry in the role — arrogant, cheeky, tireless — as the film's centre of gravity.

The name, translated

Wikipedia's cast listing preserves the joke in the character's name: Wang Bu Er, literally "not stupid Wang" — a gag that survives translation and organises the whole performance.

12 / LEGACY & SIGNIFICANCE

Legacy & Significance

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Wang Bu'er is the Galaxy's fullest portrait of the comedy-precinct detective as true engine: the officer whose superiors, partner and name all testify against him, and whose two contributions — the pattern read correctly, and himself, offered as bait — are the case entire. In the 2013 run he stands opposite the year's solemn investigators as proof that the buffoon and the detective can be the same file.
