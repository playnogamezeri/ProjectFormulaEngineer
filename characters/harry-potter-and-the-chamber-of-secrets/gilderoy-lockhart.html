01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Lockhart is the film's satire with a wand: a man who has converted other wizards' courage into his own bibliography, and maintains the asset with the one spell he has truly mastered — the Memory Charm that makes his sources forget they were the heroes. Hogwarts hires the gleaming fraud to teach defence in the year of the basilisk, and the film conducts his audit in public: the pixies he cannot control, the bones he cannot mend, the flight he attempts when the Chamber needs entering, and the broken wand that finally applies his own speciality to himself. He ends the year as his own last victim: famous, smiling, and entirely erased.

ROLE

The false teacher

Comic antagonist; the institution's credential failure — celebrity appointed to competence's chair in a crisis year.

DEFINING TRAIT

Authorship without acts

His books are other people's deeds with his name on the spine; his one genuine skill is making the witnesses forget.

FATE

Obliviated by his own speciality

His Memory Charm, cast with Ron's broken wand, backfires and erases his own mind; he is left cheerfully amnesiac, asking who he is.

Place in the story

Lockhart's page carries the film's institutional satire: while the governors suspend Dumbledore and the ministry arrests Hagrid, the school's actual fraud draws a salary at the front of a classroom. The archive treats his file as the record of the year's second investigation — the one into competence — whose verdict arrives, fittingly, from his own wand-hand.

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

Lockhart arrives trailing bibliography: Magical Me, Break with a Banshee, Year with the Yeti — a shelf of adventures that are, the film will reveal, all stolen. At his Flourish and Blotts signing he seizes on Harry's fame as a photo opportunity, announces his own appointment to Hogwarts, and begins the year as its resident celebrity. His teaching is theatre without a syllabus: a first lesson that releases a cage of Cornish pixies he cannot recapture — Hermione freezes them while he retreats — a duelling club where Snape disarms him in one gesture, and a hospital-wing visit where his attempt to mend Harry's broken arm dissolves the bones instead, leaving the arm a rubber glove to be regrown overnight.

The film audits him in escalating registers. His Valentine's Day dwarves, his detentions spent signing fan mail, his constant self-quotation — the comedy curdles as the attacks worsen and the school's need becomes real. The audit's conclusion is delivered by Lockhart himself, in confidence, when Harry and Ron catch him packing: he is a fraud. The books' heroes were other wizards, unglamorous ones; his talent was finding them, hearing their stories, and then applying the Memory Charm he had spent years perfecting, so that the deeds had only one owner left who remembered them. He is preparing to apply the same charm to Harry and Ron — to erase the witnesses of the Chamber case and compose himself a new adventure out of it.

The wand he seizes for the purpose is Ron's — snapped, Spellotaped, treacherous. The charm backfires with the force of a landslide: it erases Lockhart's own memory entire and brings the tunnel roof down, separating him and Ron from Harry. The film leaves him in the passage under Ron's guard, placidly asking who he is and whether he lives in the tunnel — the celebrity reduced to his true bibliography: nothing, cheerfully bound. The archive records that the film shows no restoration for him; his fate beyond the passage is not this film's business, and his file closes where the film leaves him.

04 / PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Psychology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation, grounded in the character's on-screen actions.

Vanity as a complete system

Interpretation: Lockhart's self-love is not a foible but an economy — fame is his only asset, so every situation is priced in publicity: a dying school is a backdrop, a famous student is a prop, a rescue is a manuscript. The film's coldest joke is that the system works until the moment competence is actually invoiced.

Cowardice with excellent diction

He flees the Chamber errand the instant it becomes real, and his confession is delivered with a showman's timing even as he reaches for a wand. The archive reads Branagh's polish as the character's tragedy in miniature: the performance is genuinely superb; there is simply nothing underneath it but more performance.

The one true skill

His Memory Charm mastery is real — years of practice on unwilling subjects. The film's moral arithmetic gives the fraud exactly one authentic accomplishment, in the service of all the false ones, and then makes him its final recipient.

05 / ABILITIES & SKILLS

Abilities, Skills & Weaknesses

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
MEMORY CHARMS

A master of Obliviation

The genuine article: he has erased the memories of the wizarding world's real heroes for years without detection — the film's darkest competence, worn by its lightest character.

AUTHORSHIP

The bestselling voice

His prose sells; the books are well-made thefts. The film is careful to give him this: the fraud is talented, which is the satire's point.

CELEBRITY

Fame as armour

Doors open, classes applaud, ministries pose: his reputation performs every function his ability cannot, until the Chamber sends an invoice.

WEAKNESS

Everything else

Pixies, bones, duelling, courage: the audit is comprehensive. His final weakness is instrumental — he reaches, at the decisive moment, for the worst wand in the school.

08 / SYMBOLISM & THEMES

Symbolism & Themes

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation.

The Memory Charm as plagiarism's wand

Interpretation: Lockhart's spell is the film's emblem of stolen credit — he does not merely take others' deeds, he deletes the witnesses, making theft indistinguishable from history. The archive reads his erasure-by-backfire as the film's neatest poetic justice: the man who lived by deleting memory is deleted by memory's own instrument.

The signed photograph

Interpretation: Lockhart's autographed photos — distributed to students like currency — are the year's false coinage, set against the film's true documents: the diary, the torn page, the messages on the wall. In a story about texts, his are the only ones that are pure advertisement.

09 / ANALYSIS & INVESTIGATION

Analysis & Investigation

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial analysis. Documented facts are cited to the sources in Section 13; readings of the character's function are the archive's own.

Function: the institution's mirror

Lockhart exists to convict Hogwarts' hiring, the ministry's celebrity culture and the public's appetite in one gleaming defendant. The analysis the archive records is that the film times his exposure to the Chamber plot deliberately: while adults chase the wrong suspect and suspend the right headmaster, the actual fraud in the building is the one they appointed. His confession scene is the film's second detective climax — the mystery of the teacher, solved by his own mouth.

Comedy as the audit's instrument

The film makes Lockhart funny so that his exposure lands as judgment rather than melodrama; the laughter is the audit trail. The archive notes the final turn's discipline: the amnesiac Lockhart is not played for cruelty — he is harmless, content and empty, and Ron's exasperated custody of him is the school beginning, in miniature, to look after the man it should never have hired.

11 / BEHIND THE CHARACTER

Behind the Character

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Kenneth Branagh

Branagh's casting is the film's meta-joke — a Shakespearean of genuine eminence playing eminence as a costume — and his performance was among the most praised in the film's reception, singled out for committing entirely to the vanity.

The books

Lockhart's bibliography is realised on screen as a wall of self-portraits — every cover his own face — a production-design summary of the character that the archive notes as the film's most efficient piece of visual criticism.

12 / LEGACY & SIGNIFICANCE

Legacy & Significance

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Lockhart's file is the saga's standing satire of celebrity expertise: the man is cited in every later discussion of the Defence post's curse, and his method — credit harvested, witnesses erased — has become the series' shorthand for a particular kind of fraud. The archive records that the film, unusually, gives its satire a complete judicial arc: exposure, confession, sentence, all in one school year.

Within the film, his erasure is also the plot's hinge: the backfire seals Harry into the Chamber alone, which is where the film needs him. The fraud's last service to the story is the accident of his punishment.
