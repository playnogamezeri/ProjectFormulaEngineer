01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Allegra Cole lives inside a public version of herself: an heiress whose romances are tabloid inventory and whose fortune makes every room she enters a transaction she did not design. The film's interest in her is the interest of everyone in it — who is she when the administration stops? Her plot gives the answer in stages: she notices an accountant because he argues with his boss in her interest and her presence; she enjoys a courtship whose ease she cannot know is partly engineered; she learns from a gossip column that a professional was involved and reads the whole courtship backward as a manufacture — the heiress's standing nightmare, that the feeling was a service; and she is won back in the film's final movement by the combined testimony of the doctor's confession (the clients won by being themselves) and the client's unscripted return. Her wedding closes the film: the storyline, married to the accountant, on the evidence.

ROLE

The beloved, as a person

The courtship's object who insists, structurally, on being its subject: her judgements drive the plot's second half.

DEFINING TRAIT

The manufacture detector

Allegra's formation — a lifetime of administered interest — has made her an expert in performed affection, which is why the exposé's revelation detonates as it does: it matches her oldest suspicion exactly.

FATE

Married, on the evidence

She ends the film as Albert's wife, the courtship re-founded on the unscripted original: the heiress's verdict, delivered in vows.

Place in the story

Allegra is the film's prize in the degraded sense only in other people's filing; her own page records her as its judge — the person whose assessment of Albert (notice, delight, revulsion, reconsideration) is the courtship's actual scoring system.

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

Allegra is introduced as the city files her: an heiress in the tabloids, her fortune administered by a firm of accountants, her presence at its meetings a formality she attends with the air of a woman used to being the room's largest fact. The film's first real event in her life is an argument: a recommendation touching her interests is moving through Albert's firm, and the junior accountant — a man she has never consciously registered — stands up to his own boss over it, on the merits, in front of her. She registers him. The archive notes the precision of her noticing: what reaches her is not charm, wardrobe or choreography (the coaching's entire inventory, which she has been performed at by professionals of several kinds for years) but competence with its guard down — a man forgetting himself in her interest, in a meeting, at professional risk.

The courtship that follows is, from her side, the story of a suspicion relaxing: Albert is awkward, sincere, and evidently terrified, and the combination reads to her manufacture detector as authentic — the film's irony being that the authenticity is real and the frame around it is professional, a distinction her detector is not built to make because no one had ever before been sincere inside a production. The exposé collapses the distinction from the other side: Sara's column presents the frame and omits the sincerity, and Allegra reads her whole courtship backward through it — every ease a technique, every moment a step in a method — and ends the engagement-in-progress with the finality of a woman whose oldest fear has been confirmed in print.

Her reconsideration is the film's thesis arriving by testimony. Hitch himself comes to argue Albert's case — the doctor, pleading for his client — and the argument he finds, mid-plea, is the one that acquits everyone: the clients won by being themselves; the coaching supplied confidence, not feeling; and the meeting she fell for was the least produced moment of Albert's life. Albert's own unscripted return completes the evidence. She takes him back, and the film ends at their wedding — the heiress and the accountant, the doctor and the columnist in the crowd — her detector's final ruling entered in vows: the feeling was the original; only the frame was hired.

04 / PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Psychology

This section is editorial interpretation, grounded in the character's on-screen actions.

Vigilance as a formation

Interpretation: Allegra's suspicion of performance is not cynicism but occupational training — a woman whose desirability is partly denominated in money learns early that affection arrives with invoices attached. The archive reads her break with Albert as her detector working exactly as built, on incomplete data: the tragedy the film corrects is that the one sincere production of her life was indistinguishable, from her seat, from the fraudulent ones.

08 / SYMBOLISM & THEMES

Symbolism & Themes

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation.

The meeting

Interpretation: Allegra's noticing is the archive's emblem of the film's economics of attention — in a life where everything is administered, the only unadministered act in the room is the one that reaches her. The film's romance is founded not on a date but on a workplace argument, and the archive files that as its most honest piece of casting: she falls for his competence because competence is the one currency in her world that cannot be counterfeited for long.

09 / ANALYSIS & INVESTIGATION

Analysis & Investigation

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial analysis. Documented facts are cited to the sources in Section 13; readings of the character's function are the archive's own.

Function: the judge

Allegra's structural role is judicial: the courtship is a case tried before her in three hearings — the meeting (notice), the exposé (conviction), the testimony (acquittal) — and the film gives her verdicts the force of plot. The analysis the archive records is that this makes her the method's true examiner: Hitch coaches and Sara exposes, but Allegra decides, and the film's thesis is whatever survives her detector — sincerity, framed or not, provided the frame is confessed.
