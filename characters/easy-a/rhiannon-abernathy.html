01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Every rumor needs a first believer, and Olive's is her best friend. Rhiannon refuses the camping-trip excuse, conducts the Monday interrogation that produces the virginity lie, and is delighted — at first — to be adjacent to fame. But the economy she helped open has no loyalty program: her own crush buys Olive's legend instead of noticing her, the crusade offers her a better faction, and the friendship becomes the rumor's most intimate casualty. The film's last text message is Olive's apology to her — the one debt in the story Olive insists on paying herself.

ROLE

Best friend; first audience

She is the lie's intended recipient and its quality control: nothing Olive invents exists until Rhiannon believes it.

DEFINING TRAIT

Proximity hunger

She wants to be near the story more than she wants to know whether it is true — until being near it costs her the boy and the friend at once.

FATE

The apology received

She ends the film inside Marianne's faction and outside Olive's life — until the closing text, which the film leaves answered only by its sending.

Place in the story

Rhiannon is the rumor's human scale. Marianne distributes the lie to the school; Rhiannon shows what it does to a single relationship — how a best friend can be, in sequence, a lie's co-author, beneficiary, victim and defector, without ever being its villain.

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

Rhiannon opens the film as its first cause: her family's camping trip is what Olive lies to escape, and her Monday interrogation — conducted in the restroom, at full prosecutorial volume — is what upgrades a skipped weekend into a lost virginity. Marianne overhears because Rhiannon insists on details. In the lie's early, comic phase Rhiannon is its happiest shareholder, basking in reflected notoriety and reporting the market back to Olive.

The turn is Anson: Rhiannon's crush, who asks Olive out — for her legend, not for her — and thereby demonstrates to Rhiannon that proximity to the story has made her less visible, not more. The friendship breaks in a confrontation over Olive's new persona (the schoolyard phrase "dirty skank" enters the record here), and Rhiannon defects to the only other faction recruiting: Marianne's. She is visible in the crusade's harassment of Olive in the film's late movement. Olive's endgame pointedly does not include winning her back by argument; it ends with a text — an apology for lying to her — sent as Olive walks out to Todd. The film closes on the sending, not the reply.

04 / PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Psychology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation, grounded in the character's on-screen actions.

The audience of one

Interpretation: Rhiannon consumes Olive's life as a serial — demanding episodes (details, updates), enjoying the hit season, and cancelling when the show crowds out her own storyline. Her defection is a viewer's, not a conspirator's.

Candour without ballast

She is the film's most honest character about wanting things — fame by adjacency, the boy, a faction — and the honesty does not save her, because wanting is all she brings to any of them.

09 / ANALYSIS & INVESTIGATION

Analysis & Investigation

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial analysis. Documented facts are cited to the sources in Section 13; readings of the character's function are the archive's own.

Why Olive apologises to her

Of everyone Olive lied to, Rhiannon is the only one whose injury was real: the lies were told to her, the fame was shared with her, and the persona was maintained at her. The closing apology is the film's moral bookkeeping — Olive settling the one account where she was the debtor.

The friendship as the rumor's true subject

The school plot resolves by webcast; the friendship plot resolves by text. Interpretation: the film trusts the smaller instrument for the larger relationship — a confession can fix a reputation in an evening, but a friendship gets one message and no guarantee.
