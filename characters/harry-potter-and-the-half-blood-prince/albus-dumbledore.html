Albus Dumbledore

The headmaster in his last year — a dying man, the blackened hand a curse's countdown, spending his remaining time as the film's great wager: that a sixteen-year-old can be told everything and survive the telling. He teaches the enemy's biography, hunts a Horcrux, and climbs the tower to keep an appointment the film does not explain.

01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Dumbledore spends his final year converting secrets into curriculum. The withered hand — cursed when he destroyed Marvolo Gaunt's ring, one of Voldemort's Horcruxes — is his deadline made visible, and his response to it is a syllabus: private lessons in which Harry is walked through Tom Riddle's life, memory by memory, until the boy understands the enemy as a biography rather than a nightmare. He recruits Slughorn for the one memory he cannot supply himself, hunts a Horcrux in a sea cave and drinks its poison on Harry's arm, and returns to a tower where Draco Malfoy — the assassin he has watched being groomed all year — disarms him. What happens next, the film presents without explanation: Snape arrives, and kills him, and the school raises its wands to clear the Dark Mark from the sky.

ROLE

The dying teacher

Principal; the war's strategist converting his remaining time into one student's education, and the tower's victim.

DEFINING TRAIT

The wager on the told truth

Every prior year he withheld; this year he discloses — the blackened hand has abolished his reasons for patience, and disclosure becomes his whole strategy.

FATE

Killed on the Astronomy Tower

Disarmed by Draco, killed by Snape's Killing Curse, mourned by the raised wands of Hogwarts — his reasons, whatever they were, buried with him for now.

Place in the story

Dumbledore is the film's author inside the film: the lessons are his editing of the past, the Slughorn recruitment his casting, the cave his fieldwork. His death removes the story's editor at the exact moment the narrative passes to the student — which, the archive records, may be the most Dumbledorean feature of the entire design.

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

Dumbledore opens the film in motion: collecting Harry from a London station, recruiting Slughorn with the boy as bait, and resuming the war's real work. His hand is already ruined — the price of destroying the ring Horcrux, a carelessness he acknowledges as an old man's vanity — and Snape's treatment has only contained the curse. The lessons proceed through the year: the orphanage, the schoolboy, the doctored memory identified and its recovery assigned. All the while, the film lets the audience see what Harry cannot yet price: the headmaster is absent for long stretches, hunting; he knows Draco has been tasked — the Order's intelligence and his own reading agree — and he does not move against the boy, a restraint the film presents without annotating.

The memory won, Dumbledore reads the arithmetic plainly to Harry: Voldemort split his soul toward seven; the diary and the ring are destroyed; others remain. He takes Harry to the cave where a Horcrux is hidden, exacts the blood price at the door, and — the film's most harrowing bargain — orders Harry to force the basin's potion down his throat whatever he says while drinking it. The potion makes him relive his guilts and beg; Harry obeys, retrieves the locket, and fights off the Inferi with fire as Dumbledore rises at the last to drive them back with a firestorm.

They return to the Astronomy Tower beneath the Dark Mark. Weakened, Dumbledore sends Harry for Snape — then, hearing Draco arrive, orders Harry instead to hide and stay hidden. Draco disarms him: the headmaster, wandless, talks to his assassin with something like pride in the boy's ingenuity and no fear at all, until Draco's nerve fails at the threshold. The other Death Eaters arrive; then Snape. Dumbledore speaks his name — the film leaves the word's register, plea or instruction, for its audience to carry — and Snape casts the Killing Curse. The school mourns him with raised wands, the light clearing the Mark from the sky; the locket in Harry's hand is a fake, and the note inside it is signed by a stranger.

The biography above is an editorial paraphrase of the documented plot accounts (Wikipedia and the cited sources), not a scene-by-scene transcript.

04 / PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Psychology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation, grounded in the character's on-screen actions.

The editor of the past

Interpretation: Dumbledore's lessons are curation as strategy — he decides which memories Harry dives, in which order, toward which conclusion. Even his honesty is an arrangement, which is not the same as calling it false.

Jauntiness over the grave

He jokes, he compliments, he takes a boy to a cave he may not leave: the film's Dumbledore treats dying as a scheduling fact, and the performance of lightness is his last act of generalship — morale, maintained to the tower.

The unannotated restraint

He watches Draco's mission proceed and does not strike first. The film gives no reason; the archive files the restraint as the character's deepest withheld fact, of a piece with his trust in Snape — two confidences the story declines, for now, to price.

05 / ABILITIES & SKILLS

Abilities, Skills & Weaknesses

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
MAGIC

The age's greatest wizard

The firestorm in the cave, performed half-dead of poison, is the film's measure of what the tower's wandlessness costs: disarmed is the only way he can be killed, and Draco knows it.

STRATEGY

The long curriculum

He converts a school year into a war college for one student, and a staff appointment into an intelligence operation.

KNOWLEDGE

The enemy's biographer

No one alive knows Voldemort as Dumbledore knows him; the lessons are that knowledge being transferred before its library burns.

WEAKNESS

The curse and the cup

The ring's countdown and the cave's potion spend him between them: by the tower he is a great wizard at a fraction of himself, which is the only arithmetic in which the ending works.

09 / ANALYSIS & INVESTIGATION

Analysis & Investigation

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial analysis. Documented facts are cited to the sources in Section 13; readings of the character's function are the archive's own.

Function: the editor removed

Dumbledore has narrated the saga to Harry for five films; this film makes the narration literal (the Pensieve lessons) and then kills the narrator mid-sentence, leaving the student holding a fake locket and a dead man's syllabus. The archive records the design's severity: the story refuses to annotate his two great confidences — in Snape, and about Draco — at the moment of his death, which converts the saga's most trusted voice into its largest open file. His page is therefore filed as the film leaves him: complete in curriculum, unexplained in conclusion.

11 / BEHIND THE CHARACTER

Behind the Character

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Michael Gambon

Gambon's third film as Dumbledore; the production removed the novel's funeral set piece, judging it against the film's design, and staged the mourning instead as the school's raised wands clearing the Dark Mark — a documented change that concentrates the death's aftermath into a single image.

12 / LEGACY & SIGNIFICANCE

Legacy & Significance

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Dumbledore's death is the saga's point of no return: the school era ends on the tower, and everything after is the hunt he designed. In the Galaxy's records his page stands as the curriculum complete and the teacher gone — the lessons delivered, the locket false, the confidences unexplained, and the student walking out of the school to finish the reading.
