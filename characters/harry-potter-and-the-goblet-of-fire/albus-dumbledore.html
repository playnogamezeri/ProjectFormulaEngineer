01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Dumbledore's fourth-year film is a study in authority discovering its own limits in public. He draws the age line himself and watches the Goblet overrule the room; he presides over a tournament his own staff has infiltrated; he keeps, in his office, a Pensieve of memories he dips into like a man auditing his own failures — including the trial where a screaming boy was sent to Azkaban while Dumbledore vouched for the man the boy's father would rather have believed. What he does with the year's catastrophe defines him in the archive: he believes Harry instantly and completely, extracts the impostor by a point of character, and then stands before the whole school at the memorial feast and names the murderer and the murder against the coming official lie — the headmaster choosing his students' knowledge over his government's comfort, in the hearing of both.

ROLE

The host and the witness's believer

The tournament's presiding authority; the first institution to accept the truth and the loudest to publish it.

DEFINING TRAIT

Belief as policy

Dumbledore's decisive acts in this film are epistemic: he accepts the contract's verdict, believes the boy's report, and publishes the conclusion. In a story about a rigged event and a coming denial, his function is to be the room where the truth is admitted.

FATE

The truth told, the alliance begun

He ends the film having addressed the school, dispatched the first feelers of resistance (the record shows him sending Sirius and Snape to their uneasy errands in the aftermath), and set his face against the Ministry's denial.

Place in the story

Dumbledore is the film's institutional conscience — the one authority whose response to the catastrophe is neither panic nor denial. His page carries the year's governance: the line drawn, the contract honoured, the memory audited, and the truth proclaimed.

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

Dumbledore opens the tournament as its constitutional author: the age line is his, the Goblet's impartiality is his assurance, and the fourth name is therefore his crisis. The record shows his handling of it as the film's first lesson in his method: the contract binds, the boy competes, and the headmaster — who seizes Harry in the trophy room and demands the truth at a volume the school can hear — accepts the denial he receives and spends the year watching. His watching has instruments: the Pensieve in his office, where Harry, waiting, falls into the memory of the Death Eater trials and watches Igor Karkaroff buy his freedom with names — Snape's among them, and Dumbledore in the memory rising to vouch for Snape as a turned spy — and watches, too, the trial's other exhibit: Barty Crouch Jr., dragged screaming from the court by Dementors while his father disowns him in public. The archive notes the memory's placement: the film gives its audience the impostor's face and history an hour before the school learns them, in the headmaster's own archive of the last war's unfinished business.

The year's catastrophe runs through his office twice. First, Crouch Sr.'s body is found in the forest — the headmaster receiving a murdered Ministry official on his grounds with a tournament in progress and no clean move available. Then the maze: the Cup's disappearance, the stadium's long wait, and Harry's return with Cedric's body. Dumbledore's response is the film's model of authority in a crisis: he is on the ground holding the boy off the body, he believes the report entire and instantly, and when "Moody" removes Harry from the scene, the headmaster reads the act against his knowledge of the real man and moves within minutes — the impostor taken, the flask emptied, the confession extracted under his direction, the real Moody found in the trunk.

His closing acts are publication and mobilisation. At the memorial feast he tells the school, in terms, that Cedric Diggory was murdered by Lord Voldemort — against the Ministry's known position, in the hearing of the foreign schools he is binding into alliance with the same speech — and the record of the aftermath shows the resistance's first errands dispatched from his office: old allies recalled, impossible envoys sent. The archive files his year as the moment the headmaster becomes a wartime leader in public: the line he drew failed, the event he hosted was stolen, and his answer to both is to tell the truth louder than the government will.

04 / PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Psychology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation, grounded in the character's on-screen actions.

The constitutionalist under fire

Interpretation: Dumbledore honours the Goblet's contract even when he suspects it, because his authority is itself contractual — the archive reads his year as a study in a ruler who accepts bad outcomes from legitimate instruments while working, relentlessly, outside them: the watching, the Pensieve, the belief.

Memory as an audit

The Pensieve scenes show a man who keeps his own past failures filed and revisit-able — the trials, the vouched-for spy, the screaming boy. The archive notes that he does not exonerate himself in any of them; the archive is kept as evidence, not as comfort.

05 / ABILITIES & SKILLS

Abilities, Skills & Weaknesses

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
MAGIC

The age line and the office

His enchantments anchor the tournament's security — and the film's point is that the conspiracy defeats them by paperwork, not power: he is out-administered, never out-spelled.

KNOWLEDGE OF PEOPLE

The extraction

He identifies the impostor by a single out-of-character kindness: his true instrument is a lifetime's file on everyone he has ever met, kept in his head.

AUTHORITY

The memorial speech

He publishes the truth to three schools against his government's line: the film's most consequential act of office.

WEAKNESS

The watched pot

He suspects the year is wrong and cannot locate the wrongness — the impostor is inside his own appointments, wearing a man he chose. His vigilance is real and it is not enough, and the film makes him know it.

08 / SYMBOLISM & THEMES

Symbolism & Themes

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation.

The Pensieve

Interpretation: Dumbledore's memory-basin is the archive's emblem of his whole method — the past kept liquid, reviewable, and admissible. In a film about a government preparing to deny the present, the headmaster's office holds the opposite technology: a bowl where what happened can be re-entered exactly. The archive notes that Harry's education in it is the year's real curriculum.

09 / ANALYSIS & INVESTIGATION

Analysis & Investigation

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial analysis. Documented facts are cited to the sources in Section 13; readings of the character's function are the archive's own.

Function: the admitting room

Every institution in the film has a response to the truth: the Goblet's is contractual, the Ministry's is denial, the school's — through Dumbledore — is admission and publication. The analysis the archive records is that the film positions him as the counter-Ministry a year before the war requires one: his memorial speech is a declaration that Hogwarts will keep its own record, and the errands dispatched in the aftermath are that record's first enforcement arm.
