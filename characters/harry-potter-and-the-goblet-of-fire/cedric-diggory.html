01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Cedric Diggory is what the tournament was supposed to be about: a champion chosen correctly — of age, able, popular, and decent in the specific ways the film takes care to document. He warns Harry about the egg because Harry warned him about the dragons; he tells his own supporters to stop wearing the badges that mock Harry; he reaches the Cup at the same moment as the boy he is supposed to have been robbed by, and agrees in one breath to share it. Then the Portkey delivers them both, and the war's arithmetic is spoken over him by the enemy himself: kill the spare. Cedric is the film's proof that the returning darkness does not kill the guilty or the scheming first. It kills the spare — the decent boy standing in the wrong light — and his echo's one request is to be carried home to his father.

ROLE

The true champion

Hogwarts' legitimate selection; the tournament's standard of conduct and its first war casualty.

DEFINING TRAIT

Fairness as a habit

Cedric's decency is procedural — debts repaid, badges discouraged, glory shared — a boy whose goodness is a set of practices, like Charlie Bucket's in another of this batch's films, and just as fatal to no one but himself.

FATE

Murdered in the graveyard

Killed by Pettigrew on Voldemort's order — "kill the spare" — and carried home by Harry at his echo's request; memorialised by Dumbledore as a boy who met his death with his head held high.

Place in the story

Cedric is the film's measure of what the tournament cost: not a plot device but the story's demonstration that the war's return is priced in the best people first. His page carries the batch's grief, as his body carries its plot home.

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

Cedric enters as the school's open secret: the champion Hogwarts actually chose, seventeen, able, and — the film's early scenes insist — embarrassed by the manner of his support. The "Potter Stinks" badges circulating in his colours displease him, and he says so. When Harry, warned himself by a friend, passes Cedric the dragons' secret before the first task, Cedric banks the debt and repays it in kind before the second: the egg, the bathroom, the clue — the tournament's honour economy running between its two Hogwarts champions while the school imagines a rivalry. His tasks are a champion's: the dragon passed, the lake swum (his hostage is Cho Chang, his Yule Ball date — the girl Harry asked too late), and the maze run at the front, where he is saved from the bewitched Krum's attack by Harry and reaches the Cup in the same moment.

The joint grab is his agreement as much as Harry's insistence: one breath, and the Cup takes them both. In the graveyard he is a champion still — wand out, ready beside Harry against the unknown — and the war disposes of him in its own vocabulary. Pettigrew emerges carrying the enemy's wasted form; the order is given — kill the spare — and the Killing Curse takes Cedric before the film has time to make a scene of it, which is the scene: the war does not duel its casualties, it tidies them. His echo emerges in the Priori Incantatem with one request — take my body back to my father — and Harry does, delivering him to a stadium where Amos Diggory's grief is the sound the series changes register on. Dumbledore's memorial closes the account publicly: Cedric Diggory was murdered by Lord Voldemort; remember him as he was — a boy who was good, kind, brave and true. The archive records the eulogy as the film's own filing of this page: the spare, entered in the record as the standard.

Decency as procedure

Interpretation: Cedric's goodness is never speeches; it is transactions — a warning repaid, a badge discouraged, a victory halved. 09 / ANALYSIS & INVESTIGATION

Analysis & Investigation

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial analysis. Documented facts are cited to the sources in Section 13; readings of the character's function are the archive's own.

Function: the cost, personified

Cedric exists so that the war's return has a price the audience has personally audited — the film spends its middle building his decency in small transactions precisely so the graveyard can spend it in one curse. The analysis the archive records is that his death is the series' hinge: before it, Hogwarts stories end with points and feasts; after it, they end with memorials and ministries in denial.
