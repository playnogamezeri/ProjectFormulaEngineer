01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Harry's fourth year is a conspiracy wearing a sporting event. Somebody puts his name in the Goblet — an act that requires defeating an age line and a binding magical contract — and the machinery of two schools and a Ministry department then processes him as a cheat while the real author coaches him from inside the staff room. Harry survives the tasks the way he survives everything: helped. Gillyweed from Neville (planted by the conspiracy), the egg's secret from Cedric (repaid conscience), the maze run beside the champion he is accused of stealing glory from. The Cup takes them both to the graveyard, where Harry watches Cedric murdered as a spare, is used as an ingredient in his enemy's resurrection, duels that enemy with his parents' echoes for a shield, and escapes carrying the body — to a school feast where his headmaster tells the truth and a Ministry that will spend the next year calling him a liar.

ROLE

The fourth champion

Protagonist; the conspiracy's instrument and its intended delivery.

DEFINING TRAIT

Conduct under false accusation

Harry's year is a study in being processed as a cheat by every institution present while doing, task by task, the most honourable things in the tournament — sharing the egg's clue's debt, rescuing two hostages, insisting on the joint victory.

FATE

The witness

He ends the film as the war's only public witness: believed by his school, denied by his government, carrying a body and a truth in the same arms.

Place in the story

Harry is the film's centre and its delivery mechanism: the tournament exists, as a plot, to move him to the graveyard. His page carries both the sporting story and the war story, which the ending reveals were always one story.

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

Harry's year begins in a graveyard dream — the Riddle House, an old man murdered for overhearing — and proceeds through the Quidditch World Cup's riot, where Death Eaters torch the campsite and the Dark Mark is cast into the sky by a man Harry does not know he has seen. At Hogwarts the Triwizard Tournament is announced, with an age line drawn by Dumbledore himself. The Goblet names Fleur Delacour, Viktor Krum, Cedric Diggory — and then, impossibly, a fourth: Harry Potter, fourteen, bound by the contract the Goblet enforces, over his own truthful denial and most of the school's belief that he cheated. Ron believes it too, and the film's first movement is the friendship's worst weather: Harry competes as a champion under a cloud, coached in secret by the new Defence teacher, "Mad-Eye" Moody, whose advice — play to your strengths, fly — gets him past the Hungarian Horntail and the golden egg.

The tournament's middle is Harry at his most himself. The egg's clue, decoded in the prefects' bathroom on Cedric's repaid tip, sends him to the Black Lake, where the task is to rescue the person he would miss most — and where Harry, arriving first, refuses to leave with only Ron: he waits, frees Hermione's and Fleur's hostages too, hauls the little Gabrielle Delacour up with Ron, and is awarded second place for moral fibre by judges whose event he has just upstaged ethically. The Yule Ball interlude gives him the year's ordinary miseries — Cho Chang already taken by Cedric, the Patil date, Ron's jealousy of Krum — the film insisting that its hero is fourteen in every register at once. In the maze, the third task, the conspiracy's hand shows openly at last: Krum, bewitched, attacks the other champions; Harry and Cedric fight through to the Cup together, and Harry — accused all year of stealing Cedric's glory — insists they take it jointly, as Hogwarts' shared win.

The Cup is a Portkey. The graveyard receives them; Pettigrew, on his master's order, murders Cedric where he stands — "kill the spare" — and binds Harry to a tombstone for the resurrection ritual: bone of the father, flesh of the servant, blood of the enemy. Voldemort rises, summons and rebukes his Death Eaters, and duels Harry for sport; the wands, brothers, lock in Priori Incantatem, and the echoes of the murdered — Cedric, Frank Bryce, James and Lily Potter — shield the boy long enough for him to reach the Cup with Cedric's body, at Cedric's echo's own request. Harry returns to a stadium that learns horror in real time, is spirited away by "Moody" — whose Polyjuice fails in the hour of triumph, exposing Barty Crouch Jr., the conspiracy's author, in Veritaserum confession — and ends the year at a memorial feast where Dumbledore names the murder and the murderer to the whole school, while the Ministry, off-screen, prepares its denial. The boy who lived has become the witness who will not be believed: the war's first casualty report, filed by a fourteen-year-old.

The biography above is an editorial paraphrase of the documented plot accounts (Wikipedia and the cited sources), not a scene-by-scene transcript.

04 / PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Psychology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation, grounded in the character's on-screen actions.

Honour as a reflex

Interpretation: Harry's tournament is a sequence of refusals to win dirty — the shared Cup, the doubled rescue, the returned tip. The archive reads the film's argument as structural: the conspiracy counts on his honour at every joint (it is why the maze plan works), and his honour is also the only thing that gets Cedric's body home. The trait is simultaneously his exploitation surface and his salvation, and the film knows it.

The accused innocent

He spends the year processed as a cheat by institutions that never ask the obvious question — who benefits? — and the archive notes his conduct under it: he neither performs innocence nor confesses falsely; he simply does the tasks. It is the series' template for the year of denial ahead.

08 / SYMBOLISM & THEMES

Symbolism & Themes

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation.

The Cup as Portkey

Interpretation: the tournament's prize is the conspiracy's vehicle — glory itself rigged as transport to a graveyard. The archive reads it as the film's thesis about the year: every institution in Harry's world (sport, school, Ministry) can be hollowed out and used as a delivery system, and the hollowing is invisible until the arrival.

09 / ANALYSIS & INVESTIGATION

Analysis & Investigation

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial analysis. Documented facts are cited to the sources in Section 13; readings of the character's function are the archive's own.

Function: the delivery and the witness

Harry's plot function is double — the conspiracy needs him delivered, and the war needs him believed. The analysis the archive records is that the film's cruelty is to grant the first function completely and deny the second: he arrives exactly where the enemy wants him, escapes by grace of echoes, and reports to a world that will call him disturbed. The tournament is the last time Harry's world processes him as a sportsman; after the graveyard it processes him as a problem.
