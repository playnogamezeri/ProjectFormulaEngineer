01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

The year's most trusted teacher is its deepest enemy. Barty Crouch Jr. — escaped from Azkaban by his dying mother's sacrifice, kept hidden for years under his father's Imperius Curse, freed at the World Cup where he casts the Dark Mark — subdues the real Alastor Moody before term begins, imprisons him in the bottom of a magical trunk, and wears him for a school year on Polyjuice Potion, harvesting the hair from the prisoner's head. In Moody's skin he does everything the plot requires and several things it merely enjoys: he enters Harry's name in the Goblet, coaches the boy task by task, converts Draco Malfoy into a ferret, murders his own escaped father in the Forbidden Forest, bewitches Krum in the maze, and turns the Triwizard Cup into the Portkey. The performance is flawless because the devotion is real — he is, as his master says, the most faithful servant — and it fails only at the moment of triumph, when he spirits Harry away too eagerly and the potion's hour runs out in front of Dumbledore. The Veritaserum confession that follows is the film's whole conspiracy, recited by its author in his own face at last.

ROLE

The conspiracy's author

The impostor in the staff room; every helpful act of "Moody's" is the plan feeding its delivery.

DEFINING TRAIT

Devotion as method acting

Crouch Jr. does not merely wear Moody; he performs him — the paranoia, the hip flask, the teaching genius — with a convert's total commitment. The archive notes the film's darkest joke: he is, by every account including his victims', an excellent Defence Against the Dark Arts teacher.

FATE

Exposed and returned

The potion fails before Dumbledore, Snape and McGonagall; under Veritaserum he confesses everything, and is taken back to Azkaban. The real Moody is freed from the trunk, alive.

Place in the story

This folded file is the Goblet batch's engine room: the conspiracy's authorship, its daily operations, and its exposure all live here. Per the run's precedent for two versions of one figure, the impostor and the imprisoned original are studied together — the performance and the man it was stolen from.

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

The backstory, confessed at the end, runs as follows. Crouch Jr., convicted Death Eater, was smuggled out of Azkaban by his parents — his dying mother taking his place on Polyjuice, his father keeping the freed son hidden at home under the Imperius Curse for years. At the Quidditch World Cup the son fights the curse off, steals Harry's wand from his pocket in the riot's confusion, and casts the Dark Mark into the sky — the film's first sign of him, unsigned. Before term, acting on Voldemort's plan, he attacks Moody at home — the real Auror, paranoid and magnificent, puts up a fight the neighbours hear as dustbins — and takes his place: the prisoner into the trunk's bottom compartment, the impostor into the classroom, the flask refilled on the hour.

The year he teaches is, by the record, superb and sinister in the same lessons. He demonstrates the three Unforgivable Curses on a spider to a class of fourteen-year-olds — the Imperius, the Cruciatus (lingered on, before Neville Longbottom, whose parents the curse destroyed; the impostor knows exactly what he is showing the boy), and the Killing Curse — under a doctrine of constant vigilance that is the real Moody's own. He turns Malfoy into a ferret for attacking Harry from behind. And underneath the teaching, the plan proceeds on rails: the Goblet's name entered by a hand strong enough to hoodwink an ancient contract; the dragon task solved by his coached advice; the egg, the lake, the gillyweed arriving via Neville at his suggestion; Krum bewitched in the maze; the Cup touched last by his hand. When his father — escaped from Voldemort's own Imperius, staggering to Hogwarts to confess — reaches the forest, the son is waiting, and murders him, transfiguring and burying the evidence: the conspiracy's one improvisation, and its coldest.

The exposure is a matter of minutes and manners. With Harry returned from the graveyard, "Moody" spirits him away from Dumbledore's side — a kindness the real Moody, Dumbledore realises, would never have performed: the real Moody would have kept the boy in sight of the whole school. The staff take him in his office; the flask is emptied; the hour turns; and Barty Crouch Jr. stands in his own face before them, to be dosed with Veritaserum and recite his year like a curriculum vitae — the escape, the Mark, the trunk, the father, the Cup — ending on the question of whether his master will reward him as the most faithful servant. He is returned to Azkaban. Below the office, in the trunk's seventh compartment, the real Alastor Moody is found — starved, stunned, alive: the year's most trusted teacher, rescued from his own luggage.

The biography above is an editorial paraphrase of the documented plot accounts (Wikipedia and the cited sources), not a scene-by-scene transcript.

04 / PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Psychology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation, grounded in the character's on-screen actions.

The convert's total commitment

Interpretation: Crouch Jr. is the film's study of devotion as a complete technology — it powers his patience, his acting, his teaching and his patricide with the same current. The archive reads his classroom excellence as the point rather than a joke: the conspiracy does not send a saboteur to teach Defence; it sends a believer, and the belief makes the teaching real.

The father, answered

His patricide is the imposture's one personal act — the son the father imprisoned, caged in turn inside another man's face, killing the father in a forest. The archive notes the film gives it no speech: the confession lists it among the year's works, in the same tone as the coaching.

05 / ABILITIES & SKILLS

Abilities, Skills & Weaknesses

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
ACTING

A year in another man's skin

He sustains Moody — voice, paranoia, teaching, drinking — before the man's own colleagues for three terms, failing only on a point of character at the very end.

DARK MAGIC

The full curriculum

The Unforgivables demonstrated as lessons; the Goblet hoodwinked; a champion Imperiused in the maze: the year's most capable dark wizard is its Defence teacher, which the archive files as the film's blackest sentence.

PATIENCE

The long enrolment

Years under his father's curse, a year in the flask's hourly rhythm: his devotion runs on any timescale the plan requires.

WEAKNESS

The triumph's eagerness

He takes Harry away too fast, against the character he is playing — the one crack in the performance, and it is made of joy: the plan worked, and the servant cannot wait to deliver the news.

Symbolism & Themes

The trunk

Interpretation: Moody's seven-compartment trunk is the archive's emblem of the imposture — a man's whole life folded into luggage, with the man himself in the bottom compartment under his own office. The impostor teaches all year standing, literally, on top of his victim. The film never underlines it; the furniture does.

09 / ANALYSIS & INVESTIGATION

Analysis & Investigation

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial analysis. Documented facts are cited to the sources in Section 13; readings of the character's function are the archive's own.

Function: the engine and the theme

Crouch Jr. is the plot's entire machinery — remove him and no event in the film occurs — but the analysis the archive records is that he is also its theme in person: a son destroyed by a father's absolute administration, answering it with absolute devotion to a worse father. The Crouch family is the film's dark mirror of every household in the batch, and its account is settled in a forest, by the son, in another man's face.
