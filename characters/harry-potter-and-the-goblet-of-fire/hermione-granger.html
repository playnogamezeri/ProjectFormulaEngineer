01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Hermione's fourth year is a study in being right in public at social cost. She is the only person in Hogwarts who believes Harry's denial on the night it is given — no evidence, no dragon required — and her belief is not sentiment but method: she asks the question the institutions skip (who benefits from Harry competing?) and files the year accordingly. Around that fixed point her year is full: she researches the tasks with Harry, endures Rita Skeeter's campaign against her in the press, is taken to the Yule Ball by the tournament's most famous champion — arriving transformed in the film's set-piece of social revision — is filed by the merpeople as Viktor Krum's treasure, and ends the year having identified, captured and blackmailed Skeeter herself, in a jar, as an unregistered Animagus. The archive files her as the year's only undefeated intelligence: every mystery she is permitted to work on, she solves; the one she cannot is being worked by a teacher she trusts, in a flask, on the hour.

ROLE

The believer and the researcher

The trio's mind; the year's epistemic standard — belief by method, not by loyalty alone.

DEFINING TRAIT

The right question, asked early

Hermione's signature act in this film happens in its first movement: she asks who would benefit from Harry's entry, and the whole conspiracy is visible in outline from that question — a year before the school can see it.

FATE

Undefeated, and underestimated no longer

She ends the year with Skeeter in a jar, Krum's invitation to Bulgaria in her pocket, and her trio intact: the social revision of the ball made permanent in the record.

Place in the story

Hermione is the film's standard of clear sight — the character against whom every institution's fog is measured. Her page carries the year's research, its press war, and its most satisfying small victory: the beetle in the jar.

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

Hermione's year divides cleanly at the Goblet. Before it, she is the trio's established engine — the World Cup's aftermath read correctly, the school year begun in the library. After it, she is the only believer: on the night of the fourth name, while Ron and the school process Harry as a cheat, Hermione accepts the denial and starts asking the productive question — not did he do it, but who profits if he competes? The film gives her no way to answer it (the answer is drinking from a flask in the staff room), but the question organises her whole year: she researches the tasks beside Harry, coaches the summoning and the strategies, and keeps the trio's intelligence function running while its friendship function is under repair.

Her public year is fought on two fronts. Rita Skeeter's journalism — fed by sources the film later identifies — makes Hermione a storyline: the scarlet woman of two champions, in print, in a school that reads it at breakfast. And the Yule Ball revises her public file in one evening: she attends on Viktor Krum's arm — the Durmstrang champion has been watching her in the library for weeks, the film's quietest courtship — descends the staircase transformed, dances the opening with the tournament's most famous name, and ends the night in the common room being accused by Ron of fraternising with the enemy, delivering the year's most quoted verdict on his timing: next time there's a ball, pluck up the courage and ask her first, before someone else does. In the lake she is Krum's treasure, rescued by his transfigured dive — the merpeople's filing of her as two champions' centre in the same year, which the archive notes the school newspaper could not have improved on.

Her closing victory is the year's neatest: she identifies Rita Skeeter's method — an unregistered beetle Animagus, listening on statues and shoulders all year — captures her in a jar with an Unbreakable charm on the lid, and sets the terms: a year's silence, or the Ministry learns the shape. The press war ends with the journalist in the researcher's luggage. Hermione ends the film on the platform in the trio's restored formation, promising to write — to both of them, and, the record allows the smile, to Bulgaria as well.

The biography above is an editorial paraphrase of the documented plot accounts (Wikipedia and the cited sources), not a scene-by-scene transcript.

04 / PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Psychology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation, grounded in the character's on-screen actions.

Method as loyalty

Interpretation: Hermione's belief in Harry is usually filed as friendship; the archive files it as method. She is the only character who runs the cui bono test on the Goblet's output, and her certainty is the conclusion of an analysis the institutions never perform. Her loyalty and her intellect are the same faculty, which is why neither can be rented by the year's lies — Ron's crack is a wound; Hermione has no equivalent surface.

The rules, audited

She captures Skeeter by breaking no law the journalist has not broken worse — the archive notes Hermione's characteristic jurisprudence: rules are instruments, assessed by their purpose, and a blackmail that silences a liar for a year is, in her filing, a proportionate sentence.

09 / ANALYSIS & INVESTIGATION

Analysis & Investigation

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial analysis. Documented facts are cited to the sources in Section 13; readings of the character's function are the archive's own.

Function: the epistemic standard

Hermione exists in this film to demonstrate what the truth was available to anyone who asked the right question — her belief is the control case against which the school's suspicion and the Ministry's coming denial are both measured. The analysis the archive records is that the film is careful to limit her by jurisdiction, not by intellect: she solves everything she is permitted to investigate, and the one mystery she cannot reach is guarded by a teacher's authority — the film's point about where conspiracies actually hide.
