01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Artemis is the 300's non-commissioned heart: Leonidas's loyal captain and friend, the officer who turns the king's decisions into the phalanx's rotations. The film gives him its most quietly devastating subplot — his son Astinos marches in the line because the father could not leave him behind and would not — and when a Persian rider's blade takes the boy's head in a skirmish's confusion, Artemis's grief is conducted the Spartan way: in formation, at volume, and then back to work. He dies with the 300 in the last stand, having buried his son inside the same battle that buries him.

ROLE

The foreman of the line

Supporting; Leonidas's captain and the phalanx's working commander.

DEFINING TRAIT

Grief, kept in formation

Artemis's loss is the film's test of its own doctrine: the agoge's product, mourning at his post.

FATE

Killed in the final stand

He dies with the 300 at Thermopylae, days after his son.

Place in the story

Artemis is where the film stores the stand's cost in its most legible form — not the king's glory or the narrator's story, but a father's roster with one name struck through, mid-battle, by chance.

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

Artemis marches as Leonidas's captain — present at the choosing of the 300, where his son Astinos is among the picked, the boy included because his father, in the film's account, could neither leave him behind nor deny him the honour.

At the Hot Gates he runs the battle's working level: the rotations of the phalanx, the reading of each assault, the line's discipline through the infantry, the arrows and the Immortals. During a skirmish, a Persian rider's blade beheads Astinos in the confusion; Artemis, watching from formation, cries out for his son — and the battle, and the film, move on at the speed of the phalanx.

He fights through the remaining assaults and stands in the last morning's remnant, dying with Leonidas and the 300 under the final arrow storm.

04 / PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Psychology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation, grounded in the character's on-screen actions.

Professional

Artemis's register is competence: he is the officer the king's will passes through to become the line's motion.

A father in the ranks

His one indulgence — his son beside him — is the film's most human personnel decision, and its price is exacted in front of him.

Enduring

His grief is neither hidden nor indulged; the film shows it absorbed into duty at formation speed, which is its portrait of what the agoge costs the men it makes.
