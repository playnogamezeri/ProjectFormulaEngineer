01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Cooper is the tour's id and its travel agent: it is Cooper who warns Scotty that "Mike" is probably a predator — the advice that produces the cruel email — and Cooper who then books the courier flights, adopts the hooligan firm, and treats the continent as a reward owed to American repression. His own education is the film's slyest: the sex tourist keeps failing upward into friendship, surviving Amsterdam's Club Vandersexxx by a safe word he cannot pronounce, Bratislava by accident, and the Vatican by detention — and flies home having acquired, in Jenny, the one thing he never put on the itinerary. He ends the film on an open phone line, dating Jenny, calling Scotty's name into a room where history is being made without him.

ROLE

The accomplice

Co-protagonist; best friend, logistics officer and comic engine of the quest — and, by his warning, its co-author.

DEFINING TRAIT

Appetite with luck

Cooper's philosophy is acquisition — of experiences, primarily — and his survival record suggests the continent rewards the shameless.

FATE

The boyfriend

Flies home with Jenny, to Scotty's eventual telephone astonishment: the tourist, domesticated by his own tour.

Place in the story

Cooper is the film's control for Scotty: same tour, opposite errand. Where Scotty travels as penance, Cooper travels as appetite — and the film, scrupulously, gives the appetite its own rewards and its own invoice (an unpronounceable safe word).

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

Cooper's film opens at Scotty's side through the dumping and the song, and reaches its hinge at Scotty's computer: reading Mieke's kind email over his friend's shoulder, Cooper supplies the interpretation — "Mike" is a man, probably a predator, certainly a danger — that turns Scotty's drunken reply vicious. When Bert's translation reverses everything, Cooper simply reverses with it, and becomes the expedition's organiser: the Midnite Express courier scheme is his, the London-instead-of-Berlin improvisation his, the adoption of Maynard's firm his natural diplomacy.

The tour is his element and his education. In Amsterdam the party's catastrophe — Jamie robbed of all money, passports and tickets — strands them into the hitchhike that lands them in Bratislava, where the exchange rate makes paupers into princes for one absinthe night. At Club Vandersexxx, Cooper's itinerary reaches its logical destination and its punchline: the establishment's elaborate attentions proceed past his wishes because the safe word is unpronounceable by an American mouth. In Rome he shares the Vatican fiasco — the bell, the balcony, detention by the Swiss Guards, liberation by hooligans.

On the flight home, Cooper and Jenny give in to their urges in the plane's lavatory — the tour's last conversion. By the fall he is dating her, and the film's final joke is his: on the phone with Scotty at Oberlin, asking what the new roommate looks like, calling his friend's name into an open line while the answer happens off-mic.

04 / PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Psychology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation, grounded in the character's on-screen actions.

The id, friendly

Interpretation: Cooper wants what he wants without a theory of it, which makes him the film's purest tourist: Europe is not a place to him but a permission. The film neither punishes the attitude nor endorses it — it simply invoices it at a dominatrix's establishment.

Loyalty, unreflective

He never apologises for the warning, because it never occurs to him that the warning was the disaster; he simply shows up for the repair with the same totality. The film presents this as friendship's actual mechanics: presence over bookkeeping.

Failing upward

Every Cooper scheme lands one country off and one experience up: London for Berlin, Bratislava for anywhere, a girlfriend out of a group tour. His arc is the film's argument that appetite, lucky and loyal, is its own itinerary.

05 / ABILITIES & SKILLS

Abilities, Skills & Weaknesses

LOGISTICS

The courier scheme

He gets two broke graduates across an ocean on a package-carrying fare: the quest exists because Cooper can book anything.

DIPLOMACY

Firms and strangers

He befriends a hooligan firm in one pub night and rides their loyalty to the Vatican: people are his native language.

SURVIVAL

Club Vandersexxx

He endures Amsterdam's most professional establishment and its safe-word regime — barely, and by rescue.

WEAKNESS

Advice

His interpretations are confident, instant and — at the film's hinge — catastrophically wrong. The predator warning is the plot.

08 / SYMBOLISM & THEMES

Symbolism & Themes

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation.

The unpronounceable word

Cooper's Amsterdam ordeal is the film's emblem for his whole philosophy: the experiences he collects come with procedures he cannot be bothered to learn, in languages he cannot speak. Interpretation: the continent keeps handing him its instruction manuals, unread.

The open phone line

The film's last sound of Cooper is his voice calling Scotty's name into a call nobody is answering — the best friend, for once, outside the room where the story ends.

09 / ANALYSIS & INVESTIGATION

Analysis & Investigation

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial analysis. Documented facts are cited to the sources in Section 13; readings of the character's function are the archive's own.

Cause and cure

Cooper is a rare construction: the sidekick as co-author of the premise. His warning is not a throwaway gag but the plot's efficient cause, and the film never stages a reckoning for it — an honesty about friendship the archive notes: most disasters between friends are never invoiced, only outlasted.

Pitts's register

The performance runs at one cheerful pressure throughout, which lets the film modulate around him: Scotty's sincerity and Jenny's dryness both read against Cooper's weather.

12 / LEGACY & SIGNIFICANCE

Legacy & Significance

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Cooper endures as the genre's definitive best-friend id — the warning, the booking and the grin. His arc's quiet payoff, ending the film in the relationship he never sought, is the part of EuroTrip that its cult audience cites as the film's hidden sincerity.
