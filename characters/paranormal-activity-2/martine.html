01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Martine is the prequel's dismissed expert: a Spanish-speaking housekeeper and nanny who walks into the Carlsbad house and diagnoses it correctly before the haunting has properly begun — burning sage, praying, naming the presence as an evil to be cleansed. Daniel fires her for it. The film then spends an hour proving her right at escalating cost, and recalls her for the endgame: it is Martine who prepares the cross, Martine who conducts the exorcism's rite, and Martine who assists at the transfer — the film's uncomfortable acknowledgement that its one functioning authority is the employee it sacked. Her file is the archive's study of expertise without standing: she is right early, right throughout, and powerless at the one moment her knowledge might have governed the decision — the choice of the demon's destination is Daniel's, and the film gives her no veto over it.

ROLE

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

Martine's documented course begins with an arrival and an immediate reading: hired as housekeeper and nanny in the Carlsbad house, she senses what is in it — the film shows her with sage and prayers before the incidents have escalated past ambiguity — and names the presence, in her own terms, as malevolent and requiring cleansing. Daniel, the reasonable man, lets her go for superstition: the film's first priced decision, recorded on his page and hers. She exits the household's employ and its story, and the haunting proceeds through the space her practices might have contested — the archive is careful to record this as sequence, not as proven counterfactual: the film never states her cleansing would have held, only that she was the one person acting on a correct diagnosis while it was cheap.

She is recalled at the crisis, on the convert's terms: after the dragging, Daniel contacts her, and Martine prepares the cross and the rite. Her terms are recorded with a professional's precision — the demon can be driven from Kristi; Kristi will retain no memory of the possession — and the rite is performed in the basement darkness, Daniel wielding the cross she prepared while the house shakes. Then the transfer: the presence is to be passed to Katie, by the burning of the childhood photograph, and Martine assists at the rite that does it. The film does not record her arguing the destination — Ali's protest is the one on the record — and the archive files her part honestly: the expert conducted the mechanism; the choice of its target was her employer's. After that night she leaves the film's record entirely. She is not in the house on October 9, and the film gives her no reaction to the outcome her rite made possible.

The biography above is an editorial paraphrase of the documented plot accounts (Wikipedia and the cited sources), not a scene-by-scene transcript.

04 / PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Psychology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation, grounded in the character's on-screen actions.

The professional

Interpretation: Martine treats the haunting the way a tradesperson treats a fault — identified, named, priced, addressed with the correct tools. Her lack of astonishment is her credential: this is, to her, a known category of problem, and the household's astonishment is merely its inexperience showing.

Dignity under dismissal

Fired for the diagnosis that proves correct, she returns when recalled without triumph or reproach on the record: the film gives her the expert's revenge — being needed — and she declines to spend it on anything but the work.

The instrument's limit

At the transfer she performs the rite her employer directs at the target he chooses. The archive reads her compliance without inventing a protest the film does not record: her authority was always technical, never moral, in the household's hierarchy — which is the hierarchy's indictment, not hers.

05 / ABILITIES & SKILLS

Abilities, Skills & Weaknesses

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
THE READING

Diagnosis on arrival

She identifies the presence's nature before the security system has produced a single persuasive frame: the film's fastest and most accurate intelligence, delivered by its lowest-paid employee.

THE RITE

Working theology

Sage, prayer, the prepared cross, the exorcism's terms stated in advance and met exactly — no memory for Kristi, the presence driven out: her practices are the only supernatural technology in the film that performs as specified.

THE TERMS

A professional's precision

She states what the rite will and will not do before it is done — the franchise's rarest commodity, an honest account of a supernatural procedure's effects, from the person performing it.

WEAKNESS

No standing

She can be fired, and is; she can be recalled, and is; she cannot set the terms of her own employment of knowledge — including the transfer's destination. Her expertise is real and her authority is borrowed, and the film's catastrophe is assembled in the gap.

The sage

Smoke in a prosperous kitchen, and a dismissal: the symbol of the film's whole epistemology — the correct instrument, in the wrong employee's hands, at the wrong price point to be believed.

The prepared cross

The exorcism's weapon is made by the housekeeper and wielded by the homeowner: the symbol of the household's division of supernatural labour — her knowledge, his authority, a child's photograph as the bill.

Her Spanish goes unsubtitled at key beats: the symbol of knowledge present in the house and inaccessible to it — heard, like everything Martine says, at a discount.

09 / ANALYSIS & INVESTIGATION

Analysis & Investigation

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial analysis. Documented facts are cited to the sources in Section 13; readings of the character's function are the archive's own.

Expertise without standing

The archive's central file on Martine: the film constructs a perfect test of whether being right matters, and answers it in the negative for exactly as long as the right person is an employee. Her diagnosis is free and is refused; her rite is expensive and is purchased; her judgement on the rite's use is never solicited at any price. The analysis: Martine is the film's measure of the household's real hierarchy, in which knowledge is a service and authority is a property right — and the haunting, which respects neither, collects from both.

The rite's honest bookkeeping

Martine is the only character who states a supernatural procedure's effects in advance and is borne out exactly — the memory wipe, the expulsion, the transfer's mechanics. The analysis notes the film's respect embedded in that precision: her tradition is presented as a technology with known parameters, in a film where the security system — the other technology — records everything and understands nothing.

Cortez's Martine

Vivis Cortez plays Martine; the Galaxy's film page frames her as dismissed by Daniel for superstition and recalled when the footage leaves him no category left. The archive notes the role's function in the production's design: the prequel's expansion of the first film's world required an authority the original never had, and the film deliberately lodges it in the character with the least power to enforce it — the theme is the casting.
