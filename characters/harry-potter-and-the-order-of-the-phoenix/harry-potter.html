01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Harry spends this film being punished for telling the truth. He watched Voldemort return and kill Cedric Diggory; his government's response is a smear campaign, a rigged hearing, a torturer installed as his teacher, and detention lines carved into his hand by a quill that writes in his blood: I must not tell lies. Dumbledore — fearing the mental link between Harry and Voldemort — will not meet his eyes. His one counter-institution is Dumbledore's Army, the secret class he teaches in a hidden room. And the link that isolates him is also the enemy's instrument: a staged vision of Sirius tortured lures him to the Department of Mysteries, where the prophecy is lost, Sirius is killed, and Harry learns the sentence his whole life has been serving — that neither he nor Voldemort can live while the other survives.

ROLE

The witness on trial

Protagonist; fifth-year student; founder-teacher of Dumbledore's Army; the prophecy's subject.

DEFINING TRAIT

Fury as integrity

Harry's anger this year is not a flaw in his testimony; it is his testimony — the film's argument that a truthful boy, systematically gaslit by his government, is entitled to his rage.

FATE

Vindicated, bereaved

Fudge himself sees Voldemort in the atrium — the smear collapses — but the price is Sirius, killed in the rescue Harry's false vision caused. He ends the film believed, and orphaned a second time.

Place in the story

Harry is the film's battleground in person: the Ministry fights over his credibility, Voldemort fights through his mind, and Dumbledore fights to keep both from using him. His arc — from isolated witness to teacher to lure to duellist's witness — is the saga's pivot from school story to war story.

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

The summer is a news drought: cut off at the Dursleys', scanning for word of the war only he saw begin. Dementors attack him and Dudley in Little Whinging; Harry's Patronus saves them both and earns him expulsion for underage magic — rescinded only after Dumbledore wins his rigged disciplinary hearing.

At 12 Grimmauld Place, headquarters of the refounded Order of the Phoenix, Sirius tells him Voldemort is seeking something he did not have before. Harry guesses a weapon. The truth — a prophecy — is withheld from him all year, by the man who loves him most, for reasons that nearly kill them both.

At Hogwarts the Ministry's appointee, Dolores Umbridge, refuses to teach defensive magic and punishes Harry's truth-telling with the blood quill. Dumbledore, fearing Voldemort will exploit the link between them, avoids Harry entirely — an isolation Harry reads as abandonment. With Ron and Hermione he answers the occupation himself: Dumbledore's Army, a secret defence class in the Room of Requirement, with Harry — the only one who has faced Voldemort — as its teacher.

The link cuts both ways: a vision of Voldemort's snake attacking Arthur Weasley lets the Order save his life. Dumbledore orders Occlumency lessons with Snape to close Harry's mind; they collapse in mutual violation after Harry glimpses, in the Pensieve, his father bullying a teenage Snape. The mind stays open.

During the O.W.L.s, a vision shows Sirius being tortured in the Department of Mysteries. It is a plant. Harry leads five friends on thestrals to the rescue; in the Hall of Prophecy he finds the orb recorded for him and Voldemort — neither can live while the other survives — and the Death Eaters' ambush. The Order arrives; Sirius, duelling his cousin Bellatrix, is killed and falls through the Veil. Harry chases Bellatrix, hears Voldemort bid him kill her, and cannot. Voldemort and Dumbledore duel in the atrium; Fudge arrives in time to see the Dark Lord himself. The prophecy is smashed in the fighting, but Dumbledore, who heard it made, tells Harry its terms at last — and the smear campaign ends the same night the godfather does.

04 / PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Psychology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation, grounded in the character's on-screen actions.

The witness's rage

Harry's fury is the film's moral instrument: every institution that fails him — Ministry, press, headmaster's silence — is indicted by it. Interpretation: the film sides with the anger; its adults keep apologizing for the conditions that produced it.

The teacher

In the DA, Harry discovers the saga's quietest fact about him: he is a gifted instructor — patient, exact, generous with praise — and leadership, not just survival, is his inheritance.

The open mind

His flaw is architectural: an unguarded heart that Voldemort can post visions into. The film's tragedy is that the same openness — love, loyalty, grief — is what finally expels Voldemort from his body in the atrium.

08 / SYMBOLISM & THEMES

Symbolism & Themes

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation.

The blood quill

The state's argument written on the witness's body: tell our lie, in your blood, until it scars. The saga's most economical image of institutional cruelty.

The thestrals

Winged horses visible only to those who have seen death — Harry's whole cohort can see them by the flight to London: innocence's census, taken mid-film.

The prophecy orb

A glass ball in a dark hall that two governments and a dark lord kill over — and which breaks unheard by its seekers. The film's verdict on destiny as an object: the words matter; the relic is glass.

09 / ANALYSIS & INVESTIGATION

Analysis & Investigation

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial analysis. Documented facts are cited to the sources in Section 13; readings of the character's function are the archive's own.

The politics of the witness

Order of the Phoenix is the saga's explicitly political film: a ministry in denial, a compliant press, a show hearing, a torturer with kitten plates. Analysis: Harry's arc is the dissident's — discredit, isolate, prosecute, and finally vindicate at a price — and the film's anger at its institutions is what distinguishes it from the series' earlier, cosier authoritarian jokes.

The withheld truth

Dumbledore's silence is the film's most debated adult decision: strategically sound, humanly catastrophic. The archive records the film's own verdict — spoken by Dumbledore himself in the aftermath: he cared more for Harry's happiness than his safety, and the invoice was Sirius.

11 / BEHIND THE CHARACTER

Behind the Character

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Production

Directed by David Yates — his first of four Potter films — from Michael Goldenberg's screenplay, adapting the series' longest novel into its then-second-shortest film; the compression is the adaptation's documented signature.

Performance

Radcliffe's fifth outing was widely noted as his strongest to that point — the anger written into the year gave the performance its register.

12 / LEGACY & SIGNIFICANCE

Legacy & Significance

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This is the film where Harry Potter becomes a war story: its Harry — smeared, scarred, bereaved and vindicated — is the saga's pivot, and Radcliffe's furious witness remains the performance the later films are built on.
