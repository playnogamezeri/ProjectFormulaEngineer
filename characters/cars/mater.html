01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Mater is the first resident to treat McQueen as a person rather than a problem, and he does it within minutes of the impoundment — appointing himself the prisoner's best friend on no evidence and less encouragement. A tow truck of cheerful rust and bottomless local knowledge, he supplies the film's comedy (tractor tipping at midnight, backwards driving as a philosophy) and its emotional control group: Mater's friendship is the one transaction in the film with no invoice attached. The ending prices it exactly: when McQueen, offered the sport's greatest sponsorship, may ask Tex Dinoco for anything, he asks for a helicopter ride for Mater.

ROLE

The first friend

Principal supporting; the town's welcome, personified and rusted.

DEFINING TRAIT

Unconditional everything

Mater's loyalty is not a virtue he practises but a condition he has: he is incapable of the film's other economy, and the film knows it is richer for him.

FATE

Airborne

Pit crew at the tiebreaker; rewarded with the Dinoco helicopter ride — "the happiest tow truck in the world," by his own filing.

Place in the story

Mater is Radiator Springs' control condition: the measure of what the Interstate world has forgotten how to do, which is to be someone's friend before finding out whether it pays.

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

Mater meets McQueen at the impound lot and adopts him on sight, unbothered by the prisoner's status or fame. He is the town's informal orientation programme: the junkyard, the local customs, and — at midnight — tractor tipping, the sport of sneaking up on sleeping tractors and honking them over, with the combine harvester Frank as its occupational hazard.

Through the sentence, Mater is McQueen's daily evidence that the town's economy runs on different fuel: he offers friendship without terms, teaches the backward-driving philosophy that will later save McQueen's race (the rear-view mirrors speech, delivered as folk wisdom), and never once prices the relationship.

When the media extract McQueen, Mater's goodbye is the town's saddest beat. At the tiebreaker he travels with the residents as pit crew — his backwards expertise now professional equipment — and after the race he receives his payment in the only currency he would have chosen: a ride in the Dinoco helicopter, shouting his joy over the stadium.

04 / PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Psychology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation, grounded in the character's on-screen actions.

Radically unguarded

Mater says everything he thinks, loves everything he meets, and has no detectable strategy — the film's one character the Interstate's economy cannot parse.

Competent where it counts

Beneath the rust: the best backwards driver alive, a professional tower, and the town's institutional memory of joy.

Loyal at zero cost-benefit

His friendship precedes McQueen's redemption and survives his extraction; it is the town's thesis with a tow hook.

05 / ABILITIES & SKILLS

Abilities, Skills & Weaknesses

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
DRIVING

Backwards, supremely

His rearward driving — "I don't need to know where I'm going, I just need to know where I've been" — becomes McQueen's race-saving technique against Chick's blocking.

LOCAL KNOWLEDGE

The town, whole

Every resident, custom and tractor in Radiator Springs is his filing system.

FRIENDSHIP

Instant and permanent

The film's rarest skill, practised without training or terms.

LIMIT

No guile at all

Mater cannot scheme, dissemble or negotiate; in the film's market scenes he is a spectator, which is precisely his function.

08 / SYMBOLISM & THEMES

Symbolism & Themes

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation.

Backwards driving

Mater's philosophy — knowing where you have been rather than where you are going — is the film's Route 66 doctrine stated as a driving tip, and it wins the race's decisive exchange.

Rust

Mater's decay is worn as comfort: the film's design argues that a thing kept and used outranks a thing polished and parked — the town's whole case, in one truck's paintwork.

11 / BEHIND THE CHARACTER

Behind the Character

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

A persona, animated

Mater was built around Larry the Cable Guy's stand-up character; the role expanded during production as the filmmakers found the warmth under the act, and he became the franchise's co-lead and the star of its spin-off shorts.

The real truck

Mater's design draws on a 1951 International Harvester boom truck found by the production's Route 66 research trips — the character is a souvenir of the road the film is about.

12 / LEGACY & SIGNIFICANCE

Legacy & Significance

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Mater became one of the most popular animated characters of his decade — the franchise's comic lead, its shorts' star and its theme-park face — on the strength of the film's simplest idea: a friend with no terms.
