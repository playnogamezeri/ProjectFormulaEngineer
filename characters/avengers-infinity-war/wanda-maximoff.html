01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Wanda Maximoff is played by Elizabeth Olsen. Living quietly with Vision in Edinburgh — two fugitives from the Accords’ world, stealing a life together — she is pulled into the war when Thanos’s lieutenants come for the Mind Stone in Vision’s forehead. Wanda is the stone’s only solvent: her power, born of the stone itself, can destroy it. The film’s climax asks her to perform the destruction on the man she loves, twice — once successfully, and once into the teeth of time’s reversal.

ROLE

The executioner

The film page’s card names her function exactly: the one weapon Earth has against the Gauntlet’s completion is her willingness to kill the person she loves most, on schedule, under fire.

DEFINING TRAIT

Love as the instrument

Her power and her grief are the same faculty. The film’s climax is built from the discovery that the stone can only be destroyed by the person for whom destroying it costs most.

ARC IN ONE LINE

From stolen peace to double widowhood

She begins the film hiding a domestic happiness and ends it having killed Vision twice in one minute — once by her hand, once by Thanos’s reversal — before the Snap grants her the mercy of following him.

Place in the story

Wanda carries the Wakanda plot’s emotional weight the way Gamora carries Titan’s: she is the price-payer of the Earth-side war. Rogers’s doctrine — we don’t trade lives — exists to spare her this act; the film honors the doctrine by making the act necessary anyway, and then honors her by letting her complete it. Thanos’s reversal is the film’s cruelest single gesture, and it is aimed at her.

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

In Edinburgh, Wanda and Vision are ambushed by Proxima Midnight and Corvus Glaive for the Mind Stone. Wounded, Vision proposes the film’s recurring bargain in its first form: destroy the stone, and him with it, before Thanos can take it. Rogers’s fugitives rescue them, and in Wakanda the bargain is refused and replaced with Shuri’s extraction — time bought by an army, so that no one has to be traded.

Wanda’s post is the laboratory’s guard: she must stay with Vision while Shuri works, though the battle outside is collapsing — and when it collapses entirely, she leaves the post and enters the field, turning the tide single-handedly in the film’s great shot of her descending on the Wakandan line. It is the war’s most powerful individual intervention by any hero, and it costs the extraction its guard.

Thanos arrives. With the extraction unfinished and no time left, Vision asks her — again, and now without alternative — and Wanda performs the act: weeping, holding Thanos off with one hand and pouring her power into the stone with the other, she destroys the Mind Stone and Vision with it. It is the film’s one completed heroic task. Thanos, almost gently, tells her he understands — and uses the Time Stone to reverse it: Vision restored, the stone ripped from his forehead, the act unhappened in front of her.

The Snap follows within the minute. Wanda, kneeling by Vision’s grey body, disintegrates — the film’s own account calls it a mercy: she is granted to follow him within the minute, rather than survive him twice-killed.

04 / PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Psychology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation, grounded in the character's on-screen actions.

The reluctant weapon

Wanda’s power is the largest on the Earth-side field and her desire to use it the smallest; she is a person who wanted a kitchen in Edinburgh, conscripted by her own capabilities into the war’s hardest job.

Devotion as capability

The film’s bleakest mechanism: the destruction only works because she loves him — her power over the stone is kinship, and the act is powered by the very bond it ends.

Endurance past the reversal

She does not break after the reversal, though the film gives her every right; her remaining minutes are spent at the body, and her dusting is staged as rest rather than defeat.

05 / ABILITIES & SKILLS

Abilities, Skills & Weaknesses

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
POWER

Stone-born energy manipulation

Telekinesis, energy projection and flight at a scale that turns a collapsing battle alone; she holds Thanos at bay one-handed while destroying a stone with the other — the film’s ceiling for a single hero’s output.

THE SOLVENT

The Mind Stone’s destroyer

Her power, derived from the stone, is its unique counter: she is the only being in the film who can unmake an Infinity Stone.

WEAKNESS

The price is personal

Her capability can only be exercised on the person she loves, at his request, while he watches — the film designs her power’s use as an act of grief, not of war.

08 / SYMBOLISM & THEMES

Symbolism & Themes

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation.

The executioner as lover

Wanda embodies the film’s counter-arithmetic to Thanos: he sacrifices what he loves for the universe; she is asked to sacrifice what she loves to refuse his universe — the same act, mirrored, and the film’s sympathy is decided entirely by which of them weeps usefully and which weeps in vain. Both weep. Only hers is reversed.

09 / ANALYSIS & INVESTIGATION

Analysis & Investigation

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial analysis. Documented facts are cited to the sources in Section 13; readings of the character's function are the archive's own.

Narrative function: the completed task, reversed

Wanda is the film’s proof that the heroes’ failure is not incompetence. Investigation of the Wakanda climax shows she is the one principal who fully succeeds — stone destroyed, price paid, on time — and the film then deploys the Time Stone specifically to annul a success rather than a failure. The archive reads this as the Snap’s emotional groundwork: by the time Thanos snaps, the audience has already watched victory itself reversed, and understands the rules of the new universe — nothing the heroes complete is safe.

11 / BEHIND THE CHARACTER

Behind the Character

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Portrayal

Elizabeth Olsen plays Wanda’s climax in a single sustained register of grief under load; the sequence — destroying the stone while repelling Thanos — is the production’s most effects-dense emotional staging, built to keep her face, not the spectacle, as its center.

12 / LEGACY & SIGNIFICANCE

Legacy & Significance

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Wanda’s Infinity War file is the saga’s purest tragedy in miniature: a complete arc — love, duty, sacrifice, success — annulled by time and closed by mercy-dust. She exits the film as the hero who did everything asked of her, which is precisely why her story becomes the franchise’s standing wound: the one the later saga keeps returning to, because it was never a failure and therefore can never be filed as one.
