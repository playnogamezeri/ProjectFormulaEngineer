01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Crash and Eddie are the possum family's foreign service: the brothers who raised — with their mother — a mammoth, and who process the entire flood as an extreme sport. They enter the film playing Whac-A-Mole with a sloth and a tiger, and they never once modulate: every cliff is a ramp, every predator a heckling opportunity, every catastrophe a venue. Underneath the act is the film's family thesis in its purest form — these two are Ellie's brothers not by species but by upbringing and by errand, and when the cave traps her it is the brothers who run the warning that saves her life.

Fearlessness as a family style

FATE

Saved by the tiger, saviours of the mammoth

Place in the story

Crash & Eddie carry the film's adoption argument in person: if Ellie is a possum, these are what possums are — and the herd's acceptance of the brothers is the acceptance of her whole upbringing. Their duo page is the Meltdown batch's family file.

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

Crash and Eddie's backstory is the film's founding adoption: their mother took in an orphaned mammoth calf in a blizzard and raised her as a possum among possums, and the brothers grew up with a sister the size of a house and the habits of the family. They meet the herd as tormentors — a Whac-A-Mole routine at Diego and Sid's expense — and join the evacuation as Ellie's escort, converting the disaster into a tour: cliffs ridden, ice surfed, predators taunted at point-blank range.

Their comedy is the film's pressure valve, but the climax assigns them its most serious errand. When the geyser quarrel splits the herd and the dam fails with Ellie trapped in a cave, it is Crash and Eddie who escape through the small hole, cross the flood zone, and fetch Manny — the rescue's entire logistics performed by the two smallest animals in the valley. The flood then collects its price from them in turn: swept into the water, they are saved by the last rescuer available, Diego, whose swim is their lives' receipt. They end the film where the film has argued they belong: inside the merged herd, brothers to a mammoth, uncles-in-spirit to whatever comes next, unmodulated.

04 / PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Psychology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation, grounded in the character's on-screen actions.

Brotherhood as a sport

Interpretation: the duo's daredevilry is a family dialect — the language their household speaks. Taunting a predator is, for Crash and Eddie, simply how possums say good morning; the film uses their recklessness as the measure of Ellie's upbringing's success.

Loyalty at full speed

Their warning run is the film's purest act of family: no deliberation, no odds assessed — the sister is trapped, so the brothers run. The archive notes that the film gives its most consequential errand to its least deliberative characters on purpose.
