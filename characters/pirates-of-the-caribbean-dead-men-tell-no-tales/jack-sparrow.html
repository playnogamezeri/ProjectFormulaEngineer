01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Captain Jack Sparrow opens the fifth film as a cautionary tale: the bank robbery in Saint Martin nets an empty vault and a dragged safe, his crew deserts him over unpaid shares, and Jack — drunk, shipless and out of legend — trades his compass for a bottle. The compass was the lock on the Devil's Triangle; giving it away frees Armando Salazar. The film is the story of Jack's luck being rebuilt the hard way: pursued by his own past, adopted by two young people with errands of their own, and finally restored — compass, Pearl and self — by the sacrifice of the rival who knew him best.

ROLE

The fallen trickster

Protagonist; the quest's necessary rogue and its least reliable instrument.

DEFINING TRAIT

Luck as a renewable resource

Jack's fortune is not a trait but a tide: the film is built on it going out and coming back in.

FATE

Restored

He sails out as captain of the Black Pearl once more, compass in hand, Barbossa's crew at his back.

Place in the story

Jack is the hinge between the saga's generations: Henry and Carina need the legend, and the film keeps showing them the ruin instead. His arc — from pawned compass to the Pearl's wheel — is the frame inside which the younger quest and Barbossa's farewell both take place.

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

Decades before the film, the young Jack Sparrow — a pirate lad aboard a ship Salazar was destroying — tricked the Spanish captain into sailing the Silent Mary into the Devil's Triangle, where Salazar and his crew died and were cursed as the undead. Jack's crewmates rewarded him with the tokens of his legend: the compass, the hat, the beads — and Salazar, mocking the boy perched in the rigging, gave him his name: Sparrow.

In the film's present, Jack's luck has run out. The Saint Martin bank robbery collapses into farce — the vault is empty, the safe is dragged through the town, and his crew, Gibbs among them, abandons him over the missing shares. Drunk and shipless, Jack trades his compass for a drink. The betrayal of the compass breaks the Devil's Triangle open: Salazar walks free.

Arrested and sentenced to hang alongside the astronomer Carina Smyth, Jack is rescued by Henry Turner and his old crew, and sails on the Dying Gull — pursued by Salazar, whose ghosts cannot step on land, and by the British under Scarfield. Jack's contributions to the quest are equal parts knowledge and ballast: he knows the sea's rules (ghosts, curses, the Trident's price) and keeps nearly getting the company killed violating them. Barbossa, initially hunting him for Salazar, switches sides, returns his compass and restores the Black Pearl from its bottle.

At the Trident's island the sea parts to reveal Poseidon's tomb. Henry breaks the Trident, and every curse at sea breaks with it — Salazar mortal again, Will Turner freed. In the climb out of the closing sea, Salazar attacks; Barbossa sacrifices himself to save his daughter Carina, and Jack — hauling Henry and Carina up the Pearl's anchor chain — is a witness to the saga's great farewell. He sails out restored: the Pearl his, the compass his, Barbossa's crew electing to follow him, and a horizon the film leaves open.

04 / PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Psychology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation, grounded in the character's on-screen actions.

Luck as identity

Interpretation: Jack experiences his luck the way other men experience their souls — its absence in this film is an identity crisis, not a cash-flow problem. The crew's desertion hurts him precisely because it is evidence: the legend's credit has finally run out, and the film is about whether the man underneath it is still worth a ship.

The compass as conscience

He trades the one object that points at what he wants most, for the thing he wants most in that moment: a drink. Interpretation: the film literalises his condition — Jack at his lowest cannot distinguish appetite from desire, and the compass, honestly consulted, would have pointed anywhere but the bottle.

Generosity in the wreckage

Even diminished, the instincts hold: he saves Henry and Carina repeatedly, usually while insisting he is doing something else. The film's verdict arrives with Barbossa's crew: men who served the saga's other captain choose Jack when it matters, because underneath the luck there was always a captain.

08 / SYMBOLISM & THEMES

Symbolism & Themes

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation.

The compass

Interpretation: the compass that points to what the heart most wants is the saga's conscience-object, and this film is about its misuse: traded for appetite, it frees the past; returned by a dying rival, it restores the future. Jack's recovery is measured entirely in compass.

The name

The flashback reveals "Sparrow" began as an enemy's mockery — a bird in the rigging. Interpretation: the legend was always an insult Jack chose to keep, which is the most Jack Sparrow fact in the saga.

09 / ANALYSIS & INVESTIGATION

Analysis & Investigation

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial analysis. Documented facts are cited to the sources in Section 13; readings of the character's function are the archive's own.

Function: the deconstructed mascot

The fifth film audits its own franchise figure: what is Jack Sparrow without luck, ship, crew or sobriety? The analysis the archive records is that the film's answer — a knowledgeable, intermittently brave drunk whom young people can still use — is less flattering and more interesting than the legend, and that the ending's restoration works because Barbossa's sacrifice, not Jack's charm, pays for it.

Jack as inheritance

Henry and Carina are both, in different ways, collecting a father from the sea — Will, and Barbossa unknown. Jack is the saga's spare father: useless at the role's duties, present for its emergencies. The film's generational design makes him the bridge the children cross to reach the real ones.

11 / BEHIND THE CHARACTER

Behind the Character

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Johnny Depp

Depp's fifth outing as Sparrow; the young-Jack flashback used digital de-aging on the actor, a documented set-piece of the production. The film was directed by Joachim Rønning and Espen Sandberg and released May 26, 2017.

A title with two names

The film was released in some territories as Salazar's Revenge — an indication of how the production weighted Bardem's antagonist in its design.

12 / LEGACY & SIGNIFICANCE

Legacy & Significance

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Jack ends the fifth film where the first began — at the Pearl's wheel, compass in hand — but by inheritance rather than by theft: everything he has was given back to him by the dead. In the Galaxy's Pirates records, this page is the trickster's audit: the saga checking whether its mascot still floats, and answering, narrowly, yes.
