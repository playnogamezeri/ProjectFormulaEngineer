01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Nana first appeared in the original film as a one-scene gag: an elderly New Yorker who beats Alex senseless with her handbag in a subway. The sequel promotes the gag to a general staff. Stranded with a safari of tourists at the dammed river, Nana takes command — organizes the camp, builds the dam that causes the drought, and runs the tourists' resistance with a drill instructor's economy. And when the plot needs a usurper deposed, it is Nana — aimed at Makunga by Alex, who remembers exactly what she is — who administers the deposition personally: handbag, ruler, wet finger, ear-drag. The franchise's most dangerous predator was never on the reserve. She was on the tour.

ROLE

The tourists' commander

Safari strandee; dam-builder; kingmaker and king-remover.

DEFINING TRAIT

Competence without wonder

Nana processes lions, droughts and usurpations as municipal problems. Nothing in Africa surprises her; she has seen the subway.

FATE

The deposition, delivered

She subdues Makunga barehanded, her dam is destroyed and the river freed — her command ends with the tourists' rescue, her record against lions standing at two for two.

Place in the story

Nana is the sequel's secret weapon and its thesis about New York: the city that made Alex also made her, and of the two exports, the reserve has more to fear from the grandmother.

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

Nana's first-film file is a single legendary beat: a grandmother in a New York subway who, confronted by an escaped lion, beats him with her handbag and walks away. The sequel opens her file properly.

On safari in Africa, her tour's vehicles fail at the river. Nana takes command of the stranded tourists — organizes the camp, the labour and the survival plan, which includes damming the river for water. The dam, upstream of the reserve, is the drought's true author: the watering hole fails because Nana's camp is holding the river.

Alex, investigating the drought, is captured by the tourists. Zuba's rescue and Alex's dancing resolve the confrontation; the dam is destroyed in the climax and the river — and the watering hole — restored.

Makunga makes his stand for the throne. Alex, remembering the subway, tricks the usurper into Nana's reach. Her deposition is administered personally and completely: kicks, a ruler across the palm, the wet finger in the ear, and the alpha dragged off by that ear to the tourists' custody. The throne reverts. Nana's African campaign closes with two lions subdued in two films and no losses recorded.

04 / PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Psychology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation, grounded in the character's on-screen actions.

The municipal sublime

Nana's register is pure administration: lions are a pest-control category, droughts are logistics, usurpers are disorderly persons. Interpretation: the film's deepest joke about New York is that it produces citizens for whom Africa is a scheduling problem.

Economy of violence

Her fighting style is a grandmother's disciplinary repertoire scaled to lions — handbag, ruler, ear. Nothing is wasted; everything is humiliating, which is the point of the repertoire.

09 / ANALYSIS & INVESTIGATION

Analysis & Investigation

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial analysis. Documented facts are cited to the sources in Section 13; readings of the character's function are the archive's own.

The gag, promoted to structure

Nana's expansion is the sequel's boldest structural joke: a one-scene bit from the first film becomes the second film's deus ex machina, twice — her dam causes the drought, her handbag ends the usurpation. Analysis: the promotion works because the character never changes; the world simply escalates until her register is the appropriate one.

Gabrielli's tempo

The performance is mixed entirely at command volume; Nana's scenes play like the film's one documentary strand, which is why lions keep losing to her — she is the only participant not acting.
