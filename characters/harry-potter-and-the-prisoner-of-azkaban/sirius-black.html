01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Sirius Black is the film's wrong file: the Ministry's most wanted man, convicted without trial of betraying the Potters and murdering Peter Pettigrew and twelve bystanders, who walks out of Azkaban after twelve years because a newspaper photograph shows him the one thing worth escaping for — the rat on a schoolboy's shoulder, at Hogwarts, beside Harry. Everything the year believes about him is inverted: he is Harry's godfather, the Potters' chosen brother, innocent of every count, and hunting not the boy but the rat. Oldman plays him, per the Galaxy card, gaunt, feral and tender by turns — a man reassembled from a wanted poster into a godfather in the course of one scene in the Shrieking Shack — and the film's ending refuses him peace: unmasked but unexonerated, sentenced to the Kiss, freed by two children and a hippogriff, he escapes into fugitivity with a godson, a signature and a Firebolt to his name.

ROLE

The wrong file

Title figure and false antagonist; the year's hunted man is its most wronged, and its godfather.

DEFINING TRAIT

Innocence as sustenance

He survived the dementors on the one thought they could not take: knowing he was innocent was no happy memory, so it stayed his. The film's bleakest psychology, and its most hopeful.

FATE

Free and fugitive

Rescued from the Kiss by Harry and Hermione, flown out on Buckbeak: unexonerated, unbowed, and a godfather at last in practice.

Place in the story

Sirius is the film's investigation made flesh: every institution in the story — Ministry, press, school, friendship — holds a wrong file on him, and the year is the file's correction by the only court available, a shack full of schoolchildren. He is the archive's standing exhibit on the difference between the record and the truth.

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

Sirius's biography, as the film corrects it: at school he was a Marauder — Moony, Wormtail, Padfoot and Prongs, the map's authors — and James Potter's brother in all but blood, chosen godfather to Harry. When the Potters went into hiding, the Secret-Keeper's role was switched at Sirius's own suggestion to Peter Pettigrew, the friend no one would suspect. Pettigrew sold them within the week. Sirius, arriving at the ruin, understood everything and went hunting; Pettigrew, cornered in a street, shouted the betrayal to the crowd, blew the street apart, cut off his own finger and vanished down a drain as a rat — leaving Sirius laughing in the wreckage among thirteen bodies, in a state the Aurors read as madness. Twelve years in Azkaban followed, without trial.

What kept him sane, by his own account in the Shack, was the knowledge of his innocence — not a happy thought, so the dementors could not feed on it — and what freed him was the Daily Prophet: Fudge's inspection copy, carrying the Weasleys' photograph, a rat with a missing toe on a boy's shoulder, bound for Hogwarts where Harry was. Sirius thinned himself through the bars in dog form and swam for the mainland. The year of the film is his hunt: the Fat Lady's portrait slashed when she would not admit him, the dog at the Quidditch edges, Crookshanks recruited as his eyes inside the castle — and, on the execution evening, Ron dragged into the Willow as the rat's custodian, delivering Pettigrew to the Shack at last. There the file is corrected before witnesses: Lupin's embrace, the rat forced back into a man, the confession extracted, the execution stayed by Harry's mercy. Outside, the moon takes Lupin; Pettigrew escapes; the dementors take Sirius by the lake and the Kiss is scheduled — until three hours are returned by two children, and the prisoner of Azkaban flies out of the tower window on the other condemned innocent of the year, a hippogriff, with a godson's wave behind him.

04 / PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Psychology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation, grounded in the character's on-screen actions.

The laugh in the wreckage

Interpretation: the film's Sirius is a man whose grief has always been misread as guilt — the laugh that convicted him was horror arriving in the wrong shape. Oldman plays the whole character inside that misreading: feral where the world expects a monster, tender where it has forgotten to look.

Vengeance, governed

He comes to the Shack to commit the murder he was imprisoned for — Pettigrew's — and the film marks his growth in its staying: Harry's word stops him, and he accepts the Azkaban he escaped from as Pettigrew's proper destination. The godfather outranks the avenger, in the end, by one boy's sentence.

The godfather's tenderness

His offer to Harry — a home, when this is over — is made with a shyness the wanted poster never advertised: twelve years of innocence sustained him, but it is the prospect of being family that the performance shows re-teaching him hope.

05 / ABILITIES & SKILLS

Abilities, Skills & Weaknesses

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
ANIMAGUS

The dog

Unregistered, self-taught: the form that thinned him out of Azkaban, walked him into Hogwarts, and read as the Grim to everyone watching — the year's most consequential disguise.

ENDURANCE

Twelve years among dementors

He survived the unsurvivable posting on a technicality of despair: innocence is not happiness, and so it could not be eaten.

DUELLIST AND MARAUDER

The old skills

A Marauder's full kit — the map's authorship, the Shack's passages, the castle's secrets — all retained through twelve years of the place designed to erase them.

WEAKNESS

The file

He cannot testify in his own defence anywhere that matters: every institution holds the wrong record, and his freedom at the end is a child's gift, not a court's correction.

08 / SYMBOLISM & THEMES

Symbolism & Themes

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation.

The Grim

Sirius's dog form is read all year as the omen of death — by Trelawney's tea leaves, by Harry's fear — and is in fact the omen of family: the film's neatest symbol of its thesis, that the record and the truth wear the same shape read differently.

Two innocents, one flight

He escapes on Buckbeak, the year's other wrongful conviction: the film pairs its innocents deliberately — the man and the creature, both condemned on theatre, both freed by children with borrowed time.

09 / ANALYSIS & INVESTIGATION

Analysis & Investigation

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial analysis. Documented facts are cited to the sources in Section 13; readings of the character's function are the archive's own.

Convicted by narrative

Sirius's case is the film's brief against story-shaped evidence: the laugh, the poster, the pub account — every proof against him is a reading, and every reading is wrong in the direction of drama. The archive files him as the series' standing warning that a good story is not a verdict.

Oldman's economy

The performance is built from the poster inward: the film gives him one long scene to be re-read in, and Oldman spends it on the Shack's registers — hunter, witness, defendant, godfather — in sequence, so the audience performs the file's correction in real time alongside the children.

11 / BEHIND THE CHARACTER

Behind the Character

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Gary Oldman

Oldman joined the series at Cuarón's invitation and has cited the role's appeal as playing a man the world had filed wrongly; his Sirius was immediately taken by readers as the books' gaunt godfather made flesh.

The Azkaban method

The film keeps Sirius's escape mechanics to his own Shack account — dog form, thinness, the swim — declining to stage what it presents as testimony: the archive's record keeps the same discipline.

12 / LEGACY & SIGNIFICANCE

Legacy & Significance

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Sirius endures as the series' great wrong file — the godfather assembled from a poster and a pub story into the saga's most loved fugitive in a single film. His Azkaban psychology — innocence as the one un-eatable thought — remains the franchise's bleakest and most quoted account of hope.
