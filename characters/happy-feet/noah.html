01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Noah leads the Elders who interpret Emperor Land's covenant: sing correctly, and the Great 'Guin provides the fish. When the fish fail, the covenant requires a cause, and Noah supplies one — the chick who will not sing, whose dancing offends the order of things. His prosecution of Mumble is the film's central injustice and its most recognisable mechanism: scarcity, doctrine, scapegoat. The film's ending is his education: confronted not with argument but with the aliens themselves, filmed and undeniable, Noah's theology collapses on camera, and the old prosecutor ends the story dancing with the accused.

ROLE

The prosecutor

Antagonist of the colony plot; the famine's official explanation, personified.

DEFINING TRAIT

Certainty as office

Noah does not investigate the famine; he adjudicates it. His authority depends on the verdict being available before the evidence is.

FATE

Reversed, and dancing

The humans' arrival and the restored fish overturn his verdict in public; he ends the film inside the celebration he tried to prevent.

Place in the story

Noah is the film's institutional antagonist — not a villain of appetite like a predator, but of interpretation. His page is where the archive files the story's politics: who gets to explain a disaster, and what happens to the explanation when the disaster's real cause swims into view with cameras.

Weaving plays Noah with a prophet's gravity — the verdicts land like weather. The archive notes the discipline of the writing: Noah is never given a selfish motive. 03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

Noah presides over Emperor Land's order: the heartsongs sung, the seasons kept, the Great 'Guin's favour maintained. Mumble is a problem in his filing system from the classroom onward — a chick who cannot sing is an anomaly; a young adult who dances in mating season, and starts the youth dancing with him, is a contagion. When the fish begin to fail, Noah's theology closes over the facts like ice over water: the famine is the price of the offence, and the offence has a name and a pair of feet.

The prosecution culminates in the exile. Memphis pleads the father's plea — the boy will stop — and Mumble himself refuses the bargain, declining to purchase his home with his nature. Noah pronounces the sentence the doctrine requires, and the colony watches its accused walk out to find, as he promises, the real cause. The record of Noah through the middle film is the record of an empty chair: the famine continues under his management, unexamined, while the exile investigates it.

The ending is the reversal, staged as spectacle: Mumble returns with the aliens behind him, the colony dances for their cameras, and the true cause of the famine — the trawlers, the taking fleets — is entered into evidence before the whole world, Noah's included. The fish are protected; the fish return. The film's last word on its prosecutor is not punishment but inclusion: the colony that dances at the end is the whole colony, Elders and accused together, and Noah's authority is last seen doing the only useful thing left to it — joining in.

04 / PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Psychology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation, grounded in the character's on-screen actions.

The sincere prosecutor

Interpretation: Noah's menace is his sincerity. He is not protecting a racket; he is protecting a covenant he believes keeps everyone alive, and he prosecutes Mumble the way a sea-wall holds back water — as a function. The film's argument is with the function, not the man.

Certainty's economy

His doctrine is cheap to run: it requires no expeditions, no evidence, no contact with the aliens — only a culprit. The archive notes that the film prices the alternative honestly: Mumble's method costs an exile, a journey and a captivity. Truth is the expensive option; Noah is what the cheap one looks like with a title.

05 / ABILITIES & SKILLS

Abilities, Skills & Weaknesses

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
AUTHORITY

The Elders' voice

He can exile a citizen and make the colony accept it: institutional power, exercised without a single soldier.

RHETORIC

The verdict form

His pronouncements convert anxiety into consensus — the famine's fear, given a name and a sentence, stops being a question.

WEAKNESS

No investigative instrument

His system has courts but no laboratories: when the facts arrive from outside it, the doctrine has no procedure except collapse.

08 / SYMBOLISM & THEMES

Symbolism & Themes

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation.

The covenant as a closed system

Interpretation: Noah's theology explains everything inside the colony and nothing outside it — it has no category for trawlers. The archive reads him as the film's portrait of any doctrine that survives by shrinking the world to the size of its explanations.

09 / ANALYSIS & INVESTIGATION

Analysis & Investigation

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial analysis. Documented facts are cited to the sources in Section 13; readings of the character's function are the archive's own.

Function: the mechanism, not the monster

The film is careful to give its predators (skuas, seals, whales) no malice and its prosecutor no appetite: Noah is a process wearing a penguin. The analysis the archive records is that this is the story's maturity — the famine's worst damage is done not by hunger but by the explanation of hunger, and the explanation is defeated not by a better sermon but by a research expedition.
