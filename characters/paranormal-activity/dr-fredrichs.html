01 / CHARACTER FILE

Overview

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Fredrichs is the film's entire professional class in one man, and his file is the record of expertise functioning perfectly and mattering not at all. Visit one: he diagnoses the case correctly in every particular — demon, attachment to Katie, nourishment by negativity, torment as intent — and issues the correct protocol: do not communicate with it, do not taunt it, call a demonologist. Visit two, weeks later, with every clause of the protocol violated: he walks in, reads the house's energy in seconds, apologises, and flees, stating that his presence now only enrages it. He is the only character in the film who is right about everything, and the plot is the measurement of what that was worth.

ROLE

The expert witness

Supporting; the diagnosis and the protocol, delivered once and vindicated nightly.

DEFINING TRAIT

Professional honesty

Fredrichs says what the case is, says what he cannot do, and — on the second visit — says so at the door, on his way out.

FATE

Withdraws from the case

He leaves the house on his second visit and does not return; he survives the film, which is more than his clients manage.

Place in the story

Fredrichs is the control instrument of the film's experiment: because his diagnosis is complete and correct in the first act, every subsequent escalation can be measured as a deviation from professional advice. His page is the protocol itself, filed as a person.

03 / BIOGRAPHY

Biography: Their Story

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋
REVEAL FULL CHARACTER STORY

Fredrichs is hired by Katie and visits the San Diego house early in the footage. He walks the rooms and delivers his assessment in full: Katie is haunted by a demon, not a ghost; it is attached to her and will follow her anywhere; it feeds off negative energy; its intent is to haunt and torment her. He advises the couple not to taunt or communicate with it, and recommends they contact a demonologist, Dr. Johann Averies, if help is needed. Micah is dismissive; the filming and the provocations continue.

Weeks later — the Ouija board bought and burned, the phenomena physical, the demonologist finally called and found to be out of the country — Fredrichs returns at the couple's plea. On entering, he is immediately overwhelmed by the house's demonic energy. He apologises and leaves despite their begging, explaining that his presence only makes the demon angrier. It is his last appearance; the haunting's final nights proceed without any professional attendance at all.

04 / PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY

Personality & Psychology

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial interpretation, grounded in the character's on-screen actions.

Exact

His diagnosis contains no hedging and no error the film ever exposes; even his estimate of his own uselessness, on the second visit, is precise.

Uncommercial

He does not sell the couple a cure, a cleansing or a return engagement; he refers them upward, to a specialist, which in his profession is the film's marker of the genuine article.

Honest under fear

His flight is not cowardice in the film's accounting — it is a professional judgment delivered at personal embarrassment: staying would worsen the case.

09 / ANALYSIS & INVESTIGATION

Analysis & Investigation

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

This section is editorial analysis. Documented facts are cited to the sources in Section 13; readings of the character's function are the archive's own.

The vindicated man

The archive files Fredrichs as the film's measurement device: a completely correct expert whose correctness changes nothing, because the household's decision-maker does not accept expertise as a category. His two visits bookend the escalation — before him, a haunting; after him, an occupancy — and the difference between the visits is entirely Micah's work.

12 / LEGACY & SIGNIFICANCE

Legacy & Significance

EXPAND SECTION DETAILS ＋

Fredrichs became the genre's template for the honest expert: right about everything, believed about nothing, and gone by the third act — the professional whose exit marks the exact depth of the protagonists' trouble.
