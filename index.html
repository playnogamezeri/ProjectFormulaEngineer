What kind of formula am I looking at?

Definition: establishes how a quantity is defined. Law: describes a well-supported relationship within a stated domain. Empirical model: fitted to observations. Approximation: simplifies a more complete model. Theoretical equation: follows from a framework and may have a limited or unsettled domain of application.

What this engine does — and does not do

V4 uses a curated set of explicit algebraic relationships and known rearrangements. It is not a general-purpose symbolic algebra system and does not infer arbitrary equations. Dimensional checks catch unit-category mismatches; they cannot prove that a physical model is appropriate. Review signs, assumptions, significant figures and boundary conditions before relying on a result.

Dimensional analysis is a filter, not a proof

A dimensionally consistent equation can still be scientifically wrong, incomplete, or used outside its validity range.
